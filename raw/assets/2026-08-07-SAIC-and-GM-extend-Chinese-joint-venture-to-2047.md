# SAIC and GM extend Chinese joint venture to 2047

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

Chinese carmaker SAIC Motor and US automotive group General Motors have extended the term of their Chinese joint venture, SAIC-GM, by 20 years to 2047. As part of the renewed agreement, the partners plan to introduce new electric models in China while expanding exports to international markets.

The contract extension builds on a partnership that began in June 1997. Following a comprehensive restructuring — including a $6 billion write-down by GM in January 2026 and several years of declining sales — the joint venture is intended to return to a more stable footing. The partners plan to strengthen cooperation by pooling resources across R&D, supply chains and international markets.

The JV's future strategy centres on electrification and greater local integration. It relies on the Pan Asia Technical Automotive Centre (PATAC) in Shanghai for vehicle development and introduced its Xiao Yao Super Hybrid and Electric Architecture in 2025. By 2030, SAIC-GM plans to launch at least 30 new NEVs (a category covering BEVs, PHEVs and range-extended EVs in China) across the Buick and Cadillac brands.

Beyond China, SAIC-GM is repositioning its operations as an export hub, planning to supply the Middle East, South America, Mexico, and markets in Africa and Asia. Exports to the US are not planned, partly due to the US ban on connected-vehicle imports from China. The first premium NEV earmarked for export is the Buick Electra E7, a plug-in hybrid luxury SUV with a combined range over 1,600 km, with exports starting in October.

SAIC-GM sold 34,773 vehicles in July 2026, down 17.7% year-on-year; over the first seven months, sales fell 7.45% to 265,927 units. This excludes SAIC-GM-Wuling, the partners' second JV, which sold ~789,000 vehicles from January–July 2026. Combined, the two JVs helped GM sell 1.9 million vehicles in China last year — still a 51% decline versus 2016.

SAIC also runs a 40+ year joint venture with Volkswagen, and in Europe is best known for its MG Motor passenger car brand and Maxus commercial vehicles.
