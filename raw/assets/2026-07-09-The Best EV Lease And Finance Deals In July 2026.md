# The Best EV Lease And Finance Deals In July 2026

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 2026-07-08 (updated)

---

The electric-vehicle tax credit is gone, but manufacturers are stepping up to offer incredible deals.

EV deals have followed a weird path over the last few years. Initially, a lack of choice stunted consumer options and led to middling deals. Then, as inventories expanded and the $7,500 tax credit "leasing loophole" took root, they became incredible. Then, last fall, the best deals disappeared.

That $7,500 tax credit to purchase or lease a qualifying EV went away at the end of September 2025. Since then, automakers have been upping their own incentives to provide a degree of compensation for consumers. But the degree to which automakers are improving their incentives varies significantly. Some automakers are holding the line, offering the same deals as before, but effectively $7,500 more expensive. Others are still pushing hard to increase their share.

Note that the deals can vary substantially by region. Most prices use a California zip code, so you could find better deals elsewhere (particularly in states with their own incentives). Despite the end of the federal EV incentive, the available lease offers are still pretty great. Some automakers, like Polestar, Kia, and Hyundai, are upping the ante with notable cash discounts.
