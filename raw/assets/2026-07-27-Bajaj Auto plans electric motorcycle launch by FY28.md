# Bajaj Auto plans electric motorcycle launch by FY28, to rev up capacity across EVs, CVs

**Author**: Amey, BikeDekho (quoting Bajaj CFO Dinesh Thapar to ET Auto) | **Date**: 2026-07-22

---

Bajaj has finally given us a clearer timeline for its first electric motorcycle. The company has confirmed that development is progressing steadily, with the bike expected to arrive around FY28 (April 2027 to March 2028). Interestingly, Bajaj will first launch the motorcycle in international markets before bringing it to India, marking the brand's entry into the budding electric motorcycle segment.

**When Is It Coming?**

Post Bajaj's recent investors call, Bajaj's Chief Financial Officer Dinesh Thapar shared a few key details with ET Auto and said, "Development of our electric motorcycle is well underway, with product work progressing steadily. We anticipate a launch around FY28, provided all goes to plan. Our initial focus will be on export markets, followed by the domestic market. Development work is already well underway and we are beginning to see a clear timeline for when the product will hit the market."

If everything stays on schedule, Bajaj's first electric motorcycle could make its global debut sometime between April 2027 and March 2028, with the India launch expected afterwards.

**What Could It Be?**

Bajaj hasn't revealed any details about its upcoming electric motorcycle just yet, but it has confirmed that the bike will first go on sale in overseas markets before making its way to India. Given Bajaj's strong foothold in Africa and Latin America, these regions are the most likely candidates to receive the motorcycle first.

As for the motorcycle itself, expectations point to a practical commuter product rather than a performance-focused one, offering performance comparable to a 150cc petrol-powered motorcycle while keeping running costs low. This strategy also makes sense given the influx of Chinese electric two-wheelers into African markets, where concerns about reliability, quality, and after-sales service remain — an opening for Bajaj to position itself as a trusted alternative, much as it did with the Chetak in India.

Electric scooters have already established themselves in India, but electric motorcycles remain a largely untapped space. New brands like Oben Electric and Revolt have entered the segment, but no mainstream two-wheeler manufacturer has yet launched a mass-market electric motorcycle. Royal Enfield's Flying Flea C6 arrived first but is positioned as a premium, niche product — leaving room for Bajaj to potentially become the first legacy brand to bring electric motorcycles into the mainstream, if pricing and performance are right.
