# BMW's First U.S.-Made EV Is Now Rolling Off The Assembly Line

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 2026-11-18

---

- BMW's plant in Spartanburg, South Carolina, is now building the company's new iX5 electric SUV.
- It's the first mass-produced EV built by BMW in the United States.
- The electric SUV will be joined by five more EVs by the end of the decade.

The first American-made electric BMW is now being assembled at the company's plant in Spartanburg, South Carolina. It's a big moment for the German automaker in the U.S., but just the beginning of a larger plan that includes no fewer than six U.S.-built EVs by the end of the decade.

Roughly 70 iX5 electric SUVs are rolling off the assembly line every day, alongside gas, diesel, and hybrid-powered models. All X5 powertrain versions are based on the same architecture, enabling shared assembly lines.

Initially, BMW's goal was to build no fewer than 100,000 iX5s in 2027 in Spartanburg, but that target has reportedly been revised to 60,000, according to Automotive News. A company representative said roughly one in four X5s will be electric for the near future.

"Currently, the market is looking like 20-25 percent EVs for the immediate three months," said Kavit Antani, general manager of vehicle assembly. "As the market sees these vehicles come through, it might shift."

Suppliers have reportedly been given a target of 175,000 EVs in 2028 and 2029, though the target could shift significantly.

The batteries come from a new plant 15 miles away in Woodruff, where the first mass-produced packs started their journey to Spartanburg earlier this week. The first Gen6 cylindrical cells will be sourced from AESC in China; toward the end of the decade LG Energy Solution will supply U.S. facilities with locally made cells, while Samsung SDI will supply European factories.

BMW is not backing down from its plan to build six EVs in America by 2030. The iX5 will likely be followed by the iX7, iX6, and a performance-oriented iX5 M, according to BMW Blog.

The 2027 BMW iX5 is powered by a 144-kWh battery that can be recharged from 10% to 80% in 22 minutes. Its estimated range is 450 miles, while its dual-motor powertrain delivers 570 hp and 594 lb-ft of torque. Pricing starts at $79,800.
