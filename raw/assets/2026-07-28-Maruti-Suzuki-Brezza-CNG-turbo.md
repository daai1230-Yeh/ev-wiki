# Maruti Suzuki ups bet in compact SUV segment, guns for majority sales from CNG vehicles

**Source**: ETAuto | **Date**: 27 July 2026

---

Maruti Suzuki has refreshed its bestselling compact SUV Brezza with a new turbo-petrol powertrain and an S-CNG variant with a redesigned underbody tank. The Brezza Turbo Boosterjet, priced from ₹7,39,900 (ex-showroom), uses a 997cc turbocharged Boosterjet petrol engine paired with a new six-speed manual transmission, producing 81 kW (110.2 PS) at 5,500 rpm and 170 Nm of torque between 2,000–3,500 rpm, with claimed fuel efficiency of 20.47 kmpl.

The S-CNG variant preserves boot space via its redesigned underbody tank and adds Idle Start-Stop technology plus the new six-speed manual, producing 64.6 kW (87.8 PS) and 121.5 Nm in CNG mode with claimed efficiency of 26.9 km/kg. The new Brezza is offered with three powertrain choices: the 1.0-litre turbo-petrol, the familiar 1.5-litre naturally aspirated petrol, and the factory-fitted S-CNG option.

More broadly, Maruti Suzuki sold over 700,000 CNG vehicles in fiscal 2025-26 and is targeting around 900,000 units this fiscal — nearly 30% growth — as CNG demand spreads beyond small cars and vans into the SUV segment, with more than half of some SUV variant sales now coming from underbody-CNG configurations.
