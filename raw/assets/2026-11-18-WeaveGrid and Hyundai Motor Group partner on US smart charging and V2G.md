# WeaveGrid and Hyundai Motor Group partner on US smart charging and V2G

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-11-17

---

The US software provider is partnering with Hyundai Motor Group to integrate smart charging and future vehicle-to-grid (V2G) services directly into the myHyundai and Kia Access apps in North America. The joint platform will optimise EV charging times for lower electricity rates while supporting local power grids.

The collaboration starts with establishing smart charging management and will later expand to include bidirectional V2G capabilities in the United States. Through WeaveGrid’s utility network, Hyundai and Kia drivers will be able to enrol in regional smart charging programmes, depending on their local electricity provider.

For drivers, the new functions for smart charging will appear inside the myHyundai and Kia Access smartphone apps. Based on the driver’s schedule, the software automatically shifts charging sessions to off-peak hours with lower electricity prices and low local grid load. At the same time, the system provides utilities with standardised battery and charging data across different vehicle models without compromising data privacy or vehicle security.

In a second phase, Hyundai Motor Group and WeaveGrid plan to expand the platform to full V2G services once bidirectional hardware, grid connection rules, and utility tariffs are in place.

Most of Hyundai and Kia’s North American EVs are built on the Electric-Global Modular Platform (E-GMP), which already supports two-way power transfer. Currently, models such as the Hyundai Ioniq 5 and Kia EV6 use this architecture to power external devices via vehicle-to-load, while the Kia EV9 provides home backup power.

Once full V2G operation is enabled, owners will be able to discharge electricity from their vehicle batteries back into the public grid for financial compensation. At the same time, power utilities will gain access to battery capacity to prevent local grid overloads.

“We see our vehicles as part of a larger energy ecosystem, and our customers should benefit from that every day,” said Woong Tae Hwang, Vice President and Head of the EV Energy Strategy Group at Hyundai Motor Group. “WeaveGrid’s work with U.S. utilities and its grid orchestration technology make it the right partner to build this platform with us, beginning with smart charging and moving toward vehicle-to-grid services.”

Apoorv Bhargava, co-founder and CEO of WeaveGrid, added: “Utilities are facing load growth unlike anything in decades, and EVs are one of the largest flexible resources available to meet it. Hyundai Motor Group has been a leader in bidirectional charging. Together, we can give drivers one streamlined experience today and a clear path to V2G tomorrow, while helping utilities keep the grid reliable and affordable.”

Since Hyundai and Kia’s initial investment in early 2025, WeaveGrid has expanded its US market reach from utilities covering over 40 per cent of the country’s EVs to providers serving more than 50 per cent.
