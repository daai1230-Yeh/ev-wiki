# Delhi EV policy to boost electric mobility as it shifts gears from subsidies to mandates: ICRA

**Source**: ETAuto Daily Morning Newsletter (Fri, Jul 17, 2026) | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

The Delhi EV Policy 2026 (EV Policy 2.0) is a comprehensive regulatory roadmap that came into effect on 1 July 2026 and will remain in force until 31 March 2030. The policy seeks to shift the city's approach from voluntary adoption to mandatory electrification, directly affecting the vehicles consumers and businesses can buy in the coming years. Unlike its predecessor, the new policy recognises that sustained EV adoption requires more than financial incentives.

Key mandates: from 1 January 2027, only electric three-wheelers and N1-category trucks will be registered in Delhi; from 1 April 2028, only electric two-wheelers will be registered. The proposal includes gradually restricting registrations of selected petrol, diesel and CNG vehicles, signalling a transition from voluntary to mandated choice of vehicle type.

The policy combines demand-side incentives with scrappage benefits, battery-swapping infrastructure, charging network expansion and regulatory measures to reduce dependence on internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicles. The Delhi Cabinet committed a budget of ₹15,000 crore over four years — around ₹7,000 crore for incentives and ₹8,000 crore for EV infrastructure and tax concessions — aiming to achieve at least 30% electrification of Delhi's total vehicle fleet by 31 March 2030.
