# BMZ integrates digital battery passports into series production

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-11-18

---

BMZ, together with Minespider, is introducing digital battery passports for its EU production in Germany and Poland. The passports will be created directly from production data and meet the requirements of the EU Battery Regulation starting February 2027.

The solution covers EV batteries, light mobility batteries such as e-bikes, and industrial batteries. Rather than a separate administrative process, passports are integrated into BMZ's manufacturing and data systems. Product and manufacturer data, compliance documents, circularity information and operational data are assigned to a unique battery identifier.

Access is granted via public or permission-based areas. Minespider supports BMZ in mapping existing data, gap analysis and platform setup, connected in parallel to BMZ's production environment.

Initially passports will be created manually, with larger datasets imported in batches; an API-based exchange will follow for higher volumes. The system is scheduled to be operational by mid-December 2026.

Under EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542, from 18 February 2027 EV batteries, light mobility batteries and industrial batteries above 2 kWh must have a digital battery passport to be placed on the EU market.

"Our customers use our battery systems across very different industries, but they increasingly need the same thing: reliable, structured information about the battery behind their product," said Robert Kowalczyk, Managing Director of BMZ Poland.

Minespider CEO Nathan Williams emphasised that battery passports must become part of production rather than additional overhead.

BMZ Battery Solutions is headquartered in Karlstein am Main, with manufacturing in Germany, Poland, China, Brazil and the USA. BMZ Poland is working with EVE Energy on an LMFP variant of its Versora platform for heavy-duty commercial vehicles; first samples are planned for 2027 and series production for mid-2028.
