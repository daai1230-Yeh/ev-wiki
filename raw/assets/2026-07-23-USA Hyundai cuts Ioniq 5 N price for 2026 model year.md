# USA: Hyundai cuts Ioniq 5 N price for 2026 model year

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 22.07.2026

---

Hyundai has reduced the starting price of the 2026 Ioniq 5 N in the US by $6,300 while introducing several technical updates. The performance EV now comes standard with a NACS charging port, bundled charging adapters and a revised drift mode.

The battery-electric model now starts at $59,900 before a $1,600 destination charge, representing a $6,300 reduction compared to the previous model year. According to the manufacturer, the price adjustment forms part of a broader strategy to make its N performance brand more accessible.

Alongside the lower price, Hyundai has introduced several equipment updates. The 2026 Ioniq 5 N replaces its CCS charging inlet with a North American Charging Standard (NACS) port, giving drivers direct access to Tesla's Supercharger network. Hyundai also includes CCS-to-NACS adapters for both AC and DC charging as standard, alongside a dual-voltage Level 1/Level 2 portable charging cable.

The list of updates also includes a revised N Drift Optimizer with ten selectable stages instead of a single setting, a Forward Attention Warning system using an in-cabin camera, automatic up/down operation for the rear windows, and a new Performance Blue Pearl exterior paint finish.

The technical package remains unchanged. The Ioniq 5 N continues to deliver up to 478 kW when N Grin Boost is activated. The model is based on Hyundai Motor Group's Electric-Global Modular Platform (E-GMP) and combines the dedicated EV architecture with technologies developed by Hyundai's N performance division.

Hyundai unveiled the high-performance Ioniq 5 N in 2023. The battery-electric model combines an 800-volt architecture with a dual-motor all-wheel drive system featuring electric motors rated at 166 kW and 282 kW. Together, they deliver up to 641 hp. The Ioniq 5 N uses an 84 kWh battery that supports DC charging at up to 238 kW, allowing a 10 to 80 per cent charge in 18 minutes when connected to a 350 kW charger. Top speed is electronically limited to 162 mph.

Among its key features is the N e-Shift system, which simulates the behaviour of an eight-speed dual-clutch transmission by varying motor torque output to recreate gear changes. The system is complemented by N Active Sound+, which generates synchronised sound profiles to provide audible feedback during spirited driving and track use.

"Our 2026 Ioniq 5 N is one of the most critically acclaimed models we've ever offered," said Ricky Lao, Director of Product Planning at Hyundai Motor North America. "Ioniq 5 N's exceptional performance, technology, and innovation without compromise is now priced within the reach of even more driving enthusiasts."
