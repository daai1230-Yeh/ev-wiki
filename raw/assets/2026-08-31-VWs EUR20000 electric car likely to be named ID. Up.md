# VW's €20,000 electric car likely to be named ID. Up

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-08-28

---

Volkswagen's planned electric small car for 2027, with a base price of around €20,000, will be called ID. Up and not ID.1 – according to a Spanish media report. The publication writes that VW has already finalised the model's name as ID. Up.

It became clear around a year ago that Volkswagen no longer wished to name its electric cars using only numbers, as has been the case for the ID.3, ID.4, ID.5, and ID.7. At the time, the German carmaking giant announced during the IAA that its new €25,000 electric car would not launch as the ID.2, but as the ID. Polo. Volkswagen stated it would instead use the names of "established bestsellers." Following the world premiere of the ID. Polo in April, the dealer premiere in Germany is set to take place this coming weekend (4–6 September) under the banner "ID. Polo Days."

Since then, it has become clear that new electric models from VW will once again receive proper names. The usually well-informed Spanish automotive news website Motor.es now reports that Volkswagen plans to use the name ID. Up for its electric city car, scheduled for launch in autumn 2027, rather than ID.1, as suggested during the presentation of its concept study, ID. EVERY1, in March 2025.

## ID. Up to be produced in Portugal

As previously reported, the ID. Up is set to be manufactured from mid-2027 at VW's plant in Palmela, Portugal. The specifications of the new ID. Up are expected to largely match those of the ID. EVERY1 show car. The new and affordable electric car measures 3.88 metres in length and 1.81 metres in width, positioning it between the old e-Up (3.60 metres) and the new ID. Polo (4.05 metres), according to VW.

The name is not entirely new, since e-Up was the name of Volkswagen's first battery-electric city car, which, like its internal combustion counterpart, the VW Up, was discontinued in autumn 2023. With the introduction of a new ID. Up, Volkswagen would revive the naming convention of this established model next year. According to Motor.es, Volkswagen also considered naming the small electric car ID. Lupo — thereby revisiting the name Lupo (Italian for "wolf"), which was used for a petrol-powered city car produced between 1998 and 2005.

It would be logical for Volkswagen to extend this approach to its other electric models. For instance, the ID.3 could eventually become the ID. Golf, and the ID.7 could be renamed the ID. Passat. Meanwhile, images of a camouflaged ID. Tiguan have surfaced online—this model is expected to replace the ID.4 and ID.5 soon.

The latest leak of Volkswagen's affordable electric car model designation was not officially announced by the VW Group, but was accidentally leaked by the trade union IG Metall in a statement regarding plant allocations in Emden.
