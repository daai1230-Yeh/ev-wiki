# Renault Trucks extends E-Tech T range to 660km

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-06-30 14:00

---

Ahead of production start, Renault Trucks has increased the usable battery capacity of its flagship electric truck, the E-Tech T 780, raising its claimed range from 600km to 660km. The truck's 780 kWh NCA battery pack can now release up to 95 per cent of its energy capacity (around 741 kWh usable), up from the 80 per cent (623 kWh) quoted at its November 2025 unveiling. Sister brand Volvo Trucks similarly raised the range of its related FH Aero Electric from 600km to 700km earlier this year by unlocking more of its shared battery platform (725 kWh usable, ~93 per cent). Renault Trucks attributes its lower range figure, despite a higher usable-capacity percentage, to differences in cab aerodynamics — the Volvo FH Aero Electric has additional side and roof spoilers the Renault cab lacks.

Charging power is unchanged (up to 350 kW CCS / 720 kW MCS), but charging times increase about 10 per cent due to the larger usable window — roughly 44 minutes via MCS and 1 hour 20 minutes via CCS for a 20-80 per cent charge. The standard battery warranty has been reduced from 8 years/1 million km to 6 years/720,000 km to align with Volvo Trucks, though an optional "Serenity Pack" extends coverage back to 10 years/1 million km within energy-throughput limits. The smaller E-Tech T 585 also gained range, from 460km to 500km, while the E-Tech T 540 stays at 450km.

Renault Trucks says the changes are backed by real-world data from more than 3,500 electric trucks that have collectively covered over 130 million km. With over 700 kWh of usable energy now on board, Volvo Trucks and Renault Trucks set a new usable-capacity benchmark versus competitors — Mercedes-Benz's eActros 600 offers 600 kWh usable, MAN quotes 560 kWh, and China's Sany reaches 623 kWh despite claiming 98 per cent utilisation.
