# China passenger NEV retail sales fall 13% year-on-year in November 1-22, penetration reaches 71.3%

**Author:** Lei Kang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-25

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- China's passenger NEVs recorded retail sales of 702,000 units from November 1 to 22, down 13% from a year earlier but up 7% from the same period last month.
- From November 1 to 22, the NEV retail penetration rate in China's passenger vehicle market reached 71.3%, while the wholesale penetration rate stood at 76.6%.

China's passenger new energy vehicles (NEVs) recorded retail sales of 702,000 units from November 1 to 22, down 13% year-on-year but up 7% from the same period last month, according to a weekly report released by the China Passenger Car Association (CPCA) on Wednesday.

Year-to-date, cumulative retail sales of passenger NEVs in China totaled 9.57 million units, down 13% from a year earlier.

From November 1 to 22, wholesale sales of passenger NEVs in China reached 965,000 units, up 3% year-on-year and up 13% from the same period last month. Year-to-date, cumulative wholesale sales of passenger NEVs hit 14.19 million units, up 9% from a year earlier.

Total retail sales in China's passenger vehicle market were 984,000 units from November 1 to 22, down 25% year-on-year and down 1% from the same period last month. This means the retail penetration rate of passenger NEVs in China reached 71.3% during the period.

Year-to-date, cumulative retail sales in China's passenger vehicle market stood at 15.96 million units, down 22% year-on-year.

In the first week of November (November 1-8), average daily retail sales in China's passenger vehicle market were 36,576 units, down 21% year-on-year but up 2% from the same period last month.

In the second week of November (November 9-15), average daily retail sales were 45,457 units, down 29% year-on-year and down 12% from the same period last month.

In the third week of November (November 16-22), average daily retail sales were 53,364 units, down 24% year-on-year but up 8% from the same period last month.

From November 1 to 22, total wholesale sales by China's passenger vehicle manufacturers reached 1.26 million units, down 21% year-on-year but up 9% from the same period last month. This puts the wholesale penetration rate of passenger NEVs at 76.6% for the period.

From November 1 to 8, average daily wholesale sales in China's passenger vehicle market were 41,675 units, down 20% year-on-year but up 63% from the same period last month.

From November 9 to 15, average daily wholesale sales were 55,514 units, down 26% year-on-year and down 13% from the same period last month.

From November 16 to 22, average daily wholesale sales were 76,825 units, down 18% year-on-year but up 7% from the same period last month.

Year-to-date, cumulative wholesale sales by China's passenger vehicle manufacturers totaled 23.63 million units, down 7% year-on-year.
