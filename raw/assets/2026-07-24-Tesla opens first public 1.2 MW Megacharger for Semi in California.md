# Tesla opens first public 1.2 MW Megacharger for Semi in California

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-22

---

Tesla has opened its first full-scale public Megacharger station for the Semi, located at 18434 Valley Boulevard in Bloomington, California. The site offers six charging stalls, each delivering up to 1.2 MW, and supports the latest-generation Tesla Semi via the new MC2 charging connector — allowing up to 60% recharge in 30 minutes. An earlier, smaller site with two stalls and the first-generation MC1 connector, delivering up to 750kW, had previously been considered Tesla's first public Megacharger site. Tesla plans a much larger Megacharger network across North America, targeting at least 64 US locations with possible later expansion into Canada.
