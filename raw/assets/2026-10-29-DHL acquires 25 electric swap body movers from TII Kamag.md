# DHL acquires 25 electric swap body movers from TII Kamag

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-28

---

TII Kamag, a German manufacturer of special vehicles based in Ulm, is delivering 25 electric swap body movers to DHL. The electric vehicles are to be used in yard logistics at various DHL parcel centers across Germany. The vehicles can quickly and precisely maneuver standardised swap body containers from conventional trucks.

Swap bodies have long been a key element of DHL's parcel logistics. Their greatest advantage is that they make it possible to separate the loading unit and transport vehicle. This makes transhipment between parcel centres, trucks and delivery locations more flexible, helping to meet the tight time windows in the parcel business.

DHL is now electrifying all of its yard logistics processes. For its parcel service, the company has procured 25 electric swap body movers from TII Kamag in Ulm. These vehicles come from the Kamag ePrecisionMover (ePM) model, designed to ensure smooth transhipment operations. With short lifting and lowering times as well as agile manoeuvring, the ePrecisionMover supports a significant reduction in downtime and waiting periods in the yard.

"The demands placed on parcel networks continue to grow, both in terms of handling performance and sustainability. With the KAMAG ePM, we offer a solution that combines both dimensions and integrates seamlessly into existing and scalable logistics processes," says Godehard Eidenhammer, Sales Manager for Yard Logistics at TII Kamag.

It is currently unclear whether the units are the third-generation ePrecisionMover introduced in 2024 (140 kWh battery, 100 kW DC charging) or the newer ePrecisionMover 101 with a new driver's cab, improved ergonomics and safety, and a 160 kWh battery.

DHL received its first diesel-powered swap body mover from TII Kamag in 1995, as the company's first customer, and has continuously expanded its fleet since. For the electric movers, TII Kamag also counts German logistics providers Dachser and Noerpel among its customers.
