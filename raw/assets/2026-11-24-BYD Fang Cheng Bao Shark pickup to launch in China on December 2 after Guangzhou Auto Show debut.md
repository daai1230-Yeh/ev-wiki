# BYD Fang Cheng Bao Shark pickup to launch in China on December 2 after Guangzhou Auto Show debut

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-24

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- The Shark was initially launched in Mexico in May 2024, with cumulative overseas sales reaching 38,367 units in the first 10 months of this year.
- The introduction of the Shark will add a pickup category to Fang Cheng Bao and pit BYD against GWM and Geely Auto in the domestic market.

BYD's (HKEX: 1211) Fang Cheng Bao brand will launch the Shark plug-in hybrid pickup in China on December 2, marking its official entry into a new segment.

Chinese automotive media outlets Auto Home and Yiche reported the schedule on Tuesday, though Fang Cheng Bao has not yet officially announced the launch date.

Yiche also reported that the pickup will make its official China debut at the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27.

The timeline aligns with previous remarks from company executives. Xiong Tianbo, general manager of the Fang Cheng Bao division, said on October 22 that the Shark was scheduled to launch and begin deliveries between late November and early December.

The Shark is a rare BYD model that debuted overseas before being introduced to its home market. It was first launched in Mexico in May 2024 and has since entered several other international markets, including Brazil and Australia.

Xiong announced in May that the Shark would join the Fang Cheng Bao lineup this year. The model subsequently completed its regulatory filing with China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) in July, followed by the release of official domestic images in September.

Built on BYD's DMO (Dual Mode Off-Road) super hybrid off-road platform, the Shark uses the same body-on-frame structure as the Bao 5.

Measuring 5,457 mm in length, 1,971 mm in width, and 1,925 mm in height, with a wheelbase of 3,260 mm, the 5-seat double-cab pickup largely retains the exterior design of its overseas counterpart.

For power, the Shark is equipped with a 1.5T engine and dual motors, with the front motor delivering a peak power of 170 kW (228 hp) and the rear motor 150 kW, for a combined motor output of 320 kW.

It has a top speed of 180 km/h (112 mph), uses lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries supplied by BYD's battery unit FinDreams Battery, and supports vehicle-to-load (V2L) external power discharge.

Adding the Shark will expand Fang Cheng Bao's lineup into the pickup segment and pit BYD domestically against GWM (HKEX: 2333) and Geely Auto's (HKEX: 0175) Radar brand.

Due to urban traffic restrictions, pickups remain a niche segment in China. According to data from the China Passenger Car Association (CPCA), China's pickup sales totaled 521,000 units in the first 9 months of this year, including 71,000 new energy pickups, up 18% year-on-year.

In the first 10 months of this year, cumulative overseas sales of the Shark reached 38,367 units, including 4,370 units in October, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.
