# Nio Firefly launches new 333-unit special edition to sustain record delivery momentum

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-20

- The fall ginkgo-inspired special edition starts at 135,800 yuan ($20,230), representing a 13.4% premium over the base Firefly model.

- Firefly delivered a record 7,738 vehicles in October, making it the only Nio Inc brand to post month-on-month growth.

Nio Inc's (NYSE: NIO) compact EV brand Firefly launched a new fall-themed special edition on Friday with production limited to 333 units.

The special edition, dubbed "Pianke Liujin" (literally "fleeting golden moment") in Chinese, starts at 135,800 yuan ($20,230) for the whole vehicle and is available for order now.

Display vehicles will begin arriving at showrooms across China starting November 21, Firefly said in a statement on its official app.

Under the BaaS (battery as a service) battery rental plan, the vehicle's purchase price drops to 95,800 yuan, with monthly battery rental fees starting at 399 yuan.

The whole-vehicle pricing matches several previous special editions and is 16,000 yuan, or about 13.4%, higher than the base Firefly model's 119,800 yuan starting price.

Inspired by fall ginkgo leaves and sunset light, the new edition features ginkgo-themed exterior decals crafted with a dual-layer metallic-base spray process, multi-spoke alloy wheels, and the brand's first-ever sunroof decal design.

Inside, the cabin features a golden-hour-themed interior, including double-stitched ginkgo embroidery, embossed headrests, an exclusive fragrance, a full-width front floor mat, and an exclusive ambient-ring theme wallpaper.

The statement made no mention of powertrain or core hardware upgrades, indicating that the release extends Firefly's strategy of using small-batch, design-focused editions to create differentiation and sustain market buzz while operating with a single model.

Previously, Firefly launched its sixth special edition, Club Spiaggia, on August 28 in a limited run of 666 units. Its 333-unit Pixel Player special edition, released in June, sold out in less than 8 hours.

While maintaining visibility through special editions, Firefly is also expanding the product. On October 23, the brand introduced a 60-kWh battery pack that increases its CLTC range to 560 kilometers (348 miles).

These moves have supported Firefly's sales performance. The brand delivered 7,738 vehicles in October, up 30.89% year-on-year and 5.61% month-on-month, setting a monthly record for the second consecutive month, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.

Firefly was also the only brand under Nio Inc to achieve month-on-month delivery growth in October, with its contribution to the company's 34,246 total monthly deliveries rising to a record 22.60% since deliveries began.

In the first 10 months of the year, Firefly delivered 55,860 vehicles. As of the end of October, the brand's cumulative deliveries since starting handovers in April 2025 reached 95,274 units.
