# Google Alerts Digest — July 10, 2026

**Source**: Google Alerts daily summary emails (7:04am and 7:15am)

## 電動車 + 泰國 (EV + Thailand)

Thailand's BOI approved EV-sector investment exceeding 130 billion baht, continuing to build out a full supply chain, at an event themed "Driving Thailand's EV Future: Powering a Competitive & Connected Supply Chain" hosted by the Thai EV Association (EVAT). Separately, Foxconn and Thailand's national oil company PTT signed a joint-venture agreement to build a vertically integrated EV production base in Thailand, targeting annual output of 150,000 vehicles. Thailand's BOI also approved a $1.99 billion investment package spanning AI supply chain projects, with Taiwanese electronics-materials makers securing a foothold, including nanocrystalline soft magnetic material demand driven by AI servers, EVs, and renewable energy.

## 電動巴士 (Electric Buses)

Taiwan's Foton Buscar (華德動能) has reached cumulative production of 1,000 electric buses and plans to list on Taiwan's Innovation Board in early August. The company became Taiwan's first VSCC-certified electric bus designer/developer in 2019 and holds Taiwan's first electric bus registration plate. This year's order pipeline is expected to exceed 300 units as the company expands overseas into Japan and Vietnam, partnering with US-based Optimal, Japan's Sumitomo Corporation group, Japan's NExT-e Solutions, and a Mexican automaker.

## 電動車 + 韓國 (EV + Korea)

Coverage referenced record EV speed claims (330 km/h) rivaling European flagship supercars, in the context of Honda's NSX supercar heritage.

## 電動車 + 印度 (EV + India)

Foxconn's expanding engagement in India, without immediately announcing new capital investment, may be laying groundwork for future chip, EV, or plant-expansion decisions via relationship-building with local governments and partners. Nissan globally unveiled its new mid-size SUV, the Tekton, in India as a strategic transformation milestone for the brand.

## 電動車 + 中國大陸 (EV + China)

Commentary on China's new-energy vehicle supply chain noted that after Ford and other Western automakers slowed EV plans (with BMW also reportedly pulling back), Chinese industry insiders describe a narrative of "the East rising as the West declines." A separate report noted Chinese EV brands' sales in Europe have overtaken Japanese brands for the first time, attributed to a complete NEV supply chain, fast product iteration, and cost advantages amid slower legacy-automaker transitions.

## 電動車 + 供應鏈 (EV + Supply Chain)

Thailand's BOI EV investment (130+ billion baht) was also covered from a supply-chain angle. Separately, commentary discussed how China's tightened EV export controls affect supply chains more broadly — export licensing requirements as a precondition are seen as potentially lengthening the entire production and shipping schedule, not just finished-vehicle exports. Honda and LG were also noted partnering on an EV-to-ESS (energy storage system) cross-sector strategy.

## 特斯拉 (Tesla)

Commentary examined how utility company procurement strategy affects Tesla's (TSLA) energy storage penetration — including whether capital budgets are rising due to data center expansion, whether procurement is shifting toward standardization and faster deployment, and whether grid stability and renewable-energy share are becoming purchasing priorities.
