# The Next-Gen Chevy Equinox EV Is Coming In 2028: Report

**Source**: InsideEVs | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

A second-generation Chevy Equinox EV is expected to enter production around late 2028 or early 2029, according to reports cited by GM Authority. It will reportedly switch platforms from the current BEV3 to GM's upcoming BEV-N architecture, bringing a new centralized computing system and redesigned electrical architecture expected to appear from 2028. The next-gen Equinox EV is reportedly set to lead GM's next generation of electric cars, though GM has not officially confirmed the vehicle, its new platform, or the 2028 launch date.
