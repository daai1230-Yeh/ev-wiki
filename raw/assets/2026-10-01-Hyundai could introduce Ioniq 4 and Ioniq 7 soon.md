# Hyundai could introduce Ioniq 4 and Ioniq 7 'soon'

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-09-30

---

The South Korean automaker Hyundai reportedly plans to expand its Ioniq family with two additional electric models within the next 12 to 18 months: the Ioniq 4 and the Ioniq 7. According to a British media report, these are said to be two differently sized electric SUVs.

Hyundai describes Ioniq as a sub-brand dedicated to next-generation electric vehicles and e-mobility, with the Ioniq 3 only recently being introduced in Germany as the fourth model in the sub-brand's current lineup. This is a hatchback with a particularly aerodynamic body, starting at €28,950.

As Auto Express now reports, Hyundai aims to expand the sub-brand in order to rapidly fill gaps in its EVs portfolio. Specifically, the Ioniq range is set to be expanded with the Ioniq 4 and Ioniq 7. The Ioniq 4, with its sharper design and more conventional SUV shape, is to be positioned between the Ioniq 3 and the Ioniq 5. The Ioniq 7, also featuring a sharper design, is to be placed as a family-friendly SUV between the Ioniq 5 and the Ioniq 9.

This would finally allow Hyundai to offer an electric compact SUV and a mid-size SUV – segments currently covered by its sister brand Kia with the EV3 and EV5. Currently, the only electric SUV in the Ioniq family is the large Ioniq 9, the sister model to the Kia EV9.

## Ioniq 4 and Ioniq 7 not yet officially confirmed

While Hyundai has not yet officially confirmed the Ioniq 4 and Ioniq 7, statements from management suggest otherwise, according to Auto Express. During the strategy day in August, a new electric SUV in the B-segment was announced, which could potentially be the Ioniq 4.

When asked whether such an Ioniq 4 would be based on the Kia EV3, Chris McKinnon, Hyundai's product chief for Europe, replied: "I really can't answer that question, to be honest. Watch this space." He acknowledged that this is an "important" market for the brand but refused to disclose further details about the vehicle. According to Auto Express, production of the Ioniq 4 is likely to start in 2027. The electric compact SUV is expected to feature an interior similar to the Ioniq 3 and offer a choice of two batteries: 42 kWh or 61 kWh.

A potential mid-size electric SUV named Ioniq 7, on the other hand, could reportedly come with an 800-volt architecture to remain competitive in the fast-paced electric SUV segment, according to Auto Express. It is said to offer single-motor and dual-motor variants, though details such as battery size and range estimates are not yet known.

## A dedicated sub-brand since 2020

Hyundai first introduced the Ioniq name in 2016 with a namesake model—at that time without a numerical suffix like the current models. The original Ioniq was launched as a hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and battery-electric vehicle. According to Hyundai, the name combines "ion" and "unique". Production of the original Ioniq ended in July 2022.

In August 2020, Hyundai then established Ioniq as a dedicated sub-brand for battery-electric vehicles. The company initially announced three dedicated electric vehicles: Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, and Ioniq 7. The Electric-Global Modular Platform (E-GMP) was intended as the technical foundation. However, the originally planned Ioniq 7 was later renamed Ioniq 9, freeing up the Ioniq 7 name for the aforementioned mid-size electric SUV.
