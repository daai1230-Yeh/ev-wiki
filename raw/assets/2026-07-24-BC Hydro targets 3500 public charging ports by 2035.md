# BC Hydro targets 3,500 public charging ports by 2035

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

BC Hydro will invest C$700 million to grow its public charging network to 3,500 ports by 2035 — more than tripling its current size of 917 ports across 190 locations. The rollout includes larger charging hubs, 200kW chargers as the standard, and selected 400kW ultra-fast charging locations, to be installed in communities, along major highway corridors and in high-demand urban areas across British Columbia. The provincial government is separately allocating a further C$18 million through the CleanBC Go Electric Public Charger Program for additional public fast chargers.
