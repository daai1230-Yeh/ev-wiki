2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文

China's central government has just laid out its clearest roadmap yet for the auto industry: under the newly announced fifteenth five-year plan, the country wants new energy vehicles, meaning battery-electric and hybrid models, to make up seventy percent of all new passenger car sales by 2030.

中國政府最近公布了汽車產業目前最明確的路線圖：在新發布的「十五五」規劃下，官方希望到2030年，新能源車（也就是電動車與混合動力車）能佔新車乘用車銷量的70%。

The plan was drafted jointly by nine separate government departments, and it doesn't stop at passenger cars. Commercial vehicles, trucks and buses, for instance, are expected to reach a forty percent electrification rate over the same period, alongside a broader push to roll out highly autonomous vehicles on highways, city expressways, and even certain downtown streets.

這項規劃由九個政府部門共同擬定，範圍不只限於乘用車。以商用車（例如卡車與巴士）為例，同一時期的電動化比例目標訂在40%，同時也將推動高度自駕能力的車輛上路，範圍涵蓋高速公路、城市快速道路，甚至部分市區道路。

On paper, China already dominates the numbers: BYD, SAIC, and Geely all ranked among the world's top ten automakers by sales volume in 2025. But officials openly admit that scale hasn't yet translated into the kind of global brand power held by Toyota, Volkswagen, or Hyundai, which is part of why this new plan pushes so hard for a handful of Chinese carmakers to become true global leaders.

單看數字，中國車廠早已名列前茅：比亞迪、上汽集團與吉利汽車在2025年的銷量都擠進全球前十大。但官方也坦言，銷量規模尚未轉化為豐田、福斯或現代那樣的全球品牌影響力，這也是為什麼新規劃如此強調要培養出幾家真正的全球領導車廠。

The urgency makes more sense once you look at the domestic market: sales in China fell more than twenty-one percent in the first eight months of 2026, and years of aggressive price wars have squeezed profit margins across the industry. That's pushing regulators toward consolidation, encouraging mergers while pressuring less efficient manufacturers to exit the market entirely.

如果看看國內市場，就能理解這股急迫感從何而來：中國車市2026年前8個月的銷量下滑超過21%，多年的激烈價格戰更嚴重侵蝕了整個產業的獲利空間。這促使主管機關朝向企業整併的方向推進，一方面鼓勵合併，一方面淘汰效率較低的製造商。

Meanwhile, on the other side of the East China Sea, Toyota is racing to catch up in a very different part of the same story: self-driving technology. The Japanese automaker has just outlined plans to use one shared, end-to-end AI system for two very different products — Level two-plus-plus driver assistance in private cars, and Level four autonomous driving in commercial vehicles.

與此同時，在東海的另一邊，豐田汽車正在這個故事的另一個章節裡急起直追：自動駕駛技術。這家日本車廠剛剛公布計畫，將用同一套端到端AI系統，支援兩種截然不同的產品——私家車的Level 2++駕駛輔助，以及商用車的Level 4自動駕駛。

Technically, the two systems are nearly identical; a driver in a Level two-plus-plus car still must keep their hands ready on the wheel, while a Level four vehicle in a defined area needs no human intervention at all, so the real difference comes down to legal liability, not hardware. Toyota is targeting mass production of the private-car system by 2028, with commercial rollout following by 2030, and it's even reportedly considering joining Waymo's robotaxi business.

從技術層面來看，這兩套系統幾乎相同；Level 2++車輛的駕駛人仍須隨時把手放在方向盤上，而Level 4車輛在特定區域內則完全不需要人類介入，所以真正的差異在於法律責任，而非硬體本身。豐田的目標是在2028年前將私家車系統量產，商用車則預計在2030年前跟進，甚至傳出有意加入Waymo的無人計程車事業。

Put side by side, the two stories make an interesting contrast: one is a government setting a top-down numerical target and reshaping an entire market to hit it, and the other is a private company scrambling to catch up with rivals in the United States and China, using shared technology to stretch a limited budget as far as possible.

把這兩則新聞放在一起看，形成了有趣的對比：一邊是政府由上而下訂出具體數字目標，並重塑整個市場來達成它；另一邊則是一家民間企業，為了追趕美國與中國的競爭對手，透過共用技術把有限的預算發揮到最大效益。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型

  1. "X is projected to reach ___ percent by [year]." — e.g. "NEV sales are projected to reach seventy percent by 2030."

    用來預測某個比例或數字在未來會達到多少。

  2. "Unlike A, B tends to ___." — e.g. "Unlike a top-down government target, a corporate strategy tends to shift with the market."

    用來對比兩件事物的不同之處。

  3. "One risk here is that ___." — e.g. "One risk here is that aggressive targets could worsen overcapacity."

    用來提出你對某件事的疑慮或潛在風險。

  4. "From a policy perspective, ___, but from a business perspective, ___." — e.g. "From a policy perspective, quotas drive investment, but from a business perspective, they can distort competition."

    用來同時從政策與商業兩個角度切入分析同一件事。

  5. "It remains to be seen whether ___." — e.g. "It remains to be seen whether Toyota can really catch up by 2028."

    用來表示某件事情的結果還不確定，需要時間才能驗證。

6. Discussion 討論題

  1. Do you think top-down government targets, like China's seventy percent NEV goal, are an effective way to grow an industry, or do they risk creating even more overcapacity?

    你認為像中國70%新能源車目標這樣由上而下訂定的政府目標，是推動產業成長的有效方式，還是可能造成更嚴重的產能過剩？

  2. China's overall car market shrank by more than twenty-one percent in the first eight months of 2026, even as EV sales held roughly steady. What do you think that gap tells us about the broader economy?

    中國整體車市在2026年前8個月萎縮超過21%，但電動車銷量卻大致持平，你覺得這樣的落差反映出整體經濟的什麼狀況？

  3. Toyota wants to merge its Level two-plus-plus driver-assist system and its Level four robotaxi system into one shared AI. What are the benefits and the risks of using the same core technology for two products with very different levels of driver responsibility?

    豐田想把Level 2++駕駛輔助系統和Level 4無人計程車系統整合成同一套AI。用同一套核心技術支援兩種駕駛責任截然不同的產品，有哪些好處與風險？

  4. Japanese automakers are often described as being "behind" the US and China in self-driving technology. Why do you think that happened, and do you believe they can catch up in time?

    日本車廠常被形容在自動駕駛技術上「落後」美國和中國。你認為原因是什麼？你相信他們能及時追上嗎？

  5. Would you personally trust a Level four vehicle, with no driver responsible at all, on a fixed bus route or delivery route in your city? Why or why not?

    如果是在你居住的城市，行駛固定路線的巴士或送貨車採用完全無需駕駛負責的Level 4自動駕駛，你會信任嗎？為什麼？

  6. Should regulators require self-driving systems to be clearly safer than the average human driver before allowing them on public roads, or is being "about as safe as" a human good enough?

    主管機關是否應該要求自動駕駛系統的安全性明顯優於人類駕駛平均水準，才能允許上路，還是只要跟人類「差不多安全」就足夠？

7. Wrap-Up 總結

Try summarizing the whole story in about 60 seconds: China's new five-year plan sets a seventy percent NEV sales target for 2030, backed by nine government departments, even as the domestic market shrinks and regulators push consolidation. At the same time, Toyota is racing to catch up in self-driving technology, merging its Level two-plus-plus and Level four systems into one shared AI to save time and money, with mass production targeted for 2028.

試著用大約60秒總結整個故事：中國新的「十五五」規劃訂出2030年新能源車銷量佔比70%的目標，由九個政府部門共同支持，即使國內市場正在萎縮、主管機關也在推動企業整併。與此同時，豐田正急起直追自動駕駛技術，把Level 2++與Level 4系統整合成同一套共用AI以節省時間與成本，目標在2028年前量產。

Now finish with one sentence that states your own position — for example: "Personally, I think government-set targets like this one make sense in a strategic industry, because the private sector alone may not move fast enough."

最後用一句話表達你自己的立場——例如：「就我個人而言，我認為在具有戰略意義的產業裡，像這樣由政府訂定目標是合理的，因為單靠民間企業的力量可能不夠快。」
