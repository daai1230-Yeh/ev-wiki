# Tesla Cybercab for private sale comes without steering wheel but with restrictions

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-16

---

Tesla is inviting "selected customers" to buy Cybercabs for private use at a price of $29,990. However, the car can only be driven with an additional $299-per-month "Autonomy subscription" and is restricted to Tesla's Robotaxi service areas in Texas and Florida.

The Tesla Cybercab has appeared in the US configurator of the electric carmaker. However, access is not open to the public: the configurator can only be unlocked with a personal email invitation. The price, at least, is public: $29,990. But this does not buy customers a drivable car. Activating the autonomous driving functions requires an additional monthly subscription of $299. As the Cybercab is designed without a steering wheel or pedals, it cannot be used without this subscription.

The configurator also indicates that deliveries could begin as early as December and that the vehicle is intended for private use only. According to InsideEVs, the subscription also includes roadside assistance and connectivity. "This allows users to set destinations via the app, manage access rights, schedule maintenance appointments, and receive support. However, the costs for registration, insurance, maintenance, and charging are not included in the fee," the German report states. Despite the car's autonomous driving capabilities, the user remains responsible for any traffic violations.

The biggest restriction, however, is that the Cybercab can currently only operate within the service areas of Tesla's Robotaxi service. These are limited to a handful of selected urban areas in Texas and Florida. Use outside these areas is prohibited and can also be prevented technically, according to the configurator. It remains unclear whether the vehicle can even be approved for road use. The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) is currently reviewing the matter.
