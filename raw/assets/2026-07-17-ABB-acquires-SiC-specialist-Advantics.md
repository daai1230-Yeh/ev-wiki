# ABB acquires SiC specialist Advantics

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

Swiss tech giant ABB is acquiring French company Advantics to strengthen its portfolio of fast-charging solutions. Advantics develops power electronics based on silicon carbide (SiC), which can be used in charging infrastructure, among other applications.

The companies have not disclosed the financial details, though the transaction is expected to close in Q4 2026. Advantics, based in Saint-Genis-Pouilly, develops hardware and software for power conversion, with power modules the company says achieve efficiencies of up to 99 per cent. ABB plans to integrate this technology with its existing portfolio for protection technology, energy distribution, digital control and energy management.

With the acquisition, ABB is strengthening its offering for direct current applications, which are gaining importance in electrification as they avoid repeated AC/DC conversions and reduce energy losses. Silicon carbide semiconductors enable higher switching frequencies, lower losses and more compact systems than conventional silicon, allowing fast-charging systems to be designed more efficiently and at higher power levels.

Massimiliano Cifalitti, Head of ABB's Smart Power Division, said Advantics brings both silicon carbide power-electronics expertise and engineering competence for compact power converters. Advantics founder and CEO Michal Elias described electrification as a key driver of increasing demand for intelligent, efficient power conversion.
