# London Ambulance Service secures charging partners

**Author**: guest author | **Date**: 2026-10-28

---

London Ambulance Service (LAS) has contracted providers to ensure its expanding fleet of electric vehicles can be charged when and where they need power across the UK capital. SEE Services will install and maintain charging facilities and has appointed Optimo Energy as its Fleet Energy Operating System partner.

SEE Services is responsible for more than 700 charging connectors as part of the programme and has been selected by LAS to deliver and maintain charging infrastructure across its estate for the NHS. SEE Services has chosen Optimo Energy to provide the intelligent software platform and operational support capabilities to manage the charging network.

"Working with London Ambulance Service means understanding what is at stake. These are vehicles that people depend on in an emergency, and we take our responsibility to deliver and maintain their charging infrastructure seriously," said Lance Curtis, EV Manager at SEE Services Limited.

The first fully electric ambulance in London entered service on New Year's Eve, 2023. LAS introduced four custom-made emergency EVs in 2024 and has been expanding its electric services ever since. In July 2026 it added 77 new electric vehicles to its fleet.

Optimo provides the software-based operational layer for the connected charging network, bringing together compatible charging equipment, vehicle energy data and site power management, with real-time monitoring, remote diagnostics and charging control, supported by round-the-clock operational monitoring. SEE Services and Optimo will coordinate identification and resolution of charging issues, combining remote oversight with on-site engineering and maintenance.

"Our role is to help make that infrastructure work as part of a connected charging operation," said Daniel Homoki-Farkas, CEO and Co-founder of Optimo Energy.
