# Used EVs Are Selling Like Hotcakes. The Fastest Movers May Surprise You

**Author**: Nico DeMattia | **Date**: 2026-11-03

---

Used EVs are getting more popular and, according to iSeeCars, are selling faster than used hybrids and gasoline cars. No Tesla cracked the top 10 best-selling used EVs in September 2026.

Compared to September 2025, used EV prices rose 9.8%, double the 4.8% increase for used hybrids; used gas car values rose only 0.3%. iSeeCars' Karl Brauer said higher fuel prices are likely driving the shift, though the EV segment has also recovered from the demand turbulence caused by the loss of federal incentives a year ago.

Used EVs spend an average of 41.9 days on the lot — 10.1% faster than September 2025 and almost two weeks faster than gas cars (54.8 days, up 19.9%). Supply is still far smaller: Autotrader lists 1,384,383 used gas and diesel cars versus 44,434 used EVs.

None of the ten fastest-selling used EV models in September were Teslas, Lucids or Rivians. The quickest sellers were the Audi Q4 E-tron Sportback, Toyota bZ4X and Lexus RZ 300e, with the Honda Prologue, BMW iX and Polestar 2 also on the list. The slowest were mostly large, expensive SUVs and trucks: the Cadillac Escalade IQ (by a huge margin), GMC Sierra EV, Chevy Silverado EV, Volvo EX90 and Hummer EV.

Used Tesla prices have risen, with the Model S and Model X up 22% and 18% — the highest increases of any used car regardless of powertrain — which may explain slower Tesla sales. The article concludes that used EV buyers want something small and practical, and that data showing EVs don't lose range as quickly as thought makes used EVs better buys.
