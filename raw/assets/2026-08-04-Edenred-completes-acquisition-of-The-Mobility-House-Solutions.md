# Edenred completes acquisition of The Mobility House Solutions

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-08-03

---

Edenred has completed its acquisition of The Mobility House Solutions, strengthening its position in the European fleet electrification market. The business, which specialises in turnkey charging infrastructure and intelligent energy management for fleets, will now operate under the name Edenred E-Mobility GmbH.

Following the transaction announced in June, the former The Mobility House subsidiary is combining its technological expertise with the global sales network of B2B mobility service provider UTA Edenred, which offers fuel and charging cards among other fleet services and serves more than 300,000 corporate customers worldwide. The portfolio continues to centre on the manufacturer-independent ChargePilot charging and energy management system, designed to prevent grid overloads and reduce operating costs for fleet operators.

Drawing on experience from more than 2,700 managed projects and over 2,500 actively supported fleet depots, Edenred E-Mobility GmbH positions itself as an end-to-end partner for businesses, logistics fleets, car dealerships and property operators, covering consulting, charging infrastructure installation, technical support, billing services, and home charging for company car drivers.

For The Mobility House, the sale marks a strategic shift towards vehicle-to-grid technology, flexibility marketing and energy trading, while remaining commercially connected to Edenred through its Energy Unit.

Heiko Bayer, Managing Director of Edenred E-Mobility GmbH, said the completed transaction marks the start of the company's next phase of growth, expecting rising demand for integrated B2B fleet charging solutions and aiming to provide a unified charging platform covering depot, public and home charging.

Pierre Jalady, CEO of UTA Edenred and Edenred Mobility EMEA, said the acquisition strengthens the company's fleet electrification strategy, highlighting the ChargePilot energy management system as a key addition enabling an integrated ecosystem for European fleet operators.

For customers and partners, the transaction will not affect existing contracts, contact persons or the company's Munich headquarters. The established The Mobility House Solutions and ChargePilot brands remain in place; only the legal name changes to Edenred E-Mobility GmbH.
