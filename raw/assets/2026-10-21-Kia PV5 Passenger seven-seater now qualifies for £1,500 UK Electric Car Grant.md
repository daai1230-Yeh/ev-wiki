# Kia PV5 Passenger seven-seater now qualifies for £1,500 UK Electric Car Grant

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-10-20

---

Kia's PV5 Passenger seven-seat electric people carrier is now eligible for the UK government's Electric Car Grant (ECG). This reduces its on-the-road price by £1,500 across all three trim levels.

The grant applies to the Essential, Plus, and range-topping Elite versions of the PV5 Passenger, which were not eligible for the incentive upon launch in June. At the time, Kia said it expected to clarify the model's status in due course.

This now appears to have happened, with the ECG applicable to the seven-seater at the point of purchase – bringing the starting price of the entry-level Essential version down to £35,495.

The seven-seat PV5 Passenger is offered in the UK with a 71.2 kWh battery, providing up to 246 miles WLTP range. It marks the latest addition to a growing list of ECG-eligible Kia vehicles. The EV4 and five-seater PV5 Passenger were among the brand's first models to qualify back in December 2025, followed by additional EV3 and EV4 versions in April 2026 and the EV2 Long Range in July of this year. Kia UK stated: "Kia is currently one of only eleven brands to offer fully electric cars with the maximum ECG, highlighting the brand's strong environmental manufacturing credentials required to qualify for the grant."

The ECG provides grants of either £1,500 or £3,750 depending on an eligible vehicle and its band. It effectively delivers a price reduction at the point of purchase for buyers of new EVs. Qualification depends on government criteria covering areas such as emissions, sustainability, and warranty support.
