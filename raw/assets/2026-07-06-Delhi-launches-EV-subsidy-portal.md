# Delhi launches EV subsidy portal under new policy to boost adoption

**Author**: BioEnergy Times | **Date**: 2026-07-04

---

The Delhi government launched the Delhi Electric Vehicle (EV) Subsidy Portal, introducing financial incentives of up to ₹1 lakh to encourage EV adoption and reduce pollution in the national capital.

Buyers of electric two-wheelers will receive a subsidy of ₹30,000, electric three-wheelers are eligible for ₹50,000, and a subsidy of ₹1 lakh has been announced for N1 category light commercial electric vehicles. The government has also exempted all electric cars priced below ₹30 lakh from road tax and registration fees.

The portal allows individuals and institutions to apply online for EV subsidies without visiting government offices, uploading documents such as Aadhaar, vehicle registration certificates, and voter ID online. Eligible beneficiaries receive subsidy payments directly via Aadhaar-authenticated Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), with incentives disbursed within a maximum of 60 days after document verification.

The policy is a key step in addressing Delhi's rising pollution levels, with vehicular emissions accounting for nearly 23 percent of total pollution in the city.
