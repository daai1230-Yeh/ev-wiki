# BYD boosts sales by 22% in July

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-08-03

---

Chinese automaker BYD increased its sales for the third consecutive month in July after eight months of year-on-year declines through April. Exports again accounted for a record 43 per cent of total sales, matching June's level, while the domestic market continued to contract.

The company sold 419,211 new energy vehicles worldwide in July, up 21.76 per cent year on year and marking its highest monthly sales total so far this year. International business remained the main growth driver, while the domestic market showed signs of recovery despite the seasonal summer slowdown.

Exports, including to Europe, rose to a record 179,841 units in July, up 124.3 per cent year on year, accounting for around 43 per cent of total sales. BYD is preparing to start series production at its first European passenger car factory in Szeged, Hungary, in Q4 2026 — vehicles built there won't be subject to the EU's additional tariffs on Chinese-made EVs.

Domestically, BYD sold around 239,370 vehicles, a year-on-year decline of about nine per cent, smaller than previous months, supported by strong demand for models with fast-charging technology.

Emerging brands performed strongly: the off-road brand Fang Cheng Bao (not yet in Europe) set a sales record with 41,213 units, up around 190 per cent year on year. Premium brand Denza, which entered the German market this spring, contributed 19,196 units (+68.8%), while Yangwang sold 485 vehicles. The Dynasty and Ocean model series remained the backbone of BYD's business at 350,178 units.

Strong demand is being driven by the transition to the second-generation Blade battery with flash-charging capability, while overseas growth is currently constrained by limited shipping capacity.

Year-to-date, BYD delivered 2.23 million new energy vehicles worldwide in the first seven months of 2026, a decline of 10.54 per cent versus 2025, though the gap has narrowed significantly — growth accelerated from 0.26% in May to 5.5% in June to 21.76% in July. BYD has now surpassed cumulative sales of 17.3 million new energy vehicles.
