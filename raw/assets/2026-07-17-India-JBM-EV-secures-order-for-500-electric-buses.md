# India: JBM EV secures order for 500 electric buses

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

JBM Electric Vehicles (JBM EV), a subsidiary of India's JBM Auto, has secured an order for 500 electric buses from Indian electric commercial vehicle leasing platform Drivn, covered by a signed MoU. JBM EV will supply the buses in phases over the next twelve months; the models are not disclosed, but the initial batches will be luxury intercity coaches.

The company may use the Galaxy e12, a 12-metre model powered by a 250 kW permanent magnet synchronous motor reaching up to 80 km/h. JBM has not disclosed cell chemistry, energy capacity or range, stating only a daily operational range of 1,000 km. Three versions are offered: Seater (49 seats), Sleeper (30 berths) and Hybrid (20 berths plus 20 seats), with features including an automatic in-swing door, kneeling mechanism, calf-support seats, reading lamps, USB ports, air suspension on both axles and ADAS provisions. Last week JBM unveiled a next-generation Galaxy e12 with a 235 kW motor and a Hybrid version of 20 berths plus 24 seats (44 passengers).

Chairman Nishant Arya said India saw 40% growth in e-bus registrations in H1 CY2026 (2,944 registrations under the PM E-Bus Sewa and PM E-Drive schemes) and that the Drivn partnership "comes in at an opportune time." JBM Auto entered electric buses in 2018 and has deployed more than 3,500 e-buses, which have clocked over 450 million kilometres and served 1.5 billion commuters, with cumulative deliveries expected to reach 5,000 units within the next twelve months.
