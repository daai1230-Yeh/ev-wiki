# Preview: Electric truck innovations at IAA Transportation

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

This year's IAA Transportation conference is set to become a major hub for electric trucks. Established players are offering extensive showcases, but Chinese market newcomers are no less impressive. Tesla also manages to bring a European version of the Semi to the halls of Hanover.

The IAA Transportation trade fair, taking place 15–20 September in Hanover, continues to justify its reputation as the most important logistics event in Europe. Organisers confirm the fair is fully booked, with hall occupancy denser than in 2024. Around one-third of exhibitors are first-time participants (up from a quarter in 2024), and 70% of exhibitors are international — China leads, followed by Turkey and Italy.

Hildegard Müller, President of the German Association of the Automotive Industry (VDA), outlined the industry's challenges at a press conference, calling the commercial vehicle industry "a powerhouse" that "keeps our country running," and promising the IAA "will once again be a showcase of innovations."

With over 750 exhibitors, suppliers form the backbone of the event, followed by 240 bodybuilding specialists and then OEMs. More than 150 world premieres and new products have been announced. Autonomous driving will occupy significantly more space this year, and test drive slots have expanded to 84 vehicles from 17 exhibitors — 40% more than 2024 — with three departure points on-site for the first time.

**Established manufacturers.** Daimler Truck (Hall 19, Stand A41) spotlights its second-generation electric trucks, the eActros 600 and eActros 400, plus the new eActros Lowliner variant for automotive logistics (orders opening Q3 2026, production starting Q2 2027). Its hydrogen NextGenH2 Truck will also feature, with 100 units entering customer operations starting late this year. Daimler is also backing content creator Tobias Wagner ("Elektrotrucker") on a world tour launching from the IAA on September 20 with a modified eActros 600, titled "Around the World in 80 Charges."

MAN (Hall 11, Stand D08) will show its completed E-truck family from 12 to 50 tonnes — eTGL, eTGS, eTGX, and the 16-tonne eTGM (which premiered in Milan in May) — plus previews of bidirectional and grid-independent depot charging.

Volvo Trucks (Hall 11, Stand D09) showcases its second-generation E-trucks, led by the Volvo FH Aero Electric with up to 700 km of range, entering production in early September, alongside FH, FM, and FL Electric models. Sister company Renault Trucks (Hall 21, Stand B52) rolls out its own second-generation E-trucks on the same modular platform, spotlighting the E-Tech T 780 for long-haul, plus the E-Tech T 540 and D14T.

**Turkish and Chinese debuts.** Ford Otosan's Ford Trucks (Hall 21, Stand C02) brings its F-Line E — first shown in Lyon in November 2025 — to Germany for the first time, in 19- and 26-tonne electric classes. Scania (Hall 12, Stand C60) will have at least four E-trucks on the outdoor area or for test drives, including the BEV 40 R, BEV 36 R XT, and BEV 30 P crane truck. DAF (Hall 21, Stand B31) is expected to bring its electric family from the XB Electric to the full-sized XG Electric, alongside the "International Truck of the Year 2026" XD and XF. Iveco, present two years ago with the S-eWay, has cancelled participation this year.

Tesla (Hall 12, Stand B01) returns with the Semi — this time promising to announce its European market launch with concrete specifications during the fair, and has registered the Semi for test drives.

A wave of Chinese OEMs are targeting entry into German and European markets. Sany (Hall 12, Stand C40, operating as Sany eTrucks with Putzmeister) will spotlight its e263 E-tractor unit, having started German deliveries in June, and plans to bring four electric truck models total. Sinotruk (Hall 19/20, Stand A10) and SuperPanther (Hall 12, Stand D46), both already producing E-trucks at Steyr Automotive in Austria, will also present. Skyworth Auto (Hall 21, Stand B32) will unveil its heavy-duty Skyworth Z9 E-truck featuring Level 4 autonomous technology. Other Chinese brands present include FAW Trucks, Dongfeng, Foton, Geely's Farizon, Wisdom Motor, and DeepWay. On the supplier side, CATL (Hall 19/29, Stand B36) will showcase new commercial-vehicle battery innovations.

**Key supplier innovations.** Bosch (Hall 19/29, Stand A31) shows a new rigid eAxle integrating motor and gearbox for 18–49 tonne commercial vehicles, plus an 800-volt high-voltage blower module and fuel cell systems (100–300 kW) for buses and trucks. ZF (Hall 21, Stand B42) showcases its TraXon 2 Hybrid drivetrain and connected system intelligence software. Mahle (Hall 11, Stand D07) presents a new range extender system that can convert battery-electric trucks into plug-in hybrids, combining a high-voltage generator with a compact combustion engine. Trailer Dynamics (Aachen) presents E-trailers as part of the Krone Nutzfahrzeug Gruppe. British startup Advanced Electric Machines will unveil a 3D model of its largest rare-earth-free motor for heavy-duty applications, prototype expected next year. Munich Electrification presents an innovative chip-circuit-based battery disconnect device for high-voltage commercial vehicle systems, an alternative to mechanical or pyrotechnic fuses.
