# Allego switches operator backend to Ampeco

**Author**: Daniel Bönnighausen | **Date**: 2026-07-09

---

Dutch charging provider Allego is switching to Ampeco for the backend of its charging network. The new charge point management software will be used to manage over 35,000 charging points across 16 European countries. The migration is expected to be completed in Q4 2026.

In recent years, Ampeco has successfully attracted several major customers and partners in the charging infrastructure sector. In September 2024, E.ON Drive Infrastructure selected the Bulgarian firm's charging management platform as a backend solution for its pan-European charge point operator (CPO) business. In July 2025, Ampeco announced partnerships with Latitude for its Italian e-mobility brand Eleven, as well as with EVerged in North America.

Allego has now joined this list, adopting Ampeco's platform for its Charge Point Management System (CPMS). The system will manage over 35,000 charging points across 16 European countries. According to Ampeco, the migration will also include around 1.3 million charging sessions per month, more than 200,000 registered EV drivers, and over 60 active roaming connections.

Before making its decision, Allego conducted a formal tender process, evaluating platforms based on migration experience, compliance with industry standards, and integration capabilities with existing company-wide software and hardware infrastructure. "The platform did not just look good on paper, it demonstrated depth across the core CPMS capabilities needed to operate and scale a large charging network, which gave us confidence that it could support both current and future requirements," said Norbert Teichmann, IT Director at Allego.

Ampeco describes its platform as hardware-agnostic and API-based, allowing Allego to integrate existing operational and internal systems while expanding the platform to meet new requirements. Ampeco will handle standards and compliance, hardware integration, ongoing maintenance, roaming and billing processes, and partner integrations. "Partnering with AMPECO gives us a modern, future-proof platform that allows us to focus on what matters most: delivering an exceptional charging experience for every driver who uses our network," said Christian Zeh, COO at Allego.

With the switch, Allego will also gain access to Ampeco's Marketplace, which offers pre-built integrations for payment, fleet management, smart charging, and energy services. Ampeco says its platform is already used by over 200 charging infrastructure operators across more than 70 markets, with more than 120,000 charging points already migrated.

The Ampeco migration pertains to the operator side of Allego's network, distinct from Allego's customer-facing mobility service provider (MSP) and app strategy — in September 2025 Allego announced plans to develop a new MSP platform with Deftpower, and in May 2026 launched its new Allego app providing access to charging points across Europe.
