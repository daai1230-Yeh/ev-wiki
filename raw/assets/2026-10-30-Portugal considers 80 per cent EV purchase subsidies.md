# Portugal considers 80 per cent EV purchase subsidies

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-10-29

---

Portugal will support low-income households in purchasing an electric car with up to 80 per cent of the vehicle price in the future. The new program is expected to cover around 20,000 vehicles. In parallel, the government will continue its regular electric car incentive program with a budget of €50 million.

Portuguese Environment and Energy Minister Maria da Graça Carvalho announced the new subsidy during parliamentary debates on the 2027 state budget. For the first time, there will be a dedicated programme for low-income households, offering subsidies of up to 80 per cent of the vehicle value for around 20,000 electric cars.

According to the Portuguese Ministry of the Environment, the eligible purchase price will be capped at €15,000 excluding VAT. If a combustion-engine vehicle is scrapped at the same time, the upper limit increases by €1,000 to €16,000. Additionally, up to €1,000 will be granted for the installation of a charging point. It remains unclear what income thresholds will apply and when the programme will launch.

## Regular EV subsidies to continue

Portugal also intends to continue its existing subsidy for the general population. Around €50 million will be available in 2027, with conditions essentially following previous programmes.

At the beginning of 2026, the government launched a €17.6 million programme for electric cars, electric two-wheelers, and charging infrastructure. Private individuals could receive up to €4,000 for an electric car, provided a combustion-engine vehicle at least ten years old was scrapped. The eligible purchase price was capped at €38,500, or €55,000 for vehicles with more than five seats. In June, another funding round of €10 million was introduced by the Portuguese Environmental Fund.

With the new programme, Portugal would deviate for the first time from fixed-amount subsidies; a percentage of the vehicle price will be covered instead.

The government also plans to support up to 9,000 electric vehicles for micro-enterprises, subsidies for lower-emission heavy-duty commercial vehicles and their charging infrastructure, and co-financing of electric buses in sparsely populated regions.

The 2027 state budget is not yet final. Parliament approved the draft in its first reading on 28 October; the final vote is scheduled for 24 November.
