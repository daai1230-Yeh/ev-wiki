# Tesla to phase out Cybertruck production in Texas in favour of Cybercab

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-06

---

Tesla will phase out production of the Cybertruck at its Texas plant mid-next year. This will free up space to allow the Robotaxi Cybercab to be built in larger quantities. It remains unclear whether the Cybertruck will be completely discontinued or if it will be produced elsewhere in the future.

Tesla CEO Elon Musk shared this update on X on Friday: "Tesla will be winding down production of the Cybertruck at Gigafactory Texas around the middle of next year to make room for increased Cybercab production."

This means production of the polarising electric pickup will end in Texas after just four years. Tesla celebrated the start of production in July 2023, but the model never gained traction in the mass market. Tesla does not release Cybertruck registration figures, grouping its sales under "other vehicles".

At the beginning of the year, Tesla introduced a new base Cybertruck with all-wheel drive from $59,990, down from the previous cheapest price of $79,990. A single-motor RWD variant at $70,000 was discontinued in September 2025 due to insufficient demand. The Cybertruck never came close to the originally promised base price of $40,000.

According to an earlier report by Electrek, Tesla was selling only about 5,000 vehicles per quarter at the time of the price cut, less than ten per cent of the original forecast of over 250,000 units per year. In October 2025 it emerged that Musk had instructed his other companies to take unsold Cybertrucks off Tesla's hands.

Tesla also cut the Cyberbeast price by $15,000 in February, from $114,990 to $99,990.

Two Swiss companies, Werk.lab and Revoltaris, are working to make the Cybertruck compliant for Swiss type approval. Whether these plans survive the announced end of production in Texas remains to be seen.
