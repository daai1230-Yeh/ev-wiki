# Volkswagen opens pre-orders for ID. Aura L5 in China

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-11-17

---

FAW-Volkswagen has opened pre-orders in China for the new ID. Aura L5. The electric mid-size sedan is based on Volkswagen's CMP platform, offers a range of 580 kilometres according to CLTC, and is expected to launch in 2026.

The ID. Aura L5 is the second model in the China-specific ID. Aura family from FAW-Volkswagen. The first model, the electric SUV ID. Aura T6, was launched in September. The L5, in turn, is based on the ID. Aura concept vehicle, which Volkswagen unveiled at Auto Shanghai in April 2025.

At launch, the sedan is available for reservation in three variants. FAW-Volkswagen has not yet announced an official pre-sale price. According to reports by CarNewsChina, interested parties can make a reservation for 99 yuan. This deposit will later be credited as 2,000 yuan towards the purchase. Additionally, owners of a passenger car from the Volkswagen Group or China FAW will receive a bonus of 3,000 yuan when purchasing an additional vehicle or trading in their current one.

The electric fastback sedan measures 4.88 metres in length, 1.88 metres in width and 1.51 metres in height, with a wheelbase of 2.82 metres. Standard equipment includes frameless doors, partially recessed door handles and an electrically operated tailgate. The boot capacity is 525 litres, expandable to up to 1,200 litres.

Technically, the ID. Aura L5 is based on Volkswagen’s Compact Main Platform (CMP), developed for China. It is also the first FAW-Volkswagen model to feature Version 1.4 of the China Electronic Architecture (CEA), which Volkswagen co-developed with Xpeng. The CEA serves as the foundation for infotainment, driver assistance systems and over-the-air updates.

Volkswagen had already announced the CEA in spring as the central architecture for its locally developed China models. It is designed to enable L2++ assistance systems, continuous OTA updates and new AI functions.

## Up to 310 kW and a 580-kilometre CLTC range

FAW-Volkswagen offers the ID. Aura L5 with either one or two electric motors. The single-motor variant delivers 155 kW and achieves a top speed of 175 km/h. In the all-wheel-drive version, 155 kW electric motors are fitted to both the front and rear axles, resulting in a system output of 310 kW.

All variants use lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries and, according to FAW-Volkswagen, achieve a range of 580 kilometres under the Chinese CLTC cycle. Details on battery capacity or charging power have not yet been disclosed. Gotion High-Tech and CATL are named as battery suppliers. Buyers of the 580 Max LiDAR variant can switch to CATL battery cells free of charge during the reservation phase. FAW-Volkswagen values this upgrade at 4,000 yuan (approximately €550).

For driver assistance, Volkswagen relies on the Carizon HS8 system, short for Hyper Sense 8. It features a 192-line lidar on the roof and a Horizon Robotics Journey-6H chip with 420 TOPS of computing power. Supported functions include assisted driving from parking space to parking space in urban areas, motorway navigation and various automated parking functions.

The HS8 system comes from Carizon, a joint venture founded in 2023 by Volkswagen’s software subsidiary Cariad and Chinese chip and AI specialist Horizon Robotics.

## Large triple-display setup in the interior

In the cockpit, the ID. Aura L5 combines a 15.6-inch 2.5K central display with a 10.25-inch instrument cluster and a 25.8-inch augmented reality head-up display. The system is powered by an automotive-grade cockpit chip manufactured using a 4-nanometre process. Supported features include Apple CarPlay, Huawei HiCar and Carlink.

Additional equipment includes ventilated, heated and electrically adjustable seats, a cooling and heating compartment in the centre console and a 14-speaker audio system. FAW-Volkswagen states that there are a total of 44 storage compartments in the interior.

FAW-Volkswagen has not yet announced a price for the ID. Aura L5. For reference, the ID. Aura T6 is priced between 129,900 and 166,900 yuan in China (approximately €17,700 to €22,800).
