# Aptera Reveals The Secret To Building Its Solar EV: China

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: Aug 21, 2026

---

*The California startup has partnered with a design-for-manufacturing company to supercharge its solar EV production plans.*

**Key points**

- Aptera has unveiled its first large strategic investor.
- The California startup partnered with China's Launch Design to fast-track production of the three-wheeled solar EV.
- Launch Design is investing millions of dollars into Aptera and offering access to its production facilities.

Aptera, the California startup that's been working for over a decade to make a solar-powered electric vehicle, has a new partner that should help it assemble the famed three-wheeler faster, more efficiently, and more cheaply.

The company has partnered with China's Launch Design, a design-for-manufacturing firm established in 2000 that specializes in turnkey solutions for automakers worldwide. With over 3,000 employees, Launch Design says it has served brands including Tesla, Cadillac, Renault, BYD, and Chevrolet, working on over 400 vehicles since its inception.

The partnership, valued at approximately $44 million (RMB 300 million), will give Aptera access to Launch Design's overseas pilot production facility, where the first units of the solar EV will be assembled. The Chinese company will also help Aptera develop the assembly fixtures and processes that will then be shipped to Carlsbad, California.

In other words, the initial batch of solar EVs will be made in China, and once processes are set, assembly will move to California. The startup previously said it has ordered the first 40 production bodies and chassis for the first batch of customer vehicles, expected to roll off the assembly line in the fourth quarter.

As part of the partnership, Aptera will pay two-thirds of approved program costs, while the remaining third — up to $15 million — will be paid by Launch Design in warrants to purchase Aptera stock. The investment will cover assembly fixtures, tooling, vehicle testing, pilot production, and high-volume production work.

> "This strategic investment enhances our resources, allowing us to bring proven design-for-manufacturing expertise and a global supply chain to Carlsbad without taking on the capital burden of building that expertise from scratch," said Chris Anthony, Co-CEO of Aptera. "We expect Launch's experience will shorten our path to high-volume production and reduce materials costs."

Aptera said it has been working with Launch Design for over a year. The partnership makes use of Launch Design's international supply chain, allowing Aptera to tap affordable suppliers without doing the heavy lifting. Launch Design's supply base can also deliver finished subassemblies directly to Aptera, rather than loose parts, making final assembly easier. With a big partner, Aptera can also expand production into other markets in the future.
