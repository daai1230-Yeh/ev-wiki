# Sany expands European e-truck portfolio

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-10

---

A few weeks ago, Sany began marketing its e263 electric semi-tractor in Europe. But this is just the beginning: at IAA Transportation, Sany is bringing four derivatives and models — including three- and four-axle versions.

Sany will set up its exhibition stand at IAA Transportation in Hanover (Hall 12, Stand C40), presenting four fully electric vehicles designed for construction, logistics, and municipal applications. Already known is the e263 tractor unit, with 420 kW continuous power, a 636 kWh LFP battery (623 kWh net), and a stated range of over 500 km — the first units are already on German roads. Further derivatives include the e435 8×4, the e345/e360 6×2, and the e363 6×2. In Sany's naming convention, the first digit indicates axle count and the next two digits represent gross battery capacity.

Sany is one of several Chinese manufacturers aiming to establish themselves in the European electric truck market, having announced plans about a year ago to expand under the Sany eTrucks division. "The first vehicles have been operating on German roads since spring 2026," said Kevin Eichele, Head of Product and Business Development at Sany eTrucks Europe. Initial target markets include the DACH region and Spain. Sany already has a European presence through construction machinery — it acquired German concrete pump specialist Putzmeister in 2012, and operates sites in Bedburg and Aichtal.

**Chinese vehicles adapted by Putzmeister.** The Sany eTrucks division carries the suffix "supported by Putzmeister," which handles adapting vehicles to local conditions, customer support, and service infrastructure across Europe. Sany has also partnered with Alltrucks, a multi-brand workshop specialist, for maintenance, diagnostics and repairs, offering 650 service workshops across Europe. Eichele: "The transition to electric heavy-duty transport will only succeed if customers see more than just a vehicle. They need confidence in product maturity, availability, service, and total cost of ownership."

**E-Chassis for long-haul transport and construction sites.** Core portfolio specs:

| | e263 4×2 S/R | e363 6×2 | e345/e360 6×2 | e435 8×4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Type | Tractor/Chassis | Chassis | Chassis | Chassis |
| Wheel config | 4×2 | 6×2 | 6×2 | 8×4 |
| Drivetrain | Dual-motor eAxle | Dual-motor eAxle | PMSM/eAxle | PMSM |
| Rated power | 420 kW | 420 kW | 210 kW | 270 kW |
| Gross combination weight | 42/38 t | 42 t | 28 t | 37 t |
| LFP battery | 636 kWh gross | 636 kWh gross | 600/451 kWh | 350 kWh |
| DC charging | 400 kW | 400 kW | 320 kW | 250 kW |

The e263 4x2R chassis variant is 9.5 m long (vs. 6.64 m for the tractor) with a 5.30 m wheelbase, rated for 38 tonnes GCW vs. 42 tonnes for the 4x2S. The e363 6×2 supports a higher rear axle load (19 t vs. 13 t) for 42-tonne GCWR long-haul applications. The e345/e360 6×2 uses a permanent-magnet synchronous motor (210 kW) and targets the construction industry as a 28-tonne platform with LFP batteries from CATL. The e435 8×4, previously shown at Bauma 2025, delivers 270 kW with a 350 kWh battery for concrete and construction work, up to 37 tonnes gross weight.

Sany says it has already deployed more than 50,000 e-trucks domestically in China and, unlike some Chinese startups relying on contract manufacturers, builds its own vehicles — the e263 is made in Changsha before shipping to Europe for market-specific adaptation by Putzmeister. No order or delivery dates have been announced for the newly presented models.
