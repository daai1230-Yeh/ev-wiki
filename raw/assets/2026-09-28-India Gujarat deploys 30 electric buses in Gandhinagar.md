# India: Gujarat deploys 30 electric buses in Gandhinagar

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-09-26

---

Gujarat, a state in western India, has deployed 30 electric buses for public transport in its capital, Gandhinagar. The state procured these e-buses under the PM e-Bus Sewa Scheme, launched by the country's Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs (MoHUA) in 2023.

The deployment marks Gandhinagar's second phase of electric bus adoption under the PM e-Bus Sewa Scheme. Gujarat commissioned 40 units in the first phase in June, and the latest addition brings the state capital's total under the scheme to 70. Gandhinagar Greenline Transport Service Limited (GGTSL), an entity specially established by the city's municipal corporation, manages this fleet.

Like the first batch, the recently deployed 30 units are all Ecolife e9 nine-metre electric buses sourced from JBM Buses, and they have the same configuration. The company manufactures this model in multiple configurations, and the 70 units plying on Gandhinagar roads can accommodate 38 commuters, including 25 seated and 13 standees.

The electric midi buses feature air-conditioning, a CCTV camera, a wheelchair ramp, a passenger information display, and Electronic Ticketing Machines (ETMs). Four seats are reserved for women and have pink covers to distinguish them from the others. JBM Auto has likely equipped this fleet with a 212 kW permanent magnet synchronous motor and a 210 kWh LFP battery pack that delivers a range of over 200 kilometres.

Gandhinagar will receive 30 more electric buses under the PM e-Bus Sewa Scheme in the future, bringing its total to 100. Seven other cities in Gujarat will also benefit from the scheme, including Rajkot (100 units), Vadodara (250 units), Bhavnagar (100 units), Jamnagar (50 units), Junagadh (50 units), Navsari (20 units), and Gandhidham (80 units). In total, the state will procure 750 electric buses under the scheme.

Across India, the PM E-Bus Scheme aims to support deployment of 10,000 electric buses in cities with a population of up to four million. In cities with a population of over four million, the Ministry of Heavy Industries (MHI) is providing support for up to 14,028 electric buses under a separate PM E-Drive Scheme.
