# Group14 to build battery material factory in Sagunt

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-10-30

---

US silicon-carbon anode specialist Group14 (Woodinville, Washington; Porsche is a key investor) will produce in Europe for the first time. The commitment is tied to a new €150 million (~$169 million) capital increase from an unnamed European investor, which lifts the Series D round above $665 million.

- Site: Sagunt, Spain — "next industrial-scale manufacturing module" for its SCC55 material, initial capacity 10 GWh, raising short-term total capacity to 40 GWh. Existing sites: pilot plant in Woodinville (since 2021) and Sangju, Korea (10 GWh); two 10 GWh facilities planned at Moses Lake, Washington in 2027. Long-term aim: "hundreds of gigawatt-hours".
- Sagunt is also home to a PowerCo (VW) cell factory, set to become a joint venture with Gotion High-tech; the unnamed investor might be linked to VW, but this is unconfirmed.
- SCC55 enables silicon-carbon anodes instead of graphite and has commercial traction in smartphones, drones, hypercars and eVTOL, with EV batteries to follow.
- CEO Rick Luebbe: battery material producers must be located where batteries are made.
- Germany ties: Porsche invested in 2022 (Cellforce, since shut down); Group14 acquired Schmid Silicon (monosilane gas) in 2023; V4Smart (ex-Varta) will use its anode in a second-gen cell; CustomCells signed a supply deal but later went insolvent; BASF and Group14 unveiled a market-ready silicon anode solution earlier this year (Licity 2698 X F binder + SCC55).
