# Vauxhall prices electric Corsa GSE from £32,995 in the UK

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

Vauxhall has confirmed UK pricing and specification for the electric Corsa GSE ahead of order books opening in September. The 207 kW hot hatch starts at £32,995 including the UK Electric Car Grant, with deliveries from winter. The on-the-road price is £34,495, reduced to £32,995 by the grant. Vauxhall positions the Corsa GSE as the electric successor to performance small cars like the Nova GTE/GSi, Corsa GSi and Corsa VXR.

Based on the Stellantis electric hot hatch platform, it's the most powerful production Corsa ever built: a front motor delivers 207 kW and 345 Nm, giving 0–62 mph in 5.5 seconds — Vauxhall's fastest-accelerating current production model. Drivers choose between Sport, Normal and Eco modes; Sport unlocks full output while Eco trims performance and top speed for efficiency.

A 54 kWh battery (51 kWh usable) gives a WLTP range up to 232 miles (373 km), with 0–80% DC charging in around 30 minutes at 100 kW. An 11 kW onboard charger with Vehicle-to-Load is standard.

Chassis upgrades shared with the Opel Corsa GSE sibling include a Torsen mechanical limited-slip differential, Nürburgring-developed suspension with lower ride height, wider tracks, revised steering, and Alcon four-piston front brakes with 355mm discs. In Sport mode, regenerative braking is disabled for what Vauxhall calls "100% hydraulic brake performance."

Two tyre options are offered: standard Michelin Pilot Sport 4S (220-mile WLTP range) or optional Hankook Ventus S1 evo3 (232-mile range). Visually, the GSE gets model-specific bumpers, a front splitter, wheel arch extensions, black roof, rear spoiler and 18-inch three-spoke alloys inspired by classic Vauxhall GTE models. Inside: heated performance seats with tartan upholstery, yellow seat belts, a flat-bottom steering wheel, 10-inch digital displays, and GSE performance pages showing G-forces, acceleration, torque and battery data.

"The Corsa GSE marks Vauxhall's return to the hot hatch segment with the new high-performance variant of Britain's best-selling small car," the company states.
