1. Warm-Up 暖身 3 min

How long are you willing to wait while your car charges on a road trip? Five minutes, twenty, or an hour?

公路旅行時，你願意等車子充電多久？五分鐘、二十分鐘，還是一個小時？

Do you charge at home, at work, or at public stations? What is the most annoying part of it?

你都在家裡、公司，還是公共充電站充電？最讓你煩的是哪一點？

Would you let your car sell electricity back to the grid if it saved you money?

如果能省錢，你願意讓你的車把電賣回電網嗎？

2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Charging speed is becoming the new battleground in the electric car market. At the Paris Motor Show this week, BYD unveiled the Atto 8, a seven-seat SUV with a 115 kilowatt-hour battery and about 405 miles of range on the European test cycle.

充電速度正成為電動車市場的新戰場。本週在巴黎車展上，比亞迪發表了七人座 SUV Atto 8，搭載 115 度電的電池，依歐洲測試標準續航約 405 英里。

The headline number is the charging time. In reasonable weather, the car can go from 10 to 70 percent in just five minutes, and four more minutes brings it to 97 percent. Even in extreme cold, as low as minus 22 degrees Fahrenheit, it takes about 12 minutes.

最吸睛的是充電時間。天氣正常時，從 10% 充到 70% 只要五分鐘，再四分鐘就到 97%。即使在零下 22 華氏度的極寒中，也只要大約 12 分鐘。

InsideEVs says that beats any non-Chinese electric car on the European market. BYD also plans to install 3,000 of its T-shaped Flash chargers across Europe by the end of 2027, because a fast car is useless without a fast station.

InsideEVs 指出，這超越了歐洲市場上所有非中國品牌的電動車。比亞迪也計畫在 2027 年底前於歐洲安裝 3,000 座 T 型閃充樁，因為車再快，沒有快充站也沒用。

Meanwhile, the demand side is already straining the grid. During China's National Day holiday, from October 1 to 3, the monitored highway charging stations completed about 2.97 million charging sessions, up 48 percent from last year.

同時，需求端已經讓電網吃緊。中國十一長假 10 月 1 日到 3 日，受監測的高速公路充電設施完成約 297 萬次充電，比去年同期成長 48%。

The electricity delivered reached 71.6 million kilowatt-hours, up about 49 percent. To cope, State Grid placed 599 emergency chargers and 32 mobile charging vehicles at highway service areas, and some sites used real-time monitoring to steer drivers away from peak hours.

供電量達 7,160 萬度，年增約 49%。為了因應，國家電網在高速公路服務區配置 599 支緊急充電設備與 32 輛行動充電車，部分站點還用即時監控引導車主避開尖峰。

There is another way to ease the pressure: make the car part of the grid. Renault and EDF will launch a bidirectional charging package in France next summer, so owners can feed battery power back into the grid or run their homes from it.

還有另一種減壓方式：讓車子成為電網的一部分。雷諾與法國電力公司 EDF 將於明年夏天在法國推出雙向充電方案，車主可以把電池的電送回電網，或用來供應家中用電。

So the future of charging has two sides: faster for drivers, and smarter for the grid. The question is which one will matter more.

所以充電的未來有兩面：對駕駛更快，對電網更聰明。問題是哪一面會更重要。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型 3 min

The headline number is ___, but what really matters is ___.

最吸睛的數字是___，但真正重要的是___。（例：The headline number is five minutes, but what really matters is the station network.）

___ is putting a real strain on ___.

___ 正對 ___ 造成很大壓力。（例：Holiday travel is putting a real strain on the grid.）

That beats anything else on the market when it comes to ___.

就___而言，這勝過市面上其他產品。

A fast car is useless without ___.

沒有___，快車也沒用。（例：A fast car is useless without a fast station.）

One way to ease the pressure is to ___.

減輕壓力的一個方式是___。（例：One way to ease the pressure is to shift charging to off-peak hours.）

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

If charging takes only five minutes, does battery size still matter? Why would a driver still want a bigger battery?

如果充電只要五分鐘，電池大小還重要嗎？駕駛為什麼仍會想要更大的電池？

BYD is building its own chargers. Should carmakers own the charging network, or should it be open to every brand?

比亞迪自建充電樁。車廠該擁有自己的充電網路，還是應開放給所有品牌？

Ultra-fast charging puts heavy stress on local grids. Who should pay for the upgrades: drivers, carmakers, or governments?

超快充對在地電網壓力很大。升級費用該由駕駛、車廠還是政府負擔？

Some people worry that frequent fast charging shortens battery life, and others say it is fine. Which side do you find more convincing?

有人擔心頻繁快充會縮短電池壽命，也有人說沒問題。你覺得哪一方比較有說服力？

Would you trust a utility to control when your car charges and discharges, in exchange for a lower bill? What are the pros and cons?

如果能換到較低的電費，你會信任電力公司控制你的車何時充放電嗎？優缺點是什麼？

In your country, is the biggest obstacle to EVs the price, the charging network, or the power grid? What would you fix first?

在你的國家，電動車最大的障礙是價格、充電網路還是電網？你會先解決哪一個？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 2 min

Give a 60-second summary: what is new in fast charging, what problem it creates for the grid, and how bidirectional charging could help.

用 60 秒做摘要：快充有什麼新進展、對電網造成什麼問題，以及雙向充電如何幫上忙。

Finish with your own view: "In my opinion, the bigger challenge is ___, because ___."

最後說出你的立場：「我認為更大的挑戰是___，因為___。」
