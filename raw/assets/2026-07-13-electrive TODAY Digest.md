# electrive TODAY Digest — 2026-07-13

**Source**: electrive global newsletter | **Date**: 2026-07-13

---

## News

**Volkswagen Group BEV sales decline in first half of 2026**: The Volkswagen Group delivered 438,500 electric vehicles in the first half of the year, down 5.8 per cent year-on-year. Europe was the exception, with BEV sales growing by eight per cent.

**BMW falls below previous year's battery-electric vehicle sales**: BMW's battery-electric vehicle sales fell in the first half of the year, with declining sales in China weighing on results. Europe remains the most stable market for the automaker's EV business.

**VW board presents restructuring package to Supervisory Board**: Volkswagen's board has presented a 12-point restructuring package, including major model and capacity cuts. Decisions on potential plant closures and further job reductions have reportedly been postponed.

**China NEV exports surpass 500,000 vehicles in one month**: China's New Energy Vehicle market reached a new record in June, with nearly 60 per cent of new sales using alternative drives. NEV exports exceeded 500,000 units for the first time in a single month.

**Mercedes-AMG unveils electric CLA 45 with three motors**: Mercedes-AMG has introduced the electric CLA 45 4MATIC+ with three axial-flux motors and 500 kW system output. The high-performance model will be available in two body variants.

## Number of the Day

Around 2 million BEV and PHEV passenger cars and light commercial vehicles were sold worldwide in June 2026, according to analysts at Benchmark Mineral Intelligence. That is 7% more than in June 2025 and 11% more than in May 2026. (electriccarsreport.com)

## Technology

**LG Energy Solution reportedly expands Tesla battery supply**: LG Energy Solution is reportedly increasing cylindrical cell production at its Nanjing plant in China. The additional capacity is expected to support growing battery orders from Tesla.

**BYD Denza Z supercar debuts in Europe with 2,000 PS**: BYD's Denza brand has unveiled the electric Z supercar at Goodwood Festival of Speed with up to 2,000 PS, sub-two-second acceleration and 1,500 kW flash charging. Customer orders open this summer.

**Alpine previews future electric A110**: At Goodwood, Renault's sports car brand Alpine has shown the electric A110 prototype in motion for the first time. The sports car is based on the new 800-volt Alpine Performance Platform.

**Toyota Gazoo Racing to enter Dakar 2027 with hydrogen Hilux**: Toyota's Gazoo Racing will compete in Dakar 2027 with a fuel cell-powered DKR GR FC Hilux, entering the 'Dakar Future Mission 1000' category. (toyotagazooracing.com)

## Fleets + Infrastructure

**Eviny and Mer merge into Nordic fast-charging giant**: Norwegian charging companies Eviny Fast Charging and Mer are merging to create the Nordic region's largest fast-charging provider. The new company may also integrate Mer's German public charging business.

**Grab to expand EV charging network fifteenfold in Vietnam**: Ride-hailing giant Grab plans to expand its Vietnamese EV charging network from around 400 to more than 6,000 ports by 2028. Nearly half of the new chargers will be installed in Hanoi.

**Plugit acquires Helen's public charging network in Finland**: Finnish charging operator Plugit has acquired energy company Helen's public charging business, becoming the largest public charging operator in the Greater Helsinki area. (plugit.com)

**QX Logistix orders 20 Tesla Semi trucks**: US logistics provider QX Logistix has ordered 20 Tesla Semi trucks, operating from Forum Mobility's new charging depot in California starting in early 2027. (linkedin.com)
