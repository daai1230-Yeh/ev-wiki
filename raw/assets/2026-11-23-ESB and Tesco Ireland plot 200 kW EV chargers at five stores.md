# ESB and Tesco Ireland plot 200 kW EV chargers at five stores

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-11-20

---

ESB and Tesco Ireland have agreed a new partnership to expand public EV charging at Tesco stores across the country. The first phase will see fast and ultra-fast chargers rated up to 200 kW installed at five Tesco sites in Clarehall, Clearwater, Drogheda, Dundalk and Maynooth.

Describing it as the first phase of a growing partnership, the companies have confirmed that additional Tesco sites have been identified for future rollouts, although they have not yet disclosed how many chargers will be installed at each location or when the first units are expected to become operational.

Darrin Honer, Head of Business Transformation at Tesco Ireland, said the agreement would make charging more accessible as part of customers' everyday shopping trips. He added that Tesco Ireland sources 100 per cent renewable electricity for its operations.

The partnership builds on an earlier collaboration between the two companies. In December 2019, it was announced that ESB and Tesco planned to install dual-outlet 22 kW chargers at 52 Tesco stores across 18 counties in Ireland. The new agreement marks a move towards higher-power charging at selected retail locations, with the latest units rated at up to 200 kW.

ESB operates a nationwide public charging network in Ireland, which the company says now includes charging facilities at 50 Tesco sites. The new installations will complement that network and give customers the opportunity to charge their vehicles while shopping.

Suzanne Ward, head of emerging markets at ESB, said: "ESB is proud to partner with Tesco Ireland in expanding access to EV charging across its sites. The electrification of transport is central to Ireland's climate goals, and by working together, we're ensuring that drivers have fast and reliable charging options in convenient locations. This partnership is another step forward in making it easier for drivers to make the switch to electric vehicles and cleaner energy."
