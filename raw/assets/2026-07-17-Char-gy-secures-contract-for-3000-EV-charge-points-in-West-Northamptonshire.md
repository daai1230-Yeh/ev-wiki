# Char.gy secures contract for 3000 EV charge points in West Northamptonshire

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

West Northamptonshire Council has appointed Char.gy to deliver more than 3,000 on-street EV charging sockets, in what the council describes as one of the UK's "most ambitious" local on-street charging programmes. Following a competitive tender, the rollout is funded by the government's Local Electric Vehicle Infrastructure (LEVI) fund plus "substantial" private investment.

The charging points will predominantly be street chargers retrofitted to lampposts at council and parish sites, targeted at residents who lack off-street parking, with a focus on "competitive user tariffs." Pricing has not been revealed, but installation is set to begin "mid 2026." No hardware has been confirmed, though similar Char.gy deployments have used its lamppost-mountable "Flow" range — small box-shaped units with Type 2 connectors, 5 kW and 7 kW variants, contactless or app payment, and ISO 15118 (Plug & Charge) capability.

Aviation, Maritime and Decarbonisation Minister Keir Mather said drivers "will soon have thousands more reasons to go electric," citing £2.85m of government funding and over £600 million to expand the UK's charging network. Char.gy CEO John Lewis added that using lamp columns on residential streets "is bringing charging closer to where people live, without major disruption to neighbourhoods."
