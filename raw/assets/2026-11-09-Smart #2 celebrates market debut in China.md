# Smart #2 celebrates market debut in China

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-06

---

Smart's two-seater electric city car is now being offered in China in the Premium trim line at a list price of 102,900 yuan, just under €14,000. The #2 is designed as the successor to the iconic Smart ForTwo and had its world premiere in Paris in October.

The Smart #2 Premium is available in China at a limited-time introductory price of 99,900 yuan (approx. €13,370). It remains unclear when more affordable trims will arrive. Smart quotes a CLTC range of up to 410 km in China.

Smart is also launching a limited "Couture Edition", restricted to 999 units across the European and Chinese markets combined. The #2 is manufactured locally by Zhejiang Haoqing Automobile Manufacturing, a Geely subsidiary, which supplies China, Europe and other overseas markets.

MIIT filings in August revealed a length of 2.75–2.76 m, width 1.67 m, height 1.56 m, wheelbase 1.88 m and kerb weight 1,130 kg. It is powered by a 60 kW InfiMotion electric motor with a 35.8 kWh LFP battery.
