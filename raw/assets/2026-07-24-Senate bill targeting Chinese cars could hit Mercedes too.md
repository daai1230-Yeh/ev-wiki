# A Bill Taking Aim At Chinese Cars In America Just Advanced In The Senate. It Could Hit Mercedes Too

**Source**: InsideEVs | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Bipartisan legislation that would bar automakers with more than 15% Chinese ownership from selling cars in the US cleared the Senate Commerce Committee. Mercedes-Benz is caught in the crossfire: Chinese automaker BAIC and Geely founder Li Shufu each hold nearly 10% of the company, meaning Chinese entities together own close to 20% of the German automaker. Committee chair Senator Ted Cruz said the bill, as written, would bar Mercedes-Benz from selling cars in the US, but that it needs to change before becoming law, adding that the US "would never consider" banning Mercedes-Benz.
