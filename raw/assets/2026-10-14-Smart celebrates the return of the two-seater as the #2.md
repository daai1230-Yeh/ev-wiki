# Smart celebrates the return of the two-seater as the #2

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-10-12

---

*Condensed summary of the electrive report, not verbatim.*

Smart unveiled the #2 at the Paris Motor Show: an electric two-seater, about 2.79 m long with a 6.95 m turning circle and roughly 1,130 kg kerb weight, due on the market in 2027. A 60 kW rear motor (from InfiMotion) and a 35.7 kWh LFP battery (CATL cells, pack assembled by Geely) give an estimated WLTP range of about 300 km (final homologation pending) and a top speed of 140 km/h per Chinese filings. Fast charging 10-80% takes about 20 minutes; Vehicle-to-Load is supported.

## Background
Since the 2020s restructuring into a 50:50 Geely/Mercedes-Benz joint venture, Smart sold only larger five-seaters (#1, #3, #5); the Fortwo ended in spring 2024. The #2 was confirmed in September 2025 and rides on the new Electric Compact Architecture (ECA), tested in prototypes built from old Fortwo bodies. "Mr. Smart" Johann Tomforde and Head of Design Kai Sieber (his former student) were present at the premiere. A Brabus variant and a 2,000-unit Couture Edition are announced. Hidden in the windscreen are the Oriental Pearl Tower and Stuttgart TV Tower.

## Production and price
Initial production is in China, exported to Europe; building in Europe first would have delayed launch by one to two years, though future European production is not ruled out. Europe chief Wolfgang Ufer: "E-mobility must be available to more people." The European entry price is EUR 19,999 (the newsletter lists pre-orders from EUR 19,990 in four trims up to EUR 25,690), competing on price with much larger electric compacts.
