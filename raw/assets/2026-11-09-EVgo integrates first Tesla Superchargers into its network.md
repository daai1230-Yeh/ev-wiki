# EVgo integrates first Tesla Superchargers into its network

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-11-06

---

EVgo has opened its first site with Tesla Superchargers near downtown Chicago. The twelve charging points mark the beginning of several hundred more Superchargers that the US operator plans to integrate into its own network.

EVgo announced its entry into Tesla's Supercharger for Business programme in August. The first site is in Illinois on property owned by Regency Centers. The sites use Tesla's V4 Superchargers, branded "EVgo Superchargers"; EVgo owns and operates the stations and sets pricing.

The hardware supports up to 500 kW and 1,000 V. With Tesla's Magic Dock, both NACS and CCS vehicles can charge without an adapter, and the longer V4 cables better suit varying port positions.

EVgo began installing NACS connectors at its own stations in 2025 and aims for 500 NACS charging points nationwide by end of 2026. Within two years, at least one NACS connector will be available at all EVgo sites built in 2023 or later.

The new sites will be integrated into Tesla's Trip Planner and in-car navigation. Sites are on Regency Centers properties, mainly shopping centres; in August EVgo announced sites with up to 20 charging points. No specific numbers or full timeline for further sites has been disclosed.
