# India's electric bus registrations grow 40% in H1 2026, reaching 2,944 units; Switch Mobility leads market

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 6 July 2026

---

India registered 2,944 electric buses between January and June 2026. According to data published by the Vahan Dashboard, India's electric bus market recorded 2,944 vehicle registrations during the first six months of 2026. Switch Mobility, JBM and PMI Electro Mobility Solutions together represented 70% of total registrations. Compared to the same period of 2025, the Indian e-bus market grew +40%.

In 2025, India registered 3,961 electric buses, with Switch Mobility ending last year as the leading manufacturer with 1,235 registrations (growing 6-fold), followed by PMI Electro Mobility (744), Olectra Greentech (592) and JBM Auto (564).

Switch Mobility ranked first in H1 2026 with 830 registrations, corresponding to a 28.2% market share. JBM Auto Limited followed with 635 units (21.6%), while PMI Electro Mobility Solutions registered 574 units (19.5%). Together, these three manufacturers accounted for 2,039 registrations. The JBM Group, combining JBM Auto Limited and JBM Electric Vehicles Pvt Ltd, registered a total of 804 buses during the first half of the year, getting very close to leading seller Switch Mobility.

Olectra Greentech Ltd ranked fourth with 280 registrations (9.5%), followed by Pinnacle Mobility Solutions Pvt Ltd (including EKA Mobility brand) with 171 units (5.8%) and JBM Electric Vehicles Pvt Ltd with 169 units (5.7%). Aeroeagle Automobiles Pvt Ltd recorded 113 registrations, while Tata Motors registered 71 units. VE Commercial Vehicles reported 53 registrations, ahead of JSW Mobility (25), AZAD India Mobility Ltd (14), Veera Vidyuth Vahana Pvt Ltd (8) and Neuton Auto Pvt Ltd (1). Thirteen manufacturers registered electric buses during the first half of 2026.

Comparing H1 2026 with 2024 and 2025, Switch Mobility Automotive Ltd recorded the strongest market share growth over the three periods: 3.6% of registrations in 2024, 21.0% in 2025 and 28.2% in H1 2026. Tata Motors continued to represent a marginal share of registrations compared with previous years — 39.2% in 2024, 5.0% in 2025 and just 2.4% in H1 2026 with 71 registered buses. The OEM recently announced it has secured 500 e-bus orders as electric commercial vehicle bookings exceed 3,400 units; the Indian group is in the process of taking over Iveco Group following a deal announced in September 2025, with closing of the transaction expected in Q3 2026.

JBM Auto Limited increased its market share from 15.0% in 2024 to 15.7% in 2025 and 21.6% in H1 2026. PMI Electro Mobility Solutions represented 14.3% of registrations in 2024 and 23.4% in 2025; during H1 2026 it registered 574 buses, accounting for 19.5% of the market and remaining among the three largest manufacturers by registrations.
