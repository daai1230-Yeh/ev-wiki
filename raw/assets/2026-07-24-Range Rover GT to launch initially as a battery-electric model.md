# Range Rover GT to launch initially as a battery-electric model

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-22

---

The GT will become the first fully electric Range Rover, based on JLR's new Electrified Modular Architecture (EMA) platform — the brand's fifth model. It will launch exclusively as a battery-electric vehicle and is currently undergoing final validation tests. JLR will build it alongside combustion-engined and hybrid models at its Halewood plant in the UK. While the GT launches EV-only, the EMA platform supports other powertrains, and Range Rover has confirmed a full-hybrid variant will follow later in the model's lifecycle.
