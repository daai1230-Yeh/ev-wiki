# Nio goes all-in on AI at 12th anniversary tech day, sees over $1.2 trillion in auto AI value

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-25

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- Nio launched new AI-powered smart driving and cabin systems and aims to manage over $52 billion in battery assets by 2030.
- Nio has deployed more than 7,500 enterprise AI agents and plans to use AI to operate over 10,000 battery swap stations by 2030.

Nio Inc (NYSE: NIO) held its third Nio IN tech day in Guangzhou on Wednesday, unveiling its next-generation smart driving world model and cabin intelligence system while showcasing its latest progress toward becoming an AI-native company.

The event coincided with the Chinese EV maker's 12th anniversary and marked the tech day's first return after a 1-year hiatus in 2025.

The company officially launched Nio World Model (NWM) 3.0 and NOMI Intelligence 4.0, and shared progress on using AI to power its corporate operations and energy network.

Smart EVs represent the most valuable form of physical AI, and the automotive industry will become the largest sector for AI applications, Nio founder, chairman, and CEO William Li said at the event.

He noted that the global automotive industry chain is worth about $7 trillion to $9 trillion, and if AI generates an additional 15% in value, it will unlock more than $1.2 trillion in new commercial value.

## NWM 3.0 smart driving upgrades

NWM 3.0 is the third major iteration of Nio's world model since its first debut in July 2024, delivering significant breakthroughs in onboard computing efficiency and response speed.

Ren Shaoqing, Nio's senior vice president and head of autonomous driving development, said NWM 3.0 introduces 4D spatiotemporal compression technology, reducing onboard computing resource requirements for attention by 36-fold.

Through vehicle-level joint optimization of algorithms, chips, and chassis control, chassis control latency is cut by 78% and overall vehicle control latency by 54%, Ren said.

On the training side, NWM 3.0 uses a dual closed-loop reinforcement learning architecture across observation and latent spaces, improving overall training efficiency by 5 times over the previous generation, while introducing a reward model to dynamically evaluate real-world road conditions.

Compared with its predecessor, NWM 3.0 reduces driver interventions by 40%, boosts users' sense of reassurance by about 60%, and increases overall data volume by 7 times compared with NWM 2.5.

System versions featuring NWM 3.0 will begin regulatory filings in China starting in December and will be rolled out to all users across 4 systems — Banyan, Cedar, Cedar S, and Coconut+ — upon approval, Nio said. Banyan, Cedar, and Cedar S power Nio-branded vehicles, while Coconut+ is for the Onvo brand.

## NOMI 4.0 overhauls cabin interaction

The newly launched NOMI Intelligence 4.0 has undergone a system-level architectural overhaul, breaking away from the limitations of traditional in-car voice assistants that only passively execute commands along preset workflows, Nio said.

Backed by an in-house foundation model matrix and the industry's first vehicle-native AI agent framework, NOMI can perceive both interior and exterior environments, call on vehicle-wide tools, and autonomously understand user intent, plan, and complete tasks, said Ted Li, Nio's senior vice president of product experience.

Cedar 2.0.0, the first software release to feature NOMI 4.0, will be submitted for regulatory filing in early December, with roll-outs starting gradually after approval.

The Cedar S and Banyan systems are expected to complete adaptation and receive the update in the second quarter of 2027, with the technology later expanding to Onvo's Coconut and Coconut+ systems.

## AI-native organization and energy network

William Li said that automakers themselves must become AI-native organizations by embedding AI deeply across all business operations, which will play a critical role in the next stage of industry competition.

Since 2023, Nio has advanced organizational transformation around 12 categories of Cell Business Units (CBUs) — including users, vehicles, R&D projects, components, batteries, and charging and swapping stations — building a 6-layer AI-native operating architecture focused on user value creation.

AI has been integrated into Nio's R&D, manufacturing, logistics, and user service operations, with the company deploying more than 7,500 AI agents that have logged over 2.9 million cumulative executions.

Among the first business units to adopt the CBU model, Nio Power is using multiple AI agents to achieve granular management of every battery and every station, improving asset value, user experience, and energy efficiency.

By 2030, Nio Power expects to manage more than 350 billion yuan ($52 billion) in battery assets and operate over 10,000 battery swap stations, said Stanley Qu, Nio's chief financial officer and head of Nio Power.

Nio Power is also projected to provide more than 200 million annual charging and swapping services and deliver over 10 billion kWh of energy annually by then.

As of November 25, Nio had built 9,896 charging and swapping stations across China — comprising 4,285 battery swap stations, 5,611 charging stations, and 32,869 charging piles — and had provided more than 130 million cumulative battery swaps.

## Stepping up AI strategy

Nio has ramped up its strategic investment in AI this year, establishing an AGI committee in January to coordinate AI applications across its entire business chain.

Its chip subsidiary GeniTech Co Ltd (Shenji), spun off in June 2025, also showcased its NX9031 chip lineup in July, spanning smart driving, embodied intelligence, and AI agent inference.

William Li said during an earnings briefing in early September that Nio is not only an automaker, but also an AI and energy company, adding that capital markets have yet to fully recognize the value of its full-stack technology, AI capabilities, and energy network.

The tech day comes just ahead of the 2026 Guangzhou Auto Show, which opens on November 27. The company is reportedly set to unveil a special edition of the ES8 at the show, adding a new option to the large all-electric SUV that has been on the market for more than 1 year and continues to anchor the main brand's sales.

Next, Nio will report its third-quarter 2026 financial results on December 1 and host Nio Day 2026 in Wuhan on December 19, where it will officially unveil the all-new ET5 Touring wagon.
