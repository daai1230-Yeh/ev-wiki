# Hero MotoCorp gets close to 10,000 bookings for E85 bikes, sees flex-fuel as ICE growth lever

**Source newsletter**: ETAuto Latest News — Daily Morning Newsletter | **Date**: September 12, 2026

---

Hero MotoCorp has over 10,000 inquiries and bookings are close to reaching 10,000, according to Ashutosh Varma, Chief Business Officer of Hero MotoCorp's India Business Unit, speaking to ETAuto. The two-wheeler major expects E85 motorcycles to gain traction as ethanol fuel availability expands, positioning flex-fuel as a growth lever for its ICE (internal combustion engine) business alongside EVs.

Hero MotoCorp launched India's first E85 flex-fuel compatible bikes with the Splendor+ E85 and HF Deluxe E85, priced at ₹82,810 and ₹72,792 respectively. Both models can run on ethanol-blended petrol with ethanol content ranging from 20% (E20) to as high as 85% (E85).

Hero MotoCorp believes the initial response to both E85 fuel-ready motorcycles is a strong indication of the potential these models have in the mass market, as fuel availability and consumer awareness around flex-fuel options continue to grow across India.
