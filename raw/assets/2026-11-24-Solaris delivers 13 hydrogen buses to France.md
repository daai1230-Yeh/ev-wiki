# Solaris delivers 13 hydrogen buses to France

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-11-23

---

Solaris has delivered thirteen Urbino 12 hydrogen buses to the Béziers Méditerranée community association in southern France. In future, the vehicles will be used by the beeMob local transport network.

Polish bus manufacturer Solaris has delivered 13 fuel-cell buses to the Béziers Méditerranée community association. The vehicles were procured through the French central procurement agency UGAP and are intended for use in the beeMob public transport network. This delivery marks Solaris’ first in the southern French region.

The 12-metre-long Urbino 12 hydrogen buses are set to contribute to the modernisation of the existing bus fleet. According to the manufacturer, the vehicles are designed to complete a full day of operation on a single hydrogen refuelling, depending on route and operating conditions. However, Solaris does not provide specific range figures or details on the fuel cell output of the delivered buses in its announcement.

Each bus offers space for more than 90 passengers. Features include passenger information systems, video surveillance, and USB charging ports. Additionally, the buses are equipped with a blind-spot assistant and the remote diagnostics system eSConnect, which enables real-time monitoring of vehicle parameters. This is intended to support operators in maintenance and fleet management.

## Hydrogen infrastructure by Qair

The delivery is part of a broader project to establish a regional hydrogen economy. This includes a hydrogen refuelling infrastructure developed by energy company Qair in Béziers. The project aims to support the local production and use of green hydrogen from renewable energy sources.

This links the procurement of the fuel-cell buses with the expansion of the necessary supply infrastructure. However, Solaris does not disclose details such as the production capacity of the hydrogen facility, refuelling capacity, or the specific costs of the project. The manufacturer also does not provide a contract value for the 13 vehicles.

For Solaris, the delivery marks a further expansion into the French market. According to the company, it has delivered nearly 700 buses to France to date. Across Europe, the manufacturer states that more than 900 Urbino hydrogen buses are now in operation in over 50 cities.

Solaris is also active in France with other electric drivetrain technologies. In October, it was announced that the manufacturer will deliver ten more trolleybuses to Saint-Étienne. The 18-metre-long Trollino 18 buses are scheduled for delivery at the end of 2027 and will expand the local Solaris fleet to a total of 41 vehicles. Unlike the fuel-cell buses for Béziers, these trolleybuses primarily draw their energy from overhead lines and can also travel on routes without catenary wires thanks to additional batteries.

Solaris is part of the Spanish CAF Group and develops and manufactures its vehicles in Poland. In addition to hydrogen buses, its portfolio includes battery-electric buses and trolleybuses.
