# Volvo launches ES90 sales in South Korea

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 27.07.2026

---

Volvo Car Korea has initiated sales of the new ES90 — the brand's flagship all-electric sedan — in one of its key global markets. It will be offered in South Korea with three powertrain options from a starting price of 72.94 million won (~€43,500), positioned as a premium alternative to models like the Mercedes-Benz EQE and BMW i5. The company says the Korean price is over 50 million won lower than in major European markets, a difference of around €29,850.

At a launch event in Seoul, Volvo Car Korea CEO Lee Yoon-mo told reporters: "The sales target is more than 3000 vehicles per year, and the strategic pricing will come as a major appeal of the premium vehicle," adding that "the ES90 represents the culmination of Volvo's vision for electrification and our next-generation flagship technologies."

The ES90 is the first Volvo model to use an 800-volt architecture and its second built on the SPA2 platform. It will be sold in Korea with three powertrain options: Single Motor Extended Range (245kW, 480Nm, 0-100km/h in 6.6 seconds), Twin Motor AWD (330kW, 670Nm, 5.4 seconds), and Twin Motor Performance (500kW, 870Nm, 4 seconds) — all electronically limited to 180km/h. The ES90 also features a 3102mm wheelbase, up to 706km of WLTP range in Twin Motor guise, and peak charging power of 350kW via its 800-volt architecture.
