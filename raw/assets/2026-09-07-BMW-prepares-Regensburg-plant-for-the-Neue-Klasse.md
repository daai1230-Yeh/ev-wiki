# BMW prepares Regensburg plant for the Neue Klasse

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 04.09.2026

---

During a three-week production pause (Aug 8–30), BMW's Regensburg plant — its highest-volume European site, building ~342,500 cars in 2024 — installed a new pre-assembly line for "battery marriage," where the high-voltage battery is fused to the body early in assembly for future Neue Klasse EVs. Batteries move automatically via conveyor bridge from a new high-bay warehouse (holding a full day's supply) to robotic assembly stations. Every vehicle-assembly facility was updated to allow flexible production of multiple models and drivetrains on shared lines, building on earlier 2024/2025 upgrades to body construction and paint shop, plus a new Neutraubling body-construction facility.

Regensburg currently builds the X1/iX1 and X2/iX2 across combustion, PHEV, and BEV variants, over 1,400 vehicles/day. BMW hasn't specified an exact Neue Klasse production start date beyond "the second half of this decade," but the new battery-marriage line is described as a critical prerequisite.
