# Mercedes-AMG sets price for three-motor CLA 45 at 80,000 euros

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-08-31

---

The Mercedes-AMG CLA 45 4MATIC+ is now available for order from dealerships and online. Equipped with three axial-flow motors, the all-electric CLA is available as a sedan and a Shooting Brake, delivers 500 kW, and costs roughly 80,000 euros in Germany.

Mercedes-AMG is expanding the electric CLA lineup with a particularly high-performance variant. The new CLA 45 4MATIC+ features the axial-flow motor technology that Mercedes-AMG recently introduced in the GT 4-door. The performance and sports car brand AMG is pricing the sedan version starting at 79,039 euros (gross) and the Shooting Brake starting at 80,408 euros.

These purchase prices apply to Germany, so delivery costs or costs for factory pickup must also be factored in. Mercedes-Benz has so far offered the CLA in three rear-wheel-drive versions (CLA 200, CLA 250, and CLA 250+) as well as an all-wheel-drive version called the CLA 350 4MATIC.

The electric model series is considered a beacon of hope within the company and the launch got off to a promising start. After Mercedes' sales of battery-electric vehicles declined in 2025, the Mercedes-Benz Group's global BEV deliveries rose again by 11 per cent to 50,400 vehicles in the first quarter of 2026. The new CLA series is considered a key driver of this growth. According to the manufacturer, demand for the model has led to three-shift production at its plant in Rastatt in the southwest of Germany. With the CLA 45, Mercedes-AMG is now expanding the model lineup to include a performance variant.

At the heart of the AMG powertrain is the combination of 800-volt architecture and axial-flow motors, designed to deliver exceptionally high power density. This enables acceleration from 0 to 100 km/h in 2.7 seconds, with a top speed of 250 km/h increasing to up to 270 km/h with the optional AMG Dynamic Plus package. Added to this is a range of up to 670 km for the sedan and up to 640 km for the Shooting Brake – as well as a charging time of 22 minutes (from 10% to 80% SoC) at a compatible fast-charging station.

As for the three-motor configuration, two motors are located on the rear axle and one on the front axle. The peak power output is 500 kW, the continuous power output is 450 kW, and the maximum torque is 1,759 Nm. According to Mercedes-AMG, all three axial-flow motors are completely decoupled from one another, which allows for maximum variable torque distribution between the front and rear wheels. The German luxury carmaker says the drive torque can be distributed individually between the two rear wheels with torque vectoring.

A large 94-kWh battery serves as the energy storage system, enabling DC charging rates of up to 330 kW and AC charging rates of up to 22 kW. This battery is even larger than those installed in the "standard" CLA models (which have a maximum capacity of 85 kWh). AMG estimates the combined energy consumption at 16.5 to 19.6 kWh per 100 kilometres, depending on the variant.

The powertrain concept is complemented by an adaptive suspension and innovative, active aerodynamic elements, which, depending on the model, include a rear spoiler or roof-edge spoiler to increase downforce at higher speeds.

In terms of design, the CLA 45 4MATIC+, measuring 4.74 x 1.86 x 1.47 metres, is tuned for athletic performance in true AMG style. The long wheelbase of 2.79 metres, the short overhangs, the dynamic roofline, plenty of high-gloss black accents, and side sill trim also give the model a powerful visual presence. The rear view is defined by a wide rear bumper and a sharply contoured diffuser. Inside, you'll find performance seats and, among other things, a number of chic design details.
