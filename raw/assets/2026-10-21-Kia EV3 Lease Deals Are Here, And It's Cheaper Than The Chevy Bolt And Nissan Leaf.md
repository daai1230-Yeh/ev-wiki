# Kia EV3 Lease Deals Are Here, And It's Cheaper Than The Chevy Bolt And Nissan Leaf

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 2026-10-20

---

Kia's new entry-level EV is coming for the competition. The Kia EV3 can now be leased or financed. On a 36-month lease, it is more affordable than its competitors, and for less than $400/month customers can get an all-wheel-drive model.

Kia has big expectations for the new EV3 crossover in the United States. After debuting in August with a $31,385 starting price, the entry-level electric hatchback is now coming after its biggest competitors with aggressive lease and finance offers.

According to Kia, the base-spec 2027 EV3 Wind with a front-wheel-drive powertrain can be had for $321/month with a $3,999 down payment, a 36-month term, and a 10,000-mile yearly limit.

The standard EV3 comes with a single, front-mounted electric motor that makes 201 horsepower. A 58.3-kWh battery pack enables an EPA-estimated range of 221 miles.

For just $8 more each month, customers can get a 2027 EV3 Wind front-wheel drive with a larger 81.4 kWh battery and 321 miles of range. The EV3 is also available with dual-motor all-wheel drive, and Kia wants $379/month for 36 months for the mid-spec Wind, which offers heated front seats, a wireless phone charger, and wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.

Kia is also putting $1,500 on the hood for new EV3 purchases, and customers who prefer financing get 0.9% APR for 48 months.

With these numbers, the EV3 is the second-most affordable EV to lease in Kia's American lineup, after the discontinued Niro EV but before the larger EV6. It also undercuts the Chevrolet Bolt and Nissan Leaf: with the same contract length and down payment, the base 2027 Chevy Bolt costs $423/month to lease, while the 2027 Nissan Leaf S+ costs $474/month (SV+ trim: $449/month). The Bolt and Leaf, both front-wheel drive only, are more expensive than the all-wheel-drive EV3. Range-wise, the longest-range Leaf delivers 303 miles, while the Bolt manages 262 miles.

When buying with cash, the 2027 Chevy Bolt is still the cheapest at $28,995, but with the $1,500 bonus the Kia EV3 starts at $29,885. The Nissan Leaf starts at $31,565.

Kia said it expects the EV3 to be its best-selling electric model in the U.S., but it remains to be seen whether Americans want a small battery-powered commuter car in their driveways.
