# Nio boosts deliveries and revenue in Q2

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

Chinese EV manufacturer Nio delivered 107,658 vehicles in the past quarter, a 49.4 percent increase compared to Q2 2025. Revenue rose 69.1 percent to 32.1 billion yuan (~€4.1 billion), while net losses decreased significantly.

Deliveries in Q2 2026 rose 49.4 percent year-over-year and 29.0 percent quarter-over-quarter, consisting of 60,945 vehicles from the main Nio brand, 29,124 from Onvo, and 17,589 from Firefly. CEO William Bin Li said the high-end SUVs ES8 and ES9 are performing particularly well, as are the Onvo L90 and L80, and the Firefly, which has been the No. 1 in China's "premium compact car" segment for 15 months. This is only the second time Nio has exceeded 100,000 deliveries in a quarter.

For Q3, management guided to 108,000–111,000 deliveries, representing 24.0%–27.5% year-over-year growth.

Vehicle sales revenue rose 80.1 percent year-over-year to approximately 29 billion yuan (~€3.7 billion), outpacing delivery growth and implying a higher average selling price — roughly €38,600 versus €32,000 in Q2 2025 by electrive's estimate. Vehicle gross margin reached 18.5 percent, up from 10.3 percent in Q2 2025, meaning vehicle manufacturing itself is now profitable, though this excludes R&D, marketing, admin, and other costs.

Net loss narrowed sharply to 528 million yuan (~€68 million) from 4.99 billion yuan (~€640 million) a year earlier.

International business remains weak: only 1,129 new registrations across all seven European countries where Nio operates last year. Nio has said it will slow international expansion to focus more on China, consistent with its recent closure of the Nio House showroom in Hamburg.
