# Lucid Gravity: new trim logic with a €94,900 price tag

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 18.08.2026

---

Lucid has introduced new equipment variants for its electric SUV Gravity in Europe and reduced its base price by €5,000 - to €94,900, in the case of the Gravity Touring. Four equipment lines are now available, with two motorisations still on offer.

Until now, Lucid has offered only two battery-electric SUV variants in Germany – the Gravity Touring and Gravity Grand Touring – which differ primarily in terms of powertrain and battery. Both could be customised with additional extras in the configurator, often pushing the price above the base figures of €99,900 and €116,900, respectively. Now, the US manufacturer is revising its trim logic and introducing a new trim level called Gravity Touring Plus, positioned between the two existing variants. Additionally, Lucid is adding a new top-tier trim, the Gravity Grand Touring Ultimate.

According to a press release shared with electrive, this change aims to "offer European customers a simpler selection process, enhanced standard equipment with technology and comfort features, and an attractive entry price for the brand." The focus is on a streamlined structure and a new starting price of €94,900. At the same time, Lucid is upgrading its standard equipment across all variants: the 'DreamDrive' Premium package, which includes high-beam assist, lane-keeping assist, blind-spot monitoring, and 3D surround-view cameras, is now included as standard. From the Gravity Touring Plus upwards, a seven-seat configuration and the 'Comfort & Convenience Package' are also standard.

The new trim variants are available for order immediately. The first customer deliveries in Europe are expected in autumn 2026. Overview of the four versions:

Lucid Gravity Touring: 418 kW of power, 89 kWh battery pack, combined WLTP range up to 545 km. Starting price in Germany €94,900. Leasing from €949/month.
Lucid Gravity Touring Plus: builds on Gravity Touring, adds seven-seat configuration and Comfort & Convenience Package. Starting price €99,900. Leasing from €999/month.
Lucid Gravity Grand Touring: 617 kW powertrain, 123 kWh battery, WLTP range up to 739 km, premium seats with massage function and higher DC fast-charging capability. Starting price €119,900. Leasing from €1,199/month.
Lucid Gravity Grand Touring Ultimate: same powertrain as above, plus DreamDrive 2 Pro package, Dynamic Handling Package, 22-speaker sound system, premium leather interior. Starting price €147,500. Leasing from €1,475/month.

The Lucid Gravity is a full-size SUV measuring 5.04 metres in length, 2.20 m in width, and 1.66 m in height, with a wheelbase of 3.04 m. It offers standard or optional three-row seating for seven passengers depending on version. Technically based on the battery-electric Lucid Air saloon, delivering 0–100 km/h in 3.6 seconds with the top powertrain. The vehicle features 926-volt architecture, allowing the larger-battery variants to recharge up to 400 km of range in just 14 minutes.

"The updated Lucid Gravity model range provides European customers with even clearer guidance when choosing the SUV that best suits their needs," says Lawrence Hamilton, President of Europe at Lucid.

In the USA, Lucid is simultaneously introducing a new performance version of the Gravity, the Lucid Gravity GT-S, delivering around 787 kW of power, available for order immediately in the USA only, starting at US$125,900. It remains unclear whether the GT-S version will be introduced in Europe.
