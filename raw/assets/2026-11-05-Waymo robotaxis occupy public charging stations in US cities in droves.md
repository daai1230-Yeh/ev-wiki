# Waymo robotaxis occupy public charging stations in US cities in droves

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-04

---

Electric car drivers in some major US cities report that Waymo's robotaxis are regularly occupying public charging stations. "They come in droves," a resident told the Los Angeles Times. Cases have been reported in San Diego, Nashville, and St. Louis, among others.

The Los Angeles Times reports that drivers in San Diego sometimes have to wait up to an hour to charge. While Waymo typically relies on its own charging stations, it reportedly also uses public charging points when necessary. According to InsideEVs, the company now operates its robotaxi service in 15 US cities.

In response to the LA Times, Waymo stated that staff are deployed at certain public charging locations to prevent too many vehicles from congregating at a single station. The Jaguar I-Pace models and Zeekr vans, which are occasionally driven by humans, can also use public chargers during breaks. Waymo emphasised that charging at its own centres remains the priority, and that building its own infrastructure can take time when entering new markets.

InsideEVs also highlights social media posts from EV drivers in Nashville and St. Louis who encountered Waymo vehicles occupying public chargers. This is not illegal. EVgo told the LA Times its San Diego chargers are available to both private drivers and robotaxis, ensuring high utilisation rates, though the situation remains frustrating for private EV drivers.
