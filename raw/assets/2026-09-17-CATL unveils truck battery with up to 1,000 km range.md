# CATL unveils truck battery with up to 1,000 km range

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-16 14:00

---

CATL has unveiled its Tectrans II battery solution for heavy-duty electric commercial vehicles at IAA Transportation. At its maximum capacity, it's said to offer a range of up to 1,000 km and, according to CATL, can be charged to 80 per cent in 25 minutes using megawatt-level fast charging.

This week, CATL has pushed for the spotlight at IAA Transportation in Hannover by unveiling a new battery solution for commercial vehicles. In addition to heavy-duty trucks, Tectrans II is also suitable for medium-duty trucks and buses. The battery will therefore be available in various housing formats, as well as for installation in the underbody or on the roof. However, CATL's new solution primarily targets long-haul electric transport, as the company's representatives emphasised the peak values for range, charging time, and operating costs in long-distance applications during the presentation.

In its full configuration, the system promises ranges of up to 1,000 km (with no test cycle specified yet) and can be charged to 80 per cent in 25 minutes using megawatt fast charging. At the same time, the battery packs are designed to be adaptable so that they can be combined with both central vehicle drives and e-axles. Additionally, Tectrans II can theoretically be installed as a swappable battery. This is because CATL is also active in the battery-swapping business in China and plans a corresponding effort in Europe in cooperation with Octopus Energy.

According to CATL: thanks to the standardisation of the battery modules used, electrical interfaces, and communication protocols, both current and future vehicle models will be able to adopt future battery upgrades without major changes to their platforms. Given the dynamic development of battery technology, this is an important detail. Truck manufacturers will thus potentially need to make few adjustments to their vehicle platforms when adopting battery innovations.

As for the battery itself: It features a gravimetric energy density of 170 Wh/kg, which CATL states is approximately 13 per cent above the industry average. With the same battery capacity, vehicles can "carry about 0.6 tonnes of additional payload" as a result, according to the official presentation. The battery is also designed for a service life of 12 years and 1.5 million kilometres with 70 per cent capacity retention. New features include a newly developed, robust corrosion protection system and an even more stable base plate. CATL has not disclosed the cell chemistry, but it is likely that the system works with both LFP and NMC chemistries, as the Chinese manufacturer is proficient in both technology strands.

At the trade fair, CATL also signed a letter of intent with the DHL Group to develop green freight corridors in Europe. Building on a first agreement between the two parties from September 2024, CATL will, among other things, provide energy storage systems and energy management platforms for DHL's logistics parks. There is also talk of establishing battery-swapping systems and ultra-fast charging systems, as well as pilot projects for future electric vehicles.

Zhu Lingbo, CTO of the CATL Global Business Unit, commented: "Commercial vehicle electrification will unfold over more than a decade. With our deep technological expertise and long-term commitment, CATL is ready to be the most reliable partner for customers worldwide and for the global electrification of freight transport."
