# BMW India targets 30% EV penetration in H2 as luxury car market expands

**Source newsletter**: ETAuto Latest News — Daily Morning Newsletter | **Date**: September 12, 2026

---

Hardeep Singh Brar, President and CEO of BMW Group India, said BMW India expects EV sales penetration to cross 30% in the second half of 2026, and to reach 40% next year, driven by local assembly of the iX1, i5, and i7. The rise is supported by the carmaker's expanding EV lineup and localization efforts.

Electric vehicles accounted for 26% of BMW India's sales in the first half of 2026, up from 21% a year earlier, with the company selling 2,359 electric vehicles between January and June — a 78% increase year-on-year.

BMW has emerged as India's largest luxury carmaker in H1 2026, registering 10,043 vehicles (including MINI), compared with 9,472 for Mercedes-Benz. BMW's registrations grew 15% year-on-year, versus just 4% growth for the overall luxury market.

BMW expects India's overall luxury car market to more than double to over 100,000 units by 2030, up from around 55,000 units last year, with particularly rapid growth expected in the ₹40–50 lakh price segment.
