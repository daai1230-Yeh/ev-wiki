# Exclusive: We Finally Know The Slate Truck's Destination Fee. Here's The Final Price

**Author**: Mack Hogan | **Date**: Jul 14, 2026

---

Slate announced the price of its no-frills electric pickup truck in June but omitted the mandatory destination charge. A company spokesperson has now confirmed to InsideEVs that the freight charge will be $1,450, making the total price of the Slate $26,400 before local sales tax.

Slate's head of public relations and communications, Jeff Jablansky, said that makes it the lowest destination charge of any pickup in the U.S., in keeping with the company's mission to deliver affordability on all fronts.

To hit that price, Slate has stripped the truck of nearly every standard feature expected in a modern car: no power windows or mirrors, no radio, no central screen, and no paint (color requires a wrap). It comes from the factory as a two-door, single-cab pickup with a five-foot bed. Customers can add a "Squareback SUV Kit" for $5,000 or a fast-back SUV body for $7,000 — meaning a two-door SUV version starts at $31,400.

The base truck delivers 205 miles of EPA range and 181 horsepower to the rear wheels. The next-cheapest electric pickup, the Chevy Silverado EV, starts around $58,000; the Tesla Cybertruck starts around $70,000. A sub-$30,000 electric Ford truck is expected in 2027 with more standard equipment.
