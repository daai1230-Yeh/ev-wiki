# The Genesis GV90 Is Here And It's Like a Luxury Lounge On Wheels

**Author**: Nico DeMattia | **Date**: Aug 19, 2026, 11:00pm ET

---

With massive interior space and the ability to turn the front seats around, the GV90 can create a lounge-like interior for four people.

Genesis just cranked its luxury up to 11. Its new electric three-row flagship, the GV90, isn't only the most luxurious electric car in the lineup, it's the most luxurious car in the entire Hyundai Group. And it's also the most impressive EV, with the biggest battery and most advanced powertrains.

The new model comes in two flavors, the normal GV90 and the GV90 Neolun. The latter is the uber-luxury version, complete with coach-style doors.

## Platform and powertrain

All GV90s are built on the same new eMP (Electric Mobility Prime) platform, built specifically for the flagship EV. They all get the same 123.5 kWh battery pack and dual-motor powertrain. Genesis claims a range of 310 miles for the normal seven-seat GV90, though this hasn't been confirmed by any government body. With 350 kW fast charging, it can go from 10%–80% state of charge in 22 minutes. Other tech includes a cloud-based Battery Management System that monitors real-time diagnostics and potential anomalies, and Thermal Runaway Protection which reduces fire risk by separating each cell and reducing the rate at which heat spreads across the pack.

Both axles get electronically controlled limited-slip differentials — an interesting way to get four-wheel torque vectoring from an EV without needing quad motors. Power is ample but not otherworldly, with 657 horsepower and 590 lb-ft. Genesis didn't provide a curb weight, but with seven standard seats and a 123.5 kWh battery, expect it to be hefty.

Multi-chamber air springs with monotube dual-valve shocks handle the ride, while rear-wheel steering sharpens handling and four-piston brakes do the stopping.

## Interior

Where the GV90 impresses most is inside. All of the interior lines are soft, with round edges, padded surfaces, and tons of quilted leather. The only harsh edges are those on the enormous 23.6-inch OLED screen on the dashboard. When parked, it can raise an additional 3.5 inches out of the dash, making it a 24.6-inch screen. Combined with the Bang & Olufsen 3D surround sound system with 25 speakers and Acoustic Lens Tweeters on the dash, the cabin becomes a movie theater when parked.

The vertical shifter behind the steering wheel stays in place when the car is parked and off; when you get in and the car automatically comes on (no start button), the shifter moves to the 2 o'clock position.

## The Neolun

The real party trick comes from the GV90 Neolun, identifiable by its coach doors, like a Rolls-Royce Cullinan — except Genesis goes further by having no B-pillar. A B-pillarless SUV with coach doors is an immense engineering flex, especially since the rear doors can open independently of the fronts. Genesis claims a frame 1.5 times thicker than conventional vehicles, and a chassis strong enough despite the lack of B-pillar. In a rollover, there's a new roof airbag — the world's first.

The Neolun also offers an Executive Suite option, a four-seat configuration allowing front passengers to face rearward. The second row has heated wood veneer floors and heated armrests. A Comfort Partition separates the cabin from the trunk, also like a Cullinan.

Genesis really swung for the luxury fences with the GV90. It'll be fascinating to see how much it costs and whether customers show up for it.
