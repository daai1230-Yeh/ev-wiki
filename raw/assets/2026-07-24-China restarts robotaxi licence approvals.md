# China restarts robotaxi licence approvals

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

China has resumed issuing robotaxi testing permits after suspending new approvals in April following a system-failure incident involving Baidu's Apollo Go fleet, in which dozens of autonomous vehicles came to a sudden halt in the middle of the road in Wuhan on 31 March 2026. Regulators are granting permits again in selected cities after completing an industry-wide safety review at the end of June. Autonomous driving specialist Momenta is among the first to receive a new permit, announcing on 21 July approval for intelligent connected vehicle road testing in Shenzhen. Previously suspended autonomous taxi services are beginning to resume.
