# Renault Twingo E-Tech: UK pricing revealed

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-11-20

---

Renault has confirmed UK pricing for the new all-electric Twingo E-Tech, with the small city car starting at £14,745 once the full £3,750 Electric Car Grant (ECG) is applied. Orders will open in the first quarter of 2027, with customer deliveries expected by March.

The entry-level Evolution version costs £14,745 after the grant, against a list price of £18,495. The mid-range Techno variant starts at £16,245, while the range-topping Launch Edition costs £17,645. All three versions use the same 60 kW electric motor and 27.5 kWh LFP battery. Broadly speaking, the Twingo has a targeted WLTP range of up to 163 miles (~262 km) and supports AC charging up to 11 kW and DC charging at up to 50 kW. Renault quotes a 10-80 per cent DC charging time of around 30 minutes.

At 3.79 metres long, the five-door Twingo is primarily designed for urban use. Its two rear seats slide independently, allowing occupants to prioritise passenger legroom or luggage space. Boot capacity is up to 360 litres, while the cabin features a 10-inch central touchscreen and a seven-inch driver display. The Techno adds built-in Google functionality, a reverse parking camera, automatic climate control and heated front seats. The Launch Edition also features additional driver assistance systems, such as blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert, and hands-free parking.

The new Twingo is being built at Renault's Novo Mesto plant in Slovenia, using the same AmpR Small platform found in the Renault 5 and Renault 4. When the model made its world debut in November 2025, we reported that Renault developed the car in less than two years through its accelerated development programme, working with its Shanghai-baed development centre and Chinese suppliers. The use of LFP cells and a simplified vehicle design were central to its efforts to bring the price below €20,000 in Europe.

The UK price announcement also confirms that the Twingo qualifies for the full UK ECG grant, which Renault has attributed primarily to its European manufacturing and battery supply arrangements. Customers who purchase a £100 priority R Pass can reserve early access to ordering; eligible retail buyers will also receive two years' free servicing if they order by 31 March 2027 and register the car by 30 June.

In a statement, Adam Wood, managing director of Renault UK, said: "Renault has long pioneered in the transition to electric cars, and now we're using all that experience to shake things up again at the most affordable end of the market. Twingo is clever, efficient, full of personality and, priced from £14,745, dramatically resetting expectations of what an affordable electric car can deliver for customers. Le Frog has landed, and we think it's going to make quite a splash."
