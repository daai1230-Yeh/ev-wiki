# BMW iX4 rolls off production line alongside iX3 in Debrecen

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-23

---

Following the BMW iX3, series production of the BMW iX4 has now also begun at BMW's plant in Debrecen - making the new SUV, with its coupé silhouette, the second model of the Neue Klasse to be produced at the Hungarian BMW plant. The iX4 will reach dealers starting March 2027 - initially in two drivetrain variants.

According to BMW, series production of the iX4 in Debrecen actually began in early November. The German brand describes it as a successful production launch, achieved just two months after the plant transitioned to a three-shift operation. The BMW iX4 complements the manufacturer’s Neue Klasse with sporty accents and will be available in three all-wheel-drive variants. Two of these versions are now being built in Hungary at launch, with the top-of-the-line BMW iX4 M60 xDrive set to follow slightly later. Pricing for the iX4 starts at €70,900, as announced at the world premiere in October.

In Debrecen, the iX4 now joins the iX3 SUV, which has been rolling off the production line there since autumn 2025. These two model series currently represent the plant’s entire production output. Fundamentally, BMW’s Debrecen site only began series production at the start of this year. It is dedicated exclusively to all-electric vehicles and covers an area of more than 400 hectares. The BMW Group states that it has invested over €2 billion into developing the site. The plant now employs around 5,000 people.

The factory operates according to a production principle that the company refers to as the ‘BMW iFACTORY’, characterised by a high degree of digitalisation and flexibility. This enables the plant to efficiently manufacture a wide range of vehicle configurations to customer specifications.

According to Hans-Peter Kemser, President and CEO of the BMW Group Plant Debrecen, the start of production for the BMW iX4 marks another important chapter in the history of the Debrecen plant: “Following the record-breaking production acceleration of the BMW iX3, we have now started the production of another Neue Klasse model – an achievement made possible by the expertise, commitment and excellent teamwork of our more than 5,000 colleagues. BMW has found a home in Debrecen and the region, and we intend to strengthen this relationship further in the future. With the BMW iX4, Plant Debrecen is taking on an even greater role in the production of the BMW Group’s fully electric models.”

It is clear that BMW already stated in March 2023, following the successful platform launch, that it planned to launch six models based on the Neue Klasse within 24 months. Automobilwoche confirmed the validity of these plans at the beginning of this year. Four of these six models are now in production: in addition to the iX3 and iX4 from Debrecen, the BMW i3 is already being produced at the main plant in Munich. Series production of the i3 began in August. Furthermore, the large E-SUV iX5 has recently started production in the USA.

The BMW iX4 plays a sporty role in this lineup. Like all Neue Klasse models, it is based on the sixth generation of BMW eDrive technology, featuring cylindrical battery cells and an 800-volt system. The model is set to launch in March 2027 with two drivetrain variants, whose production has now begun in Debrecen: the BMW iX4 50 xDrive offers a range of up to 828 kilometres, while the BMW iX4 40 xDrive provides up to 638 km according to WLTP. The top model, the BMW iX4 M60 xDrive, is reportedly set to follow shortly afterwards. Here is an overview of the technical data for the model range:

Many of the specifications are already familiar from the iX3. This E-SUV is BMW’s first representative of the Neue Klasse. In September, BMW increased production of the iX3 at the Debrecen plant from two to three shifts – thus moving to round-the-clock production. The reason is high demand: BMW confirmed in this context that 100,000 orders for the iX3 had been received in Europe alone by September.

BMW emphasises that the Hungarian plant succeeded in transitioning from series production to a three-shift operation in about eight months. This is “fastest ramp-up ever achieved at a new BMW Group plant”. Specifically, BMW celebrated the start of production in Debrecen in October 2025, with actual series production beginning shortly afterwards. In March, BMW already announced that it would increase production to two shifts due to high demand. This September, the next step to three shifts followed. Incidentally, parallel production of the iX3 is also planned to start in Mexico in 2027, while China will see a locally produced long-wheelbase version of the iX3.
