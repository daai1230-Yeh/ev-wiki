2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文6 min

The European Union wants to build a self-sufficient loop for EV batteries — making them, using them, and recycling them, all inside Europe. But right now, that loop has a hole in it: the recycling technology basically works, yet nobody has found a business model that turns a steady profit.

歐盟希望在境內打造電動車電池「自製—使用—回收」的完整內循環。但這個循環目前有個破洞：回收技術大致成熟了，卻還沒有人找到能穩定獲利的商業模式。

A mid-term report from Germany's HHL Leipzig Graduate School of Management, part of the EU-funded "Safeloop" research project, found that after recyclers strip down a used battery pack, sell the recovered raw materials, and subtract transport, disassembly, and other costs, they still lose about 1.9 euros for every kilogram they process. For a typical 500-kilogram EV battery pack, that works out to a loss of roughly 950 euros per pack.

德國萊比錫商學院（HHL）在歐盟「安全循環」（Safeloop）研究計畫下發表的中期報告指出，回收商拆解廢電池包、賣出回收原料，扣掉運輸、拆解等成本後，平均每處理一公斤電池，仍虧損約1.9歐元。以一個典型500公斤的車用電池包計算，等於每個電池包虧損約950歐元。

Part of the problem is transport. Because used lithium-ion batteries are classified as hazardous goods, shipping them comes with extra paperwork and safety requirements — and on average, moving them the same distance costs 16 times more than shipping ordinary freight.

問題之一出在運輸。由於廢鋰電池被歸類為危險品，運送時需要額外的文件與安全規範，同樣距離的運輸成本，平均是一般貨物的16倍。

Disassembly is another obstacle, because it is hard to standardize. Battery pack designs differ from one carmaker to the next, and sometimes even within the same brand. That forces recycling plants to juggle multiple disassembly processes at once, which makes full automation difficult and keeps the work heavily dependent on manual labor.

拆解則是另一個障礙，因為很難標準化。不同車廠、甚至同一品牌不同型號的電池包設計都不一樣，回收廠必須同時應付多套拆解流程，難以全面自動化，只能大量仰賴人力。

New regulation will raise the stakes further. Under the EU Batteries Regulation, starting in August 2031, new EV, industrial, and starter batteries must contain a minimum share of recycled material. Modeling a 20 percent recycled-content requirement, the HHL team estimated that if recyclers simply pass their losses straight through, battery pack costs for automakers could rise by about 6 percent.

新法規則會讓成本壓力更大。根據歐盟《新電池法》，自2031年8月起，新的車用、工業用及啟動電池都必須含有一定比例的回收原料。HHL團隊以20%回收比例建模估算，若回收商的虧損直接轉嫁出去，車廠的電池包成本恐上升約6%。

Recycling, though, isn't the only path for a retired battery. Giving it a "second life" in stationary energy storage — instead of shredding it right away — can lift the total value created across its lifecycle by as much as 64 percent. The catch is that most of this extra value flows to vehicle owners and storage-system operators, while automakers, who shoulder the higher recycling costs, capture almost none of it — leaving the value chain badly out of balance.

不過，退役電池並非只有回收一條路。讓電池轉作定置型儲能、延長使用壽命，整個生命週期創造的總價值最高可提升64%。問題是，這部分額外價值主要落入車主與儲能系統營運商手中，真正承擔較高回收成本的車廠卻幾乎分不到，造成價值鏈嚴重失衡。

HHL's researchers propose five fixes: design batteries that are safer and easier to take apart, shorten transport distances, push disassembly automation further, encourage more specialized recycling firms, and set stricter binding transport standards. If all five land, they estimate recycling costs could fall by about 34 percent — enough to flip that 1.9-euro-per-kilogram loss into a 0.13-euro-per-kilogram profit. On top of that, HHL suggests creating a "coordinator" role to manage a battery's entire life — from first use, to second-life storage, to final recycling — so costs and revenues can finally be shared more fairly.

HHL提出五項改善方向：設計更安全、更容易拆解的電池、縮短運輸距離、提升拆解自動化程度、扶植更專業的回收企業，以及制定更具約束力的運輸標準。若五項都能落實，估計回收成本可降低約34%，足以讓現在每公斤虧損1.9歐元，翻轉為每公斤獲利0.13歐元。此外，HHL建議設立「協調者」角色，統籌電池從首次使用、二次儲能利用到最終回收的整個生命週期，讓成本與收益能被更公平地分配。

描述「付出成本的人」和「獲得好處的人」不是同一群人，凸顯制度失衡。

6. Discussion 討論題5 min

  • Should the EU go ahead with its 2031 recycled-content mandate even though recyclers are currently losing money, or is that premature?

    既然回收商目前仍在虧損，歐盟是否該照原計畫在2031年推行回收原料強制比例？還是為時過早？

  • Who do you think should ultimately bear the cost of battery recycling — automakers, battery makers, consumers, or taxpayers through government subsidies?

    你認為電池回收的成本最終該由誰負擔——車廠、電池廠、消費者，還是透過政府補貼讓納稅人分攤？

  • Is it fair that storage operators capture most of the value from second-life batteries while automakers absorb the recycling costs? How would you redesign the incentives?

    儲能營運商拿走大部分電池「第二人生」的價值，車廠卻承擔回收成本，你覺得公平嗎？若換你設計誘因機制，你會怎麼改？

  • HHL suggests putting one "coordinator" in charge of a battery's entire lifecycle. Do you think a single coordinating body is realistic, or would open market competition work better?

    HHL建議設立單一「協調者」統籌電池整個生命週期。你認為單一協調機構可行嗎？還是開放市場競爭效果更好？

  • Compare this to other circular-economy challenges you know of — plastics, electronics, textiles. What makes EV batteries especially hard to recycle profitably?

    拿這個案例跟你知道的其他循環經濟難題比較——例如塑膠、電子產品、紡織品。電動車電池為什麼特別難做到有獲利的回收？

  • If recycling costs stay high, could that slow down Europe's overall EV transition? Or is this a separate problem from EV adoption itself?

    如果回收成本持續居高不下，會不會拖慢歐洲整體的電動車轉型？還是這其實跟電動車普及是兩件不同的事？
