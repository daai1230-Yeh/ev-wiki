# Loganair secures BETA electric aircraft for regional services

**Date**: 24 July 2026

---

Loganair is set to order five all-electric ALIA CX300 aircraft from BETA Technologies, with options secured for five more, with UK regional deployment planned from 2029. Scotland's regional airline and BETA signed a letter of intent at the Farnborough International Airshow; Loganair could become Europe's first commercial airline to integrate fully electric aircraft into scheduled operations.

The airline's route network suits relatively small electric aircraft — most Loganair connections are shorter than 100 miles, linking islands and remote regions of Scotland. The agreement builds on the UK's first electric flight demonstration programme completed in March, when BETA's CTOL flew 23 flights in 10 days across Loganair's network, covering more than 1,000 nautical miles in real-world operational conditions. Certification of the CX300 remains a key requirement before the scheduled 2029 launch.
