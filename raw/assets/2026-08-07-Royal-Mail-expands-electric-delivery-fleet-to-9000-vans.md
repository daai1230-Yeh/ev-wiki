# Royal Mail expands electric delivery fleet to 9,000 vans

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

Royal Mail has placed its 9,000th electric delivery van into service, less than eight months after reaching the 8,000-vehicle milestone. Almost one-third of its delivery routes are now served by zero-emission vehicles.

The 9,000th battery-electric van entered service at Royal Mail's Colchester Delivery Office, joining 120+ electric vans already there. 44 UK delivery offices are now fully electric.

Stuart Murphy, Royal Mail's Head of Fleet Transformation: "Reaching 9,000 electric vehicles is a major milestone as we work to transform our fleet. This shift is helping us cut emissions while delivering a better service for our customers, with quieter, cleaner deliveries in communities across the UK."

Royal Mail added roughly 1,000 electric vans in under a year since the 8,000 milestone. Electrification extends beyond last-mile vans: the company introduced its first electric heavy goods vehicles in December for its national distribution network, and is deploying 100+ micro electric vehicles in London, Bristol, Newcastle-under-Lyme, Solihull, Brighton and the Isles of Scilly, plus a fleet of 20 electric cargo bikes for urban deliveries.

Royal Mail began electrifying its fleet in 2017 with an initial order of 100 electric vans, part of its wider push to reach net-zero emissions by 2040. The company says battery-electric vans reduce lifetime running, servicing and maintenance costs versus diesel while cutting emissions and noise in the communities served.
