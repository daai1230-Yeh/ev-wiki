# DSV orders 10 Windrose electric trucks for US-Mexico freight

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Logistics firm DSV, in partnership with transport company Allogic and charging provider Greenspace E-Mobility, is deploying ten battery-electric Windrose E700 Class 8 electric trucks on the I-35 freight corridor — the first commercial fleet deployment on Greenspace's planned "Electric Highway" network linking the US and Mexico. The trucks will run freight between Dallas, San Antonio and Laredo, continuing to Monterrey in northern Mexico. Greenspace will provide charging infrastructure while Allogic operates the vehicles for DSV. The deployment is tied to Greenspace's wider plan for four Texas-based "Green Hubs" opening by Q4 2026.
