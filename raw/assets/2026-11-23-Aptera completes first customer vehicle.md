# Aptera completes first customer vehicle

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-11-20

---

California-based solar electric vehicle developer Aptera has completed its first vehicle destined for a customer. The three-wheeled solar car is set to be delivered to an early investor outside the US.

Aptera Motors' Accelerator Programme targeted early investors who supported the company in financing vehicle development. The planned delivery order is based on the amount of each investment. Hartono, who is also CEO of GDP Venture, is at the top of the list.

According to the company, the California-based manufacturer has completed the first customer-destined example of its three-wheeled solar-electric vehicle. The vehicle is intended for Martin Hartono, the first participant in the Accelerator Programme to invest early in the company. The car features the customer's chosen matte-black paint finish, called 'Noir', and is set to be exported from the USA.

With this completion, Aptera has reached another milestone on the road to market launch. However, the vehicle has not yet been handed over to the customer, nor has delivery been completed. The transition to regular series production cannot yet be inferred from the completion of a single customer vehicle. Aptera has announced its intention to complete further vehicles for early customers as soon as possible.

## From validation to the first customer vehicle

As early as March 2026, Aptera completed the first vehicle on its validation assembly line. The 14-station line is used to test manufacturing processes and produce vehicles for technical inspections. These include brake performance tests, thermal analyses, and crash tests. Vehicles from this production were also intended to prepare for the official certification of consumption and range figures.

Unlike the validation vehicles at the time, the newly completed example is now intended for sale to a customer. Whether all the necessary approval and certification procedures have since been completed is not clear from the current announcement.

The three-wheeled electric vehicle features solar cells integrated into the bodywork, which, under favourable conditions, are said to generate enough electricity daily for up to 40 miles (or around 64 km) of driving. Additionally, the vehicle can be charged externally like a conventional electric car. Aptera states the total range as up to 400 miles (or around 643 km). These figures are currently manufacturer estimates.

According to earlier statements, Aptera had nearly 50,000 reservations for the vehicle. However, these are not binding orders. With the first completed customer vehicle, the company is now approaching commercial market launch. However, the start-up has not yet disclosed when deliveries will actually begin or the scale at which production will be ramped up.
