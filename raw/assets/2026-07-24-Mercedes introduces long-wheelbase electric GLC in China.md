# Mercedes introduces long-wheelbase version of the electric GLC in China

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-22

---

Mercedes-Benz has launched the long-wheelbase electric GLC L in China, offered in three five- and six-seat variants starting at 299,900 yuan. The model uses an 800-volt architecture paired with an 85.5kWh NMC battery from CATL, delivering a CLTC range of up to 703km. Mercedes says 10-80% charging takes 22 minutes, though it has not disclosed peak DC charging power. The all-wheel-drive variant uses two electric motors with a combined 310kW output, enabling a 0-100km/h sprint in 4.9 seconds.
