# Europe registers 8,141 electric buses in H1 2026, growing +49%: Yutong first, Iveco takes second place with 1,156 units

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-08-31

---

Electric bus registrations in Europe reached 8,141 units in the first half of 2026, up 49% from 5,477 units in the same period of 2025. The figures, released by DVV Media Group in its Q2 2026 European Bus Data report, cover buses above 8 tonnes GVW and exclude trolleybuses. The 8,141 battery-electric registrations recorded in six months are equivalent to almost 69% of the 11,819 units registered during all of 2025 in the updated DVV dataset (which also revises 2024 to 7,843 units).

Registrations of buses with alternative drivetrains reached 11,315 units in H1 2026, versus 8,292 in H1 2025. Battery-electric buses accounted for 8,141 of that total (71.9% of the four alternative-driveline categories). Hybrid bus registrations rose from 1,348 to 1,449 units, CNG buses from 1,195 to 1,328, and fuel-cell buses from 272 to 397 (46% YoY growth). The longer-term DVV series shows 4,152 electric buses in 2022, 6,354 in 2023, 7,843 in 2024, and 11,819 in 2025.

**Country rankings**: Italy led H1 2026 with 1,559 battery-electric registrations (up from 369 in H1 2025), followed by the UK with 1,075 (down from 1,299) and Poland with 1,060 (up from 148). Norway registered 660, Germany 553, the Netherlands 510, Portugal 473, France 337, Sweden 326, Spain 223, Romania 343 (up from 161), Denmark 208 (up from 161), Belgium 140, Austria 128, Switzerland 96, Finland 88, Lithuania 69 (through March only), and Greece just 8 (down from 100).

**Manufacturer rankings**: Yutong led with 1,636 electric buses, ahead of Iveco/Heuliez with a combined 1,156 units (911 Iveco + 173 Iveco/Heuliez + 72 Heuliez) and Solaris with 1,022. BYD registered 681, MAN 584, Mercedes 553, Wrightbus 542, Karsan 321, VDL 202, Alexander Dennis 157, and Volvo 153, followed by King Long (146), Zhongtong (131), Irizar (109), Golden Dragon (103), CRRC (100), Hess (76), Oceantia (68), SOR (56), Scania (44) and Isuzu (37).

**City bus segment**: Battery-electric buses represented 61.2% of the European city bus market in H1 2026 (7,480 of 12,215 registrations), up from 57.9% in H1 2025 and 49.6% in H1 2024.

**Hydrogen buses**: Fuel-cell registrations reached 397 units, led by Italy (178) and Poland (119). Solaris accounted for 256 of these, followed by Neso Bus (39), MCV (36), Mercedes (34), Wrightbus (21) and Caetano (6). Combining BEV and FCEV, Yutong still leads with 1,636 zero-emission buses, followed by Solaris (1,278) and Iveco/Heuliez (1,156).

**Hybrid and CNG**: Hybrid registrations totalled 1,449 units, led by Germany (718), Spain (296) and France (148). CNG registrations reached 1,328 units, led by France (593), Italy (295) and Greece (189).
