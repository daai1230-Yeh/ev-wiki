# VW ID. Tiguan enters series production

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-11-12

---

Just a few weeks after the unveiling of the VW ID. Tiguan, Volkswagen has now started series production of the electric SUV at its Emden plant. Although the vehicle will not be available at dealerships until early 2027, thousands of pre-orders have already been placed.

Volkswagen unveiled the new ID. Tiguan in early October. It replaces the electric SUV ID.4 and its coupé variant, the ID.5, although there will be no coupé version of the ID. Tiguan. With the new name, the group emphasises the model's close relation to the combustion-engine bestseller Tiguan, which has sold more than eight million units to date.

The new VW ID. Tiguan offers drivetrains with up to 220 kW, enabling ranges of up to 600 km and towing capacities of up to two tonnes. However, the charging power has drawn considerable criticism: the smaller 58-kWh LFP battery only achieves 105 kW at a DC fast charger, while the larger 77-kWh NMC battery manages 165 kW.

Volkswagen has now launched series production – not at its main plant, where the "Gamechanger" project was recently scrapped, but in Emden as previously planned. The plant near the Dutch border has been producing only electric vehicles for around two years, most recently the ID.4, the ID.7, and the ID.7 Tourer.

## ID.4 production now discontinued

Although Volkswagen has not mentioned this, production of the ID.4 appears to have ended with the start of ID. Tiguan production. Configurable ID.4 versions are no longer available for order; only stock vehicles are offered. ID.5 production must have stopped weeks ago.

The ID. family has delivered more than two million vehicles since 2019. Production in Hall 20b in Emden began in the presence of VW brand chief Thomas Schäfer, deputy general works council chairman Jürgen Mahnkopf, and Lower Saxony's minister-president Olaf Lies (SPD).

## Emden plant has transitioned to electromobility

Christian Vollmer, Board Member for Production and Logistics at Volkswagen: "The start of production of the ID. Tiguan is a special moment for the Emden plant. This launch represents an enormous joint achievement. Over the past years, our colleagues have actively shaped the transition to electromobility..."

According to NDR, the plant has recently been producing 780 vehicles per day across all model series. More than 500,000 EVs have been produced in Emden. However, the plant is on the list of endangered sites that VW might close, as utilisation from 2031 onwards is uncertain.

## Major concerns about the plant's future

Mahnkopf said: "We are very confident that the ID. Tiguan will make a decisive contribution to ensuring that VW can celebrate 70 years of history in Emden in 2034."

Minister-President Olaf Lies, who sits on VW's supervisory board, stated according to Handelsblatt that there is "no end date for car production in Emden". The first collective bargaining negotiations take place on 24 November; IG Metall has called for a demonstration that day under the slogan 'Save VW Emden'.
