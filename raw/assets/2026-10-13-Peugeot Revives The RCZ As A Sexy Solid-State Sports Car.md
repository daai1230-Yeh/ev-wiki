# Peugeot Revives The RCZ As A Sexy Solid-State Sports Car

**Author**: Andrei Nedelea | **Date**: 2026-10-12

---

Peugeot says the electric concept’s solid-state battery and 1,000-volt architecture enable a 10–80% charge in just 10 minutes.

- Peugeot revives the RCZ as an electric concept with a solid-state battery and claimed 10-minute charging.
- Its low-slung coupe design previews a new styling direction for future Peugeot models.
- The bold Hypersquare steering controller is confirmed for the next E-208, but a production RCZ isn’t.

The RCZ Vision revives Peugeot’s distinctive coupe (sold 2009–2015) as an electric concept. It is purely a concept with no stated production intent, but previews Peugeot’s design direction and ambitions.

Underneath sits an architecture based on the Stellantis STLA One platform, with a Factorial-developed solid-state battery and a 1,000-volt electrical system. Peugeot claims 500 horsepower, up to 373 miles (600 km) of range, and a 10–80% charge in 10 minutes. Provisional WLTP consumption target is 11.5 kWh/100 km (about 5.4 miles per kWh).

The shape keeps the low coupe with broad shoulders, sculpted sides, and a double-bubble glass canopy flowing toward the tail. Horizontal “three-claw” lights introduce the new “Feline Future” design direction. An active rear spoiler hides when parked, and forged wheels measure 21 inches front and 22 inches rear (a staggered setup that may suggest rear-wheel drive, unconfirmed).

The battery is just 110 mm thick, helping the low silhouette, whereas Porsche and Alpine keep batteries out of the floor for a similar look.

Inside is the “Hypersquare” rectangular steering controller, with steer-by-wire (no mechanical connection to the front wheels), technology Peugeot has already demonstrated in its Polygon concept. Peugeot confirms the production Hypersquare helm is destined for the next 208, a fully electric B-segment hatchback expected next year, which will also be the first model with the new family face.
