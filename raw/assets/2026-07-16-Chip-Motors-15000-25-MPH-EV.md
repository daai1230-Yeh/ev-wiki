# EV Startup Reveals $15,000 25-MPH Car That You'll Never Have To Park

**Author**: Tim Levin | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

---

Miami-based startup Chip Motors emerged from stealth with preorders open for its first product: a boxy, low-speed electric vehicle with no doors, a top speed of 25 mph, and a standout feature called Chip Go! — the ability to summon a remote human teleoperator to park the car for you.

Cofounder Jameson Detweiler said the idea grew from watching golf carts "escape" private communities and get adopted widely by families, especially in warm climates, as a practical alternative to a second SUV. The Chip starts at $15,000 for a four-seat model with a soft-top roof and roughly 100 miles of range from a 15-kWh battery pack using in-wheel motors, though its top speed is capped at 25 mph. A six-seat version costs $18,000.

Deliveries of the U.S.-assembled vehicle are scheduled to begin in 2027 in Florida. The remote-driving feature will initially use human operators based in the U.S. and only activate when no one is in the car; Chip says it is liable for anything that happens while a teleoperator is in control, and is working with Florida state and local officials on the rollout. Longer term, the company hopes to license autonomous-driving technology to expand Chip Go!'s capabilities toward Level 4 autonomy, using surround-view cameras and radar but no lidar.
