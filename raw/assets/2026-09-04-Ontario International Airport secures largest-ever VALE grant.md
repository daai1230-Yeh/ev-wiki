# Ontario International Airport secures largest-ever VALE grant

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

Ontario International Airport (ONT) has secured almost $4.15 million from the US Federal Aviation Administration to invest in its clean-energy infrastructure. The grant will cover 75 percent of the cost of installing electric ground support equipment (eGSE) charging infrastructure at Terminals 2 and 4.

The award is ONT's third and largest federal Voluntary Airport Low Emissions (VALE) grant in three years, following earlier grants that supported ground power unit and pre-conditioned air projects. Together, the projects aim to reduce the need for aircraft and ground equipment to burn fuel while parked at the terminal. A competitive procurement process produced a bid below the airport's original project estimate, reducing the airport's own remaining financial commitment.

Atif Elkadi, CEO of the Ontario International Airport Authority, said: "This latest investment by the FAA is a tremendous vote of confidence in the work we are doing to build a more sustainable Ontario International Airport. Three VALE grants in three years represents meaningful progress, and this latest award will allow us and our airline partners to continue reducing emissions while investing in the infrastructure ONT will need for the future."
