# Aurora deploys second-generation driverless trucks

**Date**: 24 July 2026

---

Aurora has started deploying its second-generation driverless trucks in the US, operating across the company's ten autonomous freight routes in the Sun Belt. The new fleet is based on the International LT Series vehicle and powered by Aurora's next-generation hardware, offering greater computing performance with a design life of one million miles (1.6 million km) — suitable for large-scale commercial deployment, and at about half the build cost of Aurora's first generation.

Aurora plans to roll the new vehicles out across its commercial network to serve additional customers, targeting a fleet of 200 autonomous trucks by the end of 2026. Manufacturing partner Roush is integrating the hardware and redundant systems, targeting an annual production run-rate of 1,000 trucks by the end of 2026.
