# AIS cuts import dependence with float glass plant, bets ₹2,700 crore on auto growth

**Author**: ETAuto Staff | **Date**: Jul 16, 2026

---

Asahi India Glass (AIS) has commissioned a ₹1,400-crore automotive float glass plant in Soniyana, Rajasthan, to localize raw material production and reduce import dependence, as part of a broader ₹2,700-crore bet on India's auto growth. The greenfield facility has an initial monthly capacity of up to 170,000 metric tonnes and targets at least 70% localization at start of production, relying heavily on renewable energy and solar power.

The plant also produces input raw material for AIS's dark-green UV-cut glass, which qualifies for PLI-scheme concessions. AIS will continue importing remaining float glass volumes via its Japanese technical partner Asahi Glass Corporation (AGC), leveraging the India-Indonesia FTA to source from AGC's Indonesian plant, as part of a broader supply-chain de-risking strategy. AIS's Patan plant is also transitioning to locally sourced float glass from the new Soniyana facility.
