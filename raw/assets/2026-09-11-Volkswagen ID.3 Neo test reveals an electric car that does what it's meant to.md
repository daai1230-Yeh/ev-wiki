# Volkswagen ID.3 Neo test reveals an electric car that does what it's meant to

**Author**: Christoph Schwarzer | **Date**: 2026-09-10

---

The controls in the Volkswagen ID.3 Neo are now haptic push buttons and rotary knobs. No one misses the sliders. But what really sets this VW apart is not the new interior – although it does have a great spacious feel – it's actually the overall package that is most pleasantly convincing. The ID.3 has become a genuinely good electric car.

Did someone say boring? Normally, no one turns to look at a Volkswagen ID.3. But the ID.3 Neo is an exception. Some passers-by glance furtively, while others stare openly. The second major revision of the electric car — which will assume the role of the e-Golf until its new presentation in 2029 — clearly piques people's curiosity. What can the ID.3 Neo do, with its price list starting at 33,995 euros?

In Germany, Volkswagen is offering a flat 2,000-euro discount on this base price for the Trend trim level, calling it a purchase premium. For the ID.3 Life, VW automatically deducts 3,000 euros, and for the Style trim (the test car), it deducts 4,000 euros. Private customers with lower household incomes can receive up to an additional 6,000 euros from the subsidy fund.

From January to July, the ID.3 was the second most-registered electric car in Germany, after the Škoda Elroq. The Neo differs little from previous ID.3 models on the outside, more on the inside, and most of all in its drivetrain. The unlit slider elements for the climate control have been replaced by sleek toggle switches, and the build quality and sound insulation reinforce the brand-typical blend of comfort and solidity.

**Small turning circle, efficient drivetrain.** The ID.3 Neo is nimble and simple to drive, with a turning circle of 10.2 metres. The previous electric motor, the APP 310, has been replaced by the APP 350. In the test car, the new motor delivers 140 kW and accelerates the ID.3 Neo to 100 km/h in eight seconds. The ID.3 Neo consumed an average of 15 kWh/100 km according to the onboard computer, even though two-thirds of the distance was on motorways. With an available energy content of 58 kWh in the battery, the calculated range is 387 km, versus a WLTP figure of 485 km.

**550 km on the motorway with one charging stop.** In flowing city traffic the minimum consumption was 10.5 kWh/100 km; in rural driving, 12.4 kWh/100 km; on the motorway at the recommended speed, 20 kWh/100 km. Volkswagen specifies maximum DC power at 105 kW, time to charge from 10-80% at 25 minutes, and range added after ten minutes of DC charging at 130 km. In practice, the ID.3 Neo exceeded spec, charging at 126 kW instead of 105 kW — 44 kWh in 25 minutes, enough for over 550 km total range with a single 25-minute charging stop.

**LFP cells in the MEB+.** The charging curve has two flat sections: around 125 kW, then dropping to around 80 kW past roughly 60% charge. The revised MEB has evolved into the MEB+, which now uses LFP cells in the 50 and 58 kWh battery sizes — cost-effective, safe, and durable, though with lower energy density than the previous NMC cells still used in models like the ID.3 GTX. Volkswagen confirmed to electrive that the 50 kWh version of the ID.3 Neo actually has a 58 kWh battery limited by software, accessing only 80% of the possible energy content — effectively driving permanently in Battery Care Mode.

**Traffic light recognition available for a surcharge.** The ID.3 Neo with the IQ. Drive Pro package (2,305 euros) includes traffic light recognition, which worked with precision in Hamburg's city traffic. VW has also improved the steering feel for lane-keeping and refined the pedal feel during braking for all customers, with no surcharge.

**Verdict.** With the ID.3 Neo, Volkswagen has consistently returned to its roots: clarity, comfort, and efficiency. How well it sells depends on pricing structures — a similarly equipped ID. Polo is only slightly cheaper. This makes the ID.3 a recommendation for those who aren't looking for an automotive spectacle but want an excellent electric car for everyday use.
