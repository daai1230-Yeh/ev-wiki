# Someone Is Already Trying To Flip One Of The Last Tesla Model S's Built. The Price Is... Ambitious

**Author**: Rob Stumpf | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

---

A New Jersey dealership, J&S AutoHaus, has listed a 2026 Tesla Model S Plaid Signature Edition — one of the last Model S units built before Tesla ended production of the line — for $259,995. The car has just 300 miles on the odometer.

Tesla made only 350 Signature Edition units (250 Model S, 100 Model X), originally priced at $159,420 and sold by invite-only to buyers who signed a no-resale agreement. That agreement gives Tesla the right of first refusal and threatens sellers with "injunctive relief" of $50,000 or more, plus a place on a do-not-sell list. It's unclear whether Tesla will enforce the clause against this listing, and a new owner may lose the Signature Edition's Full Self-Driving and free Supercharging perks, which do not transfer with ownership.

By comparison, the original 2012 Tesla Model S Signature Edition now sells for well under $20,000 on the used market — a reminder that limited-edition premiums don't always hold.
