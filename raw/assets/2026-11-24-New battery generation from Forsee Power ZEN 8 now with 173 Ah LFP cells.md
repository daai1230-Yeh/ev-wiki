# New battery generation from Forsee Power: ZEN 8 now with 173 Ah LFP cells

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 24.11.2026 - 15:00

---

Forsee Power introduces the ZEN 8 LFP for light construction, agricultural, and municipal vehicles. The battery uses the same dimensions and mounting points as the ZEN 8 NMC, enabling deployment without changes to the vehicle design.

Forsee Power is a French company that develops intelligent, modular battery systems for electric commercial vehicles and stationary energy storage. The manufacturer has now unveiled the latest generation of its LFP battery, the ZEN 8, designed for compact off-highway and municipal vehicles.

The battery uses new 173 Ah LFP cells with a nominal module voltage of 45 V and an energy density exceeding 245 Wh/L. Compared to the previous NMC battery modules, the energy capacity and voltage range remain unchanged. However, the new LFP model delivers improved performance, higher cycle stability, and 0.7C fast-charging capability, equating to a theoretical charging current of 121.1 A per cell.

Thanks to the optimised thermal design, liquid cooling is no longer required. This not only reduces costs but also simplifies vehicle integration.

The ZEN 8 LFP battery is suitable for a wide range of applications, from compact municipal vehicles such as sweepers to light construction and agricultural machinery like mini excavators, loaders, or tractors. These can be equipped with 50 to 130 kWh batteries for an operating duration of approximately eight hours.

The dimensions and mounting points match those of the predecessor model, the ZEN 8 NMC. This allows manufacturers to switch to LFP batteries quickly and without redesigning their vehicles.

Forsee Power produces the new battery in France. It complies with the EU Battery Regulation (EU 2023/1542) and comes with CE marking and a battery passport. Future EU requirements are also being considered to ensure local value creation.
