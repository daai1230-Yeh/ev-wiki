# Porsche appoints new production chief, reportedly puts more EV projects on hold

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Christian Friedl will join Porsche's Executive Board as Member for Production on 1 September 2026, succeeding Albrecht Reimold. Friedl, 49, is a long-standing Porsche executive who most recently headed Corporate Quality and has held various management positions in the company's production division. Separately, reports suggest Porsche CEO Michael Leiters is deprioritising new electric vehicle projects, reportedly delaying an all-electric Panamera "well into the next decade." Reimold said the company would "perhaps have assessed certain market assumptions more cautiously," noting EV adoption has progressed more slowly than expected in many markets.
