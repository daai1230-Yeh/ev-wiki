# IVECO BUS Academy: turning innovation into operational performance for customers

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-15

*(Sponsored content by IVECO BUS)*

---

IVECO BUS Academy supported more than 6,500 participants through classroom training across Europe in 2025, with around two-thirds of its courses now dedicated to electric vehicles. Its activities are built around four areas: locally organised training adapted to customer operations, technical vehicle skills, training quality monitoring, and virtual reality for selected procedures. It serves service networks, dealers, workshop technicians, drivers and internal teams.

Programmes cover conventional, battery-electric and hydrogen technologies, including high-voltage systems, diagnostics, maintenance and safety procedures, plus electrical systems, chassis and brakes, onboard systems and ADAS (to help technicians and drivers use, diagnose and maintain assistance systems and avoid downtime). The Academy is organised in zones across Europe, with content adapted to fleet composition, workshop needs and customers' experience with electric mobility. Delivery combines in-person sessions with e-learning, virtual classrooms and VR/AR; the company says this supports uptime and total cost of ownership. Technical modules average a CSI score above 9.3 out of 10.

**Virtual reality**: VR is used in selected programmes, mostly on EV technologies, so participants can practise complex or safety-sensitive procedures (even incorrect ones, without consequences) before working on real vehicles. IVECO BUS says it complements, and does not replace, classroom and hands-on training.
