# Battery shortage: Stellantis pauses EV production in France

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-30

---

Stellantis is halting production at its French plants in Sochaux, Rennes, and Mulhouse for at least one week. In two cases, this is due to shortages in the supply of EV batteries, as supplier ACC struggles to keep up with demand for its nickel-based long-range batteries.

According to Reuters, the plants in Sochaux and Rennes are temporarily halting production due to a shortage of ACC batteries. The pause is only indirectly related to the lack of large batteries: Stellantis primarily wants to avoid producing too many other vehicles to "avoid building up vehicle inventory that does not match customer demand". Demand is particularly high for long-range electric models, which customers face very long wait times for.

Sochaux (Peugeot 3008 and 5008) pauses from 23 to 30 October. Rennes (Citroën C5 Aircross, among others) closes from 22 to 30 October. The previously announced Mulhouse halt from 15 to 30 October has other reasons unrelated to the battery shortage.

ACC is a joint venture between Stellantis, Mercedes, and TotalEnergies, which originally planned three plants in France, Germany, and Italy but now focuses on its only cell factory in Douvrin, France; the other two projects were scrapped. Bloomberg reported in February that ACC could equip only around 1,000 cars per month with long-range batteries at the start of the year, and brought in a team of experts from China to boost production and reduce defect rates.

Stellantis uses ACC cells in STLA Medium models including the Peugeot E-3008 and E-5008, Opel Grandland Electric and DS N°8, with delivery delays of up to eight months reported. Customers pay nearly €5,000 more for the 97 kWh battery instead of 73 kWh. ACC is said to have reduced defect rates, but a Stellantis spokesperson told Reuters: "Unfortunately we are not yet receiving enough of the batteries". Demand from fleet customers remains high and exceeds supply.
