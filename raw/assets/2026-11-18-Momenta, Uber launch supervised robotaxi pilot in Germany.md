# Momenta, Uber launch supervised robotaxi pilot in Germany

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-18

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- This marks the first time riders in Germany can experience a supervised autonomous ride through the Uber app.
- The pilot initially covers large parts of northern Munich and is free of charge to pre-selected riders, with a safety operator on board every trip.

Chinese autonomous driving technology firm Momenta (HKEX: 6880) and US ride-hailing giant Uber Technologies (NYSE: UBER) have launched a robotaxi pilot in Germany, advancing their commercialization partnership in the European market.

The 2 companies announced today the start of a supervised autonomous vehicle pilot in Munich, marking the first time riders in Germany can experience such an autonomous mobility service through the Uber app.

The pilot service initially covers a defined area across large parts of northern Munich, including neighborhoods surrounding the Olympic Park, and will be expanded step by step to additional areas of the city within the applicable regulatory framework.

Riders in Munich can join the interest list in the Uber app starting today, with access to the service granted in phases.

Pre-registered and pre-selected riders who request an UberX, Electric, or Comfort trip within the area may be offered the option to ride in an autonomous vehicle, with pick-ups and drop-offs at designated stops.

Operating under test permits granted to Momenta by Germany's Federal Motor Transport Authority (KBA), the pilot is non-commercial and free of charge to riders throughout, with 1 trained safety operator on board every ride.

Momenta announced in July that it had obtained the KBA test permit, becoming the first Chinese company approved to conduct Level 4 autonomous driving tests on urban roads across Germany without needing city-by-city applications.

Launching the Munich pilot within Germany's rigorous regulatory framework sets a high bar for further expansion across the region, said Samarth Kejriwal, head of autonomous mobility operations at Uber.

Real autonomy requires a system that not only sees the road but understands it, and this pilot with Uber provides a foundation for advancing toward a regular service in Germany, said Xie Shuo, robotaxi general manager at Momenta.

Founded in 2016, Momenta pursues a dual-track strategy of mass-produced driver assistance and robotaxis, with its driver assistance systems currently deployed in over 1.4 million customer vehicles globally.

The company has spent years cultivating its presence in Germany, partnering with major global automakers including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, and Volkswagen.

Momenta listed on the main board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on July 8, raising a total of about HK$6.8 billion, and plans to use around 20% of the proceeds to advance robotaxi commercialization.

During the Hong Kong initial public offering (IPO) placement in July, Uber made an additional investment through a wholly-owned subsidiary, bringing its total shareholding in Momenta to about 1.19 million shares.
