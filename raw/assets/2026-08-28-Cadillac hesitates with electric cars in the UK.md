# Cadillac hesitates with electric cars in the UK

**Author**: Carrie Hampel | **Date**: 27.08.2026

---

General Motors is reconsidering its plan to relaunch Cadillac in the UK, citing uncertainty in a "rapidly evolving" marketplace. Competition from European and Chinese brands poses potential headwinds for the US company.

Speaking to British publication Autocar last year when he was CEO of GM Europe, Pere Brugal had assured UK drivers the brand would return "soon", starting with the Lyriq — which competes against the BMW iX5 and Mercedes-Benz EQE SUV. He called the UK "one of the [markets] that we're focusing on" and said a launch was "coming close".

A year later, the US company has yet to announce a firm launch date and has set up only one dealer for right-hand-drive Cadillacs.

According to Autocar, only 20 Cadillacs were registered in the UK between July 2025 and March 2026, of which 16 were the new EV models. In London, GM-authorised dealer Clive Sutton continues to import left-hand-drive Cadillac Escalades.

This week, a GM spokesperson told Autocar that as the UK market and EV landscape continue to evolve rapidly with new competitors emerging, the company is actively reviewing its future Cadillac plans to ensure they remain aligned with market conditions and customer demand.

General Motors had been moving forward with electrification in recent years, making enormous investments in technology and production. When the Trump administration cut support for emission-free vehicles last year, the legacy US carmaker was forced to cut 5,500 jobs. At the beginning of this year GM recorded another write-down for its EV business — six billion US dollars — directly affecting the company's liquidity.

At the same time, Chinese carmakers are launching high-tech, lower-price electric cars in the UK, and European carmakers have upgraded their electric offerings.

The decision comes as Cadillac changes tack with its wider global strategy. While the carmaker had originally planned to go electric-only by 2030, during the recent first-half earnings call GM CEO Mary Barra said that starting next spring and continuing into 2028, the company will begin launching the next generation of Cadillac ICE vehicles.
