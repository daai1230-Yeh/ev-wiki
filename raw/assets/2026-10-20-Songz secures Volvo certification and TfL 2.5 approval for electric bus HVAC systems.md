# Songz secures Volvo certification and TfL 2.5 approval for electric bus HVAC systems

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-09-22

*(Sponsored content by SONGZ)*

---

Thermal management supplier Songz obtained Volvo HVAC Compatibility Certification for its LMD-VI electric bus HVAC family (certified 28 May 2026), and its systems for electric double-decker buses received TfL 2.5 approval for London. Songz says 854 of its HVAC systems had been installed on zero-emission double-deckers in London by 2026. Its JLE series is certified to both TfL 2.2 and TfL 2.5 and includes waste-heat recovery, with claimed energy savings of up to 17.8% and a compact integrated design.

Songz reports having supplied 307,195 electric bus HVAC systems worldwide, with 19 plants, more than 3,000 employees (387 engineers) and 1,243 patents. It exhibited at FIAA 2026, IFEMA Madrid, 22–24 September 2026, Hall 9, Stand 9C21.
