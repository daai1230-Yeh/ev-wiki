# Bajaj Auto plans electric motorcycle launch by FY28, to rev up capacity across EVs, CVs

**Author**: Karan Dhar | **Date**: 21.07.2026

---

*Note: ETAuto's newsletter linked to this story via a tracking redirect that could not be resolved directly to the ETAuto/Economic Times article. The same reporting was found on Business Today, which is cited below as the source.*

Bajaj Auto Ltd, India's second-largest electric scooter maker by volume, is looking to expand manufacturing capacity of the Chetak e-scooter from 50,000 units to 60,000 units over the next couple of months.

"We are looking to expand capacity of Chetak from about 50,000 units to about 60,000 in the immediate term. This will kick in in the next couple of months itself," said Dinesh Thapar, chief financial officer of Bajaj Auto, in a media conference call after the company announced its first-quarter earnings.

Demand for Chetak continues to outpace capacity, the company said, adding that investments are underway to augment production to improve availability, support international expansion and unlock the next phase of competitive growth. Industry monthly volumes for electric scooters have climbed from an average of around 120,000 units in FY26 to nearly 175,000 units during the first quarter, touching close to 200,000 units per month by the end of June. Bajaj Auto's Chetak also recorded its highest-ever quarterly volumes, approaching 130,000 units.

Bajaj Auto's electric scooter business turned EBITDA positive for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. "Our electric business, both two-wheelers and three-wheelers, is double-digit EBITDA margin... our electric scooters are now EBITDA positive, and our electric three-wheelers are at double-digit EBITDA margin," said Thapar.

Overall, Bajaj Auto plans to increase manufacturing capacity across its businesses by roughly 20% through a two-phase expansion programme, spanning motorcycle plants, commercial vehicle facilities at Waluj and Chetak production at Akurdi. Current installed capacity stands at roughly 600,000 units per month across businesses.

On the recently launched Delhi EV policy that bans registration of ICE two-wheelers and three-wheelers from April 2028 and January 2027 respectively, Thapar said it leaves the customer with very little choice: "We should leave some of these choices to the customer, and let the customer decide." Bajaj Auto sells about 2,000 ICE vehicles in Delhi each month.

On the company's upcoming electric motorcycles, Thapar said plans are under development with some launches possible in FY28, and that the company's priority will likely be export markets first, then the domestic market.
