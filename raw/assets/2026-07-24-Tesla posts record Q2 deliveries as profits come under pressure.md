# Q2 financial results: Tesla posts record deliveries as profits come under pressure

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Tesla reported record second-quarter deliveries and stronger revenue. Lower prices, higher costs and rising investment spending weighed on profits and drove free cash flow into negative territory.

Tesla delivered 480,126 vehicles worldwide during Q2, up 25% year-on-year and a new Q2 record, beating Wall Street estimates by nearly 20%. Quarterly revenue reached $28.24 billion, up 26% year-on-year — the first time trailing-twelve-month revenue has exceeded $100 billion. Model 3 and Model Y accounted for 467,762 of the deliveries. Production rose 10% to 451,758 vehicles, with Europe posting the biggest gain at an estimated 40% jump. It was Tesla's strongest Q2 ever and its first year-over-year delivery growth in two years.
