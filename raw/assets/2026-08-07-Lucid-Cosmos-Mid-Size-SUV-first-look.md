# Lucid Cosmos Mid-Size SUV: Here's A First Look At The Tesla Model Y Rival

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

Lucid's long-term stability hinges on successfully launching its most affordable model yet, the smaller Cosmos SUV. The company published the first official images of the ~$50,000 Tesla Model Y rival — a couple of shots slipped into its latest earnings report — even as it officially delayed the mid-size EV's launch to next year.

As known from earlier patent images, the Cosmos has a sloping roofline similar to coupe SUVs like the BMW X4. The new photos show horizontal LED taillights and a wide third brake light set low at the bottom of a near-horizontal rear windshield (unusual placement versus above the window, which could affect visibility). Up front, a very compact, ultra-slim headlight integrates all beams into one enclosure. Inside, a wide dashboard screen enclosure confirms the previously disclosed single ultra-wide curved display.

**Timeline**: Originally slated for late-2026 production at Lucid's new AMP-2 factory in Saudi Arabia, the Cosmos now starts production in early 2027, ramping up in the second half of the year. The plant isn't finished yet, and Lucid says it doesn't want to rush the launch given past software issues with the Air and Gravity.

Lucid CEO Silvio Napoli: "Our objective is clear: Mid-size will launch only when every process and quality requirement has been met. We will not repeat the mistakes of the past by bringing a product to market before it is ready."

Lucid has framed the mid-size portfolio (Cosmos plus two more models) as one of four "must-win projects," alongside robotaxis, the Saudi Arabia factory, and an "operating reset" that includes layoffs and a targeted $1.4 billion cash-flow improvement.
