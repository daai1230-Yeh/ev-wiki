# Thailand eyed $700 million EV incentive scheme

**Date**: 24 July 2026

---

Thailand is considering a subsidy programme worth roughly $714 million (24 billion baht) to replace up to 80,000 older vehicles with EVs, with a broader promotion of EV purchases also under examination. Potential measures under discussion include subsidies, low-interest loans and tax incentives. The proposed plan would initially focus on commercial transport vehicles — taxis, motorcycle taxis, tuk-tuks, buses and trucks — with the government considering whether to expand assistance to other vehicle categories.

Through the programme, Thailand aims to accelerate fleet electrification while supporting its domestic automotive industry. Discussions on the final structure are expected to continue for another month, with implementation targeted for this year.
