# VW secures trademark rights for 'ID. UP'

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-11-18

---

A new entry from VW has appeared at the EUIPO, the EU Intellectual Property Office. The company has applied to register the name ID. UP. This suggests that the upcoming electric model will also bear this name. A report from Portugal already mentions the production of the first pre-series vehicles.

It's already well-known that VW plans to launch an electric compact car in 2027. First unveiled in spring 2025, the VW ID. EVERY1 concept provided an initial glimpse of Volkswagen's intentions. The production version was long referred to as the ID.1, but since the debut of the ID. Polo and the ID. Tiguan, VW is returning to traditional model names. The application to secure the trademark for 'ID. UP' with the EUIPO, filed on 22 October 2026, aligns with this shift.

Can we therefore assume the car will be called the ID. UP? Most likely, yes – but without a guarantee. The 'UP' name fits a new electric model better than 'Lupo' – also reportedly under consideration – thanks to the e-Up, discontinued in 2023, which already carries electric associations.

According to Portuguese magazine Razão Automóvel, the first pre-series vehicles have already rolled off the line at Volkswagen's Autoeuropa plant in Palmela. The run tests new tools, assembly processes, work steps and cycle times, and reportedly proceeded without issues. Regular series production is set to begin in the final quarter of 2027.

VW chose Palmela due to the plant's high cost efficiency, crucial for the planned starting price of under €20,000. The ID. UP will share the assembly line with the T-Roc, allowing flexible volumes and full use of capacity of up to 1,000 vehicles per day.

## ID. EVERY1 concept and ID. Polo serve as technical benchmarks

The ID. UP will be the first VW Group series model to use the electronics and software architecture co-developed with Rivian, which requires significantly fewer control units and enables OTA updates. Visually, it is expected to closely resemble the ID. EVERY1 concept.

At 3.88 metres in length, it will sit between the previous Up (3.60 m) and the ID. Polo (4.05 m). On the MEB+ platform with front-wheel drive, the four-seater offers 305 litres of boot space. The concept uses a 70 kW front motor, top speed 130 km/h, and range of at least 250 km WLTP.

VW has not released battery or charging figures. The most likely candidate is the 37-kWh LFP battery from the base ID. Polo or a slightly smaller variant. The ID. Polo achieves up to 329 km WLTP on 37 kWh and charges at up to 90 kW DC, 10–80% in around 27 minutes.
