# Devon and Torbay commission nearly 3,000 public EV charging points

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 2026-08-03

---

Devon County Council (DCC) and Torbay Council have appointed Believ, char.gy and Wenea to deploy almost 3,000 public EV charging points. Backed by £8 million in LEVI funding and around £30 million in private investment, the rollout will begin within the next few months.

The charge points will be installed over the coming years in communities, public car parks, on streets and on existing lamp columns. Wenea will work with Devon County Council, Torbay Council and district councils to deploy rapid and fast charging points in public car parks managed by East Devon, Exeter, Mid Devon, South Hams, Teignbridge, Torbay and West Devon authorities. Believ will install standard on-street charging units and chargers in parish council and community car parks, while char.gy will integrate charging infrastructure into existing lamp columns.

The project combines around £30 million of private investment from the three charge point operators with £8 million from the UK Department for Transport's Local Electric Vehicle Infrastructure (LEVI) fund.

Councillor Jaqui Hodgson, Devon County Council's Cabinet Member for Environment and Biodiversity, said expanding the charging network is essential since road transport accounts for more than a quarter of the county's carbon emissions: "Making it as easy as possible for people to charge their vehicles when and where they need to — outside their home, on streets, in public car parks and even on lamp posts — is crucial to encourage the take-up of EVs."

Councillor Adam Billings, Torbay Council's Cabinet Member for Pride in Place and Parking, said: "This investment will help remove one of the biggest barriers to EV adoption, especially for residents without off-street parking." He noted the infrastructure would complement other local initiatives including cross-pavement cable channel trials and the transition to an electric Stagecoach bus fleet in Torbay.
