# Ford and Geely to pool production in Valencia

**Date**: 24 July 2026

---

Ford and Geely plan to manufacture new models from both brands at Ford's Valencia plant in Spain from 2028, through a joint venture. Ford will hold a 66% stake in the venture, with Geely owning the remaining 34%. Subject to regulatory approvals, the new company is expected to start operations in the first half of 2027, with production of the first models beginning in 2028.

The joint venture is expected to produce three Ford-branded multi-energy vehicles and two electric Geely-branded models. The partners aim to optimise use of the Valencia plant's existing capacity and lower production costs through the collaboration, and plan to work together on development, engineering and manufacturing.
