# Szczecin orders 20 additional battery-electric articulated buses from Solaris

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-09-08 12:20

---

The Polish city of Szczecin is further expanding its electric bus fleet. 20 Urbino 18 electric buses have been ordered from Solaris, with deliveries scheduled for 2027 and 2028. The articulated buses can be charged via plug and pantograph.

The electric vehicles will be operated by the transport company Szczecińskie Przedsiębiorstwo Autobusowe Klonowica (SPAK) and stationed at its depot on Klonowica Street. The ordered vehicles are the 18-metre-long Urbino 18 electric. Solaris plans to equip the buses with its High Energy batteries, though the company's announcement does not specify their capacity or the power output of the electric drivetrain. The manufacturer also does not disclose the order value.

The batteries can be charged both via plug-in and pantograph. The latter enables opportunity charging at equipped terminal stops. According to Solaris, this combination provides sufficient range and flexibility to serve long urban and suburban routes in Szczecin, but the manufacturer does not specify a concrete range.

The order follows previous electric bus contracts with the city. In 2024, Szczecin had already ordered 14 electric buses from Solaris, including four Urbino 12 electric and ten articulated buses of the Urbino 18 electric type. These vehicles were also designed for charging via plug-in and pantograph. After their delivery, the Solaris electric bus fleet in the city was set to grow to 30 vehicles.

## Szczecin continues expansion of its electric bus fleet

Solaris has announced that 34 electric buses have been delivered to Szczecin. With the newly ordered 20 vehicles, this number will eventually rise to 54. The manufacturer has delivered nearly 200 buses to the city to date, including 17 hybrid buses alongside the electric vehicles.

The new Urbino 18 electric buses are designed to accommodate up to 120 passengers. The buses feature 35 seats, 11 of which are directly accessible from the low-floor area. Standard equipment includes air conditioning, a passenger information system, and various driver assistance systems, such as turning and blind-spot assistants, intelligent speed assistance, and systems for detecting fatigue and waning attention.

Photovoltaic modules are to be installed on the roofs to power auxiliary systems, particularly during layovers at terminal stops. Solaris does not specify the power output of the vehicle rooftop solar system or the energy savings it is expected to achieve. For remote diagnostics and vehicle monitoring, Szczecin has also ordered Solaris' fleet management system, eSConnect.

Szczecin's fleet transition is not exclusively battery-electric. The city also recently ordered twelve Urbino 18 mild-hybrid buses. Solaris is currently manufacturing 44 hybrid buses for the second Szczecin operator, SPAD, which are also scheduled for delivery in 2027 and 2028. While the current order significantly expands the electric bus fleet, Szczecin continues to invest in hybridised vehicles in parallel.
