# ChargePoint and Cariqa partner on QR code payments

**Date**: 26.08.2026

---

Berlin-based startup Cariqa, which offers a direct payment solution for charging electric vehicles, is partnering with US charging infrastructure provider ChargePoint to enable charging station operators to process QR code-based ad hoc payments via the Cariqa platform.

The process consists of three steps: first, the electric vehicle driver scans the QR code at the charging station with their smartphone – no app download or account creation required. A mobile website then opens on the smartphone, featuring the charging provider's custom design and displaying prices and payment options such as Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit card, or PayPal. Users then pay using the method of their choice, and the charging station is activated for the charging process.

Cariqa Go is integrated into ChargePoint's be.Energised platform. In addition to ad-hoc payments via QR code, the partnership also includes a new direct sales channel for ChargePoint customers, providing access to Cariqa's growing network of "Demand Partners," such as A Better Routeplanner, Chargeprice, Here Technologies, TariffEV, and other charging apps.

The partnership will initially be launched in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Italy, the United Kingdom, and Ireland. The Cariqa integration will be demonstrated live at the ChargePoint stand at the ICNC in Berlin from September 1–3, 2026.
