# Hero MotoCorp to raise stake in Ather Energy with ₹1,000 crore additional investment

**Author**: ETAuto Staff | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

---

Hero MotoCorp has approved an additional investment of up to ₹1,000 crore (~$116 million) in Ather Energy through a preferential allotment of equity shares or other convertible instruments, subject to regulatory and shareholder approvals. The deal is expected to close within about 15 days of final approval.

India's largest two-wheeler manufacturer currently owns a 29.48% stake in the electric two-wheeler maker as of June 30, 2026; upon conversion of the new securities, its stake would rise to 30.68%. The investment is part of Ather's broader ₹2,500-crore fundraising plan and follows the company's board approving a ₹1,200-crore fund raise. News of the investment sent Ather Energy shares up over 9% to an all-time high.
