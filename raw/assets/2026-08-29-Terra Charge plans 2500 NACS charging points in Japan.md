# Terra Charge plans 2,500 NACS charging points in Japan

**Author**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-08-27

---

Terra Charge plans to widely introduce the NACS charging standard in Japan starting in 2028. By 2033, a total of 2,500 charging points are expected nationwide. In addition to fast chargers, the operator will also focus on NACS solutions for AC charging for the first time.

Japanese charging infrastructure provider Terra Charge has announced plans to integrate the North American Charging Standard (NACS) into its network, starting in fiscal year 2028. By the end of fiscal year 2033, the company aims to deploy a total of 2,500 NACS charging points.

The new charging points will be installed gradually from 2028 at commercial sites, along key transport routes and at hotels. This expansion could significantly accelerate the adoption of the predominantly North American standard, which currently makes up a small minority of chargers in Japan. As of 19 August this year, Terra Charge reported 1,323 publicly registered NACS charging points nationwide. In total, Japan currently has around 68,000 charging points, most of which are the CHAdeMO standard, according to the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry.

The Japanese charging infrastructure company says its NACS chargers will not be limited to fast charging. Terra Charge is also exploring the development of a corresponding AC charger for locations with longer dwell times, such as hotels and multi-family residential buildings. A prototype is expected to be introduced as soon as possible, with a market launch to follow, but the company has not yet provided technical details or a concrete timeline.

As reported by Nikkei Asia, Terra Charge aims to address the specific demand from Tesla drivers in Japan by building NACS infrastructure. However, the operator's long-term vision extends beyond Tesla: Terra Charge anticipates that additional manufacturers will adopt NACS and is preparing its infrastructure for the wider adoption of Plug & Charge technology.

This development is particularly notable in the context of North America, where the connector originally developed by Tesla has become a standard adopted by numerous car manufacturers. For example, General Motors will fully transition its electric vehicles under the Chevrolet, Cadillac, and GMC brands to native NACS ports starting with the 2027 model year. Older vehicles can already use NACS charging points in some cases via adapters.

Terra Charge is positioning itself early for potential wider adoption of NACS but is likely to increase the variety of connectors rather than replace the established CHAdeMO standard. Terra Charge already has ambitious expansion plans with existing charger types, meaning NACS will remain a small minority of chargers. By the end of fiscal year 2030, the Terra Charge network is expected to grow to a total of 105,000 charging points across Japan. In total, across all charging infrastructure providers and types, the Japanese government wants to expand electric vehicle charging points to 300,000 by the end of 2030.
