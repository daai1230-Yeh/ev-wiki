2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Around the world, governments are making very different choices about Chinese electric vehicles. Some are opening the door, and some are building a wall. This week's news shows three different answers.

全世界的政府，對中國電動車做出了截然不同的選擇：有些開門歡迎，有些築起高牆。本週的新聞呈現了三種不同的答案。

Canada is opening the door, at least partly. After Prime Minister Mark Carney visited China in January, the two countries agreed on a new trade arrangement. Canada now allows up to 49,000 Chinese-made electric vehicles per year to enter at a tariff of 6.1 percent, instead of the previous 100 percent.

加拿大至少是半開了門。總理卡尼1月訪問中國後，兩國達成新的貿易安排。加拿大現在每年允許最多49,000輛中國製電動車以6.1%的關稅進口，先前則是100%。

The policy is already changing the market. According to Canadian government data, nearly 16,000 electric vehicles have been shipped from China to Canada since May, and about half of them cost less than 35,000 Canadian dollars. Interestingly, Tesla is an early winner. Sales of its China-built Model 3 rose in the third quarter, and some Tesla models are priced about 25 percent lower in Canada than in the United States.

這項政策已在改變市場。依加拿大政府資料，自5月以來已有近16,000輛電動車從中國運往加拿大，其中約一半售價低於35,000加幣。有趣的是，特斯拉是早期贏家：中國製Model 3第三季銷量增加，部分特斯拉車款在加拿大的售價比美國低約25%。

Argentina has gone even further. Under President Javier Milei, the car market has moved from heavy protectionism to open competition. The country just held its first Chinese car show in Buenos Aires, and Chinese brands now hold about 10 percent of passenger and light commercial vehicle sales, up from roughly 2 percent at the end of last year.

阿根廷走得更遠。在總統米雷伊領導下，汽車市場已從高度保護主義轉向開放競爭。該國剛在布宜諾斯艾利斯舉辦首屆中國車展，中國品牌目前約占乘用車與輕型商用車銷量的10%，去年底只有約2%。

A policy that allows up to 50,000 electric and hybrid vehicles to be imported duty-free helped drive this surge. Brazil and Mexico have introduced tariffs to slow down BYD, but Milei says he is not worried: what Argentines buy, he argues, is their own business. At the same time, Toyota plans to invest 1.34 billion dollars in an electric vehicle plant there.

一項允許至多50,000輛電動與油電混合車免稅進口的政策，推動了這波成長。巴西和墨西哥已祭出關稅來減緩比亞迪的湧入，但米雷伊表示並不擔心：他認為，阿根廷人買什麼是他們自己的事。同時，豐田計畫在當地投資13.4億美元興建電動車工廠。

In the United States, the mood is the opposite. A bill in the Senate would ban automakers with more than 15 percent Chinese ownership from selling cars in the country. Senator Bernie Moreno says the goal is national security, not a ban on Mercedes. He says negotiations are under way to block hostile control without harming legitimate international business.

在美國，氣氛則完全相反。參議院一項法案將禁止中資持股超過15%的車廠在美國銷售汽車。參議員莫雷諾表示，目標是國家安全，而不是禁售賓士；目前正在協商，希望在防範敵對勢力控制的同時，不傷及正當的國際商業。

European brands like Mercedes and Volvo might have to change their ownership structure to keep selling there. Meanwhile, BMW and Audi are shifting their focus to the American market, because the price war in China has hurt foreign profits. So the same Chinese cars are welcomed in Buenos Aires, tolerated in Toronto, and possibly shut out of Washington.

賓士與Volvo等歐洲品牌，可能必須調整股權結構才能繼續在美國銷售。同時，BMW與奧迪正把重心轉向美國市場，因為中國的價格戰重創了外資的獲利。於是，同樣的中國車，在布宜諾斯艾利斯受歡迎、在多倫多被容忍、在華盛頓卻可能被拒之門外。

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

1.Canada cut its tariff from 100 percent to 6.1 percent but kept a quota of 49,000 cars. Is a limited opening a smart compromise, or the worst of both worlds?

加拿大把關稅從100%降到6.1%，但保留49,000輛的配額。有限度的開放是聰明的折衷，還是兩頭落空？

2.Argue that Argentina's open-door policy is good for its consumers. Then argue the opposite: that it puts local jobs and industry at risk.

請先主張阿根廷的開放政策對消費者有利，再主張相反的立場：它讓本地就業與產業處於風險之中。

3.Milei says what Argentines buy is their own business. Do you agree? Should governments ever limit what cars people can buy?

米雷伊說阿根廷人買什麼是他們自己的事。你同意嗎？政府是否該限制人們能買哪些車？

4.The Senate bill would cap Chinese ownership of carmakers at 15 percent. Is that a fair national security measure, or a disguised form of protectionism? Give both sides.

參院法案要把車廠的中資持股上限設在15%。這是合理的國安措施，還是變相的保護主義？請說明兩邊的論點。

5.Tesla is benefiting from Canada's new rules even though it is an American company. What does this tell us about how global the car industry really is?

特斯拉雖是美國公司，卻從加拿大的新規定中受益。這說明汽車產業有多麼全球化？

6.If your own country had to choose between the Canadian, Argentine, and American approaches, which would you pick, and why?

如果你的國家必須在加拿大、阿根廷、美國三種做法中選一個，你會選哪一個？為什麼？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 2 min

60-second summary: In short, governments are split on Chinese electric vehicles. Canada now lets in 49,000 Chinese-made cars a year at a 6.1 percent tariff, and Tesla is an early winner. Argentina has opened its market even wider, and Chinese brands have jumped to about 10 percent share. The United States Senate, however, is considering a 15 percent cap on Chinese ownership of carmakers, for national security reasons.

60秒摘要：簡而言之，各國政府對中國電動車的態度分歧。加拿大現在每年以6.1%關稅放行49,000輛中國製汽車，特斯拉是早期贏家。阿根廷開放得更徹底，中國品牌市占率已跳升至約10%。然而美國參議院正考慮以國家安全為由，把車廠的中資持股上限設在15%。
