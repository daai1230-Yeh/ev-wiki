# Autonomous driving specialist Imagry and UNVI partner on electric buses, first deployment in Germany

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-08-08

---

Autonomous driving technology provider Imagry has partnered with Spanish bus manufacturer UNVI to integrate its AI-based, HD-mapless driving system into electric buses, with the first 10.3-metre vehicle set for deployment in Germany in Q3 2026.

Imagry has announced a strategic partnership with UNVI for the development of integrated autonomous electric buses. The cooperation combines its AI-based autonomous driving technology with UNVI's M3-category electric buses. UBSI is involved as the vehicle integration, procurement, leasing and lifecycle support partner.

The first vehicle resulting from the partnership is a 10.3-metre self-driving electric bus. Imagry states that integration was completed in less than three months and that the vehicle is pre-certified for WVTA (GSR2). The first deployment is scheduled in Germany during the third quarter of 2026.

## Imagry technology on UNVI buses

Imagry provides AI-based autonomous driving technology covering perception, motion planning and vehicle control. According to the company, its system operates without HD maps and enables location-independent driverless operation.

The technology is being integrated into UNVI's electric bus platform for applications including regular public transport services in cities and towns and operations within areas such as airports, medical centres and university campuses.

UNVI said that the initial project involves a complete electric bus solution, while the cooperation can also cover the integration of Imagry technology into different UNVI body configurations.

UBSI supplied the vehicle and acts as the strategic partner for vehicle integration, procurement, leasing and lifecycle support, covering regulatory compliance, technical readiness, fleet deployment and operational support.

## First bus in Germany

The first integrated Imagry and UNVI vehicle measures 10.3 metres and features a fully electric powertrain. Integration of the autonomous driving technology was completed in under three months. Imagry identifies public transportation operators and public transit authorities among the target users of the system.

Carlos Rivo, Managing Director of UNVI, said: "Although this first project involves a complete electric bus solution, the broader opportunity lies in adapting Imagry's technology to different UNVI body configurations."

Eran Ofir, CEO of Imagry, said: "We are excited to bring Imagry autonomous driving capabilities to additional markets via UNVI's electric bus models."
