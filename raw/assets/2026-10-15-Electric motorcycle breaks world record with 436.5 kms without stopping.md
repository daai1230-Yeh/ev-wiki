# Electric motorcycle breaks world record with 436.5 kms without stopping

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-14

---

A Zero DS covered 436.5 km on a single charge. According to the company, Zero Motorcycles is the new Guinness World Record holder for the longest ride without a charging stop on an electric motorcycle.

Zero Motorcycles, founded in the USA in 2006 and now relocated to the Netherlands, beat the previous record (a Verge TS Pro, just under 311 km last year) by 125.8 km, an improvement of more than 40%.

Three riders on three standard Zero models rode from the French-Italian Alps south through France to the Mediterranean coast. Louis Renaud's Zero SR/S managed 350 km with 4% battery left. Jenny Brooks' Zero DSR/X reached 384 km, riding part of the route with the videographer as a passenger in torrential rain and covering 15 km more after the display showed 0%. Jelle Verstaen's Zero DS with Power Tank kept going through a severe storm in the Camargue, with gale-force crosswinds in the final 25 minutes, until the battery was depleted at 436.5 km.

The Zero DS is an electric dual-sport bike. Its standard 15.6 kWh battery (249 km urban range) was supplemented with the 3.6 kWh Power Tank for 19.2 kWh total. Efficiency came from steady moderate speeds on quiet roads, minimal night traffic, regeneration on Alpine descents and attention to tyre pressure. The bike uses a Z-Force high-density battery, a passively cooled IPM motor and a low-loss drivetrain; no special modifications were required.
