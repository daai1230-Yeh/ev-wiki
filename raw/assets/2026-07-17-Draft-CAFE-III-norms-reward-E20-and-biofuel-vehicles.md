# New draft CAFE III norms reward E20 and biofuel vehicles with emission benefits

**Source**: ETAuto Daily Morning Newsletter (Fri, Jul 17, 2026) | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

The Ministry of Power released the draft Corporate Average Fuel Economy (CAFE) 2027 regulations, proposing a sharper tightening of fuel-efficiency norms for passenger vehicles from 1 April 2027, in force for five years. Fuel consumption targets are proposed to be progressively tightened from 3.996 litres/100 km (94.76 gCO₂/km) in 2027-28 to 3.3273 litres/100 km (78.90 gCO₂/km) in 2031-32.

The introduction of enhanced "volume derogation factors" — commonly called super credits — for cleaner vehicle technologies is one of the biggest proposed changes. Battery electric vehicles (BEVs) and range-extended electric vehicles (REEVs) will receive a multiplier of 3.0 (each counting as three vehicles for compliance); plug-in hybrids and flex-fuel strong hybrids receive 2.5, strong hybrids 1.6, and flex-fuel ethanol vehicles 1.1.

For the first time, Carbon Neutrality Factors (CNFs) are proposed to recognise the carbon-neutrality of ethanol, biofuel and Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) by permitting specified reductions in declared tailpipe CO₂ emissions before compliance assessment. An 8% CNF is proposed for present ethanol levels, while CBG and biofuel reductions would be based on prevailing actual blending levels. The draft also proposes an 8% reduction in declared tailpipe CO₂ for E20-compatible vehicles, including strong hybrids and PHEVs.

Manufacturers beating their targets earn compliance credits that may be carried forward; those falling short may meet obligations through carry-forward provisions, voluntary pooling with other manufacturers, or by purchasing compliance credits from the BEE. The last date for feedback is 6 August 2026.
