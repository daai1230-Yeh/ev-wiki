# Einride plans North America's largest charging ecosystem for electric HGVs

**Author**: Stefan Koeller | **Date**: 22.07.2026

---

Einride plans to acquire charging software specialist Flipturn in an all-stock deal. The move would more than double its managed charging capacity and create what the company says will be North America's largest heavy-duty EV charging network.

This acquisition will lay the foundation for North America's largest charging ecosystem for heavy-duty electric trucks (HGVs), connecting available charging capacity with fleets transitioning to battery-electric powertrains. Through this purchase, Flipturn's customer base — including over 250 megawatts of charging capacity — will be integrated into Einride's portfolio.

According to Einride, the acquisition will create the first fully integrated technology platform for electric freight transport, combining charging point management, energy systems, and a matching service that connects fleets with third-party charging infrastructure.

Flipturn will strengthen Einride's software layer for charging infrastructure: using AI, it predicts charging times, optimises power delivery, and adapts the system over time to the vehicle and battery behaviour of each fleet. Additionally, Flipturn ensures charger uptime, manages driver access and payments, and coordinates on-site controls.

The Flipturn acquisition is expected to be completed in July 2026, subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals. The purchase price is anticipated to be $38.4 million.

"This acquisition is a decisive step in our U.S. scaling strategy. Flipturn will further strengthen Einride's software layer for electric heavy-duty freight optimisation and improve our customer offering by adding more cost-efficient, more reliable, and accessible charging to our customers," said Roozbeh Charli, Chief Executive Officer, Einride.

"Einride has spent a decade at the forefront of autonomous and electric freight, and that technical depth is what drew us to them. Our customers will keep the same team and platform they rely on today, with Einride's technology and scale behind us to deliver an even better charging experience," added Katie Siegel, co-founder and CEO, Flipturn.

Saga AI, Einride's AI-powered platform for optimising electric freight transport, has so far supported over 19 million electric kilometres worldwide.
