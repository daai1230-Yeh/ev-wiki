# Commercial vehicle manufacturer Iveco now officially part of Tata Motors

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-03

---

Tata Motors has completed the first settlement stage for the acquisition of Iveco. Through its Dutch subsidiary TML CV Holdings, it now holds approximately 89.42% of share capital, so the Italian commercial vehicle maker officially belongs to Tata Motors.

Tata announced the plan in July 2025. A majority of shareholders accepted the voluntary offer (acceptance rate 87.8%, above the 80% threshold set on 16 October). A second acceptance period runs 2–6 November at €14.40 per share, with settlement scheduled for 13 November. The offer was raised from €14.10 to €14.40, lifting transaction value from about €3.8 billion to about €3.91 billion.

Iveco Group's board will have six members, with Natarajan Chandrasekaran as Chairman and Olof Persson as Executive Director; Damiano Cretarola became CFO on 2 November. Iveco's defence business was sold to Leonardo earlier this year for about €1.6 billion.

The combined group expects annual sales above 590,000 vehicles and revenue of about €21 billion (Europe 46%, India 32%, South America 8%, rest of world 14%). The partnership aims to advance emission-free transport, sharing supplier networks and industrial capacity, with FPT Industrial playing a key role. Tata committed to preserving Iveco's factories and jobs and keeping headquarters in Turin for two years; Iveco employs about 33,325 people.
