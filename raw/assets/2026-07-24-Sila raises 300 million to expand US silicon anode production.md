# Sila raises $300 million to expand US silicon anode production

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-22

---

US battery materials company Sila has secured $300 million in fresh private funding to accelerate the next expansion phase of its silicon-carbon (Si/C) anode plant in Moses Lake, Washington. The facility, which completed construction and began operations in autumn 2025, currently has capacity equivalent to 2 GWh of battery materials per year, with plans to scale up to 250 GWh over the next five years — which would make Moses Lake the world's largest anode production facility if completed as planned. The round was led by Atreides Management and Sutter Hill Ventures, with participation from 8VC, Bessemer Venture Partners, Matrix Partners and funds advised by T. Rowe Price Associates.
