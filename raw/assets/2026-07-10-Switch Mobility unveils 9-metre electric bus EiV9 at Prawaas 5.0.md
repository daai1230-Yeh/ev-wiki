# Switch Mobility unveils 9-metre electric bus EiV9 at Prawaas 5.0

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-07-10

---

*Note: this story also ran in today's ETAuto newsletter ("Switch Mobility launches EiV9 electric bus at Prawaas 5.0"); ETAuto's own site (auto.economictimes.indiatimes.com) is not directly accessible, so this clip is sourced from Sustainable Bus, which covered the same launch in full.*

Switch Mobility has introduced its new 9-metre electric bus EiV9 at the Prawaas 5.0 transport exhibition in India, alongside an updated version of its EiV12 Intercity electric bus (a model that was recently delivered in Chennai). Switch Mobility ranked first with 830 registrations in the sum-up of the Indian e-bus market in the first half of 2026, corresponding to a 28.2% market share. The company states it has deployed over 2,400 electric buses globally, covering more than 150 million kilometres of operations, alongside over 2,500 electric light commercial vehicles currently in service.

At the same Prawaas 5.0 expo, EKA Mobility launched a new 9-metre electric school bus and Tata introduced its new Ultra Prime and Starbus Prime bus range.

**SWITCH EiV9 platform and technical configuration**

The EiV9 is built on a dedicated 9-metre electric bus platform designed for urban operations, integrating a lightweight structure and electric driveline architecture aimed at optimizing energy consumption and operational performance. The bus is equipped with a Permanent Magnet Variable Reluctance (PMVR) motor delivering up to 213 kW of peak power. Battery configurations are modular, allowing multiple pack options depending on route requirements and duty cycles. Charging is enabled through dual-gun CCS2 fast charging, with regenerative braking supporting energy recovery.

**Interior layout, passenger features and safety systems**

The vehicle is fitted with front and rear air suspension systems. Passenger amenities include individual USB charging points and seating configurations adaptable for different use cases, including pushback seating for staff transport. Safety systems include a Fire Detection and Suppression System (FDSS) and disc brakes on both axles.

**EiV12 Intercity showcased alongside EiV9**

Switch Mobility also displayed an updated version of its EiV12 Intercity electric bus, configured for long-distance operations with reclining seats and multi-port charging points. It integrates floor-mounted LFP batteries for a lower centre of gravity, front and rear air suspension, dual-gun DC fast charging, a pneumatic suspension seat, and camera-based digital mirrors. The vehicle uses the Switch iON connected platform for real-time diagnostics, remote monitoring, and battery management.

**#SwitchKaro campaign introduced**

Switch Mobility launched the #SwitchKaro campaign in parallel with the EiV9 unveiling, aimed at fleet operators, businesses, and transport stakeholders, focused on electric mobility adoption across commercial transport segments.

Ganesh Mani, CEO of Switch Mobility, said: "The launch of the all-new Switch EiV9 marks an important milestone in our growth journey as we strengthen our presence across key public and institutional mobility segments. We see strong momentum in this category, with the 9m electric bus market expected to grow at a CAGR of nearly 34% by FY30, driven by increasing demand. The EiV9 has been purpose-built to deliver what operators value most: higher efficiency, superior passenger comfort, advanced safety and lower operating costs."
