# Zenobē expands into Germany

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-11-20

---

British commercial fleet electrification specialist Zenobē is bringing its offerings to Germany. Zenobē provides fleet operators with a fixed-price solution that includes EVs, charging infrastructure, battery management, and fleet management software.

Founded in the UK in 2017, Zenobē has developed a business model called 'Electric Transport-as-a-Service' (ETaaS), which it describes as an all-inclusive, worry-free package for operators of commercial vehicle fleets. The company has already convinced several bus operators in the UK, such as Transport UK, as well as retail chains like Woolworths in Australia.

Zenobē's portfolio now includes more than 3,500 EVs across over 150 electric depots in the UK, Belgium, Spain, Canada, the US, Australia and New Zealand. In addition to electric vehicles and charging infrastructure, Zenobē is also heavily invested in battery storage operations. According to the company, it is the leading owner and operator of grid-connected battery energy storage systems (BESS) in the UK's transmission network, with a capacity of 2.5 GWh.

## Zenobē acquires Munich-based BESS developer

In Germany, Zenobē has already acquired Munich-based battery storage developer sdp energie this year, including a pipeline of 1.75 GW of grid-connected battery energy storage systems. This was essentially a preparation for entering the market with its full range of services.

Zenobē CEO Steven Meersman said: "German operators have paid record prices for diesel this year, subsidies for electric fleets are being pulled back and a grid connection can take years to secure. That is precisely when a partner with its own capital and its own grid expertise matters. We are entering Germany with both sides of our business: sdp energie in Munich building grid-scale storage that strengthens the network, and our fleet team electrifying the depots that depend on it."

In Germany, Zenobē is targeting operators of bus and truck fleets, logistics service providers, public transport operators and last-mile delivery services. The first partnerships are expected to be announced in the coming months.

## Expansion backed by strong financing

Zenobē's expansion into the European market is supported by €325 million, provided by a consortium of British and global banks, including BayernLB and Siemens Financial Services.

In June, the fleet electrification specialist secured £980 million in new funding for its electric vehicle fleet platform in the UK and Ireland. The funds will be used to purchase over 1,200 additional electric buses and the associated charging infrastructure over the next three years.

This announcement follows a series of international expansion steps by Zenobē. In March, the company launched a £100 million fund in Australia to finance zero-emission truck fleets. In the same month, Zenobē entered the North American commercial vehicle market by acquiring Revolv, expanding its portfolio to include 13 locations and more than 100 electric trucks.

## Zenobē's concept overlaps with Einride

Zenobē's business model shares some similarities with the approach of Swedish company Einride: both companies offer fleet operators comprehensive solutions for transitioning to electric commercial vehicles, which include not only the vehicles but also charging infrastructure and digital management solutions. While Einride focuses on electric trucks and autonomous trucks, Zenobē's portfolio is broader, encompassing electric trucks, electric buses and electric vans. Additionally, Zenobē places a strong emphasis on energy infrastructure, including battery storage.
