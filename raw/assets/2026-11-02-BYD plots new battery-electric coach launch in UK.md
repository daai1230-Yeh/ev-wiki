# BYD plots new battery-electric coach launch in UK

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-10-30

---

BYD plans to launch a battery-electric coach in the UK by the end of the year, according to Frank Thorpe, BYD's Managing Director for Commercial Vehicle UK (as reported by Route One). The model is not a derivative of an existing BYD long-distance bus but was developed specifically for the UK market; technical specifications are not yet known, though it will feature innovative safety features.

Thorpe points to BYD's success with electric single- and double-deckers in the UK as motivation: more than 1,000 electric buses have reportedly been delivered or ordered. The 150 kW×2 powertrain used in the city and intercity buses is expected to power the coach too; battery capacity is unknown.

BYD's current UK range includes solo buses of 11.8 m, 10.8 m, 10.4 m and 9.9 m, plus the BD11 double-decker (10.6 m or 10.9 m, up to 532 kWh battery).
