# Nio sets Nio Day 2026 for December 19 in Wuhan, to unveil all-new ET5 Touring

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-24

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- The all-new ET5 Touring will debut on December 19, after Nio CEO William Li said deliveries will begin in the first half of 2027.
- Setting the date for December 19 also marks the return of Nio Day to its year-end tradition.

Nio Inc (NYSE: NIO) announced it will hold its most important annual event, Nio Day 2026, on December 19 in Wuhan, the capital of Hubei province, where it will officially unveil the all-new ET5 Touring wagon.

The Chinese EV maker said on Tuesday that the event will take place at the Optics Valley International Tennis Center in Wuhan under the theme "Journey Ahead."

Nio said it chose December 19 to align as closely as possible with its new vehicle launch schedule, while the theme reflects the brand's commitment to China's premium smart EV market priced above 300,000 yuan ($44,700).

The all-new ET5 Touring will be upgraded to a mid-to-large-size station wagon and, according to Nio, will usher in a golden age for Chinese station wagons with its new positioning.

That means the model will be larger than its predecessor — a midsize wagon — as cars in China continue to get bigger.

Just days earlier, Nio founder, chairman, and CEO William Li revealed at a user communication event that deliveries of the updated ET5 Touring and ES6 SUV will begin in the first half of 2027, while the ET5 sedan and EC6 have no refresh plans for next year.

The next-generation ET5 Touring and ES6 are expected to transition to Nio's latest NT 3.0 platform, featuring a 900-volt high-voltage architecture and 5C ultra-fast charging technology.

As the all-new ET5 Touring approaches, sales of the main Nio brand have become heavily concentrated in 2 large SUVs — the third-generation ES8 and the flagship ES9 — while the "5566" lineup, once its sales pillar, has lost significant momentum this year.

In October, the ET5 Touring delivered 1,182 units, down 74.45% year-on-year, remaining the only model under the main brand other than the ES8 and ES9 to exceed 1,000 monthly deliveries, according to data from the China Passenger Car Association (CPCA) compiled by CnEVPost.

In the same month, combined deliveries of the ES8 and ES9 reached 15,452 units, accounting for 85.25% of the main Nio brand's monthly deliveries.

Nio's current 2026 ET5 Touring was launched in April this year, with a starting price of 298,000 yuan including the battery pack.

Since its debut in 2017, every edition of Nio Day has featured major product and technology launches. In July this year, Wuhan beat Harbin and Xiamen in a user vote to win the right to host Nio Day 2026.

Scheduling the event for December 19 also marks the return of Nio Day to its year-end tradition. Last year, Nio directly selected Hangzhou, Zhejiang province, to host Nio Day 2025 on September 20, where it officially launched the third-generation ES8.
