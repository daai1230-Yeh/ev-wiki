# Li Auto to enter four new Asian markets and launch right-hand-drive cars

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-11-16

---

Chinese carmaker Li Auto is expanding its presence in Asia, with plans to enter Hong Kong, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam. The company has also announced its first right-hand-drive models, starting with its flagship Li Mega electric MPV.

In Hong Kong, Li Auto has appointed Zung Fu Group as its general distributor. The local partner has 70 years of experience in the automotive industry in Hong Kong and Macau and currently represents brands including Mercedes-Benz, Denza, smart, Hyundai and Foton. In Singapore, Li Auto has partnered with Wearnes Automotive, which has been active in the industry since 1906 and operates in seven markets. Its portfolio includes brands such as Bentley, Bugatti, Mazda, Renault, Porsche, Volvo Cars and Zenvo.

In Thailand, Li Auto will operate through a local subsidiary, which will work with two dealer partners: Evante and Infinite Automobile Company. The more direct approach could indicate that the company has bigger plans for Thailand or regards it as a more significant overseas market than the other newly announced destinations.

In Vietnam, Li Auto has partnered with Neera Motors, which recently became the official distributor of Dongfeng Group's Forthing brand. The Chinese carmaker has also announced a new partnership with Dangdang New Energy Auto Service in the Philippines, where it opened its first showroom and launched its first models in 2025.

As for right-hand-drive vehicles, Li Auto has selected its flagship model, the Li Mega, as the first to receive the configuration. The company will present the right-hand-drive version of the electric MPV for the first time at the 2026 Thailand International Motor Expo, which runs from 1 to 13 December. Li Auto plans to introduce a second right-hand-drive model within a year.

Last month, Li Auto made its European debut and unveiled the European-specification Li 6 electric SUV at the 2026 Paris Motor Show. The company plans to launch sales of the Li 6 in the Netherlands and Norway, its first European markets, in the first quarter of 2027.
