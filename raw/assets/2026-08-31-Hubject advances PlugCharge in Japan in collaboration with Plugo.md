# Hubject advances Plug&Charge in Japan in collaboration with Plugo

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-08-28

---

The roaming specialist Hubject is advancing the Plug&Charge initiative with a cooperation in Japan. There, the Berlin-based company is joining forces with Plugo, a charging infrastructure company that wants to implement Plug&Charge in the Japanese market.

Plug&Charge is set to make charging electric vehicles significantly easier for EV drivers, as it eliminates the need for charging cards or apps. Instead, the vehicle is automatically recognised when connected to a charging station, and the charging process and payment occur automatically in the background — provided the technology has been set up once in the vehicle.

Hubject, long known in the industry primarily for its roaming platform Intercharge, has been advancing Plug&Charge for several years. The technology is based on the international ISO 15118-2 standard. The company has now gained another partner in the Japanese firm Plugo, which plans to adopt the solution.

## Plugo to support the expansion of Plug&Charge

The partnership between the German and Japanese companies combines their strengths with Hubject's Plug&Charge portfolio and technical expertise and Plugo's presence in Japan's charging infrastructure sector. This collaboration aims to target charge point operators (CPOs), electric mobility service providers (eMSPs), and vehicle manufacturers (OEMs) in Japan.

Plugo is a highly versatile company that develops and manufactures its own AC and DC charging stations, operates its own public charging network, and simultaneously offers charging management software and white-label solutions for other companies. This enables Plugo to cover the entire charging value chain from hardware and backend systems to apps and the operation of charging points, while acting as both a technology provider and a CPO.

"We have been working to bring a seamless 'just plug in and charge' experience to drivers in Japan through the practical implementation of Plug&Charge compliant with the CHAdeMO standard. Through our partnership with Hubject, we will expand this experience to NACS and other charging standards used internationally," says Plugo CEO Naoki Okawa. The goal, he said, is "to create an environment where drivers can use EVs without having to think about differences between charging standards, delivering a more convenient and flexible charging experience to EV drivers, vehicle manufacturers, and charging operators across Japan."

## Seamless charging experience with Plug&Charge

Hubject CEO Christian Hahn adds: "Plugo has already proven that a seamless EV Charging Experience is fundamental for Japanese drivers. This partnership brings Hubject's full support for Plug&Charge, from certification to everyday operations, to make that available across Japan."

The rollout of Plug&Charge has been underway internationally for several years. In Germany, for example, Aral Pulse implemented Plug&Charge at its charging stations back in 2022, followed later by Mer Germany and Allego. According to Hubject, Plug&Charge is already supported by over five million electric vehicles worldwide, and this number is growing daily.
