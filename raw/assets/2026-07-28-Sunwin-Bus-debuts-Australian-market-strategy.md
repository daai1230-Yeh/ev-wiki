# Chinese Sunwin Bus debuts Australian market strategy at Australasia Bus & Coach Expo 2026 (showcasing iEV12 and B13E)

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 27 July 2026

---

Chinese manufacturer Sunwin Bus (part of the SAIC group) showcased two battery-electric bus products developed for Australia — the iEV12 city bus and B13E bus chassis — at the Australasia Bus & Coach Expo 2026 in Sydney, under the theme "Green Means More". Wrightbus also announced plans for the local market at the same expo.

The iEV12 and B13E were developed specifically for Australian operating conditions, including right-hand-drive configuration, long-distance operation, coastal environments and high-temperature conditions. Both feature a fully low-floor, barrier-free layout and Cell-to-Pack (CTP) battery technology. The powertrain combines a new-generation electric drive motor with silicon carbide (SiC) auxiliary control technology, and an integrated thermal management system designed for Australia's hot summers, plus additional anti-corrosion protection for coastal applications.

The B13E chassis supports multiple wheelbase configurations and front/rear overhang options. According to Australian trade publication Australasian Bus & Coach, the chassis displayed in Sydney is a 12-metre low-floor platform available with two or three passenger doors, featuring a corrosion-resistant aluminium body, low centre of gravity, wheelchair accessibility and high-efficiency electric motors.

Sunwin Bus General Manager Ma Zhenggang said Australia is continuing its public transport sector's transition to zero-emission buses, with local governments advancing fleet electrification and charging infrastructure. He described Australia as a long-term strategic market and said the company plans to strengthen its understanding of local technical standards and regulatory requirements while developing localized supply chains and lifecycle service capabilities.
