# Germany: RMV, Deutsche Bahn and HOLON launch KIRA+ for Level 4 autonomous public transport in Rhine-Main region

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-09-28

---

RMV, Deutsche Bahn and HOLON have launched the KIRA+ project to test SAE Level 4 autonomous on-demand public transport with HOLON urban vehicles in Germany's Rhine-Main region. First mapping drives are scheduled for 2027 in parts of Darmstadt and the district of Offenbach, in areas served by local partners HEAG mobilo and Kreisverkehrsgesellschaft Offenbach (kvgOF).

KIRA+ is one of five test fields within the MILA.mobility project, funded by Germany's Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR), intended to prepare a model region where autonomous mobility services operate as part of regular local public transport. It builds on the predecessor KIRA project (running until end of 2026), under which RMV and DB launched Level 4 shuttle operations in 2024. DB is also involved in a just-announced project on standardized approval processes for autonomous shuttles in Germany. MOIA has started autonomous testing in Hamburg, and HOLON has urban-platform projects in several locations.

## HOLON urban to operate in KIRA+

KIRA+ will use the accessible HOLON urban with Mobileye autonomous driving technology. Each vehicle offers five passenger seats and one wheelchair space (three seats remain when the wheelchair space is occupied). Safety personnel remain on board throughout. Ride requests are managed via an app from ioki GmbH, acquired by HOLON's parent Benteler in early 2026.

HOLON provides the vehicles and acts as vehicle keeper (approvals, maintenance), supported by DB Regio. DB Regio handles passenger-transport-law approvals and operates the vehicles, including fleet management, technical supervision, operating-area management and on-board passenger support. For HOLON this is another German public-road deployment after Hamburg and Paderborn.

## Builds on KIRA

The operating area and control centre from KIRA continue to be used, and experience from earlier projects feeds into booking procedures, operating processes and vehicle equipment. KIRA+ focuses on scalability and technical supervision. DLR provides scientific support; VDV participates as industry partner.

## MILA.mobility

MILA.mobility ("Middleware Integration of Control Centre and Dispatch Systems for Autonomous Mobility") develops and harmonises a middleware architecture connecting vehicle, fleet dispatch and control centre software. Within KIRA+, RMV is project initiator and transport association, HOLON supplies vehicles and DB Regio operates them.

## Quotes

- Prof. Knut Ringat (RMV): the goal is to integrate autonomous services into regular public transport in the near future.
- Harmen van Zijderveld (DB Regio): autonomous vehicles must be integrated intelligently into public transport so as not to add more cars to the roads.
- Dr Sven Herzig (HOLON): the Rhine-Main region aims at a model region where autonomous vehicles become part of regular service in large numbers.
