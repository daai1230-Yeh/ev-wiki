# America Lost Over 111,000 Jobs Due To Trump's Clean Energy Pullback

**Author**: Nico DeMattia | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

---

A new report from the BlueGreen Alliance, a U.S. non-profit representing labor unions and environmental groups, quantifies the fallout from the Trump administration's reversal of Biden-era clean energy incentives: 223 projects were stalled or cancelled, totaling $82.9 billion in lost investment and 111,765 jobs lost.

"The resulting figures clearly illustrate the staggering loss of investment and job creation that the policies of this administration and Congress have brought about," said BlueGreen Alliance vice president of research Roxanne Johnson.

The report also warns that 3,034 more projects now face stricter tax credit requirements, putting $695.2 billion and nearly 1.2 million jobs at risk. Separately, the Trump administration's "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" ended federal tax credits and incentives for new and used EV purchases, and the EPA has rolled back some hazard-protection rules, including silica-dust protections for coal miners.
