# As E20 backlash grows, wider E0 availability may calm consumer outrage

**Author**: ETAuto | **Date**: 2026-08-04

---

With the government using different channels to douse the E20 fire, making pure petrol (E0) available at more pumps could help calm consumer outrage over the mandatory 20% ethanol-blended fuel rollout.

Note: the ETAuto/Economic Times auto vertical could not be accessed directly to pull the full article. Related reporting (LocalCircles survey cited via Autocar Professional and Deccan Herald) found that 53% of petrol vehicle owners called the Road Transport and Petroleum Ministries' handling of the E20 rollout "disastrous" or "ineffective," with 66% reporting a mileage drop of over 10% after switching to E20. Union Petroleum Minister Hardeep Singh Puri has maintained the mileage impact is minimal, and Nitin Gadkari has described the backlash as a "paid campaign." The Supreme Court has separately declined to entertain a PIL challenging the E20 rollout without a consumer choice of fuel blend.
