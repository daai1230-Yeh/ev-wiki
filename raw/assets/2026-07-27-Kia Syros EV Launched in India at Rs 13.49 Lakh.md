# Kia Syros EV Launched in India at Rs 13.49 Lakh With Up to 526km Claimed Range

**Author**: Aditya Yadav | **Date**: 23 July 2026

---

A few days back, Kia pulled the covers off the Syros EV and gave everyone a first look at its newest electric SUV. Now, the company has officially launched it in India with prices starting at Rs 13.49 lakh (ex showroom). It is Kia's second locally made electric vehicle after the Carens Clavis EV. Buyers get two battery choices, a claimed driving range of up to 526km, premium features, and a long list of ownership benefits that make the Syros EV one of the most interesting new electric SUVs in its segment.

**Kia Syros EV Price**

HTK: Rs 13.49 lakh | HTK+: Rs 14.99 lakh | HTX: Rs 15.99 lakh | HTK+ ER: Rs 16.99 lakh | HTX ER: Rs 17.99 lakh | HTX+ ER: Rs 19.49 lakh | X Line ER: Rs 19.99 lakh (all ex showroom)

Deliveries of the Syros EV will begin from 30 July 2026.

**Two Battery Options With Long Driving Range**

The Syros EV is available with two battery packs: a 42kWh battery with a claimed range of 443km, and a 51.4kWh battery with a claimed range of 526km. The larger battery also delivers 171PS and can accelerate from 0 to 100kmph in 8.1 seconds. It supports 100kW DC fast charging, which charges the battery from 10 to 80 percent in 39 minutes. Buyers also get battery conditioning, regenerative braking with paddle shifters, i Pedal driving mode, Vehicle to Load function, Virtual Engine Sound System, battery heating, and a 10.8kW AC onboard charger.

**Fresh EV Styling**

The Syros EV carries a modern look with several electric vehicle specific design details. It gets Kia's Digital Tiger Face, Ice Cube LED headlamps, Star Map LED lighting, flush door handles, aerodynamic alloy wheels, gloss black skid plates, roof rails, LED fog lamps, and new front and rear bumpers. Buyers can choose from nine exterior colours, including the exclusive Matte Graphite finish for the X Line version.

**Premium Cabin Packed With Features**

Highlights include a 30 inch Trinity panoramic display, wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, a Harman Kardon eight speaker sound system, wireless phone charger, dual pane panoramic sunroof, ventilated front and rear seats, four way powered driver seat, 64 colour ambient lighting, Kia Connect with connected car features, OTA software updates, a smart dual camera dashcam, rear seat slide and recline function, a 16 litre front storage compartment, and multiple USB Type C charging ports including a 100W port.

**Safety Features**

The Syros EV offers six airbags, Level 2 ADAS with 16 safety functions, a 360 degree camera, Blind View Monitor, front, rear and side parking sensors, Electronic Stability Control, Hill Start Assist, Brake Assist System, an electronic parking brake with Auto Hold, Tyre Pressure Monitoring System, ISOFIX child seat mounts, and Rear Occupant Alert.

**Ownership Benefits**

Kia has introduced several ownership benefits along with the Syros EV: a 15 year unlimited kilometre high voltage battery warranty for the first owner, assured buyback with up to 80 percent resale value after three years for eligible customers, a Battery as a Service option with vehicle prices starting from Rs 7.99 lakh and battery usage charged separately, the My Convenience e Plus maintenance package, access to the K Charge network with more than 20,300 charging points, and support from 129 Kia dealerships with DC fast chargers and 275 EV ready workshops.
