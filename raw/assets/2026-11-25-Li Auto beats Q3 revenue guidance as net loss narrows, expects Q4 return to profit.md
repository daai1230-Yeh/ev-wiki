# Li Auto beats Q3 revenue guidance as net loss narrows, expects Q4 return to profit

**Author:** Lei Kang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-25

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- Third-quarter revenue rose 5.4% year-on-year to $4.29 billion, while net loss narrowed 81.8% sequentially.
- The company expects fourth-quarter deliveries of 110,000 to 115,000 vehicles and is extending its model refresh cycle to about every 2 years.

Li Auto's (NASDAQ: LI) third-quarter revenue topped the upper end of its previous guidance as its net loss narrowed sharply, with the company expecting a return to profitability in the fourth quarter amid an improved product mix and the scaled adoption of in-house technologies.

The Chinese EV maker reported unaudited third-quarter total revenue of 28.8 billion yuan ($4.29 billion) on Wednesday, up 5.4% year-on-year and up 12.3% from the second quarter.

The result beat its earlier guidance range of 26.6 billion yuan to 28.0 billion yuan. Vehicle sales revenue reached 27.1 billion yuan in the third quarter, rising 4.7% year-on-year and 12.6% sequentially.

Net loss narrowed to 309.7 million yuan in the third quarter, down 50.4% from 624.4 million yuan in the same period last year and down 81.8% from 1.7 billion yuan in the second quarter.

On a non-GAAP basis, third-quarter net loss was 112.9 million yuan, narrowing 68.6% year-on-year and 92.5% from the second quarter.

The improvement in profitability was primarily driven by a rebound in margins. Overall gross margin reached 16.7% in the third quarter, up from 16.3% a year earlier and 11.0% in the second quarter.

Vehicle margin rebounded to 15.3% in the third quarter, improving significantly from 9.4% in the second quarter, though slightly below 15.5% in the same period last year, mainly due to changes in product mix.

The company's product portfolio was optimized as deliveries began in September and October for the updated flagship battery electric MPV (multi-purpose vehicle) Li Mega, the flagship SUV Li i9, and the 2026 Li i6.

Cash flow also turned positive in the third quarter. Net cash provided by operating activities was 3.2 billion yuan, while free cash flow reached 2.0 billion yuan, reversing a negative 1.3 billion yuan in the second quarter.

Research and development expenses rose 4.8% year-on-year to 3.1 billion yuan in the third quarter, while selling, general, and administrative expenses fell 14.7% year-on-year to 2.4 billion yuan.

As of September 30, Li Auto's cash position stood at 88.2 billion yuan.

For the fourth quarter, Li Auto expects vehicle deliveries to be between 110,000 and 115,000 units, representing a year-on-year increase of 0.7% to 5.3%.

The company projects fourth-quarter total revenue of 31.7 billion yuan to 33.1 billion yuan, up 10.1% to 15.1% from a year earlier.

Management said on the earnings call that gross margin is expected to continue improving in the fourth quarter, enabling a return to profit.

Li Auto delivered 29,025 vehicles in October, implying that average monthly deliveries in November and December are expected to rebound to over 40,000 units.

Management also announced on the call that the company will shift its model refresh cycle from annually to about once every 2 years, with plans to update only 1 to 2 individual models in 2027 rather than refreshing its entire lineup.

In overseas markets, Li Auto plans to launch the right-hand-drive Li Mega in Hong Kong in December and begin deliveries of the Europe-spec Li 6 (Li i6) in the Netherlands and Norway in the first quarter of 2027, expecting overseas sales to double in 2027.
