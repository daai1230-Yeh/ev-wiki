1. Warm-Up 暖身 3 min

  1. Have you ever used a ride-hailing app like Uber? What was the experience like? 你用過像 Uber 這樣的叫車 App 嗎？搭乘經驗如何？
  2. What's the first thing you think of when you hear the word "robotaxi"? 聽到「無人計程車」這個詞，你腦中第一個浮現的畫面是什麼？
  3. Would you trust a car with no driver and no steering wheel more or less than a car with a human driver? 一台沒有駕駛、沒有方向盤的車，你會比有人駕駛的車更信任它，還是更不信任？

2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Uber just announced its biggest layoff since May 2020, cutting about 3,300 jobs — roughly 10 percent of its workforce. The company says the move is meant to help it respond more effectively to the rise of robotaxis, the very technology that could one day replace the human drivers Uber's business depends on.

Uber 剛宣布自2020年5月以來規模最大的一次裁員，將裁減約3,300個職位，約占全公司員工總數的10%。公司表示，此舉是為了更有效因應無人計程車（Robotaxi）崛起帶來的衝擊——而這項技術，未來有可能取代Uber賴以維生的人類駕駛。

As of the end of 2025, Uber employed about 34,000 people worldwide. The cuts will remove 20 percent of employees who sit more than seven layers below the CEO, and nearly cut in half the number of teams where a manager oversees just one or two direct reports. Some teams will be merged, and most remaining staff will be concentrated in hub cities like New York and San Francisco.

截至2025年底，Uber全球員工總數約為34,000人。此次裁員將削減自執行長以下超過七個層級的20%員工，並將僅有一到兩名直屬下屬的團隊數量削減近一半。部分團隊將被合併，多數留任員工將集中部署於紐約、舊金山等關鍵樞紐城市。

Fully remote employees will now be capped at about 1 percent of staff, and the company will keep its policy requiring people to be in the office three days a week. CEO Dara Khosrowshahi did not blame artificial intelligence for the cuts. Instead, he said the goal was to flatten management layers and reduce the organizational complexity that had been slowing down decisions.

完全遠距工作的員工比例將被限制在約1%，公司也將維持員工每週需進辦公室三天的政策。執行長Dara Khosrowshahi並未將裁員歸咎於人工智慧，而是表示此舉是為了簡化管理層級，降低過去拖慢決策速度的組織複雜性。

The pressure is real. As robotaxi fleets grow, they could squeeze the profit Uber earns simply by matching riders with drivers. Analysts note that a business built around autonomous vehicles needs a very different kind of workforce than one built around human drivers and all the costs that come with them.

這股壓力確實存在。隨著無人計程車車隊規模擴大，它們可能擠壓Uber作為乘客與司機媒合平台所能賺取的利潤。分析師指出，一個建立在自動駕駛技術之上的商業模式，所需的員工類型與規模，將與圍繞人類駕駛及其相關成本打造的模式截然不同。

Waymo, currently the largest robotaxi operator in the U.S., already runs some of its vehicles through the Uber app in Austin and Atlanta — but it's also expanding into new markets on its own, without Uber. Meanwhile, Tesla is accelerating its own robotaxi push. To defend its position, Uber plans to invest more than $10 billion over the next several years in robotaxi technology and partnerships, positioning itself as the go-to platform for autonomous rides.

目前美國最大的無人計程車營運商Waymo，已在奧斯汀與亞特蘭大透過Uber App提供部分車輛服務——但它也正獨立於Uber之外，拓展新市場。與此同時，Tesla也正加速推展自家的無人計程車業務。為捍衛市場地位，Uber計劃在未來數年投入超過100億美元發展無人計程車技術與合作關係，將自己定位為無人駕駛乘車服務的首選平台。

Tesla, for its part, quietly opened its purpose-built Cybercab to the public in Austin — no livestream, no press event, Elon Musk reportedly not even present. The two-seat vehicle has no steering wheel and began series production back in April at Giga Texas, but Tesla currently has approval for only 45 Cybercab units in the state. Like Waymo, Tesla still runs a remote command center to step in when needed.

Tesla方面則悄悄在奧斯汀對外開放其專為載客打造的Cybercab——沒有直播、沒有記者會，據報導連馬斯克本人都不在場。這款兩人座車輛沒有方向盤，早在今年4月就已在德州超級工廠展開量產，但目前Tesla在德州僅獲准部署45輛Cybercab。與Waymo一樣，Tesla仍設有遠端指揮中心，在必要時介入接管。

Just days after the launch, regulators stepped in. The U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration confirmed that Tesla had self-certified the Cybercab as meeting federal vehicle safety standards — and the agency has now opened an audit into that process, including how Tesla decided that certain standard requirements simply didn't apply to this vehicle.

上路才短短數天，監管機關便介入了。美國國家公路交通安全管理局（NHTSA）證實，Tesla是「自我認證」Cybercab符合聯邦車輛安全標準——該機構如今已對此認證流程展開稽核，包括調查Tesla是如何判定某些標準規範根本不適用於這款車輛。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型 3 min

  • "X is betting big on ___." 表示某公司大舉押注在...上。例：Uber is betting big on robotaxi technology.
  • "X is bracing for ___." 為即將到來的衝擊或變化做準備。例：Uber is bracing for the rise of driverless competitors.
  • "This puts pressure on X to ___." 這使X面臨壓力，必須...。例：This puts pressure on Uber to prove it still has a role in a driverless future.
  • "Regulators are looking into whether ___." 主管機關正在調查是否...。例：Regulators are looking into whether Tesla's self-certification was valid.
  • "X positions itself as the go-to platform for ___." 將自己定位為...的首選平台。例：Uber positions itself as the go-to platform for autonomous rides.

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

  1. Do you think Uber's layoffs are really about preparing for robotaxis, or could there be other reasons the CEO isn't mentioning? 你認為Uber裁員真的是為了因應無人計程車，還是執行長沒說出口的其他原因？
  2. If robotaxis eventually replace most human rideshare drivers, what do you think should happen to the people who lose that work? 如果無人計程車最終取代了大多數人類共乘司機，你認為那些失業的人應該獲得什麼協助？
  3. Instead of building its own self-driving cars, Uber is investing billions in partnerships with companies like Waymo and Tesla. Is that a smarter strategy than competing head-on? Uber沒有自己造自駕車，而是投入數十億美元與Waymo、Tesla等公司合作，這比正面競爭更聰明嗎？
  4. Tesla self-certified that the Cybercab meets safety standards, and regulators are now auditing that process. Should companies be allowed to self-certify safety, or should an independent body always review a vehicle before it's allowed on public roads? Tesla自我認證Cybercab符合安全標準，如今監管機關正在稽核這個流程。你認為安全認證應該允許企業自我認證，還是應該一律由獨立機構審核後才能上路？
  5. Tesla launched the Cybercab quietly, with no livestream or press event. Why might a company choose to launch a major new product this way? Tesla悄悄推出Cybercab，沒有直播也沒有記者會。你認為一家公司為什麼會選擇用這種方式發表重大新產品？
  6. Would you get into a robotaxi with no steering wheel and no driver? What would it take to earn your trust? 你會搭乘一台沒有方向盤、沒有駕駛的無人計程車嗎？要讓你信任它，需要具備什麼條件？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 2 min

60-SECOND SUMMARY 一分鐘摘要

In 60 seconds, summarize Uber's layoffs and its $10 billion robotaxi bet, plus Tesla's quiet Cybercab launch and the regulatory audit that followed.

用一分鐘摘要Uber的裁員與百億美元無人計程車投資計畫，以及Tesla悄悄推出Cybercab後隨之而來的監管稽核。
