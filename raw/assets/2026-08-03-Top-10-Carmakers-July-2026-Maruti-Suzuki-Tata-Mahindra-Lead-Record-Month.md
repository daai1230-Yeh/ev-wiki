# Top 10 Carmakers (July 2026): Maruti Suzuki, Tata, Mahindra Lead Record Month

**Author**: Dennis Abraham James | **Date**: 2026-08-02

---

India's passenger vehicle industry posted its strongest month on record in July 2026, with total wholesale dispatches crossing 4.7 lakh units, a 33% jump over July 2025. Maruti Suzuki led the standings by a wide margin, while Tata Motors held a slim lead over Mahindra for second place. Hyundai rebounded sharply after a supplier fire disrupted its June output, while Nissan's wholesales more than tripled on the back of the newly launched Tekton.

1. **Maruti Suzuki** — 1,96,203 units, up 42.41% YoY and 33.30% over June.
2. **Tata Motors** — 62,611 units, up 58.42% YoY and 0.86% over June.
3. **Mahindra** — 60,048 units, up 20.41% YoY, down 0.57% from June.
4. **Hyundai** — 54,210 units, up 23.30% YoY and 36.77% over June.
5. **Toyota** — 30,516 units, up 4.65% YoY and 7.30% over June.
6. **Kia** — 28,200 units, up 27.40% YoY and 14.86% over June.
7. **MG** — 8,158 units, up 22% YoY and 7.80% over June.
8. **Honda** — 6,014 units, up 48.49% YoY and 14.71% over June.
9. **Nissan** — 4,518 units, up 218% YoY and 50.30% over June.
10. **Renault** — 3,293 units, up 27.90% YoY, down 18.95% from June.

Several factors fed into the record month. Dealers stocked up heavily ahead of the festive season, with Onam, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri and Diwali all falling between August and November, prompting automakers to push extra volumes into showrooms early. SUV demand continued to grow steadily through July, while demand for smaller cars also picked up meaningfully.

New model launches added to the tally, with strong advance bookings translating into higher dispatches, alongside festive discounting from some brands. Production at Hyundai and Kia's supplier plants also normalised after a fire had disrupted output, while last year's GST rate reforms on vehicles and a favourable market backdrop through the year gave the industry the boost it needed despite rising manufacturing costs.
