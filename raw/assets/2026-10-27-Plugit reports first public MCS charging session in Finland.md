# Plugit reports first public MCS charging session in Finland

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-10-26

---

Charging provider Plugit has, according to its own statements, completed Finland's first public megawatt charging session. A Volvo FH Aero Electric was recharged at Plugit's MCS station in the southern Finnish port city of Kotka.

The Volvo FH Aero Electric was charged in Kotka both via the Volvo Open Charge service and directly through Plugit – and both methods worked flawlessly. Plugit currently offers public MCS charging at two locations in Finland: Kotka and Lieto. The company operates around 350 public charging points, nine of which are designed for heavy-duty electric trucks.

In the future, Plugit aims to focus more on e-truck charging infrastructure in Finland and Sweden, with more MCS chargers to follow. Janne Silvonen, Product Manager for Volvo's electric trucks, said: "Finland's first public MCS charging session is a significant step towards the electrification of heavy goods transport in Finland. A long operating range and fast MCS charging make electric trucks a viable option even for longer transport journeys."

Plugit is a partner of the Volvo Open Charge service in Finland. The service, part of the Volvo Connect fleet management system, enables logistics companies to search for charging stations suitable for heavy-duty vehicles, view details, and plan charging; payment can be made in the associated app.

To accelerate the roll-out, Plugit has partnered with fellow Finnish company Kempower under a deal agreed at the end of January 2026, supplying turnkey charging solutions for Plugit's own charging parks and third-party customers. The two also plan R&D collaboration on intelligent booking systems, energy management, truck guidance systems, and charging hardware pilots.

The Kotka station is based on Kempower technology: up to two 600 kW power units (1.2 MW total) with Kempower Mega Satellite charging columns. It can be equipped with one MCS connector for up to 1.2 MW or up to two CCS connectors of up to 560 kW each. Initial sites in Norway, Denmark and Sweden already use the system with MCS connectors in depot applications.
