# Euro Bus Expo 2026 to feature over 80 vehicles, with new launches from Zhongtong, Custom Denning and CBC alongside Irizar, VDL and Scania

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-15

---

Euro Bus Expo 2026 brings more than 80 buses, coaches and minibuses to the NEC Birmingham on 3–5 November, with over 200 exhibitors (31 vehicle manufacturers, dealers and converters). Highlights: Australian maker Custom Denning's first UK exhibition appearance, two new vehicles from Zhongtong, the UK debut of CBC's Voyage E14 battery-electric coach, and Irizar's 18.7 m articulated electric ie tram for airport operations.

**Coaches**
- VDL: first right-hand-drive VDL Futura 3 (FHD-129 RHD; the LHD version debuted at Busworld 2025) and the Van Hool Astron.
- Scania (GB): next-generation Touring and the Scania Irizar i6S Efficient plug-in hybrid coach, awarded the Sustainable Bus Award 2027. Irizar UK: two i6S Efficient integral coaches.
- Volvo Group UK: first UK trade show appearance of the B13R UNVI XL. Daimler Buses UK: Mercedes-Benz Tourismo M/3 and battery-electric eCitaro.
- CBC: Voyage E14 battery-electric coach and diesel Voyage HD12 (RHD UK debuts). Harris Bus & Coach: Higer Azure 9 midibus in RHD for the first time in the UK, plus V10 and V12.
- MAN Neoplan Tourliner P10 (PSVAR), MOBIpeople Premium and Beulas Jewel double-decker from B.A.S.E.; Pelican shows five Yutong vehicles (GT14, GT12, TC9, E12, U11DD); Temsa HD and MD9.

**Electric buses**: BYD B11, BD11A and BD11B; Alexander Dennis next-generation Enviro200EV plus diesel Enviro400; Wrightbus StreetDeck Electroliner 2.0 in latest TfL spec plus a diesel-to-electric converted First Bus StreetDeck (NewPower/NewLife); Custom Denning with two Element 2.2 battery-electric single-deckers.

**Minibuses and accessible vehicles**: global debuts from Nicholson Coachbuilders (Altair, Meridian, Horizon) and Mellor Treka Bus (e-Mobility and e-Mobility+ on the Renault Master E-Tech; CitySprint, Strata Ultra, Strata Plus). EVM UK & Ireland brings eight vehicles, including three new additions (AOS Grand Toro II XL, long-wheelbase X-Clusive, long-wheelbase Tribus eSprinter).

**Programme**: 25 Masterclass Theatre sessions (bus reform, coach operations, AI and automation, fleet transition, regulation, workforce) and the Euro Bus Expo Connect meeting-planning platform. Event Director Helen Conway called the show a showroom where operators can step aboard and compare options first-hand.
