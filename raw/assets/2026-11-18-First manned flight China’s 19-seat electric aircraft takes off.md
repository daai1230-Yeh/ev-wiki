# First manned flight: China’s 19-seat electric aircraft takes off

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-17

---

The CE-25A electric aircraft from Chinese manufacturer Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (Comac) completed its first manned flight on Saturday. The CE-25A is a joint development by Comac, battery manufacturer CATL, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and other partners.

According to media reports, the CE-25A completed its first manned flight over the weekend, carrying two pilots and a test engineer. The flight took place in Binzhou, in the eastern Chinese province of Shandong, and lasted just under 17 minutes, during which the aircraft covered 60.4 kilometres. This follows its first unmanned flight in February and several subsequent test flights. The first flight with people on board has now also been successfully completed.

The CE-25A is a collaborative effort between state-owned manufacturer Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (Comac), CATL, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and other partners. The 10-tonne-class aircraft can accommodate 19 passengers. In a post-flight announcement, Comac confirmed that the aircraft behaved normally and all test results met expectations.

The CE-25A is Comac’s first electric passenger aircraft and is intended primarily for intercity routes. According to the developers, it has a maximum take-off weight of 10 tonnes, a cruising speed of 300 kilometres per hour, a cruising altitude of up to 2,400 metres and a range of up to 400 kilometres.

In terms of design, the aircraft is powered by a decentralised electric propeller system mounted on the wings. Images show ten propulsion units, technically referred to as ‘ducted fans’ – enclosed propellers. No details about the batteries have been disclosed yet. Development work on the electric aircraft reportedly began in 2023.

That same year, CATL joined the project, marking its first involvement in electric aircraft development. In this context, the battery giant established a joint venture with Comac and the Shanghai Jiao Tong University Enterprise Development Group in July 2023. According to a report at the time by news portal Yicai Global, the joint venture had registered capital of 600 million yuan.

Even then, it was noted that CATL was likely to contribute its ‘Condensed Battery’, introduced in April 2023, with an energy density of up to 500 Wh/kg at cell level, to the joint venture. This is because the battery manufacturer’s chief scientist had previously stated that CATL was testing its ‘Condensed Battery’ against both automotive and aviation standards. However, this has not been officially confirmed.
