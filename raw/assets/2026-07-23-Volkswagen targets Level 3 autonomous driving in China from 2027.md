# Volkswagen targets Level 3 autonomous driving in China from 2027

**Author**: Lei Kang | **Date**: 22.07.2026

---

Volkswagen is stepping up its automated driving efforts in China. Together with Horizon Robotics, the carmaker aims to bring its first Level 3 system into series production in the second half of 2027.

Volkswagen is accelerating the localized development of automated-driving technology in China, with deliveries of vehicles equipped with L3 capabilities set to begin as early as the second half of 2027.

The German automotive giant and Chinese intelligent-driving solutions provider Horizon Robotics (HKEX: 9660) will further deepen their technological cooperation in AI foundation models through their joint venture Carizon. Under a white-box licensing model, Carizon will leverage Horizon's AI foundation-model capabilities to develop and accelerate the creation of a unified AI driving solution for Volkswagen Group China.

Horizon's AI foundation model will work with Carizon's C7H system-on-chip, currently under development, and its GAIA world-model data platform to support the deployment of Volkswagen's L3 and L4 automated-driving capabilities. The technology will also provide a foundation for future passenger-vehicle and robotaxi applications, and will be deeply integrated with Volkswagen's first locally developed zonal electronic and electrical architecture, the China Electronic Architecture (CEA).

"China has become one of Volkswagen Group's key innovation and technology hubs, particularly in software, artificial intelligence and automated driving," Volkswagen Group CEO Oliver Blume said in a statement. Combining world-class AI capabilities with the group's engineering expertise will enable Volkswagen to better serve Chinese customers and support its business development in selected international markets, he said.

Carizon is a joint venture between Volkswagen's software subsidiary Cariad and Horizon, headquartered in Beijing and formally established in December 2023. Volkswagen invested about €2.4 billion in the joint venture and holds a 60% stake.

Carizon delivered its first advanced driver-assistance system for use in the ID. UNYX 07 sedan and ID. UNYX 06 SUV, offering functions including highway navigation-assisted driving and memory parking. Its full-scenario ADAS solution has now entered mass production and can additionally support urban navigation-assisted driving. The solution will be deployed in seven new electrified models from Volkswagen's three joint ventures in China starting in the third quarter of 2026, gradually expanding to a broader range of products based on the CEA architecture from next year.

Intelligent driving is becoming a key area of competition in China's automotive market. More than 30% of new vehicles sold in China in the first half of this year were equipped with advanced navigation-assisted driving functions, according to data from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology. Under pressure from local competitors, a growing number of international automakers are turning to Chinese technology companies for support in developing intelligent-driving capabilities — Stellantis is working with Pony AI on robotaxi trials in Europe, while Mercedes-Benz has deployed robotaxi services with Momenta in Abu Dhabi.
