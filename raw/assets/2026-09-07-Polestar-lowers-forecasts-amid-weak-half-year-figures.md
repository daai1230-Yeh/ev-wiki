# Polestar lowers forecasts amid weak half-year figures

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 04.09.2026

---

Polestar's H1 2026 sales were essentially flat (30,423 vehicles, +0.4%) while revenue fell ~4% to $1.36B on pricing pressure and lower CO2-credit income. The operating loss narrowed 43% to $629M, but net loss fell only 29% to $842M because US restructuring — triggered by the US Commerce Department denying Polestar an exemption under the Connected Vehicle Rule banning Chinese-tech connected vehicles from 2027 — added $211M in costs. A New Jersey dealership is now suing Polestar, alleging the rule was a pretext to exit the US market; sister company Volvo (also Geely-owned) did secure an exemption by meeting a longer list of conditions.

Polestar cut its full-year 2026 sales growth guidance from low-double-digit to low-to-mid-single-digit percent, citing the aging Polestar 2 and the Polestar 4 SUV derivative not launching until Q4. Its Nasdaq-listed shares fell 30% to an all-time low on the news.
