# California's $3,500 EV Rebate Program Starts 'Later This Summer'

**Author**: Tim Levin | **Date**: Jul 13, 2026

---

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed SB 168, creating a new $3,500 EV rebate program to partially replace the expired federal $7,500 EV tax credit. The Governor's office says the incentive will become available "later this summer," with participating automakers to be announced next month.

New EVs with an MSRP up to $50,000 qualify for a $3,500 credit; used EVs sold for $25,000 or less qualify for $1,750. California has allocated $135.5 million, matched by automakers, for total funding of about $270 million. The rebate will be an upfront discount rather than a tax credit claimed later.

The program is less restrictive than the old federal credit — it doesn't enforce income caps or domestic-battery rules — but any EV manufacturer headquartered in California (such as Rivian and Lucid) is exempt from the price caps. Tesla, which moved its HQ to Texas, would not qualify for that exemption. The rebate is limited to first-time EV buyers.
