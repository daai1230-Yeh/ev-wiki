# New York Port Authority launches $45m electric truck programme

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 04.09.2026

---

The Port Authority of New York and New Jersey launched a $45M program with CALSTART to electrify drayage trucks and terminal tractors at its marine terminals — the East Coast's busiest port. It splits into a $39M Clean Truck Incentive program offering point-of-sale vouchers for vehicles and charging infrastructure, and a $5M Green Drayage Accelerator funding up to five electric-truck charging hubs within 10 miles of the terminals. CALSTART will build tracking dashboards for deployment and charger utilization, handing full control to the Port Authority by 2028. The initiative supports the Port Authority's net-zero-by-2050 goal and is funded through a $451M EPA Clean Ports Program grant that also covers electric cargo-handling equipment and vessel shore power. It follows APM Terminals' recent order of 96 electric yard trucks and 34 electric gantry cranes for the same ports.
