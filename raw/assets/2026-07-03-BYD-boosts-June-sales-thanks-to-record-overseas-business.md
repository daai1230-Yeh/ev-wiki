# BYD boosts June sales thanks to record overseas business

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-07-02 13:30

---

BYD sold 403,472 all-electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles worldwide in June, up 5.5 per cent year-on-year and 5.2 per cent versus May — the second consecutive month of growth. While exports reached new record highs, the domestic Chinese market remained significantly below the previous year's level.

In China, BYD sold 228,123 "new energy vehicles" in June, a 22 per cent year-on-year decline. Overseas sales hit a new record of 175,349 vehicles, up nearly 95 per cent year-on-year, with international markets accounting for 43 per cent of total NEV sales for a second straight month. Sales of BEVs fell 2.6 per cent to 201,472 units, offset by a 14.7 per cent rise in plug-in hybrids to 195,820 vehicles. Electrified commercial vehicles rose 24.7 per cent to 6,180 units.

For Q2 overall, BYD sold 1.108 million NEVs, down 3.2 per cent year-on-year but up more than 58 per cent versus Q1, with overseas sales climbing nearly 47 per cent quarter-on-quarter to 471,091 vehicles. First-half sales totalled 1.81 million vehicles, 15.7 per cent below last year, as a nearly 40 per cent domestic decline was offset by a 70 per cent-plus jump in international sales to 792,256 vehicles — almost 44 per cent of global H1 sales. According to CnEVPost, the recovery may be linked to BYD's rollout of its second-generation Blade Battery, after restructuring caused bottlenecks and longer delivery times for several high-volume models. The company is also expanding its premium lineup, having launched its first D-segment SUV, the Da Tang, in mid-June.
