  1. How long are you willing to wait when you refuel or recharge a car? What would you do during that time?

    你在網路上買東西時，會想到把包裹送來的那輛卡車嗎？
  3. Where does the electricity in your home come from, and do you think the grid in your area is reliable?

2. Read Aloud6 min　朗讀短文

Charging at Megawatt Speed　百萬瓦級的充電速度

A normal home charger delivers about seven kilowatts. The newest fast chargers are now measured in megawatts, and this week two very different stories showed where that race is heading: heavy trucks in Europe and passenger cars in China.
一般家用充電器約提供 7 瓩的功率。最新的快充設備已經以「百萬瓦」為單位計算。本週兩則截然不同的新聞顯示了這場競賽的方向：歐洲的重型卡車，以及中國的乘用車。
A new study by Fraunhofer ISI and the ICCT estimates that charging electric trucks in Europe could require around 100 terawatt-hours of electricity a year by 2035, and roughly 200 terawatt-hours by 2045. Germany, France, the UK, Spain and Italy would see the highest demand.
Fraunhofer ISI 與 ICCT 的新研究估計，歐洲電動卡車的充電用電量到 2035 年每年可能達約 100 太瓦時，2045 年約 200 太瓦時。德國、法國、英國、西班牙與義大利的需求最高。
Most of that energy will be delivered quietly at company depots, which account for 70 to 80 percent of demand in most countries. But the rest is highly concentrated. In many countries, just one percent of locations handle a quarter to a third of all truck charging, and some hotspots could see peak loads of 20 to 50 megawatts.
大部分電力會在企業車庫中默默完成，在多數國家占需求的 70% 到 80%。但其餘部分高度集中：在許多國家，僅 1% 的地點就承擔了四分之一到三分之一的卡車充電量，部分熱點的尖峰負載可能達 20 到 50 百萬瓦。
Germany is already planning for this. Its state-backed motorway network is set to include about 1,800 megawatt charging points and 2,400 standard fast chargers. One service area on the A2, Garbsen Nord, is planned with a grid connection of almost 25 megavolt-amperes, roughly what a small town might use.
德國已經在做準備。其國家主導的高速公路充電網預計包含約 1,800 個百萬瓦級充電點與 2,400 個一般快充樁。A2 公路上的 Garbsen Nord 服務區規劃的電網連接容量將近 25 百萬伏安，大約是一座小鎮的用電規模。
Meanwhile in China, Geely has unveiled a charging station with a peak output of 2,250 kilowatts, beating the 1,500 kilowatts of BYD's Flash Charging system. Paired with a battery that accepts a 12C charging rate, test cars went from 10 to 70 percent in four and a half minutes.
另一方面在中國，吉利發表了峰值功率 2,250 瓩的充電站，超越比亞迪「閃充」系統的 1,500 瓩。搭配可承受 12C 充電倍率的電池，測試車從 10% 充到 70% 只花了四分半鐘。
Speed creates heat, so Geely uses an AI model to predict battery temperature 30 seconds ahead and aims to keep the average below 55 degrees Celsius. The competition is also about scale: BYD had built 10,000 flash-charging stations by the end of August and wants 20,000 by the end of this year.
速度會產生熱，因此吉利用 AI 模型提前 30 秒預測電池溫度，目標是把平均溫度控制在攝氏 55 度以下。競爭也在於規模：比亞迪到 8 月底已建成 1 萬座閃充站，目標年底達到 2 萬座。
Supporters say megawatt charging finally removes the last excuse for not going electric. Critics reply that a few ultra-fast sites can strain local grids, and that most drivers and fleets would be better served by cheaper, slower charging where vehicles already park. The real question may be not how fast we can charge, but where the power will come from.
支持者說，百萬瓦級充電終於消除了不改用電動車的最後藉口。批評者則認為，少數超快充站點可能讓地方電網吃緊，而多數駕駛與車隊其實更適合在車輛原本停放的地方使用較便宜、較慢的充電。真正的問題也許不是能充多快，而是電從哪裡來。

5. Useful Expressions3 min　實用句型

This week, two very different stories showed … — This week, two very different stories showed how fast charging is evolving.
「本週兩則截然不同的新聞顯示……」——用來開場、串連兩個例子。
Most of … happens quietly, but the rest is highly concentrated. — Most charging happens quietly at depots, but the rest is highly concentrated.
「大部分……默默發生，但其餘部分高度集中。」——對比「普遍」與「集中」。
That's roughly what … would use. — Twenty-five megavolt-amperes is roughly what a small town would use.
「那大約相當於……的用量。」——用生活化比喻讓數字更好懂。
Supporters say …; critics reply that … — Supporters say it removes range anxiety; critics reply that it strains the grid.
「支持者說……；批評者則回應……」——並陳正反兩方意見。
The real question may be not … but … — The real question may be not how fast we charge, but who pays for the grid.
「真正的問題也許不是……而是……」——把討論帶到更深的層次。

6. Discussion5 min　討論題

  1. Geely's cars charge from 10 to 70 percent in four and a half minutes. Is that fast enough to change your mind about EVs, or was charging time never the real issue?
    吉利的車 4 分半就能從 10% 充到 70%。這個速度足以改變你對電動車的看法嗎？還是充電時間從來都不是真正的問題？
  2. Some argue the megawatt race is mostly marketing, since few drivers need it. Others say it is essential for people without home charging. Which side do you agree with?
    有人認為百萬瓦競賽主要是行銷，因為很少駕駛真的需要；也有人說對家裡無法充電的人來說它不可或缺。你支持哪一方？
  3. One percent of locations could handle a third of all truck charging. Who should pay for the grid upgrades at those hotspots: governments, utilities, or the charging companies?
    1% 的地點可能承擔三分之一的卡車充電量。這些熱點的電網升級該由誰付費——政府、電力公司，還是充電業者？
  4. Why do you think depot charging is expected to remain the backbone for trucks, even as public chargers get faster?
    即使公共充電越來越快，為什麼車庫充電仍被認為是卡車充電的骨幹？
  5. Charging very fast creates heat and may shorten battery life. Would you pay extra for a battery designed for ultra-fast charging?
    超快充會產生熱，也可能縮短電池壽命。你願意多付錢買專為超快充設計的電池嗎？
  6. Could megawatt truck charging work in Taiwan, where distances are short and the grid is already under pressure? What would you do first?
    在距離短、電網已承壓的台灣，百萬瓦級卡車充電可行嗎？你會先做什麼？

7. Wrap-Up2 min　總結

60-second summary 一分鐘摘要：

Charging is moving into the megawatt era. In Europe, a new study says electric trucks could need 200 terawatt-hours a year by 2045, mostly at depots, but with intense hotspots along freight corridors that may draw up to 50 megawatts. In China, Geely's 2,250-kilowatt station can add most of a battery's charge in under five minutes, while BYD races to build 20,000 flash chargers. Both stories point to the same challenge: faster charging only works if the grid can keep up.
充電正進入百萬瓦時代。在歐洲，新研究指出電動卡車到 2045 年每年可能需要 200 太瓦時，主要在車庫充電，但貨運走廊上會出現最高達 50 百萬瓦的密集熱點。在中國，吉利 2,250 瓩的充電站能在 5 分鐘內補足大部分電量，比亞迪則全力要建成 2 萬座閃充站。兩則新聞指向同一挑戰：電網跟得上，快充才有意義。

Your position 表達立場的句型：

"In my view, the winner of the charging race won't be the fastest plug, but the country that plans its grid early enough to power it."
「在我看來，充電競賽的贏家不會是最快的插頭，而是最早規劃好電網、能供得起電的國家。」
