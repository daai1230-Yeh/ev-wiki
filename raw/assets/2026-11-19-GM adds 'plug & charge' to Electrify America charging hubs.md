# GM adds 'plug & charge' to Electrify America charging hubs

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-11-18

---

EV drivers enrolled in GM's Energy Pass service now have access to Plug & Charge at participating Electrify America fast-charging hubs in the US. Compatible vehicles start charging simply by connecting the cable — without an app, touchscreen or payment card.

Plug & Charge is now available for GM EVs with native CCS ports; models with native NACS ports are expected to receive it in early 2027.

Once connected, car and charger automatically authenticate the session. The cost is billed to the payment method registered with Energy Pass, with session details and receipts in the myChevrolet, myGMC or myCadillac apps. According to GM testing, energy flow begins within about 45 seconds.

Drivers must first enrol in Energy Pass via one of the GM apps, add a payment method and complete setup. App-started sessions remain available.

"Electrify America has been a pioneer in making public charging more seamless... It removes friction from the charging experience," said Robert Barrosa, president and CEO of Electrify America.

In January, GM integrated more than 6,200 Electrify America fast-charging points into its vehicle apps. In June, GM launched Energy Pass to access multiple charging networks under one service; supported networks account for around 60% of US DC fast chargers.
