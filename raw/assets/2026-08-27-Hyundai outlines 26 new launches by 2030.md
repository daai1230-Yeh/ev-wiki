# Hyundai outlines 26 new launches by 2030

**Date**: 26.08.2026

---

Hyundai Motor Company plans to launch or refresh 26 models in India by 2030 as part of a wider global product offensive that will see the automaker introduce or update more than 100 vehicles worldwide. The company announced the plans at its 2026 CEO Investor Day.

Hyundai will add 26 launches in India by 2030, including an all-new electric SUV in the fourth quarter and a new internal combustion engine (ICE)-powered mid-size SUV. The new electric SUV will be localised and designed for the Indian market, featuring a next-generation infotainment system and Level 2 assisted driving technology.

Hyundai plans to add 320,000 units of additional capacity in India as part of its global expansion plans. Hyundai aims to export around 30 per cent of its India production volume by 2030 to markets across the Middle East, Africa, Asia and South America.

Globally, the company will launch or refresh more than 100 vehicles by 2030, with 58 launches planned in North America, 49 in Korea, 41 in Europe, 26 in India and 22 in China.
