# Genesis' First Performance Model Is Here. It Costs $71,495

**Source**: InsideEVs | **Date**: 2026-08-01

---

The 2027 Genesis GV60 Magma is finally here, and it can be had for $71,495, including delivery. It's the brand's first-ever performance model, designed to fight Mercedes-AMGs and BMW Ms, and — as expected from Genesis' EV lineup — it's electric.

The 2027 Genesis GV60 Magma took its time getting here, having been unveiled as a working prototype in 2024 and slated for a production reveal in 2025. For a little over $70,000, Genesis promises "exhilarating performance capabilities" — for $10,000 less, Hyundai's recently discounted Ioniq 5 N delivers similar power, virtual gear shifts, and a drift mode, though Genesis offers a nicer interior and dealership experience as a luxury brand.

The GV60 Magma packs a dual-motor setup making 600 horsepower for daily driving, and up to 641 hp and 583 lb-ft of torque with Boost Mode enabled. Like the Hyundai Ioniq 5 N and the discontinued-in-America Kia EV6 GT, it gets a virtual transmission that recreates the feel of gear shifts through simulated jolts, plus an electronic limited-slip differential for improved handling.

The simulated shifting pairs with an e-Active Sound Plus (e-ASD+) system that pumps synthetic engine sounds through external speakers, with driving sounds inspired by the Goodwood Festival of Speed. A Drift Mode makes it easy to break the rear tires loose, while Boost Mode enables a 0-60 mph time of under four seconds.

Outside, the Magma gets aerodynamic canards, fender garnishes and breathers, a winglet-style rear spoiler, and exclusive 21-inch wheels. Inside are Genesis' first performance bucket seats, a Magma-exclusive steering wheel with dedicated Boost and Magma Mode buttons, alloy pedals, and a special digital gauge cluster.

The GV60 Magma is powered by an 84-kWh battery that can charge from 10-80% in about 20 minutes on its 800-volt architecture, with range and charging figures expected to be similar to the Ioniq 5 N's (221 miles EPA-estimated range, 10-80% in as little as 18 minutes). It arrives at select dealerships in California, New York, and New Jersey in the coming weeks.
