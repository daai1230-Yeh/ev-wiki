# The 2027 Porsche Cayenne EV Can Open Its Doors For You At The Press Of A Button

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: Sep 16, 2026, 8:00am ET

---

Just like a Rolls-Royce, Porsche's flagship SUV makes one of the most mundane tasks redundant—if you're willing to pay extra.

It's been less than a year since Porsche debuted the first-ever electric Cayenne, but that hasn't stopped the German automaker from giving its flagship SUV a few improvements.

The 2027 Porsche Cayenne Electric, available to order now in the United States, can be specced with a few new optional features that make it stand even higher in the luxury car hierarchy. For the first time in the Cayenne's history — which goes all the way back to 2002 — the electric SUV can be optionally specced with electric doors.

All four doors can be controlled from inside or outside, through the door handles, buttons in the cabin, the curved Flow Display on the center console, or remotely via the Porsche Digital Key. The driver's door also automatically closes when the seatbelt is fastened or the brake pedal is pressed.

Porsche said the system is designed to work seamlessly in pretty much any conditions, including on inclines, with a set of servo motors doing all the heavy lifting. A sensor suite monitors the area around the doors at all times and stops the doors from opening or closing if a person or an obstacle is detected. The system can also prevent the doors from opening if the exit warning feature detects an approaching vehicle.

The system, popularized by ultra-luxury automakers like Rolls-Royce, first appeared in Porsche's portfolio when the Cayenne Electric Coupe debuted four months ago, but it hasn't been available to order until now. The doors can still be opened and closed manually even if the car is fitted with electric doors.

The 2027 Porsche Cayenne Electric can also now be optioned with front massaging seats featuring a 4D audio function. Each front seat has 16 air cushions and six sound actuators in the backrests and seat cushions, with five massage programs, each with adjustable intensity levels and vibration settings. Porsche says the integrated sound actuators create a four-dimensional musical experience, though the massage function can run independently of the music function.

For 2027, the Cayenne Electric joins the Porsche Exclusive Manufaktur Paint-to-Sample program, letting owners pay more to get their car painted in pretty much any way they want, with 105 custom finishes on top of the 13 standard and optional colors. Inside, the Extended Package Interior Style — adding leather-trimmed surfaces and decorative stitching — is now available for the EV, alongside additional leather and Race-Tex options.

Starting prices and tech specs remain unchanged: the base Cayenne Electric SUV starts at $111,350, while the top-spec Cayenne Turbo Electric Coupe starts at $170,350, including destination, processing, and handling. Whatever the trim, the Cayenne EV comes with a dual-motor setup and a 108-kWh (usable) battery that can recharge from 10% to 80% in as little as 16 minutes, thanks to 800-volt architecture enabling charging power up to 400 kW. Depending on the version, driving range is between 298 and 339 miles, with power output ranging from 435 to 1,139 horsepower.
