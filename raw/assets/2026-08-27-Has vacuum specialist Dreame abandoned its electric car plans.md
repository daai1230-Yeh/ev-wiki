# Has vacuum specialist Dreame abandoned its electric car plans?

**Date**: 26.08.2026

---

According to Chinese media reports, Dreame has apparently decided to abandon its automotive project. The company has reduced the division's workforce to just a few dozen employees.

Last year, Dreame Technology announced plans to enter the luxury electric vehicle market, with its first model due to reach the market in 2027. At one point, the company employed nearly 1,000 people in its new automotive division and even considered an EV plant in Brandenburg.

Earlier this year, Dreame presented the Nebula 1 electric supercar at CES in Las Vegas. On 27 April, it followed up with the so-called Rocket Car, a further development of the CES concept featuring additional rocket propulsion. However, these vehicles will now not be produced.

According to Car News China, the remaining staff are solely responsible for "winding down operations, handling supplier debts, and processing the legal annulment of the related companies."

Interestingly, Dreame is not the first vacuum cleaner manufacturer to pursue electric vehicle ambitions. The renowned company Dyson also surprised the industry in 2017 with its own electric car project, only to abandon it in 2019.
