# ÖBB Postbus to test Otokar autonomous e-Centro at SAE Level 4 in Austria

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-08-14

---

ÖBB Postbus has selected Otokar for a pilot project involving a fully electric SAE Level 4 autonomous bus, starting at the European Forum Alpbach before a planned deployment in scheduled service in Wolfurt.

The Austrian public transport operator is introducing Otokar's Autonomous e-Centro as part of a project focused on testing automated driving technology in public transport operations. The vehicle was presented at a press conference in Vienna on 13 August and will begin pilot operation during the European Forum Alpbach 2026, scheduled from 24 August to 4 September.

The project will initially operate with a safety driver on board. Following the Alpbach pilot, ÖBB Postbus plans to deploy the autonomous electric bus at the Campus Wolfurt industrial park, where it is intended to provide a connection between companies located at the campus and the S-Bahn network.

## ÖBB Postbus and Otokar launch SAE Level 4 autonomous bus pilot

The Autonomous e-Centro is equipped with SAE Level 4 automated driving technology. At this level, the vehicle can perform the driving task autonomously within a defined operational area. ÖBB Postbus and Otokar state that Austrian legislation still requires a trained safety driver to remain on board.

The fully electric vehicle has capacity for up to 32 passengers and features a low-floor layout, barrier-free access and a wheelchair ramp.

According to ÖBB Postbus, the project is intended to provide operational experience with autonomous mobility applications on defined routes. The operator identifies regional mobility services and first- and last-mile connections among the potential fields of application to be examined.

## Autonomous e-Centro planned for scheduled operation in Wolfurt

The first operational stage will take place on a defined route during the European Forum Alpbach. After these tests, the vehicle is planned to move to Campus Wolfurt, where ÖBB Postbus intends to test it in regular scheduled operation connecting the industrial park with the S-Bahn.

The Austrian project follows an autonomous e-Centro deployment in Madrid, where the manufacturer says the vehicle has already entered regular operation.

Otokar is supplying the vehicle and autonomous driving technology, describing the system as an integrated solution based on its own engineering and proprietary software.

ÖBB CEO Andreas Matthä said: "Autonomous driving is no longer a vision of the future, but a technology with great potential for public transport. Our aim is to actively help shape this development and gain experience at an early stage."
