# Electric BMW i3 shines with 900 km range

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-30

---

BMW has unveiled its new 3 Series - which for the first time includes an electric sedan, the i3. Based on the Neue Klasse platform, the i3 shares its technical toolkit with the iX3 SUV but pulls ahead in terms of range. While the Munich-based automaker is calling for a starting price of €59,900, those who want the full 900 kilometers will pay at least €65,900.

Today marks the premiere of the new BMW i3, the first all-electric BMW 3 Series. It is pivotal for BMW, as the electric saloon represents the scaling of the Neue Klasse: following the iX3 electric SUV, the i3 is the second representative of BMW's new platform generation, including 800-volt architecture. The model rolls off the production line at the main plant in Munich, where series production began in August.

Since June, a "First Edition" has been available to order – from €75,340. BMW has also released the price for the i3 50 xDrive (from €65,900).

## Two all-electric variants, all-wheel drive only for now

The i3 40 xDrive and i3 50 xDrive will be joined by mild-hybrid combustion variants and, from 2027, a plug-in hybrid. Dimensions are 4.76 x 1.87 x 1.48 m, wheelbase 2.90 m. The drivetrain uses a 240 kW current-excited synchronous rear motor and a 123 kW asynchronous front motor, 800-volt technology and an NMC battery with round cells.

The i3 50 xDrive: 345 kW, 645 Nm, 108.7 kWh net battery, 0-100 km/h in 4.7 s, WLTP range 912 km (over 100 km more than the iX3).

## Charging

DC charging up to 400 kW; 10–80 per cent in 21 minutes. AC 11 kW / 22 kW. Bidirectional charging: V2L 3.7 kW, V2H and V2G 11 kW.

The i3 40 xDrive (from €59,900): 195 kW rear + 123 kW front = 275 kW, 82.8 kWh net battery, up to 710 km range, 300 kW DC, also 21 minutes for 10–80 per cent.

An "extremely high-performance" M version, the i3 M60 xDrive, is set for 2027. Entry-level versions are likely to follow.

## Design and interior

The design stays close to the "Vision Neue Klasse" study (IAA 2023), with the "four-eyed face" headlights and horizontal taillights. Flush door handles, 19–21 inch wheels, new Blue Metallic paint. Inside: minimal design, central touchscreen, BMW Panoramic iDrive, voice interaction with Amazon Alexa+ integration, panoramic roof, 420 l boot and 31 l frunk, towing capacity 1.8 t braked.

## Driver assistance

SAE Level 2 systems incl. the Symbiotic Drive motorway assistant with hands-free driving up to 130 km/h; urban partially automated functions to follow next year.

The i3 is built in Munich, which will become the group's second pure EV plant after Debrecen, Hungary; combustion production is being phased out in just over a year.
