# Mercedes-Benz VLE production finalises in China

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-11-18

---

Three months after applying for local sales approval, the first series-production vehicles of the new electric VLE van are rolling off the line at Mercedes-Benz's Chinese plant, setting the stage for the market launch just before the public premiere at the Guangzhou Auto Show.

The all-electric successor to the EQV is manufactured at the Fujian-Benz plant in south-eastern China, a joint venture between Mercedes-Benz, BAIC and the Fujian Motor Industry Group, which already produces the V-Class and Vito.

The plant, running on 100% renewable energy, will assemble additional series based on the new electric van architecture VAN.EA, sourcing around 60% of components from local suppliers.

At launch in China, the VLE offers two powertrains: the front-wheel-drive VLE 300 (120 kW continuous, 203 kW peak) and the all-wheel-drive VLE 400 with an additional rear motor (50 kW nominal, 105 kW peak) for 305 kW total system output. Both are limited to 180 km/h.

NMC batteries use cells from a CATL–Mercedes-Benz joint venture; Fujian CATL handles final pack assembly. Battery capacities and ranges for Chinese versions have not been disclosed.

At 5.31 m long, 2 m wide, 1.92 m tall with a 3.34 m wheelbase, the VLE is a large luxury MPV with air suspension and rear-axle steering, seating up to seven in a 2+2+3 layout. It weighs 3,030–3,220 kg, with a gross weight of 3,780 kg.

## European version in production since June 2026

Production for Europe has been underway since June 2026 at the Vitoria plant in Spain. With 800-volt technology, European models charge at up to 300 kW DC — up to 355 km (WLTP) in 15 minutes. With a maximum range of around 700 km, trips of nearly 1,000 km are possible with one short stop.

In Germany, the VLE starts at approximately €71,530. China pricing is expected at the trade fair premiere at the end of November.
