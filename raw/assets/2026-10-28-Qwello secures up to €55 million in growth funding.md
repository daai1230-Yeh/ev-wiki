# Qwello secures up to €55 million in growth funding

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

Munich-based charging provider Qwello has secured up to €55 million from the ABN AMRO Sustainable Impact Fund and existing shareholders to fund expansion of its public charging network in European cities and targeted acquisitions.

Qwello operates over 19,500 charging points in more than 800 municipalities across Denmark, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Poland, Sweden, Spain and the UK. It just acquired a majority stake in Dutch specialist Groen-Eco, broadening a business previously focused on public concessions to private and semi-public sites and adding over 1,400 charging points. Over the next ten years Qwello plans to significantly expand, prioritising densely populated urban and residential areas, and will keep pursuing acquisitions that fit its model.

CEO Henrik Thiele said the investment reflects the confidence of long-standing financial partners (Tiger Infrastructure since 2021, ABN AMRO since 2024) and positions Qwello to pursue acquisitions and accelerate network growth. Gaetano Giuffré of the ABN AMRO Sustainable Impact Fund said its investment of up to €25 million alongside a similar amount from Tiger Infrastructure and other shareholders reflects confidence in Qwello's model across eight European markets.

This year Qwello won a contract for around 230 new charging points at 65 locations in the Harburg district (DE), where it operates the "Harburg Model" with Vattenfall and Wirelane. In its hometown of Munich it has faced obstacles for years, having sued over earlier city tenders; after courts identified flaws, Munich is preparing a new concession tender.
