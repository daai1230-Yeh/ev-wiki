# Ebusco receives further €5.86 million support from Gotion (but needs additional short-term funding)

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-09

---

Ebusco has secured €5.86 million in production support from shareholder Gotion to complete its remaining Rouen bus order, but warns that additional short-term funding is required to sustain its operations.

The Dutch electric bus manufacturer announced the development on 9 October 2026, ahead of its Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders the same day. The support is restricted to materials and manufacturing costs and cannot cover operating expenses. Ebusco is discussing additional funding with shareholders but has no binding commitment.

Ebusco reported 19 buses delivered in the first nine months of 2026, an order book of 195 vehicles at 30 September and a further 173 buses in ongoing tenders. Workforce is down to 230 FTE; talks on potential strategic transactions involving its bus operations continue. The company has moved from in-house manufacturing to an Original Equipment Designer (OED) model with outsourced production. In June 2026 it reported an order book of 225 buses.

## Gotion allocates €5.86 million to the Rouen order

Gotion, a strategic partner and shareholder since late 2024, approved the support through a Gotion group company, paid directly to suppliers and contract manufacturers. It is dedicated exclusively to materials and manufacturing for the French order (a 2022 contract for the Rouen BRT network).

Ebusco states it "requires significant additional short-term funding", has no binding commitment so far, and that available liquidity covers operations for a short period only. A previously announced ~€30 million Letter of Credit facility with an Asian partner, backed by a Gotion corporate guarantee, can only be implemented with a concrete new bus order, remains subject to Gotion's internal approval, and has no completion date. Without funding in the coming weeks there would be material uncertainty about Ebusco's ability to continue as a going concern.

## Q3 2026 figures

- 3 buses delivered in Q3; 19 year to date (123 in 2025, 157 in 2024; net loss of €71 million for 2025).
- Order book at 30 Sept 2026: 195 buses (77 firm, 118 call-off), versus 225 at end of May (107 firm, 118 call-off), so firm orders fell by 30.
- Pipeline: 173 buses in ongoing tenders, separate from the order book.
- Workforce: 248 FTE at 30 June to 230 at 30 Sept (-18, 7.3%); it was 522 at end of 2024 and 282 at end of 2025.
