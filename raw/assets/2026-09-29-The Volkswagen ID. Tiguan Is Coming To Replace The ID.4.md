# The Volkswagen ID. Tiguan Is Coming To Replace The ID.4

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 2026-09-28

---

The Volkswagen ID.4 is officially being replaced by a new electric crossover named ID. Tiguan, which is also coming to the U.S. (the ID.4 was recently discontinued there). Six years after its introduction, VW is retiring the ID.4 name. The ID. Tiguan will be revealed early next month, with first European deliveries in early 2027. A company representative said the ID. Tiguan is planned to succeed the ID.4 in North America, with timing to be shared after the European launch.

VW's Chattanooga factory stopped assembling U.S.-spec ID.4s in April to make room for gas-powered SUVs; VW is offering discounts of up to $12,500 on remaining ID.4 inventory.

The original ID.4 suffered software issues (since ironed out), a lack of physical controls and an electronic door latch recall that prompted a stop sale. The ID. Tiguan uses the same underpinnings but is set to get the interior treatment of the ID.3 Neo and ID. Polo: physical buttons for windows, volume and climate, plus VW's latest Android Automotive OS-based infotainment with native apps. The exterior gets a comprehensive overhaul with a new light signature.

Powertrain details are not yet known; if VW follows the ID.3 Neo route, expect slightly larger batteries, faster charging and more powerful motors. For context, the outgoing 2026 ID.4 offers 282 hp (single rear motor) or 335 hp (dual-motor AWD), a 77 kWh usable battery, and 291 miles (RWD) / 263 miles (AWD) of range.
