# Škoda introduces Epiq Sportline with Up to 440 km range

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-10-14

---

Škoda is expanding the Epiq lineup with a Sportline version, available in Germany as the Epiq 40 and Epiq 55, with up to 155 kW and 440 km of range. Orders open at the end of October.

The Epiq 40 has a 38.5 kWh gross (37 kWh net) battery, 99 kW and 267 Nm; range exceeds 310 km and 10–80% charging takes 23 minutes. The Epiq 55 has 55 kWh gross (52 kWh net), 155 kW and 290 Nm, accelerates 0–100 km/h in 7.1 s, reaches up to 440 km and charges 10–80% in 24 minutes. Top speed is 160 km/h for both.

The Sportline uses the drivetrains of the standard series. The Epiq 40 starts at €27,900 in Germany and the Epiq 55 at €32,100; an entry-level Epiq 35 (85 kW) can be ordered from October for €25,900. Sportline pricing has not been announced.

Visually the Sportline has black accents (glossy black lettering, roof rails, mirrors), standard 19-inch wheels (20-inch optional) and is the only Epiq with a black contrast roof. Standard equipment includes Matrix LED headlights, electrically adjustable sports seats with microfibre, a heated sports steering wheel with regeneration paddles, dual-zone climate control, parking sensors, rear-view camera, keyless entry, 15 W inductive charging and, in Germany, the Travel Package (Travel Assist 3.0, navigation, Škoda Connect Premium). A Premium Package adds a 360-degree camera, Canton sound, a 25-litre frunk and remote/trained parking.

The 4.17 m Epiq is part of the VW Group's "Electric Urban Car Family" alongside the VW ID. Polo, ID. Cross and Cupra Raval, based on MEB+. The Epiq 35 is already available in Austria.
