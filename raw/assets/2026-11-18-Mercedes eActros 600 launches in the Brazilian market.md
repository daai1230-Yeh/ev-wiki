# Mercedes eActros 600 launches in the Brazilian market

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-17

---

Mercedes-Benz Trucks is bringing its all-electric eActros 600 truck to the Brazilian market for the first time. Among the first transport companies to deploy the vehicles are fleet customers Bravo Serviços Logísticos, Reiter Log and Ever Express.

Mercedes-Benz do Brasil launched a customer programme last year to test the practical suitability of battery-electric trucks in Brazil under real-world conditions, beginning with the eActros 300 and 400 models. Now, the commercial vehicle manufacturer is expanding its electric line-up with its flagship truck: the long-haul eActros 600, which has been available in Europe since late 2024, is now entering the South American market. The first 70 units of the eActros 600 are being offered as a special edition to mark the 70th anniversary of Mercedes-Benz do Brasil.

This month, the vehicle made its Brazilian debut at the Fenatran 2026 trade fair in São Paulo. Daimler Truck describes Brazil as a key market for its global commercial vehicle business. The country is not only a major sales market but also a production hub for the group: since 1956 – 70 years ago – Mercedes-Benz do Brasil has been manufacturing a variety of commercial vehicles locally.

In the aforementioned customer testing programme, eActros 300 and 400 trucks recently completed around 200,000 kilometres with ten Brazilian customers across various transport applications – ‘proving their suitability for distribution, logistics, and agricultural transport operations‘, as the manufacturer states. This paves the way for the eActros 600, which, with a gross combination weight of up to 44 tonnes and a battery capacity of 621 kWh, offers a range of up to 560 kilometres. Among the first logistics companies to add the new model to their fleets are long-standing customers Bravo Serviços Logísticos, Reiter Log and Ever Express.

Achim Puchert, CEO of Mercedes-Benz Trucks, comments: “For 70 years, Mercedes-Benz do Brasil has contributed to the development of the country. Today, Brazil is one of the most important markets for Mercedes-Benz Trucks worldwide. With the launch of the eActros 600, we are taking the next decisive step toward the decarbonization of transport. At the same time, we remain committed to an approach that is fundamental to us: shaping the transformation together with our customers and focusing consistently on their real-world requirements. That is exactly what the eActros 600 stands for.”
