# Iveco delivers 47 electric midi-buses to Sardinia

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-07-02

---

The Italian public transport operator CTM Cagliari has received 47 units of the new electric Iveco E-Way — each 10.7 meters long, making these vehicles midi-buses. The e-buses were procured under Italy's National Recovery and Resilience Plan (NRRP).

The new electric buses are now operating on the heavily used urban routes 8, 20, 30R, and 1Q in Cagliari, a port city in southern Sardinia and the island's capital. At 10.7 metres in length and 2.33 metres in width, the operator says the Iveco E-Way is highly manoeuvrable in its midi-bus configuration on hard-to-access routes, particularly narrow city centre streets, making it well-suited to Cagliari's operational needs. The new e-buses are equipped with NMC batteries with a total capacity of 350kWh, enabling a range of up to 350km on a single charge.

With the arrival of the 47 Iveco e-buses, CTM Cagliari has completed its procurement campaign under the NRRP and concluded the city's largest fleet renewal project to date. The entire investment programme amounts to approximately €92 million, allocated to CTM to support its ecological transition.

Thanks to this funding, CTM Cagliari has achieved a 70 per cent electric share in its bus fleet and aims to operate only zero-emission vehicles by 2030 — well ahead of EU deadlines. CTM Cagliari's fleet now comprises a total of 236 e-buses, following an earlier order of 98 e-buses from Polish manufacturer Solaris three years ago. Looking ahead, the city of Cagliari and CTM hope to further improve the bus system's quality, increase travel distances, strengthen connections, and extend services to previously underserved municipalities in the hinterland as part of upcoming public transport reforms.
