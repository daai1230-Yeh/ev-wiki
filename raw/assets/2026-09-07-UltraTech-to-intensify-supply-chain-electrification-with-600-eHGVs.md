# UltraTech to intensify supply chain electrification with 600+ eHGVs

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 04.09.2026

---

UltraTech Cement — the largest cement producer outside China — plans to deploy over 600 new electric heavy trucks by December 2026 across Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, and Odisha, moving over 5 million tonnes of clinker and materials annually. The company projects net CO2 cuts of 117,000+ tonnes/year (equivalent to 39 million liters of diesel) and has signed service contracts with Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, IPLTech, Energy In Motion, and Sany. UltraTech says it already runs 850+ "green logistics" trucks (CNG and electric combined) and calls itself the first cement company to deploy eHGVs at scale for long-haul clinker transport, building on a June rollout of 45 electric trucks that brought its e-truck fleet to 89 units at the time.
