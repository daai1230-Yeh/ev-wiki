# Volgren delivers 95 electric buses to Transport for NSW

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-11-18

---

Australian bus manufacturer Volgren has completed delivery of 95 battery-electric buses to Transport for NSW. The fleet was delivered in just seven months and will be operated by Transit Systems Australia from the Leichhardt depot in Sydney.

The buses combine Volgren's locally manufactured Optimus Electric body with a BYD electric chassis. The first vehicles entered service in April.

Volgren described the delivery as a milestone that marked the production of its 500th zero-emission bus. "A key part of Volgren's strategy has been working closely with global chassis manufacturers who are leaders in electric drivetrains, battery systems, and high-voltage technology," said CEO Thiago Deiro.

The Leichhardt depot has already been upgraded with more than 40 chargers, rooftop solar and battery storage. A further phase is expected to add around 77 charging points and remove diesel and gas refuelling infrastructure. It is one of 11 Sydney depots being converted for electric buses.

By the end of 2026, Volgren expects more than 600 zero-emission buses with its bodies to be operating across Australia, including battery-electric and hydrogen fuel-cell models on chassis from several international manufacturers.
