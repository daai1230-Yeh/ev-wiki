# Hanoi deploys 53 new VinFast electric buses on four routes, targeting full fleet conversion by 2030

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

Hanoi operator Transerco introduced 53 new VinFast electric buses on four routes on 5 and 6 October 2026, replacing diesel vehicles as the city pursues full conversion of its public bus fleet to electric and green-energy vehicles by 2030. The deployment comprises 28 VinFast Green Bus vehicles on routes 22A and 22B (5 October, Cau Buou Bus Enterprise) and 25 VinFast EB8 buses on routes 30 and 50 (6 October, South Hanoi Passenger Transport Enterprise). All were built in 2026, carry 60 passengers, have low floors and accessibility equipment, and wear the green Hanoibus livery. Transerco aims to exceed 51% green-converted vehicles by end-2026.

Hanoi's municipal programme, revised in May 2025, sets a 2030 deadline to replace all petrol and diesel buses, with all buses inside Ring Road 4 required to be electric. The second phase (2027–2030) replaces the remaining vehicles progressively. The 2030 deadline is 20 years ahead of the national 2050 target under Decision No. 876/QD-TTg of 22 July 2022.

Electric bus operations in Hanoi began in December 2021; the city's e-buses carried nearly 100 million passengers in about three years (figures published by Vietnam's Ministry of Construction in 2025). During Sustainable Bus's May 2026 visit, about 2,000 electric buses were reported in the city, around 600 run by VinFast's sister company VinBus, whose fleet had accumulated about 73 million km by the end of 2025.

The EB8 is part of VinFast's international programme: the EB8 and EB12 for Europe were shown at Busworld Europe 2025 and European-spec versions were under development this spring. The plant reports annual bus capacity of 3,000 units, and VinFast is also targeting the US transit bus market.
