# Family at heart: How Forthing aims for success in Europe

**Author**: Robin Engelhardt | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

*Condensed summary of the electrive report, not verbatim.*

At its third major European show (after Paris two years ago and the Munich IAA), Dongfeng's Forthing brand showed three models for Europe, positioning itself on family-friendliness.

- **TK5 (mid-size SUV):** the most relevant for Europe. BEV with 75 kWh battery and 800 V architecture (20-80% in under 18 minutes, about 150 kW average; quoted DC peak only 160 kW, probably an average-power figure); PHEV with a 39 kWh battery. Electrically adjustable seats even in row two; front seats heated, ventilated, with massage.
- **TK7:** about 29 cm longer, PHEV only, up to seven seats. The show car had an unusual 2-2-3 layout (individual front and middle seats, three-seat rear bench) so passengers can walk through to the third row.
- **V9:** large PHEV with four- or seven-seat versions. The four-seater is a campervan (sink, shower curtain, fridge, extra storage); both have a roof tent, awning, 6 kW V2L, and a 'double-decker' bed option.

Forthing has been in Europe since 2022 and sold over 10,000 vehicles by 2025; it is present in Germany and Italy, with France, Norway and Denmark to follow. TK5 and TK7 launch in the coming months; pricing is not yet announced.
