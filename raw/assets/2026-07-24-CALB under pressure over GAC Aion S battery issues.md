# CALB under pressure over GAC Aion S battery issues

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

The Aion S, a popular electric car in China, is experiencing growing issues with LFP battery cells — including swollen cells, leaks, insulation faults, range losses and, in some cases, power losses while driving. Complaints have risen since the start of the year, particularly in vehicles with 150,000–300,000km on the odometer; 182 complaints were lodged on Chinese consumer platforms within a fortnight. GAC is extending the battery warranty from eight years/150,000km to eight years/300,000km for affected vehicles, offering free inspections and repairs or replacements where needed. CALB has also announced free inspection and maintenance services. No official recall has been issued so far.
