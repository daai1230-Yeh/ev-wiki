# Spain approves Auto+ EV subsidy scheme through 2030

**Date**: 24 July 2026

---

Spain has approved the legal framework for Auto+, a €400 million EV incentive programme running until 2030 that offers up to €4,500 in purchase support for eligible electric and electrified vehicles. It replaces MOVES III with centrally managed grants and covers only vehicles carrying the country's ZERO-emissions label, aiming to cut processing times to a matter of weeks after delays plagued the previous scheme.

The programme has two pillars: Pillar 1 targets private individuals not engaged in economic activity, covering new vehicles or nearly-new models up to 12 months old already registered in Spain. Pillar 2 targets companies, the self-employed and other economic-activity buyers, covering outright purchases as well as financial leasing and rental agreements of at least three years.

Subsidy amounts: up to €4,500 for an M1 passenger car, €5,000 for an N1 electric van, €1,100 for electric motorcycles (L3e, L4e, L5e categories), and €1,500 for L6e/L7e quadricycles.
