# VW starts production of the long wheelbase ID. Buzz Cargo

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 24.11.2026 - 11:15

---

Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles has started series production of the long wheelbase ID. Buzz Cargo at its main Hanover plant. The long version of the electric transporter, which has been available to order since September, offers 4.4 cubic meters of cargo volume, an 86-kWh battery, and a range of up to 490 km.

Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles (VWN) has begun production of the long wheelbase ID. Buzz Cargo, expanding the range of models manufactured at its main plant in Hanover. The manufacturer had already announced the long version of the electric transporter in December 2025 and opened order books in September 2026. This means the commercial vehicle variant is now catching up with its passenger car counterpart, for which a long wheelbase version has been available since summer 2024.

According to VWN, integrating the additional model into ongoing production required adjustments, particularly in the manufacturing and installation of side panels and sliding doors. The company implemented many of the necessary changes internally.

“Our teams have integrated the model into the production of the current model range with only minor modifications and smart conversion solutions,” explains Stefan Depka, Head of the Hanover Plant.

At the Lower Saxony site, VWN produces all variants of the ID. Buzz range on two production lines, as well as the Multivan and, since summer 2026, the California camper van. The spectrum of all-electric models ranges from the ID. Buzz Cargo with a standard wheelbase to the five-, six-, and seven-seater passenger car variants. In the future, the ID. California Cruise, an all-electric camper van, will also roll off the production line in Hanover. Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles employs around 11,000 people at the site.

With the long wheelbase version of the ID. Buzz Cargo, VWN is responding to growing demand from commercial customers for electric transporters with greater cargo volume. Compared to the standard version, the wheelbase increases by 250 millimetres to 3.25 metres, while the overhangs remain unchanged. The total length of the vehicle thus increases from 4.71 to 4.96 metres.

As a result, the cargo floor length increases to 2,458 millimetres, and the cargo volume grows from 3.9 to 4.4 cubic metres. Additionally, the two standard sliding side doors are wider, allowing a Euro pallet to fit through the side door openings for the first time.

The long wheelbase ID. Buzz Cargo comes with a battery offering a usable energy content of 86 kWh as standard. Customers can choose between a rear-wheel-drive variant with 210 kW and an all-wheel-drive version with 250 kW. Both variants have a maximum braked towing capacity of 1,800 kg, even on a 12 per cent gradient. The maximum payload is up to 700 kg for the rear-wheel-drive model and up to 606 kg for the all-wheel-drive version.

As a company spokesperson already stated during the order opening in September in response to an electrive inquiry, the range of the rear-wheel-drive variant is 450 to 490 km, and that of the all-wheel-drive model is 430 to 460 km. The maximum DC charging power is 200 kW for both, allowing the battery to charge from 10 to 80 per cent in 28 minutes. At AC charging points, both variants can charge at up to 11 kW.

The long wheelbase Cargo also benefits from the technology update for the ID. Buzz range introduced in April. Standard features include the latest generation of ID. Software and the new infotainment system with an expanded app store. Additional features include the optional Travel Assist, which can also recognise traffic lights within its system limits, as well as standard one-pedal driving.

VWN also offers the so-called PowerSocket for all variants of the ID. Buzz. The optional adapter connects to the vehicle’s charging interface and enables the power supply of external electrical devices. The Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) function provides a continuous output of 3.0 kW and a peak output of 3.6 kW.

“The start of production of the long-wheelbase ID. Buzz Cargo in Hanover sends a clear signal in the B2B business of the VWCV brand,” said Lars Krause, Member of the Brand Board for Sales, Marketing, and After Sales at Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles. “Our commercial customers need reliable vehicles that are tailored to the intended purpose and that prove themselves in everyday work.”

At the order opening in September, VWN communicated a starting price of €48,490 for the rear-wheel-drive variant and €50,630 for the all-wheel-drive model of the long wheelbase ID. Buzz Cargo. In its latest announcement regarding the start of production, the manufacturer only states the entry price for the entire Cargo range, i.e. €40,645 for the standard wheelbase variant. All prices are exclusive of VAT, delivery, and registration costs.
