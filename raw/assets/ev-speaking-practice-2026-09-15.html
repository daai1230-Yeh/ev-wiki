2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 · 6 min

The global EV market is moving at two very different speeds right now. In the United States, Tesla is tightening its grip even as the overall market shrinks. In China, millions of early electric cars are aging out of their warranties, and the used-car market doesn't quite know what to do with them.

目前全球電動車市場正以兩種截然不同的速度前進。在美國，特斯拉即使在整體市場萎縮之際，仍持續鞏固自己的優勢；在中國，數以百萬計的早期電動車正逐漸過了保固期，而二手車市場還不太知道該如何處理這些車。

According to the Wall Street Journal, Tesla's share of the U.S. EV market climbed to fifty-two percent by August, up from forty-three percent a year earlier. But that rise reflects resilience, not recovery: Tesla's own sales actually fell sixteen percent, to three hundred twenty-five thousand, three hundred fifty-one vehicles, while the entire U.S. EV market contracted by thirty percent.

根據《華爾街日報》報導，特斯拉在美國電動車市場的佔有率到八月已攀升至52%，高於去年同期的43%。但這樣的成長反映的是韌性，而非復甦：特斯拉自身的銷量其實下滑了16%，來到325,351輛，而整個美國電動車市場則萎縮了30%。

Tesla's position still rests heavily on a single model. The Model Y alone accounts for roughly a third of all EV purchases in the U.S. and slipped just two percent this year. Meanwhile, as federal subsidies expired, traditional automakers pulled back hard — Honda discontinued the Prologue, and Ford ended the F-150 Lightning.

特斯拉的地位其實高度依賴單一車款：光是Model Y就佔美國電動車購買量的約三分之一，且今年僅下滑2%。與此同時，隨著聯邦補貼到期，傳統車廠大舉收手——本田停產了Prologue，福特也終止了F-150 Lightning。

Halfway around the world, China is facing a very different kind of reckoning. With forty-four million electric vehicles already on its roads, China has become the first country in the world with a large, aging EV fleet — and the battery, its most expensive component, still has no standard way to measure its health.

在地球的另一端，中國正面臨另一種截然不同的考驗。中國道路上已有4,400萬輛電動車在行駛，使其成為全球第一個擁有大量「老化」電動車的國家——而電池作為電動車最昂貴的零件，卻仍然缺乏一套標準化的健康檢測方式。

A report cited by the International Energy Agency found that about eighty percent of used-car dealers in China now refuse to take in EVs older than five years, mainly over fears about battery life and replacement costs. A three-year-old EV now resells for around forty-five percent of its original price, down from nearly fifty-five percent back in 2023.

國際能源署（IEA）引述的一份報告發現，中國目前約有80%的二手車商拒絕收購車齡超過五年的電動車，主要原因是擔心電池壽命與更換成本。一輛三年車齡的電動車，現在轉售價約為原價的45%，低於2023年將近55%的水準。

Part of the problem is access to information. EVs can technically report battery health through a diagnostic port, but in China, many battery management systems are tightly encrypted, so ordinary used-car dealers can't get a reliable, legally valid report on how much a battery has degraded. Manufacturers typically offer an eight-year or one-hundred-sixty-thousand-kilometer warranty — and once that runs out, any battery failure becomes the owner's expense.

問題的一部分在於資訊取得困難。電動車理論上可以透過診斷端口回報電池健康狀況，但在中國，許多電池管理系統（BMS）都採高度加密，一般二手車商很難取得可靠、具法律效力的電池衰退報告。車廠通常提供八年或十六萬公里的保固——一旦保固期滿，電池若出現故障，費用就得由車主自行承擔。

Even with this domestic headache, China's EV exports keep surging — more than two point five million vehicles went abroad last year, double the year before, giving China fifty-five percent of all EV sales outside Europe and the U.S. Officials expect over one million tonnes of battery scrap by 2030, with some industry estimates running as high as three point five million tonnes — and no fully safe recycling system yet in place to handle it.

儘管國內面臨這樣的難題，中國的電動車出口仍持續激增——去年出口超過250萬輛，是前一年的兩倍，讓中國在歐美以外市場的電動車銷售中佔比達到55%。官方預估到2030年，電池報廢量將超過100萬噸，部分業界估計甚至可能高達350萬噸——而目前尚未建立完全安全的回收體系來處理這些廢棄電池。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型 · 3 min

"X reflects Y rather than Z."

用來釐清一個現象背後真正的原因，而非表面看起來的原因。例："This reflects resilience rather than a full recovery."

"Even though X, Y."

讓步句型，先承認一個事實，再說出相對的結果。例："Even though exports are surging, the domestic resale market is struggling."

"It remains to be seen whether..."

表示事情尚無定論、有待觀察。例："It remains to be seen whether battery health reports will ever become standardized."

"There's a growing concern that..."

用來描述一個逐漸擴大的疑慮或擔憂。例："There's a growing concern that early EV adopters will be left holding depreciating assets."

"On one hand..., but on the other hand..."

呈現一件事的兩面，方便討論利弊。例："On one hand, cheap EVs help consumers; on the other hand, they raise safety and security questions."

6. Discussion 討論題 · 5 min

  1. Tesla's U.S. market share is rising even as the overall EV market shrinks. Is that a healthy sign for the EV industry, or a warning sign?

    特斯拉在美國的市佔率上升，但整體電動車市場卻在萎縮。你認為這對電動車產業來說是健康的訊號，還是警訊？

  2. Now that federal subsidies have expired and some automakers are discontinuing EV models, should governments step back in, or should EVs compete on their own?

    既然聯邦補貼已經到期、部分車廠也停產了電動車款，你認為政府該重新介入補貼，還是該讓電動車靠自己競爭？

  3. If you were buying a five-year-old EV, what would you want to know about the battery before paying for it?

    如果你要買一輛車齡五年的電動車，你會想在付款前了解電池的哪些資訊？

  4. Is China's used-EV problem a preview of what other countries will eventually face, or is it specific to China's market conditions?

    你認為中國目前的二手電動車困境，會是其他國家未來也會遇到的情況，還是屬於中國市場獨有的問題？

  5. Battery health data is often locked inside encrypted, proprietary systems. Should regulators require carmakers to provide standardized, accessible battery health reports?

    電池健康數據常被鎖在加密且專屬的系統裡。你認為主管機關應該要求車廠提供標準化、可公開取得的電池健康報告嗎？

  6. Some people say cheap Chinese EVs flooding global markets is simply good for consumers; others call it a national security and economic risk. Which concern do you think should weigh more heavily?

    有人認為廉價的中國電動車大量進入全球市場，對消費者來說是好事；也有人認為這是國家安全與經濟上的風險。你認為哪一種考量應該更被重視？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 · 2 min

60-second summary: In your own words, summarize both stories in under a minute — Tesla's rebound inside a shrinking U.S. market, and China's battery-aging, residual-value problem in its used-EV market. Which of the two trends concerns you more, and why?

請用一分鐘以內，以自己的話總結這兩則故事——特斯拉在萎縮的美國市場中逆勢回升，以及中國電動車市場面臨的電池老化與殘值下滑問題。這兩個趨勢中，哪一個更讓你擔心？為什麼？
