# Opel prepares all-electric Corsa for 2028

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 24.08.2026

---

Opel has pushed back the launch of the next-generation Corsa from 2027 to 2028. CEO Florian Huettl confirmed the new timeline in an interview, saying: "It will arrive in 2028, be fully electric, and cost around €25,000."

It has long been known that Opel is working on an all-electric successor to the Corsa based on Stellantis' STLA Small platform. Opel CEO Florian Huettl has now confirmed in an interview with Welt am Sonntag that the company is targeting a 2028 launch. "With this model, we are helping to establish electromobility in the entry-level segment," he said.

The current Corsa is available with petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains and uses Stellantis' e-CMP platform. The next generation, however, will be offered exclusively as a battery-electric model. Huettl's comments to Welt am Sonntag confirm that strategy. While the STLA Small platform can theoretically accommodate other powertrains, Stellantis designed it primarily to drive the electrification of its brands.

The Corsa is one of Opel's most important models. It is not only the Rüsselsheim-based carmaker's best-selling vehicle but also one of Germany's most popular compact cars. Opel originally planned to launch the next generation in 2027 but has since pushed the introduction back to 2028. Huettl nevertheless sees favourable conditions for its market launch. "The range of electric vehicles is strong, the charging infrastructure is improving, and policymakers are providing targeted support for electromobility. Additionally, rising fuel prices mean that an electric car becomes cost-effective more quickly for many customers. When a litre of petrol costs €2.50, we see the impact on demand directly," Huettl explained in the interview. Opel has not, however, set a fixed date for phasing out internal combustion engines.

Whether the new Corsa will retain a similar design to the current model remains to be seen. What is clear, however, is that it will come with larger batteries and significantly more range – with prices starting at €25,000, according to Huettl. The current Opel Corsa Electric is available with a 46 or 49 kWh net NMC battery and offers a claimed range of up to 429 kilometres. List prices currently start at €30,440.

More details about what the new Corsa could offer may emerge when Peugeot unveils its first model based on the STLA Small platform: the second-generation Peugeot e-208, which is due to launch later this year.

Before then, the electric Corsa GSE will arrive at dealerships this year. The all-electric performance version of the compact car is expected to accelerate from 0 to 100 kph in just 5.5 seconds, making it faster than any current production Opel model. The hot hatch still uses the e-CMP platform.
