# Colombia's first 50 battery-electric articulated buses enter service

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 20.08.2026 - 15:30

---

Bogotá is electrifying its articulated bus fleet: 50 new electric articulated buses are joining the TransMilenio system. By 2029, the Colombian capital aims to integrate a total of 900 additional electric buses.

According to city authorities, the 50 new additions are Colombia's first battery-electric articulated buses. The vehicles accommodate up to 160 passengers and feature doors on both sides, allowing them to serve both TransMilenio network stations and bus lanes, as well as conventional roads and stops. Media reports indicate the rollout will be gradual and linked, among other factors, to the expansion of the corridor along Avenida Ciudad de Cali.

The vehicles were procured by the operators Masivo Capital and ETIB. The bodies are manufactured in Colombia: 25 units by Busscar in Pereira, and another 25 by Marcopolo in Cota. The buses feature cameras, blind-spot monitoring, real-time telemetry, digital rear-view mirrors, Wi-Fi and USB ports, plus designated spaces for wheelchairs and prams and boarding aids for passengers with limited mobility.

The new articulated buses are part of a larger electrification programme. Between 2026 and 2029, Bogotá plans to integrate 900 new battery-electric buses into its public transport system. Of these, 631 vehicles will replace older buses, while 269 battery-electric articulated buses are earmarked for a later expansion phase and the extension of the TransMilenio network towards Soacha. Including the 97 battery-electric minibuses and standard buses already added, the latest procurement round comprises 147 vehicles. Once all 900 planned e-buses are in service, TransMilenio estimates they will avoid around 57,500 tonnes of CO₂ annually.

Bogotá is following a broader Latin American trend: the region now has over 10,000 electric buses in operation. Santiago de Chile leads with 4,222 e-buses, while São Paulo commissioned 500 additional battery-electric buses in June, bringing its electric bus fleet to 1,759 vehicles including 189 trolleybuses.
