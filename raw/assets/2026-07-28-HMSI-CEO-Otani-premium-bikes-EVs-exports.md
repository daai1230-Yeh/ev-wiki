# HMSI CEO Tsutsumu Otani on premium bikes, EVs & exports

**Source**: ETAuto | **Date**: 27 July 2026

---

Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (HMSI) President & CEO Tsutsumu Otani discusses the company's strategy across premium motorcycles, electric vehicles and exports. Otani brings 26 years of Honda experience across Europe, Japan, China and South America to the role.

Under his leadership, HMSI is building a portfolio that lets buyers move from commuter motorcycles and family scooters into premium bikes, larger scooters and electric mobility without leaving the Honda brand; Yogesh Mathur has been named the new Director responsible for the Premium Motorcycle Business, alongside Sales and Marketing.

On EVs, HMSI is recalibrating its electric strategy after studying customer concerns around battery life and resale value, and plans to launch another electric scooter this year. On exports, they currently account for around 10% of HMSI's production and could potentially double in coming years, subject to Honda's global production allocation strategy and supplier capacity.
