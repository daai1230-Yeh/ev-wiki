# Americans Can't Have These Affordable Chinese EVs. But They're Coming To Canada

**Author**: Kevin Williams | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

China's Dongfeng Motor showed off a series of cars in Montreal, with plans to sell its first two starting in 2027. The brand is going through the homologation process, and the Dongfeng Nammi Box and Vigo will be the first two on sale, with a target price of under $35,000 CAD (about $25,000 USD).

Dongfeng joins BYD, Chery and Geely in bringing cheap (and not-so-cheap) EVs to Canada. A new trade agreement has reduced Canada's 100% tariff on Chinese-made cars to 6.1% for up to 49,000 cars for 2027, as the government aims to give drivers more lower-cost EV choices. At the Montreal event, the state-owned manufacturer showed seven models across its brands: the Voyah Dream (van), Voyah Free (mid-sized premium EREV crossover), Dongfeng Eπ 007 (mid-sized coupe-sedan, EV or EREV), Dongfeng Eπ 008 (mid-sized SUV, EREV only), M Hero 817 (body-on-frame PHEV SUV), the Nammi Box EV hatchback, and the Vigo EV crossover.

The Nammi Box and Vigo — to be distributed by North World Industry — are both front-motor, front-wheel-drive designs. The subcompact Box has a 94 hp motor and up to a 42.3 kWh battery for 196 miles WLTP range. The Vigo crossover (about the size of a Nissan Kicks) has a 181 hp motor and up to a 51.87 kWh battery for 211 miles WLTP. In China both models sell well under $13,000; for Canada they aim to slip under $35,000 CAD.

The author notes a crowded field — BYD, Zeekr, Chery and now Dongfeng are racing to the Canadian market, alongside Tesla's returning Shanghai-made cars — but concludes Canadian drivers win with newfound access to cheap EVs.
