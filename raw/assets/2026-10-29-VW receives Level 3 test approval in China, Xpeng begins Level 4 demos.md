# VW receives Level 3 test approval in China, Xpeng begins Level 4 demos

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-28

---

Volkswagen Group China has been granted permission to test highly automated Level 3 driving on public roads in Hefei. Through this testing, VW aims to advance development to achieve the targeted technical maturity by 2027. Meanwhile, Xpeng has received approval for demonstration drives with Level 4 robotaxis in Guangzhou.

Volkswagen has begun testing a highly automated driving assistant at SAE Level 3 in real-world traffic in Hefei and is collecting extensive data. The approval is another step towards bringing Level 3 technology to series production in 2027, using technology developed with local partner Horizon Robotics in the joint venture Carizon.

At Level 3, the vehicle can take over driving under defined conditions. The driver may take their hands off the wheel and look away from the road but must be ready to resume control when prompted, typically within ten seconds.

Local development will benefit from an in-house AI world model called GAIA 2.0, which according to Volkswagen can already perform daily simulations covering around five million kilometres, based on data from 30 million real-world traffic scenarios.

Xpeng has received approval to conduct demonstration drives with Level 4 robotaxis in Guangzhou, allowing it to test ride services across the entire city. The focus is no longer solely on technical validation but also on the processes of operating a mobility service, from order acceptance and vehicle dispatch to passenger transport and handling exceptional situations. Access will initially be rolled out gradually via invitation codes.

Xpeng does not plan to operate large robotaxi fleets itself but rather to provide vehicles, software, and AI technology and collaborate with partners, with revenue from hardware sales, technology services, and commissions. In the first half of October, Xpeng unveiled its robotaxi brand Yoyo and opened registration for the first external users in China. For international expansion, the company is already in discussions with European mobility platforms.
