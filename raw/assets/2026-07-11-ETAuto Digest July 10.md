**Private fleets, school buses to drive India's electric bus demand from FY27: Switch Mobility's Ganesh Mani** — While government procurement has been the primary catalyst for electric bus adoption so far, Switch Mobility's CEO expects private fleets and school buses to become the next growth driver.

**E25 only being tested, no rollout decision yet: Puri** — Road transport minister Nitin Gadkari said that the petroleum ministry will take a call on matters relating to E25 fuel rollout.

**JK Tyre plans additional 5%-6% price hike by September** — The tyre maker said raw material costs have risen over 20% due to geopolitical tensions, prompting phased price increases.
