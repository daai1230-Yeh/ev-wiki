# Volkswagen’s U.S. EV Sales Tumble 90%

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

*Condensed summary of the InsideEVs report, not verbatim.*

Volkswagen sold 1,421 EVs in the US in Q3 2026, a 90% drop year over year and one of the steepest of any EV maker. The ID.4 sold 989 units (-92.1% from 12,470); the ID. Buzz sold 432 (-82.5% from 2,469). Total VW US sales were 78,179 (-10.9%); EV sales for January-September were 6,107 (-77%).

The cause is the lineup: the US-spec ID.4 left production in April (the Chattanooga plant is making room for gas SUVs) and ID. Buzz imports stopped, so VW has sold only inventory (the Buzz units are 2025 models). The ID.4 was discounted by up to $12,500 to clear lots; the ID. Buzz still starts at $61,545 with 234 miles of range.

Relief is expected next year with the ID. Tiguan (front trunk, more physical controls, optional passenger screen); the ID. Buzz returns as a 2028 model with a native NACS port and better software.
