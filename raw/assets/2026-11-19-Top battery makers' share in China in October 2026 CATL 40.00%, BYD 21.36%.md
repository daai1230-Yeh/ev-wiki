CATL (HKEX: 3750) continued to lead China's power battery market in October, although its market share retreated further, while BYD (HKEX: 1211) continued to narrow the gap with the industry leader.

CATL's domestic power battery installations reached 39.60 GWh for the month, ranking first with a 40.00% share, according to data released Thursday by the China Automotive Battery Innovation Alliance (CABIA).

The company's installations rose about 11.6% month-on-month from 35.49 GWh in September, but the growth trailed the broader market, dragging its market share down by 0.93 percentage points from the previous month.

China's total power battery installations hit a record high of 99.4 GWh in October, up 18.2% year-on-year and 14.0% month-on-month, refreshing the year's high for the third consecutive month.

BYD remained second with 21.14 GWh in installations, up about 15.6% month-on-month from 18.29 GWh in September. Its market share increased by 0.26 percentage points from the previous month to 21.36%.

The market share gap between the 2 companies narrowed from 19.83 percentage points in September to 18.64 percentage points, marking the fifth consecutive month of contraction. Together, they accounted for 61.36% of the market, down from 62.01% in September.

CALB (HKEX: 3931) remained third with 8.55 GWh in installations and an 8.64% share, up 1.67 percentage points from September.

Gotion High-tech (SZSE: 002074) surpassed Eve Energy (SZSE: 300014) in October to rise to fourth place with 5.99 GWh in installations, seeing its share increase by 0.44 percentage points to 6.05%.

Eve Energy slipped to fifth place with 5.48 GWh in installations, with its share down 0.26 percentage points to 5.54%.

The top 5 manufacturers combined accounted for 81.59% of the market, up from 80.39% in September.

Rept Battero Energy (HKEX: 666) rose to sixth place with 3.64 GWh in installations and a 3.67% share, overtaking Sunwoda (SZSE: 300207). The latter slipped to seventh with 3.53 GWh and a 3.57% share.

Energee remained eighth with 2.37 GWh in installations and a 2.39% share, while Svolt Energy surpassed Zenergy to rise to ninth, with the 2 holding shares of 2.09% and 1.90%, respectively.

LG Energy Solution, which ranked eleventh in the overall market in September, fell out of the top 15 list in October.

In the ternary power battery market, CATL continued to lead with 8.06 GWh in installations and a 63.12% share, though its share dropped 4.66 percentage points from the previous month.

CALB remained second in the ternary market with 2.49 GWh in installations, seeing its share jump 8.26 percentage points from September to 19.50%.

Svolt Energy and Sunwoda ranked third and fourth in the ternary battery market, with their shares rising to 7.61% and 6.50%, respectively.

LG Energy Solution's ternary battery installations fell to 0.21 GWh for the month, with its share dropping 7.48 percentage points to 1.68%, slipping from third in September to fifth.

Total ternary battery installations in October came in at 12.8 GWh, down 22.8% year-on-year while up 3.8% month-on-month, accounting for 12.9% of total domestic power battery installations.

Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery installations reached a record 86.3 GWh in October, up 27.8% year-on-year and 15.8% month-on-month, accounting for 86.9% of total installations.

CATL remained first in the LFP market with 31.54 GWh in installations and a 36.57% share, up 0.09 percentage points month-on-month.

BYD ranked second with 21.14 GWh and a 24.52% share, edging down 0.06 percentage points month-on-month.

The market share gap between the 2 companies in the LFP segment widened slightly from 11.90 percentage points in September to 12.05 percentage points. All of BYD's domestic installations continued to come from LFP batteries.

CALB jumped from fifth in September to third in the LFP market with 6.06 GWh in installations, as its share rose 0.77 percentage points to 7.03%.

Gotion High-tech remained fourth in the LFP market with 5.97 GWh in installations and a 6.93% share, while Eve Energy slipped from third in September to fifth with 5.44 GWh in installations and a 6.31% share.

In the January-October period, China's cumulative power battery installations reached 675.6 GWh, up 16.9% year-on-year. CATL ranked first with 293.28 GWh and a 43.55% share, up 0.76 percentage points from the same period last year.

BYD recorded 127.58 GWh in installations over the same period, ranking second with an 18.94% share, though its share fell 3.44 percentage points year-on-year.

CALB, Gotion High-tech, and Eve Energy ranked third to fifth in cumulative installations for the first 10 months, with shares of 6.77%, 6.07%, and 5.42%, respectively.

Across the market segments in the first 10 months, CATL led the ternary and LFP battery markets with shares of 72.23% and 37.86%, respectively, while LG Energy Solution and BYD ranked second in those 2 markets with shares of 9.62% and 22.64%, respectively.
