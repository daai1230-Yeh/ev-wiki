# Africa, Europe to power TVS Motor's overseas growth: Chairman Sudarshan Venu

**Date**: 22.07.2026

---

TVS Motor Company expects its international business to maintain growth momentum in the current financial year 2026-27 (FY27) as it deepens its presence across Africa, Latin America and Asia while expanding into Europe, Chairman Sudarshan Venu said at the company's annual general meeting (AGM). "We are now present in more than 90 countries," Venu said.

Overseas operations now contribute more than a quarter of the company's revenue, with international sales rising to over 1.59 million units in FY26. TVS Motor reported its highest-ever annual performance in FY26, with total sales volumes rising 24 per cent year-on-year to 5.89 million vehicles.

Africa remains a key long-term growth market for the company, supported by favourable demographics and rising mobility demand. TVS is also entering the European market, starting with Italy, and plans to expand to other markets such as Spain and Portugal. The company has established a design and engineering hub in Bologna, Italy, underscoring its commitment to the European expansion.

Sudarshan Venu has played a pivotal role in TVS Motor's international expansion into Africa, Southeast Asia, Latin America and, more recently, Europe, since being elevated to Managing Director and later Chairman of the company.
