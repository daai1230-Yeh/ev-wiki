# A New Kind Of Hybrid Is Coming. See The 9 Extended-Range EVs Hitting America Soon

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 2026-08-31 (updated)

---

Extended-range EVs (EREVs) from Scout, Ford, Hyundai and more promise all the benefits of electric cars without the range anxiety.

As Americans come to grips with the new reality of the car market, where the $7,500 federal tax credit is a distant dream, a new type of hybrid vehicle is preparing to take the country by storm. Companies like Ford, Hyundai, Ram, and Jeep are all jumping into the fray to launch "extended-range electric vehicles" in the U.S. over the next few years.

## What is an extended-range EV (EREV)?

Just like other hybrids, EREVs combine a combustion engine, an electric motor (or motors), and a battery. But while traditional hybrids are essentially gas vehicles with a little electric assistance, EREVs are electric vehicles first and foremost. They're propelled by an electric powertrain that's virtually identical to what you'd find in an electric car, but they feature a combustion engine that solely acts as a generator to boost range.

As a result, EREVs promise many of the benefits of regular EVs—smooth acceleration, quiet operation, and zero-emission travel—but without the range anxiety or charging concerns. The concept is similar to a plug-in hybrid, but typically with more electric driving range.

EREVs can be plugged in at home or at a public fast charger for daily trips. But the gas component can come in handy too for longer journeys. That gas engine can't drive the wheels directly in an EREV, but it can create electricity for the car's electric motors.

In particular, automakers are embracing the idea for large pickup trucks and SUVs. By pairing an electric powertrain with a gas tank, companies can offer more range for towing. Plus, bulky vehicles are harder to electrify in a cost-effective way than smaller ones, due to the enormous batteries required.

EREVs are popular in China and have existed in the U.S. before; think the first-generation BMW i3, Fisker Karma, or subsequent Karma Revero. But the concept is getting a modern reboot in America.

## Ram 1500 REV (2026)

Despite several delays and a name change, the Ram 1500 REV is slated to become one of the first next-generation EREVs to hit America's streets. Production is expected in the second half of 2026 as a 2027 model — three years after its debut wearing the Ramcharger name.

Everything changed after Stellantis decided to pull the plug on the all-electric Ram 1500, placing its bets on this extended-range version instead.

The 2027 Ram 1500 REV will have a hefty 92-kWh main battery pack, sending power to a 250-kW front unit and a 248 kW rear unit — a combined 647 hp and 610 lb-ft. With a full battery it has an estimated 145 miles of range; a full tank of gas increases that to 690 miles, burned by a 3.6-liter Pentastar V-6 spinning a 130 kW generator. A CCS-1 port accepts up to 145 kW, adding up to 50 miles in 10 minutes.

## Scout Motors Terra pickup and Traveler SUV (2027)

Scout's "Harvester" EREV pickup and SUV are expected to go into production at a new South Carolina factory by the end of next year.

The EREV versions will have a main LFP battery of roughly 63 kWh, giving around 150 miles of all-electric range. A naturally aspirated four-cylinder gas engine mounted near the rear axle provides extra range, for around 500 miles total. Approximately 85% of Scout reservation holders opted for the EREV versions, CEO Scott Keogh told InsideEVs. Pricing starts under $60,000.

## Jeep Grand Wagoneer (2026)

The same layout found in the Ram 1500 REV will power the refreshed Jeep Grand Wagoneer. Stellantis claims this will be "America's first range-extended electric vehicle."

A 92 kWh battery feeds two motors for a maximum 647 hp and 620 lb-ft, hitting 60 mph in five seconds — the fastest Grand Wagoneer in the lineup. Jeep says combined electric and combustion range is "upwards of 500 miles," using the same 3.6-liter Pentastar V-6.

## Hyundai Santa Fe (2027)

The Hyundai Santa Fe EREV will arrive in 2027, manufactured at the automaker's Alabama plant. Hyundai says its EREVs will use its own high-nickel battery cells, with packs less than half the size of a comparable EV's. The Santa Fe will offer more than 600 miles of range.

## Genesis SUV (TBD)

Genesis will launch an EREV SUV before the end of the decade with more than 640 miles of range. Together, the Hyundai and Genesis EREV SUVs have a production target of 80,000 units per year in the U.S. alone.

## Kia Telluride & Pickup Truck (2029/2030)

Kia announced plans for two extended-range EVs by the end of the decade: the Telluride three-row gets an EREV option in 2029, and Kia will enter the U.S. pickup market with both EREV and conventional hybrid powertrains. The system is based on a 2.5-liter gas engine.

## Ford F-150 Lightning EREV (TBD)

The all-electric F-150 Lightning is dead, but will return as an extended-range EV. With an expected range of over 700 miles, it will address towing — one of the biggest issues faced by current Lightning owners. It replaces the canceled Project T3.

## Honorable mentions

**Nissan Rogue e-Power (2026)** — Uses Nissan's latest e-Power system. Works like an EREV but cannot be externally charged, making it a series hybrid. A 1.5-liter turbo three-cylinder feeds a 2 kWh battery powering the drive motors; north of 40 mpg with dual-motor AWD.

**Volvo's next-generation plug-in hybrids (timing unknown)** — Expected to offer around 100 miles of electric range. Unlike a true EREV, the gas engine will also be able to deliver power directly to the wheels, similar to the Chevrolet Volt.

**BMW X5 & 7-Series EREV (unconfirmed)** — Bloomberg reports BMW is considering EREV powertrains for larger, higher-end vehicles.

**Audi SUV (unconfirmed)** — Reportedly a U.S.-bound SUV using the same platform as Scout's Traveler and Terra.

**Lotus Eletre PHEV (unconfirmed)** — In China, the Eletre has been retrofitted with a PHEV powertrain that can also act as an EREV. The "Eletre For Me" goes on sale in China in H2 2026 with a 70 kWh pack, two motors and a 2.0-liter gas engine, totalling 982 hp.
