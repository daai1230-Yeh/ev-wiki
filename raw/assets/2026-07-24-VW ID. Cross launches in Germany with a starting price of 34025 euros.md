# VW ID. Cross launches in Germany – with a starting price of €34,025

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Volkswagen has started sales of the new ID. Cross in Germany, initially offering two trim levels with a 155kW motor and 52kWh battery. The ID. Cross Life with the 155kW powertrain is priced at €36,525 standard, reduced to €34,025 with a current €2,500 purchase incentive. The higher-equipped Style version lists at €39,110, reduced to €35,610 with a €3,500 incentive. The entry-level €27,995 base Trend version is not yet orderable — it becomes available from mid-October.
