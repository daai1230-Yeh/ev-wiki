# LiDAR maker Hesai announces buyback of up to $100 million as Hong Kong shares hover near record low

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-18

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- Hesai posted its sixth consecutive quarterly profit in the third quarter.
- Hesai plans to fund the buyback with existing cash and expects to cancel the repurchased shares.

Chinese LiDAR maker Hesai (HKEX: 2525) said on Wednesday that its board had approved a share buyback of up to $100 million as its Hong Kong-listed shares traded near their record low.

Hesai's Hong Kong shares fell as low as HK$12.00 on November 18 before closing up 0.24% at HK$12.58, just 9.4% above the record low of HK$11.50.

Hesai may repurchase Class B ordinary shares, American depositary shares (ADSs) or both over the 12 months beginning November 18, it said in a filing with the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. Each ADS represents 8 Class B ordinary shares.

Hesai plans to fund the buyback with existing cash and expects to cancel the repurchased Class B ordinary shares in due course, including those represented by ADSs.

Hesai co-founder and CEO David Li expressed confidence in the company's business outlook and said he expects the cancellation to boost earnings per share and increase long-term shareholder value.

Depending on market conditions, Hesai may buy back shares on the open market at prevailing prices, through privately negotiated transactions or in block trades, among other methods. The board will review the program periodically and may adjust its terms and size.

Hesai held 6.98 billion yuan ($1.04 billion) in cash reserves at the end of September, according to its November 10 earnings release. The maximum buyback amount represents about 9.6% of those reserves.

Hesai shipped 729,673 LiDAR units in the third quarter and posted its sixth consecutive quarterly profit as total revenue rose 34.3% year-on-year to 1.07 billion yuan.

Net profit fell 68.5% year-on-year to 80.8 million yuan, mainly because Hesai had recorded a one-time gain of 185.1 million yuan in the same quarter last year from the sale of an equity investment and the transfer of intellectual property.
