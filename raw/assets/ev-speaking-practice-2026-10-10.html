2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Polestar just had its strongest third quarter ever, but the good news comes with an awkward twist. Worldwide sales edged up 1 percent to an estimated 14,371 vehicles, yet without the United States, sales actually fell 8 percent.

Polestar剛創下史上最強的第三季，但好消息帶著尷尬的轉折。全球銷量小幅成長1%、估計達14,371輛，但如果不計美國，銷量其實下滑了8%。

In other words, the growth came from a market that has effectively shut the brand out. U.S. deliveries jumped from 966 to 2,160 cars, an increase of about 124 percent, while sales in the rest of the world dropped from 13,256 to 12,211.

換句話說，成長來自一個實際上已把這個品牌拒於門外的市場。美國交車量從966輛躍升至2,160輛，成長約124%，其他地區銷量則從13,256輛降到12,211輛。

The reason is a rule from the U.S. Department of Commerce, announced in June, that bans certain Chinese connectivity and driver-assistance technology. Volvo, which belongs to the same Geely group, received an exemption, but Polestar's request was denied, even though the Polestar 3 and the Volvo EX90 share a production line in South Carolina.

原因是美國商務部6月公布的一項規定，禁止某些中國製的車聯網與駕駛輔助技術。同屬吉利集團的Volvo獲得豁免，Polestar的申請卻被駁回，儘管Polestar 3和Volvo EX90在南卡羅來納州共用同一條產線。

After the decision, Polestar slashed prices to clear its inventory, with discounts on the Polestar 4 reaching 25,000 dollars. That may explain the surge, but it looks more like a sugar high than sustainable growth, especially since the company lost around one billion dollars in 2025.

裁定公布後，Polestar大幅降價清庫存，Polestar 4的折扣最高達25,000美元。這或許解釋了銷量激增，但那更像是一時的亢奮，而非可持續的成長，尤其這家公司2025年虧損了約十億美元。

Meanwhile, battery maker LG Energy Solution is showing a different path. Its preliminary third-quarter revenue grew 59 percent to 7.21 billion dollars, and operating profit rose 25 percent to 565 million dollars.

同時，電池製造商LG新能源展現了另一條路。其第三季初步營收成長59%、達72.1億美元，營業利益成長25%、達5.65億美元。

The company has slowed some electric vehicle battery production in North America because of weak demand. But strong orders for energy storage batteries, driven by power-hungry AI data centers and grid upgrades, helped make up the difference, and demand from Volkswagen and Renault in Europe lifted its plant in Poland.

該公司因需求疲弱，放慢了北美部分電動車電池的生產。但AI資料中心與電網升級帶動的儲能電池強勁訂單彌補了差距，而歐洲Volkswagen與Renault的需求則推升了其波蘭廠。

Policy is helping too. U.S. manufacturing tax credits, which run through 2032, boosted earnings, and LG is securing supply by signing a deal for 240,000 metric tonnes of lithium concentrate from a mine in Quebec. It also opened a two-billion-dollar plant in Michigan that makes storage batteries for Tesla and EV batteries for Toyota.

政策也有幫助。持續到2032年的美國製造稅額抵減提振了獲利，而LG也簽下協議，從魁北克一座礦場取得24萬公噸鋰精礦以確保供應。它還在密西根州開設了一座20億美元的工廠，為Tesla生產儲能電池、為Toyota生產電動車電池。

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

1.Should the U.S. be allowed to ban a car brand because of the software and connectivity technology inside it? Argue either side: national security versus fair competition.

美國是否可以因為車內的軟體與車聯網技術而禁止某個汽車品牌？請任選立場論述：國家安全 vs. 公平競爭。

2.Polestar was denied an exemption while Volvo got one. Is it fair to treat two brands under the same group differently? What might justify it?

Polestar的豁免被駁回，Volvo卻獲准。同一集團的兩個品牌受到不同對待，公平嗎？可能有什麼理由？

3.Is heavy discounting a smart way to clear inventory, or does it damage a brand in the long run?

大幅折扣是清庫存的聰明方法，還是長期會傷害品牌？

4.LG is earning more from storage batteries than from EV batteries in North America. Is it wise for a battery maker to shift its focus away from cars?

LG在北美靠儲能電池賺的比電動車電池更多。電池製造商把重心從汽車移開明智嗎？

5.AI data centers are driving demand for large batteries. Do you think the AI boom and the EV transition help each other or compete for the same resources?

AI資料中心正推升大型電池的需求。你認為AI熱潮與電動車轉型是互相幫助，還是在搶同樣的資源？

6.U.S. manufacturing tax credits are lifting battery profits while consumer EV credits were cut. Should governments support factories, buyers, or both?

美國製造端稅額抵減正在提升電池獲利，消費者購車補助卻被取消。政府該支持工廠、買家，還是兩者都要？
