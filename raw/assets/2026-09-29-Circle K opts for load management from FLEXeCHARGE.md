# Circle K opts for load management from FLEXeCHARGE

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-09-28

---

Fuel and charging operator Circle K will cooperate with software provider FLEXeCHARGE: cloud-based load management will be implemented for 1,000 charging points across several European markets.

For Danish FLEXeCHARGE this is one of its largest CPO contracts and the first large-scale deployment of its HARMON-E software in a "headless" architecture, aimed at large CPOs with mature internal platforms that want to integrate specialised energy-management functions into their own stack via APIs. Circle K already has an energy management system, so this is a supplier change for a defined subset (load management), keeping its existing workflows and interface.

Hallgeir Horne, Senior Manager Charging Infrastructure at Circle K, said they needed a load-management supplier that fits into their environment rather than introducing another interface.

Circle K belongs to Alimentation Couche-Tard (nearly 17,300 locations in 27 countries and regions). In Europe it is present in Scandinavia, the Baltics, Belgium, Ireland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands and Poland, and in 2023 acquired TotalEnergies' fuel station network in Germany and the Netherlands. It reached 100 fast-charging sites in Germany in June, is the preferred charging provider in Sweden with over 1,000 charging points, and operates more than 4,400 charging points across Europe.
