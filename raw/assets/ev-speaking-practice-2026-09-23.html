1. Warm-Up3 min　暖身

If an electric car cost exactly the same as a petrol car, would you buy one tomorrow? Why or why not?
如果一台電動車的價格跟汽油車完全一樣，你明天就會買嗎？為什麼？
When you make a big purchase, do you look at the sticker price, the monthly payment, or the total cost over several years?
你在做大筆消費時，看的是標價、每月付款金額，還是好幾年下來的總成本？
Have you ever rushed to buy something because a discount or subsidy was about to end?
你有沒有因為某個折扣或補助即將結束，而急著買下某樣東西？

2. Read Aloud6 min　朗讀短文

For years, the first question people asked about electric cars was not about range or charging. It was about price. That gap is now shrinking. According to Kelley Blue Book, the average new electric car in the United States cost only 9.4 percent more than the average new car in August, down from a gap of 16 percent a year earlier.
多年來，大家問電動車的第一個問題不是續航也不是充電，而是價格。這個差距如今正在縮小。根據 Kelley Blue Book 的資料，8 月美國新電動車的平均售價只比新車整體平均高出 9.4%，一年前這個差距還有 16%。
The two sides of the market are moving in opposite directions. The average transaction price of a new vehicle rose nearly 2 percent to 50,089 dollars. Electric cars, meanwhile, fell 2.7 percent year over year to an average of 54,813 dollars.
市場的兩端正朝相反方向移動。新車的平均成交價上漲近 2%，來到 50,089 美元；電動車則較去年同期下跌 2.7%，平均為 54,813 美元。
Interestingly, the best-known models barely moved. The Tesla Model Y was down just 0.2 percent, and the Hyundai Ioniq 5 fell 0.4 percent. What pulled the average down was the arrival of cheaper newcomers. The refreshed Chevy Bolt starts at 28,995 dollars, and the Toyota bZ at 36,575. As these models win market share, the whole average shifts lower.
有趣的是，最知名的車款價格幾乎沒動。Tesla Model Y 只降了 0.2%，Hyundai Ioniq 5 降了 0.4%。真正把平均拉低的，是較便宜的新車款。改款後的雪佛蘭 Bolt 起價 28,995 美元，Toyota bZ 起價 36,575 美元。隨著這些車款搶下市佔，整體平均也跟著往下移。
There is a catch, though. These figures do not include incentives. In August, discounts made up 12 percent of an electric car's average price, almost double the industry-wide figure of 6.5 percent. Yet that share is falling. The average incentive dropped by about 20 percent, from 8,200 dollars a year ago to 6,600 dollars today.
不過，這裡有個但書：這些數字並未計入優惠。8 月時，折扣佔電動車平均售價的 12%，幾乎是全產業 6.5% 的兩倍。但這個比例正在下降。平均優惠金額減少約 20%，從一年前的 8,200 美元降到現在的 6,600 美元。
In the words of Cox Automotive's Stephanie Valdez-Streaty, the trend is clear. Parity is not only about lower prices, but also about electric cars depending less on incentives. This autumn will be the first major sales season in years without a federal tax credit, and she expects the story to be one of normalization, where buyers decide on choice, price and inventory rather than a deadline.
用 Cox Automotive 的 Stephanie Valdez-Streaty 的話來說，趨勢很明確。價格平價不只是價格下降，也代表電動車越來越不依賴補貼。今年秋天將是多年來第一個沒有聯邦租稅抵免的銷售旺季，她預期主軸會是「常態化」：消費者依據車款選擇、價格與庫存來決定，而不是趕在期限前購買。
Carmakers have noticed. Rivian's chief executive, RJ Scaringe, recently said its next model, the R3 hatchback, will be priced materially lower than the R2. The R2 currently starts at close to 60,000 dollars for its launch edition, with a 45,000-dollar version promised by late 2027. That suggests the R3 could land below 40,000 dollars when production begins in Georgia in 2028.
車廠也注意到了。Rivian 執行長 RJ Scaringe 最近表示，下一款車型 R3 掀背車的定價將「明顯低於」R2。R2 首發版目前起價接近 6 萬美元，並承諾在 2027 年底前推出 4.5 萬美元的版本。這意味著 R3 在 2028 年於喬治亞州投產時，售價可能低於 4 萬美元。
He even hinted at an R4 that would be cheaper still. But none of this is guaranteed. Rivian first has to deliver the R2 smoothly, and it hopes to sell up to 70,000 vehicles this year. Affordable electric cars are clearly coming. The real question is whether they will arrive fast enough to replace the subsidies that are disappearing.
他甚至暗示還會有更便宜的 R4。但這一切都還不是定局。Rivian 得先讓 R2 順利交車，公司希望今年能賣出最多 7 萬輛。平價電動車顯然正在到來，真正的問題是：它們能否來得夠快，填補正在消失的補貼。

5. Useful Expressions3 min　實用句型

The gap has narrowed from X to Y. — The price gap has narrowed from sixteen percent to just over nine percent.
描述差距縮小的標準說法，比 "became smaller" 更精準專業。
A and B are moving in opposite directions. — Petrol car prices and EV prices are moving in opposite directions.
「A 和 B 走勢相反」。比較兩組數據時非常好用。
There's a catch, though: ... — There's a catch, though: these prices don't include incentives.
「不過有個但書／陷阱」。口語中用來引出反面資訊，讓論點更平衡。
It's not just about X; it's also about Y. — It's not just about cheaper cars; it's also about less reliance on subsidies.
「不只是 X，也關乎 Y」。拓展論點層次，讓表達更有深度。
The real question is whether ... — The real question is whether cheaper models will arrive in time.
「真正的問題在於是否……」。把討論引導到核心，適合當結論句。

6. Discussion5 min　討論題

  1. Is a 9.4 percent price gap small enough for most families to ignore, or is it still a deal-breaker?
    9.4% 的價差小到大多數家庭可以忽略嗎？還是仍然是決定性的阻礙？
  2. Some argue that subsidies were necessary to get the market started. Others say they distorted prices and mainly helped wealthier buyers. Which side do you find more convincing?
    有人認為補貼是啟動市場的必要手段；也有人說補貼扭曲了價格，主要幫到的是較富裕的買家。你覺得哪一方比較有說服力？
  3. The average EV price fell mainly because cheaper models sold more, not because existing models got cheaper. Does that difference matter?
    電動車平均售價下降，主要是因為便宜車款賣得多，而不是既有車款降價。這個差別重要嗎？
  4. Rivian is moving from premium vehicles toward cheaper ones. Is it smarter for a young carmaker to start expensive and move down, or to start cheap?
    Rivian 正從高價車走向平價車。對新創車廠來說，先做高價再往下走比較聰明，還是一開始就做平價車？
  5. In Taiwan, what matters more to EV buyers: purchase price, charging access, or resale value?
    在台灣，對電動車買家來說，哪個最重要：購車價格、充電便利性，還是二手價值？
  6. If you were a government, would you keep EV subsidies, cut them, or redirect the money to charging infrastructure? Defend your choice.
    如果你是政府，你會保留電動車補貼、取消它，還是把錢轉投入充電基礎建設？請為你的選擇辯護。

7. Wrap-Up2 min　總結

60-second summary — try saying this in your own words. 60 秒摘要，試著用自己的話說一遍。

In the US, the price gap between electric and petrol cars has narrowed from sixteen to about nine percent in a year. That is mainly because cheaper models like the Chevy Bolt and Toyota bZ are selling well, while incentives are shrinking. Carmakers such as Rivian are planning even cheaper models, but the question is whether they can arrive before the loss of subsidies slows the market down.
在美國，電動車與汽油車的價差在一年內從 16% 縮小到約 9%。主因是雪佛蘭 Bolt、Toyota bZ 等較便宜的車款賣得好，同時優惠正在減少。Rivian 等車廠計畫推出更便宜的車款，但問題在於，它們能否在補貼消失拖慢市場之前及時到位。

Your position 表達立場的句型：

"To me, the real turning point won't be when EVs match petrol cars on price, but when people stop needing a subsidy to say yes."
「對我來說，真正的轉捩點不是電動車和油車價格打平的那一刻，而是人們不再需要補貼也願意買單的時候。」
