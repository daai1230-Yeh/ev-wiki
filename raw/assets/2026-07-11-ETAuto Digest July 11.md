# ETAuto Daily Digest — July 11, 2026

**Source**: ETAuto Latest News newsletter (auto.economictimes.indiatimes.com — source site inaccessible for full article text; headlines and summaries below are from the newsletter email itself)

---

**BMW India CEO Hardeep Singh Brar on growth, EVs and the luxury car market** — In this interview, Brar, President & CEO of BMW Group India, explains the reasons behind the company's 17% H1 growth. BMW India posted its highest-ever H1 sales with 9,075 deliveries, EV sales up 78% (2,359 EVs sold, 26% share), long-wheelbase sales up 24% (52% share), and luxury SAVs up 35% (65% share of volumes). 14 more product launches are planned in 2026, and Brar believes BMW could reach the No. 1 spot in India's luxury car market as early as next year.

**Nissan to leverage global partnerships across regions** — From Renault and Dongfeng to Honda and Mitsubishi, Nissan is looking to leverage the strengths of its alliance partners across different regional markets.

**Uno Minda puts AI, digital infrastructure in driver's seat as it expands aftermarket play** — The auto component maker is deploying AI across forecasting, distributor support, and customer management as it expands its aftermarket business, running 15-16 AI initiatives across manufacturing analytics, safety, HR, finance, productivity, and cost optimization, alongside a network of eight strategically positioned warehouses.
