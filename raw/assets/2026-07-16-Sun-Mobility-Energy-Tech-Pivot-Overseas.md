# Sun Mobility bets on energy-tech pivot for bigger play in the EV-era; begins overseas journey

**Author**: ETAuto Staff | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

---

Bengaluru-based SUN Mobility, an electric-mobility energy infrastructure and battery-swapping company, is separating its technology business from network operations as it pivots toward a broader energy-tech play and begins expansion beyond India.

Helios Climate and the Private Infrastructure Development Group (PIDG) have backed SUN Mobility with a significant capital infusion to launch what is described as Africa's largest battery-swapping network, while continuing to scale its domestic operations. The funding is expected to support production of more than 232,000 batteries, enable adoption of over 138,000 electric vehicles, and roll out more than 2,000 quick-interchange stations across Africa and Southeast Asia. SUN Mobility, founded in 2017, develops and operates battery-swapping infrastructure for electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and larger commercial vehicles.
