# Scotland allocates £1.9m to zero-emission haulage

**Date**: 24 July 2026

---

The Scottish Government has announced almost £2 million in new funding to help the haulage sector prepare for electrification, combining £1 million for SME fleet transition planning with a further £900,000 for skills development.

The £1 million is directed to the SME Fleet Analysis Support fund, providing up to £15,000 per business to help SMEs gain practical, business-focused insight into fleet transition, identify suitable zero-emission technologies, and plan future investments. The remaining £900,000 goes to the next phase of the Zero Emission Heavy Duty Vehicle Skills Challenge Fund, supporting training providers, colleges and industry organisations in developing skills to maintain and operate battery-electric and hydrogen-powered heavy-duty vehicles. The announcement expands existing support programmes helping Scotland's haulage sector transition away from diesel.
