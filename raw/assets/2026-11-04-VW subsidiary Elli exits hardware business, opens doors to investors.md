# VW subsidiary Elli exits hardware business, opens doors to investors

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-11-03

---

Volkswagen subsidiary Elli is fundamentally realigning its business model: in future, the company will focus on fleet solutions, charging cards, energy management, and bidirectional charging. Its own hardware business will be phased out, and its in-house AC wallbox will be discontinued. Elli is also opening itself up to external partners.

Volkswagen Group Charging GmbH (Elli) will focus primarily on digital services related to charging and energy management, as confirmed by CEO Giovanni Palazzo. "We will significantly scale back our hardware business in the future," he says. Elli intends to focus on recurring-revenue, scalable models such as fleet management, charging cards and charging apps.

The new strategy has two core areas: Mobility and Energy. The decision is driven by an increasingly competitive market; Palazzo expects further consolidation in the charging industry.

## AC wallbox production to be discontinued

Elli is abandoning production of its Flexpole battery-buffered fast-charging stations in Hanover and phasing out its own AC wallbox. Existing stock will be sold off, then no further orders accepted. Existing customers are unaffected; service and maintenance remain unchanged. Elli will deprioritise unidirectional charging and sees the future of private charging in V2G and V2H.

## Fleet hardware installation to end

For business customers and the VW Group, specialised partners will take over planning, construction and maintenance of physical infrastructure. Elli will focus on backend connectivity, charging, energy and billing services. Implementation is to be completed by the end of 2027.

## Digital charging and fleet services

Elli will expand its Mobility Service Provider business (fuel and charging cards, fleet management), with LogPay's fuel card business integrated to serve mixed ICE/EV fleets. Its MSP solution, used by Sixt since early 2024, will be opened to further customers, with a first deal soon to be closed. In energy, the Elli Naturstrom tariff remains the anchor, to be extended with dynamic tariffs and bidirectional charging services.

## Bidirectional charging

A V2G offering with The Mobility House is being prepared for launch in November, using a bidirectional DC wallbox from Chinese manufacturer EV-Tech. Customers could receive credits of up to €720 per year on their electricity bill. Palazzo calls V2G a potential game-changer.

## Investors and jobs

Volkswagen remains committed to charging and energy; Palazzo expects revenue in the high three-digit million range this year. Elli is seeking external partners or investors, as MOIA and PowerCo are; several companies have expressed interest, nothing finalised. Of its just under 400 employees, roles in hardware, infrastructure and parts of central administration are most likely affected; negotiations with the works council are underway and no number has been given.
