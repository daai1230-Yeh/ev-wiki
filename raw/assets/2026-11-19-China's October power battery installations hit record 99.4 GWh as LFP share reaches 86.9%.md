- Lithium iron phosphate battery installations reached 86.3 GWh, with market share rising to a record 86.9%.

China's power battery installations reached a record high in October, with year-on-year growth rebounding from a slowdown in September as lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries strengthened their market dominance.

Domestic power battery installations totaled 99.4 GWh in October, up 18.2% year-on-year and 14.0% month-on-month, according to data released on Thursday by the China Automotive Battery Innovation Alliance (CABIA).

The figure surpassed the previous all-time monthly high of 98.1 GWh set in December 2025, marking the third consecutive month to set a new high for the year, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.

Year-on-year growth in October installations accelerated from 14.7% in September, ending a 2-month streak of slowing expansion.

LFP battery installations reached a record 86.3 GWh in October, jumping 27.8% year-on-year and 15.8% month-on-month.

LFP batteries accounted for 86.9% of total installations in October, up from 85.5% in September and surpassing the previous record of 85.6% set in August this year.

Ternary battery installations stood at 12.8 GWh in October, down 22.8% year-on-year, though they rose 3.8% month-on-month to mark a second consecutive monthly increase.

The market share of ternary batteries fell to 12.9% for the month, down from 14.1% in September and hitting the lowest monthly level on record.

From January to October, China's cumulative domestic power battery installations totaled 675.6 GWh, up 16.9% year-on-year, slightly faster than the 16.7% growth recorded in the first 9 months.

Cumulative LFP battery installations reached 563.5 GWh in the first 10 months, up 19.9% year-on-year and accounting for 83.4% of the total. Cumulative ternary battery installations rose 2.7% year-on-year to 110.6 GWh, representing a 16.4% share.

Alongside rising installations, China's combined output of power and energy storage batteries hit a record 245.1 GWh in October, up 43.6% year-on-year and 3.0% month-on-month. Cumulative output in the first 10 months reached 2,078.1 GWh, surging 60.8% year-on-year.

Combined sales of power and energy storage batteries totaled 230.4 GWh in October, up 38.8% year-on-year and 3.4% month-on-month.

Power battery sales reached 159.3 GWh, up 28.1% year-on-year and accounting for 69.1% of total sales. Energy storage battery sales jumped 70.5% year-on-year to 71.1 GWh, making up 30.9% of the total.

In the first 10 months of the year, cumulative sales of the 2 battery categories rose 55.1% year-on-year to 1,912.1 GWh. Cumulative power battery sales grew 36.8% year-on-year to 1,245.6 GWh, while energy storage battery sales surged 106.5% year-on-year to 666.4 GWh.

China's combined exports of power and energy storage batteries hit a record 46.1 GWh in October, up 63.4% year-on-year and 4.8% month-on-month, accounting for 20.0% of total monthly sales.
