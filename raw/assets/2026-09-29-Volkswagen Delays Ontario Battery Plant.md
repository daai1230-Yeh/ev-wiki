# Volkswagen Delays Ontario Battery Plant

**Author**: Mack Hogan | **Date**: 2026-09-26

---

Volkswagen's EV plans took another hit as its subsidiary PowerCo Canada announced that its forthcoming Ontario battery plant will be delayed by two years; production is now expected to start in 2029. This follows news that the ID. Buzz will not return to the U.S. as a 2027 model as previously planned; an upgraded version is now due as a 2028 model in the first half of next year.

Both the ID. Buzz and the Canadian plant have been battered by U.S. trade policy — a 25% tariff on the already expensive ID. Buzz — while battery plants worldwide are being scaled back or rethought amid slower-than-expected demand and a new trade environment.

VW faces a contracting business in China, more competition at home, steep U.S. tariffs, and higher energy prices in Europe, where it is shuttering factories and laying off workers (last year it closed a German plant for the first time in its 88-year history). Its response: localizing supply chains, improving electric products and leveraging partnerships — a joint venture with Rivian for software-defined vehicle architectures, and work with Xpeng on next-generation vehicles for China.

In North America, VW's two Mexican factories face U.S. tariffs and the Chattanooga plant now builds only the Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport (the ID.4 was discontinued to make room for gas SUVs). VW still plans to localize EV production in the U.S. with Rivian-JV software-defined models, but it will take longer than expected.
