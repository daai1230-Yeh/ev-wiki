# The New BMW i3 EV Is Coming To The U.S. Sooner Than Expected

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 2026-09-23

---

*The German automaker is opening the order books for the sleek electric sedan ahead of schedule.*

- BMW will start taking U.S. reservations for the new i3 EV earlier than expected.
- Pre-orders for the 440-mile sedan were supposed to open next month.
- The first cars are expected to be shipped stateside in the first quarter of 2027.

BMW is making a habit of expediting everything related to the launch of its Neue Klasse cars. First came the iX3 crossover, which has seen massive success in Europe so far, prompting BMW to ramp up production at its new factory in Hungary faster than ever.

Now, the same strategy applies to the new i3 sedan, the second Neue Klasse-based EV. The electric 3 Series will go up for pre-order in the United States on September 29, according to emails sent by BMW of North America to people who registered for updates.

That's at least one week ahead of the original schedule, according to BMW Blog, which quoted company sources saying the order books were supposed to open in the first half of October.

This is probably due to higher-than-expected demand. Earlier this year, BMW made a similar move in Europe, where it started taking orders ahead of schedule because customers kept asking to buy the new i3.

In the U.S., BMW has only moved up the reservation date. The i3's starting price and definitive delivery schedule are still unknown.

As with the iX3, just one version of the i3 will be available at first. The 2027 BMW i3 50 xDrive has a dual-motor, all-wheel-drive setup making a combined 463 horsepower, powered by a 108.7 kWh battery that enables an EPA-estimated range of up to 440 miles. It can accept up to 400 kW from a DC fast charger through a native NACS port that can also export power thanks to built-in bidirectional charging.

A lower-powered, more affordable option is also in the cards, as well as a hotter i3 M60 xDrive. With the iX3 50 xDrive starting at $61,500 before destination, it's not far-fetched to think the i3 would start at less than $60,000 in the U.S.

Deliveries are expected to start in the first quarter of next year, with more details likely next week when BMW officially opens the i3 order books.
