# Indonesia: Hyundai deploys Bali's first electric buses for public transport

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

Hyundai has handed over a fleet of eleven electric buses for public transport in Bali, Indonesia – the first e-buses used for public transport on the tourist island.

The buses are part of the Bali Sustainable Transportation System ODA Project, managed by the Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) with South Korea's Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment and the Indonesian government. Hyundai won the tender GGGI floated in October last year and delivered all eleven buses at the project launch ceremony on 22 October. The fleet is the eight-metre mid-size County EV, built with Indonesian bus body maker Laksana. Hyundai will also set up a local monitoring centre to track vehicle quality and driving performance during the initial deployment.

The Bali configuration has 20 seats, a 150 kW motor and a 128 kWh battery for a range of over 200 km. Hyundai claims over 30% better acceleration in the 50–80 km/h range than diesel models.

This follows delivery of 11 County EV buses to Surabaya, Indonesia's second-largest city, in November 2024. Hyundai wants to grow in Indonesia's e-bus market and plans to expand deployments in Surabaya, Bali and Jakarta. A Hyundai official said it is "meaningful to contribute to the transition toward eco-friendly transportation in Bali" and that Hyundai will keep expanding its electrified commercial vehicle business in key markets.
