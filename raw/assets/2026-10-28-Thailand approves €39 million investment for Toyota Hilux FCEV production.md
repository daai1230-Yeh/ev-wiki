# Thailand approves €39 million investment for Toyota Hilux FCEV production

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

Thailand's Board of Investment (BoI) has approved Toyota's investment of 1.49 billion baht in its Ban Pho factory (Chachoengsao Province) for production of the Hilux FCEV – the first hydrogen fuel-cell electric vehicle manufactured in Thailand.

Toyota is setting up a new line at Ban Pho, where it also builds ICE, mild-hybrid and battery-electric variants of the ninth-generation Hilux. Production of the fuel-cell variant is expected to start in December 2027, and a hydrogen refuelling station will be built on site for production and testing.

Initially the Hilux FCEV will be built only for export; European countries, Australia and New Zealand are the priority markets. Toyota plans just 3,000 units a year, a niche offering.

At IAA Transportation 2026 Toyota showed first teasers: a new daytime running light pattern with three segments per headlamp, a closed upper grille like the Hilux BEV, and a paint similar to the Mirai's Force Blue. Targets are towing capacity up to 2.5 tonnes and range over 400 km. Launch is planned for 2028; before that, Gazoo Racing will likely test the technology in the DKR GR FC Hilux in the Dakar Rally's Future Mission 1000 category in 2027. The rally build uses two fuel-cell stacks with a combined 400 kW and four high-pressure tanks holding 8.4 kg of hydrogen.
