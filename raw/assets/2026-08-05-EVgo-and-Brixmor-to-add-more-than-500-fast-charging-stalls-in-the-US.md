# EVgo and Brixmor to add more than 500 fast charging stalls in the US

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 04.08.2026

---

EVgo and Brixmor Property Group are expanding their partnership with more than 500 additional DC fast charging stalls at shopping centres across the United States. The rollout will bring EVgo chargers to at least 90 Brixmor properties, covering more than a quarter of the real estate company's portfolio.

The new charging locations will be built in Florida, Illinois, Minnesota, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Texas. Deployment is scheduled to begin later this year, with the first site under the expanded agreement planned for Barn Plaza in the suburbs of Philadelphia.

According to the companies, the new locations will feature up to 12 high-power DC fast chargers each. The charging hubs are primarily intended for shopping centres anchored by grocery stores, allowing drivers to charge their vehicles while completing regular errands. EVgo says this approach integrates charging into existing travel routines, while also benefiting retailers through increased customer dwell time, footfall and spending.

"Drivers across the US want infrastructure options that integrate seamlessly into their routines, and shopping centers are an ideal place to get groceries, grab a bite or shop while charging," said Scott Levitan, Executive Vice President, Growth at EVgo. "Expanding our partnership with Brixmor will help make EV charging even more accessible for drivers across the country while supporting the growing demand for public charging in everyday, convenient locations."

"At Brixmor, we're committed to ensuring our shopping centres continue to meet the evolving needs of our customers and communities," said Laura McLaughlin, Vice President, Specialty Leasing at Brixmor Property Group. "Expanding EV charging infrastructure across our portfolio enhances convenience, supports growing consumer demand and reinforces our focus on creating vibrant destinations where people can seamlessly shop, dine and access everyday services."

The two companies have worked together since 2016, when EVgo installed its first fast charger at a Brixmor shopping centre in Pleasanton, California.
