# Denza enters India, simultaneously launches D9 and Z9 GT

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-11-17

---

After launching operations in Europe earlier this year, BYD Group's premium and luxury brand Denza has entered the Indian market. The company has launched the D9 MPV and Z9 GT shooting brake as its first two models in the country.

Initially, Denza is planning a retail network in six cities across India, including Gurugram in the north, Ahmedabad and Mumbai in the west, and Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad in the south. It has appointed dealer partners in all six locations and has already opened showrooms in Gurugram and Mumbai.

Denza offers the D9 and Z9 GT in plug-in hybrid and all-electric variants internationally, but in India, it has launched both models exclusively as EVs. The company imports both vehicles from China as completely built-up (CBU) units and offers each in a single, well-equipped configuration. They are priced at 9 million rupees (approximately 87,500 euros) and 15.68 million rupees (approximately 152,400 euros), respectively, excluding registration costs, insurance and 1 per cent TCS (Tax Collected at Source).

The D9 accommodates seven people in a 2+2+3 seating layout and uses a single front-mounted permanent magnet synchronous motor that develops 340 kW and 365 Nm of torque. It has a second-generation Blade LFP battery pack with an energy storage capacity of 115.07 kWh. This configuration allows the MPV to accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in 8.5 seconds and travel 730 kilometres on a single charge, according to the NEDC cycle. Charging from 10 to 97 per cent state of charge (SoC) via a Flash Charger takes nine minutes. However, Flash Chargers are not available in India, and neither BYD nor Denza has announced plans to install them.

The Z9 GT has three electric motors, including two at the rear and one at the front. The rear motors each generate 310 kW and 400 Nm of torque, while the front motor produces 230 kW and 410 Nm of torque. With a combined output of 850 kW and 1,210 Nm of torque, the electric shooting brake accelerates from 0 to 100 km/h in 2.7 seconds and reaches a top speed of 270 km/h. A 122.49 kWh second-generation Blade LFP battery pack gives the car an NEDC range of 670 kilometres.

Denza sees itself as a niche brand in India’s luxury car market, which is dominated by European brands and accounts for around 50,000 sales a year. Speaking about its sales expectations in an interview with Autocar Professional, Rajeev Chauhan, Senior Vice President and Head of Electric Passenger Vehicles (EPV) Business at BYD India, said that “close to 80-90 numbers are for sure happening in couple of months.”
