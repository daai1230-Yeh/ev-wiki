# MAN starts electric truck production in Kraków

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-17

---

Series production of the MAN eTGL began in Kraków in mid-July, with the new eTGM for the medium-duty segment following later this year. MAN Truck & Bus is investing €1 billion in its Kraków plant to expand capacity and prepare the site for the next generation of TRATON Modular System (TMS) vehicles. Following Munich, where the heavy-duty eTGX and eTGS are already built, Kraków becomes MAN's second electric truck manufacturing site. With the eTGL, eTGM, eTGX and eTGS, MAN will offer a fully electric truck lineup covering 12 to 50 tonnes, from urban distribution to long-haul. New production halls will nearly double the site's footprint, from 130,000 to around 270,000 square metres.
