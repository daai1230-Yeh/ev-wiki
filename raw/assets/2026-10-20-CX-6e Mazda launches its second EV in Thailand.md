# CX-6e: Mazda launches its second EV in Thailand

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-10-19

---

Mazda has launched the CX-6e in Thailand, beginning the rollout of its latest EV in Southeast Asia. The mid-size SUV is the Japanese automaker's second electric car in the country, following the 6e, which it launched in March.

As in other international markets, Mazda is importing the CX-6e as a CBU from its joint venture with Changan in China, which manufactures the model at a factory in Nanjing, Jiangsu. It gives a choice of two trims: Premium and Premium Sports, priced at 1.259 million baht (approx. €33,500) and 1.299 million baht (~€34,600), respectively.

Mazda offers the CX-6e in Thailand in only one technical configuration, featuring a rear-mounted permanent magnet synchronous motor producing 190 kW and 290 Nm of torque and a 77.9 kWh LFP battery pack that supports charging at up to 11 kW (AC)/194 kW (DC). It accelerates from 0 to 100 kph in 7.9 seconds and reaches a top speed of 185 kph.

Via an AC charger, the LFP battery pack fully charges in as little as 7 hours and 53 minutes, while via DC a 10 to 80 per cent session takes as little as 24 minutes. In the Premium trim, the CX-6e consumes 168 Wh/km and travels 484 km on a single charge (WLTP). The Premium Sports trim consumes 170 Wh/km with a WLTP range of 468 km.

The Premium trim includes 19-inch alloy wheels, power tailgate, panoramic glass roof, ventilated and heated front seats, AR-HUD, ambient lighting, a 26.45-inch 5K touchscreen and a 23-speaker sound system. Premium Sports adds digital side mirrors, 21-inch wheels, a digital IRVM, and heated and ventilated rear seats. Both trims share a broad set of ADAS features and nine airbags.

In addition to Thailand, Mazda has introduced the CX-6e in New Zealand and Australia, where dealers expect first units later this month. In Australia, Mazda has received more than 2,000 pre-orders, and 56 per cent of customers have chosen the brand for the first time with this model (Drive.com.au). It has yet to announce plans to launch either Changan-Mazda JV-built EV in its home market.
