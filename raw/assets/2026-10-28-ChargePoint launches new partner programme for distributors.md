# ChargePoint launches new partner programme for distributors

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

US charging specialist ChargePoint has introduced its 'Channel Partner Program', giving European sales companies access to tiered pricing, growth bonuses and marketing budgets. In return, ChargePoint expects a high level of business commitment and completion of central training and certifications.

The initiative rolls out across the UK, France, the DACH region, the Benelux countries and the Nordics. Distributors receive financial incentives, project protection, marketing resources and technical training. Three levels – Bronze, Silver and Gold – are determined by annual revenue and completed training and certifications. "As partners advance through Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers, they unlock increasing discounts on charging hardware, software subscriptions, and services, along with performance-based rebates that reward business growth," the company stated.

The programme is open to all authorised distributors; those without a level can still benefit via ChargePoint's wholesale channel. It already bears the mark of John Saffrett, ChargePoint's new Europe chief, who recently told electrive that Europe is increasingly important to ChargePoint's global success. Saffrett: the programme is "designed to help resellers build sustainable, recurring revenue businesses while helping customers deploy charging faster and operate it more effectively."

ChargePoint stresses that, unlike hardware-focused partner programmes, this one combines hardware, cloud software, services, training, marketing support and business planning in a single framework. Its European portfolio includes AC solutions like the CP6000 and the DC Express line, including the Express Solo with up to 600 kW.
