# Indian Blackbuck EV unveils 15-metre ELON 3X electric sleeper coach, with production planned for mid-2027

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-11-09

---

Blackbuck Electric Vehicles has unveiled the ELON 3X, a 15-metre, three-axle electric sleeper coach featuring 42 berths, battery options of up to 565 kWh and commercial production scheduled to begin in mid-2027.

The Indian manufacturer presented the vehicle on 3 November 2026, following a four-year development programme that included two years of real-world road testing. According to Blackbuck EV's official announcement, the ELON 3X has been designed, engineered and developed entirely in-house in India, based on a purpose-built electric platform. The company claims it is India's first 15-metre multi-axle electric sleeper coach. Reservations have already opened, while the manufacturer is engaging with prospective intercity transport operators ahead of the planned start of production.

Blackbuck EV has been developing its electric bus portfolio across urban and intercity applications. The company's activities include the VIRO 2X nine-metre electric city bus and a 300-electric-bus project with Chalo. In the intercity segment, the manufacturer has introduced its ELON electric sleeper coach programme and presented the ELON 3X electric coach platform, followed by road trials of the vehicle.

## Blackbuck ELON 3X: 42 sleeper berths and up to 565 kWh battery capacity

The ELON 3X has been developed for long-distance intercity operations, with a three-axle configuration accommodating 42 sleeper berths, in addition to the driver and co-driver. The vehicle also provides more than 9 cubic metres of dedicated luggage space.

The coach is based on Blackbuck EV's proprietary Born-Electric Skateboard Architecture, which integrates the structural platform, electric propulsion, energy storage and electrical systems. Unlike a vehicle converted from a conventional diesel-powered chassis, the ELON 3X has been developed around an electric vehicle architecture from the outset. Lightweight construction has been a central element of the engineering programme.

Two battery configurations are available, with capacities of 425 kWh and 565 kWh. Driving range figures have not been announced.

## Independent suspension, active tag-axle steering and ECAS

The ELON 3X features independent suspension combined with active tag-axle steering and Electronically Controlled Air Suspension (ECAS), developed for highway stability, ride comfort and low-speed manoeuvrability. Standard braking and stability equipment comprises EBS, ABS, ESC and disc brakes on all wheels. The vehicle has been engineered with reference to European automotive standards, combining in-house architecture with components sourced through international suppliers.

The company has confirmed mid-2027 as the target for the start of commercial production. Blackbuck EV has not specified initial production volumes, vehicle pricing or confirmed customer delivery dates.

R K Raju, Founder and CEO of Blackbuck Electric Vehicles: "I founded Blackbuck EV with a belief that electric mobility gives us the freedom to rethink the bus from the ground up. Our vision is to make every journey more comfortable for passengers, every vehicle more valuable to its operator, and every unit of energy go further. We are building that future through original engineering — with the ambition to take Indian innovation to the world."
