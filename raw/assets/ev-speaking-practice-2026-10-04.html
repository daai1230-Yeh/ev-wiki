1. Warm-Up 暖身 3 min

1.Have you ever waited a long time for something you ordered, like a car, a phone, or furniture? How did you feel about the wait?

你有沒有訂過東西等很久，像是車子、手機或家具？等待的時候心情如何？

2.When fuel prices go up, do people around you think more about buying an electric car? Why or why not?

油價上漲時，你周圍的人會更認真考慮買電動車嗎？為什麼？

3.If you ran a car company, would you rely on one supplier for your most important part? What are the risks?

如果你經營一家車廠，會把最重要的零件交給單一供應商嗎？風險在哪裡？

2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Stellantis is halting production at several of its French plants for at least a week. The Sochaux plant, which builds the Peugeot 3008 and 5008, will pause from the 23rd to the 30th of October, and the Rennes plant, home of the Citroën C5 Aircross, will close from the 22nd to the 30th.

Stellantis將讓旗下數座法國工廠至少停產一週。生產寶獅3008與5008的Sochaux工廠將於10月23日至30日暫停，生產雪鐵龍C5 Aircross的Rennes工廠則於22日至30日關閉。

Two of these pauses are linked to a shortage of batteries from ACC, which is struggling to keep up with demand for its nickel-based, long-range packs. Stellantis also says it wants to avoid building up inventory that does not match what customers are actually asking for.

其中兩項停產與電池短缺有關：供應商ACC跟不上鎳系長續航電池的需求。Stellantis也說，不想累積與客戶實際需求不符的庫存。

ACC is a joint venture between Stellantis, Mercedes, and TotalEnergies. It once planned three plants, in France, Germany, and Italy, but now it is focused on a single cell factory in Douvrin, France. Early this year, it could equip only about 1,000 cars per month with long-range batteries, so it brought in a team of experts from China to reduce high defect rates.

ACC是Stellantis、賓士與道達爾能源的合資公司。原本規劃在法國、德國與義大利設三座工廠，現在只專注於法國Douvrin這一座電芯廠。今年初，它每月只能為約1,000輛車配備長續航電池，因此引進中國專家團隊來降低偏高的不良率。

Customers are feeling the pain. In February, Bloomberg reported delivery delays of up to eight months, and one dealer said deliveries were pushed back to the first or second quarter of 2027. Buyers pay nearly 5,000 euros more for the 97-kilowatt-hour battery instead of the 73-kilowatt-hour one, and then they wait.

客戶正受影響。彭博在2月報導交車延遲最長達八個月，一位經銷商也說交車被推遲到2027年第一或第二季。買家多花近5,000歐元，選97度電池而非73度電池，結果還得等。

Demand is strong, especially from fleet customers. Digitimes reports that the war involving Iran, which has disrupted oil tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, has set off a rush to buy electric vehicles.

需求強勁，尤其來自企業車隊客戶。DIGITIMES報導，涉及伊朗的戰事擾亂了荷莫茲海峽的油輪運輸，引發了電動車搶購潮。

The trouble is not only about batteries. The Poissy plant near Paris will close from the 19th to the 23rd of October, and Mulhouse will shut for the last two weeks of the month because demand for models like the Peugeot 308 is lower than expected. In Italy, Mirafiori will be on unpaid leave from the 19th to the 30th, citing high energy costs and weak demand for the hybrid Fiat 500.

問題不只在電池。巴黎附近的Poissy工廠將於10月19日至23日關閉，Mulhouse則在月底最後兩週停工，因為寶獅308等車款需求低於預期。在義大利，Mirafiori工廠將於19日至30日放無薪假，理由是能源成本高，以及混合動力飛雅特500需求疲弱。

A Stellantis spokesperson admitted, "Unfortunately we are not yet receiving enough of the batteries, despite big progress in ACC's production." Meanwhile, the company is reportedly talking with Huawei and JAC about a long-term partnership, and CEO Antonio Filosa says Stellantis is pushing for more manufacturing partnerships.

Stellantis發言人坦言：「很遺憾，儘管ACC的生產已有大幅進展，我們收到的電池仍然不夠。」同時據傳該公司正與華為及江淮汽車洽談長期合作，執行長Antonio Filosa也表示，Stellantis正推動更多製造合作夥伴關係。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型 3 min

"X is struggling to keep up with demand for Y."
描述供給跟不上需求。
The battery maker is struggling to keep up with demand for long-range packs.
"The pause is only indirectly related to X."
說明某事與原因只有間接關係。
The pause is only indirectly related to the battery shortage.
"Despite big progress in X, we are still not ___."
承認進步，但目標尚未達成。
Despite big progress in production, we are still not getting enough batteries.
"It only affects customers who are willing to ___."
說明影響範圍僅限特定人群。
It only affects customers who are willing to pay more for a bigger battery.
"The bigger risk is relying on a single ___."
指出依賴單一來源的風險。
The bigger risk is relying on a single supplier for the most important part.

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

1.Stellantis depends heavily on one battery supplier, ACC. Was that a smart partnership or a risky bet? What would you have done differently?

Stellantis高度依賴單一電池供應商ACC。這是明智的合作，還是高風險的賭注？你會怎麼做不同的決定？

2.Argue that carmakers should build their own battery plants. Then argue the opposite: that they should buy from specialists and share the risk.

請先主張車廠應該自建電池廠，再主張相反立場：應向專業電池廠採購並分擔風險。

3.Some buyers pay nearly 5,000 euros more for a bigger battery and still wait up to eight months. Would you wait? What would make you switch brands?

有些買家多付近5,000歐元買大電池，還要等到八個月。你願意等嗎？什麼情況會讓你換品牌？

4.Fuel price shocks from conflicts, such as the one near the Strait of Hormuz, can boost EV demand. Is this a lasting shift or a short-term rush?

荷莫茲海峽附近的衝突引發的油價衝擊會推升電動車需求。這是長期轉變，還是短期搶購？

5.ACC brought in experts from China to fix its defect rate, and Stellantis is reportedly talking to Huawei and JAC. Should European carmakers cooperate more with Chinese partners? Give both sides.

ACC引進中國專家來改善不良率，Stellantis據傳也在與華為和江淮洽談。歐洲車廠應該更多與中國夥伴合作嗎？請說明正反兩面。

6.Stellantis is also closing plants because of weak demand for hybrids and small cars. What does this tell us about what customers really want right now?

Stellantis也因混合動力與小型車需求疲弱而停工。這顯示消費者現在真正想要什麼？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 2 min

60-second summary: In short — Stellantis is pausing several French plants in October. Part of the reason is a shortage of long-range batteries from ACC, which has struggled to ramp up its only cell factory. Buyers face delays of up to eight months, even as an oil shock linked to the Iran war pushes more people toward EVs. At the same time, weak demand for hybrids and small cars is closing other lines. The big question is how carmakers can protect themselves from depending on one supplier.

60秒摘要：簡而言之，Stellantis十月將暫停多座法國工廠。部分原因是ACC的長續航電池短缺，而ACC唯一的電芯廠一直難以拉升產能。買家的交車等待最長達八個月，同時與伊朗戰事相關的油價衝擊又讓更多人轉向電動車。另一方面，混合動力與小型車需求疲弱也讓其他產線停工。核心問題是：車廠如何避免依賴單一供應商？
