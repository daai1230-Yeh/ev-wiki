# Solaris to build 20 electric buses for Olomouc in the Czech Republic

**Date**: 26.08.2026

---

Solaris has signed a contract with the public transport operator in the Czech city of Olomouc for 20 electric buses. Delivery is scheduled for 2028, with an option to double the order.

The firm order placed by Solaris and the public transport operator in Olomouc initially comprises ten units each of the Urbino 12 electric and Urbino 18 electric – an equal split of battery-electric solo and articulated buses. The recently signed contract between the two parties also includes an option to expand the order by an additional 20 vehicles, consisting of 15 battery-electric solo buses and five articulated buses.

Solaris has delivered a total of 96 buses to Olomouc to date. However, the vehicles from the latest contract will be the first battery-electric buses from Solaris to join the transport company's fleet.
