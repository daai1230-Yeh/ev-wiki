# Volkswagen launches long-wheelbase version of the ID. Buzz Cargo for €48,490

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-23

---

Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles is expanding the ID. Buzz Cargo lineup with a new variant: the Cargo with a long wheelbase (LWB). This model increases battery capacity to 86 kWh and cargo volume to 4.4 cubic meters. Prices start at €48,490 net.

At the end of last year, Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles (VWN) announced its intention to launch the ID. Buzz Cargo with a long wheelbase and the larger 86-kWh battery during 2026. Now, the longer ID. Buzz Cargo with all-wheel drive is available to order from today, while the single-motor variant can be ordered from next week. This means the cargo van is catching up with its passenger counterpart, which has had a long-wheelbase version since summer 2024.

Pre-sales for the long-wheelbase ID. Buzz Cargo with 250 kW and all-wheel drive start today at prices from €50,630. The rear-wheel-drive variant will follow next week from €48,490. The surcharge for the extended version compared to the standard model in Germany thus sits around €2,340. This includes an increased battery capacity from 79 to 86 kWh and a second sliding door as standard, among other things.

In the long-wheelbase version, it is not the rear overhang that is extended, but the wheelbase. From the 4.71-metre-long standard version with a wheelbase of exactly three metres, the long model is stretched by 25 centimetres between the axles to 3.25 metres. Since the overhangs remain identical, the vehicle grows to a total length of 4.96 metres. The cargo space increases from 3.9 to 4.4 cubic metres. The loading area measures 2.46 x 1.23 metres.

The extended cargo van is offered with the larger 86-kWh battery and 210 kW of power. With the optional all-wheel drive ("4MOTION"), the system output increases to 250 kW. This variant is the result of a recent technology update for the ID. Buzz, which also introduced one-pedal driving and a vehicle-to-load (V2L) function.

The maximum payload is up to 700 kg in the single-motor variant and up to 606 kg in the all-wheel-drive version. The maximum towing capacity is 1,800 kg. Volkswagen did not mention the range in its press release, but a company spokesperson told electrive the range for the all-wheel-drive model is 430 to 460 km and for the rear-wheel-drive model 450 to 490 km. The DC charging capacity is 200 kW (10 to 80 per cent SoC in 28 minutes) and the AC charging capacity 11 kW.

As standard, the long cargo van is equipped with a wooden floor and the second sliding door. The width of the door also increases by 19 centimetres – "so a Euro pallet can now fit through the sliding door," as VWN emphasises. The model is delivered with a large tailgate, though rear hinged doors are available as an option. The Cargo LWB is available as a two- or three-seater and with manually adjustable lumbar support. A multifunction steering wheel with newly designed push buttons is also included.

Like all ID. Buzz models, the extended ID. Buzz Cargo comes with the new infotainment system ("Innovision") and an improved software package. The optional "Travel Assist" now also responds to traffic lights: "At a red traffic light, the assist system automatically brakes the ID. Buzz Cargo to a standstill." Also new are the V2L function (3.0 kW continuous and 3.6 kW peak power) and one-pedal driving.
