# EVs now 33% cheaper to run than gasoline vehicles

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 07.09.2026

---

The ICCT's latest 'EV Transition Check' report finds that running an electric car in the EU was 33% cheaper than a gasoline car in 2025 (28% cheaper even for EVs relying solely on pricier public charging), and the gap widened further after the oil crisis that began in February 2026 pushed combustion fuel costs up 12–36% while EV energy costs stayed flat.

Falling battery costs (down 35% globally 2020–2025) have let BEV purchase prices in Germany drop 18% over the same period even as combustion-car prices rose 2%, with EVs now reaching price parity with petrol cars in the three largest segments. In Germany, electric trucks are already 11% cheaper than diesel trucks in long-haul total cost of ownership thanks to toll exemptions, and the EU-wide truck market is projected to reach parity by 2030. The report also finds EVs produce 73% fewer lifecycle greenhouse gas emissions than petrol cars (86% for electric long-haul trucks vs. diesel), while plug-in hybrids show real-world emissions averaging 4.6x higher than official type-approval figures.
