# Audi adopts "two brands, two partners" strategy in China

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

Audi is restructuring its joint ventures in China and will pursue a "two brands, two partners" approach. The joint venture with SAIC will focus exclusively on the local sub-brand 'AUDI' (without rings), while business with the four-ring vehicles will be consolidated in the joint venture with FAW.

Audi announced on WeChat that it has signed new agreements with FAW and SAIC Motor to differentiate the positioning of the two brands – Audi (four rings) and AUDI (no rings) – adapt their strategies and realign distribution. The FAW joint venture, established in 1988, will focus on the classic four-ring brand; the SAIC joint venture, founded in 2021, will concentrate entirely on the AUDI brand introduced in 2025.

## AUDI (without rings) focuses on electric vehicles

With SAIC, AUDI has already introduced the electric estate E5 Sportback and the electric SUV E7X and will continue to offer NEVs. The AUDI Innovation Tech Center (AITC) in Shanghai, dedicated exclusively to the AUDI brand, is officially opening.

SAIC-Audi had reportedly drastically reduced or halted production of four-ring ICE models such as the A5L Sportback, A7L and Q6, and will no longer be responsible for them. It will remain active in distributing four-ring models until the end of 2027 and continue to provide aftersales and service beyond that.

FAW-Audi will continue to produce both premium ICE vehicles and NEVs, including Chinese long-wheelbase PPE-based EVs: the Audi A6L e-tron and Q6L e-tron.

Johannes Roscheck, outgoing President of Audi China, said the two-brand strategy gives a "solid foundation for Audi's long-term and sustainable development in China". In November he will be succeeded by former Chery manager Jeff Zhang.

## Achieving joint progress

FAW CEO Liu Yigong said FAW-Audi will be "customer-centric" and pursue "joint progress in combustion engines, hybrids, and fully electric intelligence". SAIC boss Jia Jianxu said AUDI carries the mission of establishing a new model of joint venture cooperation, building on "Vorsprung durch Technik".
