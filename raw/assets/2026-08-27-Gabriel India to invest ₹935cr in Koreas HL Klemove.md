# Gabriel India to invest ₹935cr in Korea's HL Klemove

**Date**: 26.08.2026

---

Gabriel India Ltd, the listed flagship of auto-component maker ANAND Group, will acquire a 30 percent stake in HL Klemove India Pvt Ltd for $98.44 million, or about ₹935 crore, as it expands into advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and automotive electronics.

The auto components maker Gabriel India has entered into a joint venture with South Korea-based HL Klemove to expand its presence in the growing demand for autonomous driving solutions, including advanced driver assistance systems and automotive electronic products.

HL Klemove India will develop, manufacture and sell products including radars, front cameras, lidar systems, and automated-driving and parking control units with embedded ADAS software. The automotive-electronics portfolio will include brake and steering electronic control units, chassis-control units and torque sensors.

This investment announcement was made on 26 August 2026 and represents Gabriel India's strategic expansion into autonomous driving and automotive electronics technologies.
