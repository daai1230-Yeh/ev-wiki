# JSW MG Motor unveils multi-powertrain ADAPT platform, lines up two new-energy SUVs

**Source**: ETAuto Daily Morning Newsletter (Fri, Jul 17, 2026) | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

JSW MG Motor India has confirmed an investment of ₹1,400 crore during FY2026-27 to support new product development, localisation and expansion of manufacturing capacity, as it prepares to introduce two all-new new-energy SUVs based on its recently unveiled ADAPT (Advanced Drive Architecture Platform Technology). The upcoming launches will include one battery-electric SUV (BEV) and one plug-in hybrid SUV (PHEV), both expected during FY27, and both 4.3-metre-plus models.

The ADAPT platform supports electric vehicles (EVs), hybrid electric vehicles (HEVs), plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs) and range-extender electric vehicles (REEVs) on a common modular architecture that will underpin the company's future NEV portfolio. By supporting four propulsion technologies on a single platform, nearly 80 per cent of the engineering work is already complete, allowing future product development to focus largely on different body styles.

The company plans to invest ₹3,000–4,000 crore over the next few years, including around ₹1,400 crore during FY27, with spending focused on localisation, new products and manufacturing expansion.
