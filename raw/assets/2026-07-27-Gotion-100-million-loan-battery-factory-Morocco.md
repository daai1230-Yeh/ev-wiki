# Gotion secures €100 million loan for battery cell factory in Morocco

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 27.07.2026

---

The African Development Bank has approved a loan of €100 million for the Chinese battery cell manufacturer and Volkswagen partner, Gotion High-Tech, to support the factory Gotion plans to build in Morocco.

Gotion's new factory is to be built in the Rabat-Salé-Kénitra Free Trade Zone and will be the first integrated battery production facility in Africa. In the first phase, the company is planning an annual capacity of 10GWh for LFP battery cells and packs for use in electric vehicles, with a long-term aim of expanding capacity to 100GWh.

In 2024, Gotion signed an investment agreement with the Moroccan government for the battery cell factory, with Gotion Power Morocco — a wholly-owned subsidiary — leading the project. At the time, an initial annual capacity of 20GWh was mentioned; the African Development Bank now states the initial capacity will be 10GWh, with a possible later expansion to 100GWh, though no timetable has been provided.

In addition to the €100 million loan, the bank plans to mobilise further funds from financial partners — up to €141 million — to support implementation. The news is particularly significant for the African continent; a battery plant in neighbouring Morocco could also be a strategic asset for electric vehicle production in southern Europe, particularly within the Spanish cluster.
