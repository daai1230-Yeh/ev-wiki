# ATM orders 110 electric buses from Karsan amid Italian e-bus boom

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-10-29

---

Turkish manufacturer Karsan has secured a tender by Milan's public transport company ATM for the delivery of 110 electric buses. The vehicles, of the Karsan e-ATA 12 model, are set to enter service gradually starting from the end of 2027.

This spring, Azienda Trasporti Milanesi (ATM) published a tender valued at over €100 million for the delivery and subsequent maintenance of 110 electric buses. The contract has now been awarded to Karsan, including a ten-year maintenance service. The order includes 80 12-metre low-floor urban buses with three doors, and 30 12-metre low-floor intercity buses with two doors. The former go directly to ATM, the latter to its subsidiary NET.

Sustainable Bus reports that the order is processed through Italian Karsan dealer Kmobility, with a total contract value of approximately €76 million, including future battery replacements. The 30 NET units are likely to be stationed in Monza. The first batch will comprise 34 vehicles: 22 for Milan and 12 for Monza.

In late 2022, ATM tendered 340 electric buses, which Iveco Bus and Solaris secured in 2023. Since then, around 600 electric buses have been purchased. ATM remains committed to converting its entire fleet to electric drive by 2030 with 1,200 electric buses, converting depots in San Donato, Sarca, Giambellino and Palmanova and building new e-bus depots.

Italy is emerging as the leading market for electric buses in Europe. Its bus market is already the largest in the EU; only the UK records higher registration numbers. For externally chargeable buses, Italy has overtaken the UK this year, according to recent ACEA statistics.
