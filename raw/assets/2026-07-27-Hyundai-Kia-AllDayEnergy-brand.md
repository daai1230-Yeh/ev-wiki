# Hyundai and Kia consolidate smart and bidirectional charging under new brand

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 27.07.2026

---

Hyundai Motor Group, which includes the brands Hyundai, Kia, and Genesis, is streamlining and unifying its global activities in bidirectional charging and intelligent energy management under a new umbrella brand, 'AllDayEnergy'. The group plans to offer V2X (Vehicle-to-Everything) services across all brands and regions under a single unified identity, aiming to give customers reliable, cross-brand access to intelligent energy services that integrate EVs into domestic power grids and public distribution networks.

The rollout is modular: it begins with classic smart charging based on the V1G principle (electricity flows only from grid to EV), dynamically adjusting charging time to real-time electricity tariffs, grid loads, and user preferences. Full bidirectional applications will follow later, enabling the EV battery to serve as temporary storage for the public grid (V2G) and home networks (V2H).

The official market launch is scheduled for the second half of 2026, initially in the UK via the Kia app, with planned expansion into continental Europe, the USA, and Korea. Woong Tae Hwang, Vice President and Head of the EV Energy Strategy Group at Hyundai Motor Group, called the launch a decisive milestone toward becoming an integrated energy service provider: "We will continue to expand our AllDayEnergy service globally, allowing customers to seamlessly optimize their energy usage, reduce their energy costs, and contribute to grid sustainability."

The group has already gained pilot experience in multiple regions: a large-scale V2G trial in Jeju, South Korea; V2H applications tested in US emergency power scenarios such as wildfires; and smart charging/V2G services responsive to dynamic pricing in the Netherlands. The new services will be accessible via Hyundai and Kia's own vehicle apps, integrating directly into existing software rather than requiring separate third-party solutions.
