# Aito plans European expansion for autumn 2027

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-10-14

---

The Chinese Seres brand Aito outlines its launch plans for Europe at the Paris Motor Show: in autumn 2027 the EV manufacturer intends to enter the European market. In Paris it presents the Aito 9, 8, 7 and 6.

Aito, founded in December 2021, is part of the Seres Group, often associated with Huawei: Seres provides the platform, Huawei the digital technology. The range is positioned as luxury, with battery-electric and range-extender models. Cumulative deliveries are said to exceed 1.2 million vehicles, almost all in China so far; the launch has begun in the United Arab Emirates.

For Europe, the brand is preparing to meet local regulatory requirements and achieve "comprehensive localisation", mainly in service and distribution. The first European countries have not been disclosed.

The vehicles displayed are already sold in China as the Aito M6 to M9. The Aito 9 (5.23 m long, 3.11 m wheelbase) is the flagship E-SUV, five or six seats, BEV or range-extender. Below it are the Aito 8 (premium E-SUV), Aito 7 (large crossover, five to six seats) and Aito 6 (mid-size crossover). All use the Seres MF platform and CATL batteries.

Seres is deepening its collaboration with CATL under a "factory-in-factory" concept at the Seres Super Factory in Chongqing. The second phase plans 10 to 12 GWh annual capacity, for a combined total above 20 GWh per year. Phase one was commissioned in June 2025 with two CTP 2.0 pack lines.
