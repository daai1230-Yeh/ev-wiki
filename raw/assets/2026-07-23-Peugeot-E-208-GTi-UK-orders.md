# Peugeot opens UK orders for E-208 GTi

**Date**: 23 July 2026

---

Peugeot has started UK sales of the all-electric E-208 GTi from £33,495 after the UK government's £1,500 Electric Car Grant (list price £34,995 OTR before the grant). The 206kW hot hatch produces 345Nm of torque, delivering 0-62mph in 5.5 seconds, a top speed of 112mph, and a quoted range of 374km (WLTP), and supports 100kW DC fast charging with vehicle-to-load capability as standard. Finance offers start at £349 per month.

The E-208 GTi shares its technical foundation with the Opel Corsa GSE, which debuted the previous month — both are Stellantis models built on the group's e-CMP platform. It marks the first fully electric GTi in the badge's history.
