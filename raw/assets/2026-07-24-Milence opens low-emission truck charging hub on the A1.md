# Milence opens low-emission truck charging hub on the A1

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-20

---

Milence has opened its fourth truck charging hub in the Netherlands, located on the A1 along the TEN-T North Sea–Baltic Corridor at the Frans op den Bult HGV rest area near Hengelo. The site initially offers five DC high-power charging bays delivering up to 400kW via CCS, supporting cross-border electric freight transport. The hub does not yet offer Megawatt Charging System (MCS) connectors, though Milence plans to expand charging capacity at a later stage.
