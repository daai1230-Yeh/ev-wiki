# Circontrol to rebrand charging business as Circutor

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 22.07.2026

---

Circontrol will market its charging solutions under the Circutor brand going forward, with the transition taking place gradually throughout 2026. Circontrol will continue to operate as a development hub for charging and energy solutions.

Both companies already belong to the same corporate group. They will now merge their previously separate brand identities by gradually rebranding Circontrol's products and solutions under the Circutor name. According to the companies, the aim is to establish a unified market presence across all regions.

According to the companies, the consolidation will provide customers with a more streamlined product portfolio and consistent service and quality standards. The existing teams, technologies and expertise will remain largely unchanged. While Circontrol specialises in EV charging infrastructure, Circutor also offers solutions for energy efficiency and energy management.

The Circontrol brand will be phased into Circutor throughout 2026, while the company itself will continue to serve as the group's strategic research and development centre, focusing on EV charging solutions and technologies for the energy transition.

The combined brand will also incorporate Circontrol's existing charging portfolio. The Spanish manufacturer has recently expanded this portfolio, particularly in the DC fast-charging segment. In 2025, the company introduced the Sonic One, a new HPC charging station with 320 or 400 kW. In parallel, the AC solutions eVolve Smart and eNext Park were further developed. Additionally, several DC chargers from the Raption series received German Eichrecht certification.

This restructuring primarily aims to consolidate branding and sales activities. While charging products will be marketed under Circutor in the future, Circontrol will remain the group's development hub. For customers and operators, the transition will mainly be visible in terms of brand identity and the consolidation of the product portfolio.
