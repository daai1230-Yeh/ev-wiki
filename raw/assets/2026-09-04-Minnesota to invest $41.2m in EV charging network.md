# Minnesota to invest $41.2m in EV charging network

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

The Minnesota Department of Transportation (MnDOT) has announced plans to invest over $41 million in the state's EV charging network. MnDOT's third — and largest — round of grants will prioritise fast-charging infrastructure in Greater Minnesota.

The request for proposals for round 3a is now open, with a deadline of 2 November 2026; conditional awards are expected between late December 2026 and January 2027. The grants come through the National Electric Vehicle Infrastructure (NEVI) program. Earlier rounds provided more than $16 million, supporting 24 fast-charging sites on the I-94, I-35, and I-90 highways, eight of which are now open. Round three will provide 80 percent Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (IIJA) funding with a 20 percent match from each grantee.

"NEVI funding is used to further develop a fast-charging network and will go a long way towards reducing range anxiety for long-distance travel needs in our state," said MnDOT Commissioner Nancy Daubenberger.

The federal NEVI programme was suspended earlier this year, but Minnesota secured $4.5 million for its second round and aimed to add $4.7 million in state funding for a $10 million total — and appears to have secured further NEVI funding for this third round despite the federal programme's end.
