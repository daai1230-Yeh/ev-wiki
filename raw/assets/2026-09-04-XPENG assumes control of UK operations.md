# XPENG assumes control of UK operations

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

XPENG is set to take direct responsibility for its operations in Britain with the establishment of a national sales company, marking a shift away from the initial distributor-led model it used to enter the UK market.

The Chinese electric carmaker has reportedly reached an agreement with its UK distribution partner International Motors (IML) which will see it take over direct control of its sales operations, with IML continuing to provide operational support services. XPENG said the move combines "XPENG's global scale, technology and investment capability with International Motors' established infrastructure, while ensuring continuity for retailers and customers."

XPENG first entered the UK via a distribution agreement with IML in November 2024, launching with the G6 eSUV and initially planning 20 dealerships via IML. It has since expanded its European line-up considerably: the flagship X9 electric MPV launched in seven European markets in July, and the more affordable L03 was unveiled at a Munich brand event the same month. Both the X9 and L03 will join the UK lineup.

Elvis Cheng, General Manager of XPENG North Eastern Europe, called the new sales company "an important next step in XPENG's long-term commitment to the market," while International Motors MD William Brown said the agreement gives the brand "greater direct support from XPENG globally, while preserving the continuity, infrastructure and local expertise" IML has built over its 50-year history.

The move comes as Chinese carmakers continue expanding their UK presence — in August, electrive reported Chery is establishing a new R&D centre at the UTAC Millbrook testing site in Bedfordshire to adapt its vehicles to British driving conditions.
