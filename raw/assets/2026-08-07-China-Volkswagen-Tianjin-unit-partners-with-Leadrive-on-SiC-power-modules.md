# China: Volkswagen Tianjin unit partners with Leadrive on SiC power modules

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

Volkswagen Tianjin has signed an agreement with Chinese supplier Leadrive Technology to jointly develop silicon carbide (SiC) power modules for electric vehicles, supporting Volkswagen's localisation strategy for key EV components in China.

Volkswagen Automatic Transmission (Tianjin), despite its name, is no longer focused solely on transmissions — it's now one of Volkswagen Group's key powertrain and component manufacturing sites in China, having expanded into electric drive components including motors.

No financial details, production targets, or vehicle programmes were disclosed. The collaboration covers power semiconductor modules, electronic control systems, and complete electric drive solutions. Leadrive said the aim is to "bring high-performance electrified powertrain technologies into production."

The agreement expands an existing technical partnership between Volkswagen Group and Leadrive — previously focused on electric drive systems and dual-controller solutions for hybrids — into power electronics, one of the most strategically important areas of EV technology.

SiC semiconductors reduce energy losses and improve inverter efficiency (converting battery DC to motor AC), and support higher voltages and faster charging than conventional silicon, making them well suited to next-generation EVs.

The partnership fits Volkswagen's "In China, for China" strategy of shifting development and sourcing to China to shorten development cycles and deepen local supplier ties amid intensifying EV competition. Leadrive Technology, founded in 2017, develops power semiconductor modules, motor controllers and integrated power bricks in-house, taking a vertically integrated approach to deliver complete electric propulsion solutions.
