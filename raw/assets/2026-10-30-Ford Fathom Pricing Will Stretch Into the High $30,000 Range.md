# Ford Fathom Pricing Will Stretch Into the 'High $30,000 Range'

**Author**: Rob Stumpf | **Date**: 2026-10-29

---

You'll be able to spec out Ford's sub-$29,000 Fathom pickup to the "high $30,000 range." Ford hasn't revealed what options will be included, but says customers buying "nicer" trims care about the digital experience, which could mean recurring revenue through software subscriptions.

The $28,350 starting price "is just that," said Andrew Frick, president of Ford Blue and Model e, on the Q3 earnings call. "We'll have a range that goes to the high $30,000 range. And we'll have a couple of different offerings within that." It isn't clear whether that is the starting price of higher trims or a fully loaded truck. Pre-orders start early next year with first deliveries slated for late 2027.

CEO Jim Farley said Ford can offer very advanced ADAS, a digital camera and dashboard at incredibly low cost, and that the digital suite including embedded Apple Maps "is going to be the most exciting upsell opportunity. It's really the long-term subscription and digital experience for the customer."

Ford's Q3 figures show 1.7 million customers paying for subscriptions (Ford Pro, BlueCruise and others), up 40% year over year. Farley said retail customer retention can be up to 9 percentage points higher when combined with a software subscription.

The open question: how much of the truck's most appealing technology will be included in the purchase price, and how much will cost extra every month.
