# BYD unveils battery-electric coach C12 for the UK

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-11-04

---

BYD unveiled its recently announced electric coach for the UK market at the Euro Bus Expo in Birmingham. The 12.3-meter-long C12 DUO seats up to 57 passengers and, according to BYD, has a range of up to 340 miles (approximately 547 kilometers).

BYD recently announced its plans to enter the UK market with an electric coach. Frank Thorpe, BYD's Managing Director of Commercial Vehicle UK, shared this news in an interview, stressing that the model was developed specifically for the UK. The C12 DUO has now made its world premiere at the Euro Bus Expo. The fully electric coach features an LFP battery, with two available battery configurations of 429 kWh and 495 kWh.

BYD has revealed the coach planned for entry in the UK has the same drivetrain setup as BYD's urban and intercity buses, with two wheel hub motors of 150 kW each. While BYD's press release does not give details on its drivetrain, the company says its high-decker can charge at up to 215 kW and has a range of up to 340 miles (approximately 547 kilometres).

The new model measures 12.3 x 2.55 x 3.54 metres with a wheelbase of 6.15 metres. BYD has equipped the C12 DUO with up to four cubic metres of luggage capacity, slightly less than in conventional coaches of this size, while seating up to 57 passengers.

BYD has installed its "Blade Battery" in the high-decker exclusively on the vehicle frame. The battery packs are directly integrated into the frame, which is said to increase torsional rigidity while reducing weight. The company offers a 15-year warranty on the battery.

BYD says the vehicle was developed primarily for tour operators, school transport, and scheduled services between cities, towns, airports, and event venues.

Thorpe said the success of BYD's BEV buses in the UK in the double-decker and single-decker segments motivated the expansion into the coach sector. BYD has reportedly delivered or has orders for more than 3,000 electric buses in the UK.
