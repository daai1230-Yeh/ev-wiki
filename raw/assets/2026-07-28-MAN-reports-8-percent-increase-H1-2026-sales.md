# MAN reports 8% increase in the first half of 2026 sales as electric bus deliveries exceed 500 units (within 3,330 bus sales)

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 23 July 2026

---

MAN Truck & Bus sold around 3,300 buses in the first half of 2026 (+2.2%), while deliveries of battery-electric buses exceeded 500 units worldwide. The company sold approximately 51,000 vehicles globally in H1 2026, an increase of 8% compared with the same period in 2025, comprising around 32,000 trucks (+10%), 3,300 buses (+2%) and just under 16,000 vans (+6%).

Across all vehicle categories, MAN delivered around 1,100 fully electric vehicles during the first six months of the year, up 37% year-on-year — around 600 battery-electric trucks and more than 500 battery-electric buses, meaning buses accounted for nearly half of the manufacturer's total EV deliveries. Demand developed positively across many European markets, while the German market remained below the broader European trend.

MAN's revenue increased by €366 million year-on-year, from €6.616 billion to €6.982 billion, with new-vehicle sales generating €4.294 billion (about 61.5% of total revenue). Adjusted operating profit rose by €80 million (almost 20%) from €406 million to €486 million, with adjusted operating return on sales reaching 7.0%.

The second half of 2026 will see MAN complete its battery-electric commercial vehicle portfolio: customer deliveries of the MAN Lion's Coach 14 E are scheduled to begin at the end of the year, offering a range of up to 650 kilometres. The truck portfolio is also expanding with new battery-electric variants — the eTGX and eTGS offer around 570 km range in a typical 4×2 tractor-semitrailer configuration, while the new eTGM rigid truck offers up to 500 km.

Friedrich Baumann, Executive Board Member for Sales & Customer Solutions at MAN, said: "The sales trend shows that our broad product range is holding its own in a challenging market environment. At the same time, battery-electric vehicles are becoming increasingly important to our customers."
