# ID. Aura T6: Volkswagen's electric SUV for the Chinese smart car market

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-08-31

---

FAW-Volkswagen has launched pre-sales of the ID. Aura T6 in China, where it is being marketed as a locally manufactured, family-friendly smart car. The five-seater mid-range SUV is available in four variants, with pre-sale prices ranging from the equivalent of 17,400 to 21,800 euros.

The T6 is the first model in a new range of electrified vehicles from FAW-Volkswagen called 'ID. AURA'. The electric car's exterior design was unveiled in April this year at the Beijing Motor Show. Key specifications for the powertrain, taken from the model's Chinese type-approval application, will follow in mid-July.

The vehicle is based on the new CEA (China Electronic Architecture) electronics and electrical architecture developed jointly by Volkswagen and Xpeng, and is set to compete in the mid-range segment as a five-seater SUV.

FAW-Volkswagen has now begun pre-sales. The ID. Aura T6 is available in four trim levels with corresponding price points. Prices start at 135,900 yuan for the 540 Pro, 149,900 yuan for the 660 Pro, 159,900 yuan for the 660 Max, and 169,900 yuan for the 660 Ultra, which corresponds to the price range mentioned at the beginning of between 17,400 and 21,800 euros.

The five-seater is 4.81 metres long, 1.88 metres wide, and 1.65 metres tall. The wheelbase is 2.84 metres. The powertrain is identical across all variants, with a maximum output of 170 kW. The differences lie in the battery, as the base version uses LFP cells from Gotion High-tech and has a capacity of 59.9 kWh, while the higher trim levels feature LFP cells from CATL and offer a battery capacity of 77.5 kWh.

FAW-Volkswagen lists the range as 540 and 660 km, respectively, according to the Chinese test cycle. The registration application filed this summer also revealed the curb weight (2.03 and 2.13 metric tons, respectively) and top speeds of 180 km/h and 186 km/h, respectively.

What really sets the ID. AURA T6 apart are its smart car features. According to Chinese automotive websites, Volkswagen aims to use this model to close the gap with Chinese smart EV brands. The two higher-end variants are equipped with LiDAR sensors and utilise a driver-assistance system developed by Carizon, a joint venture between Volkswagen and the Chinese specialist Horizon Robotics.

Thanks to this technology, the ID. AURA T6 is capable of so-called NOA driving (navigation in autopilot mode) on highways and in the city, as well as parking with a memory function across multiple floors. The computing power of the Horizon Robotics chip used for this is said to amount to 420 TOPS.

The interior features a 15.6-inch central display up front, flanked by a 10.25-inch instrument cluster. Customers also have access to a 25.8-inch augmented reality head-up display. The vehicle is also compatible with the smartphone connectivity services CarPlay, HiCar, and CarLink.

In terms of storage space, the ID. AURA T6 features a 90-litre front trunk. The traditional trunk in the rear has a capacity of 576 litres, or 1,796 litres with the rear seats folded down. The front seats in this electric SUV can also be folded down, allowing them to lie flush with the rear seat cushions. This creates a 1.8-meter-long sleeping surface — which Car News China attributes to FAW-Volkswagen's commitment to offering features that are more tailored to Chinese family buyers.

The ID. AURA T6 is part of Volkswagen's current product offensive in China. This month, for example, SAIC-Volkswagen also unveiled the ID. ERA 5X, a compact electric SUV. The ID. UNYX 09 electric sedan from VW Anhui and the Jetta M6 from FAW-Volkswagen are expected to hit the Chinese market later this year.

Volkswagen has been losing market share in China for some time. The company announced as early as 2025 that it intends to counter this trend with a model offensive from its three joint ventures in China. The goal is to launch 30 new vehicles in China by 2027. According to CN EV News, FAW-Volkswagen is expected to contribute four electrified models this year alone, including two ID. AURA models.
