# BMW iX1 and iX2 qualify for UK's ECG after price cuts

**Date**: 24 July 2026

---

BMW has reduced UK prices for selected iX1 and iX2 variants, making them eligible for the government's Electric Car Grant (ECG) — the first premium German brand to qualify for the scheme's highest funding band. The £3,750 grant applies to the eDrive20 versions of both compact electric SUVs, including the newly introduced iX2 eDrive20 Sport.

The iX1 eDrive20 Sport now starts at £37,065 before the grant (down from £43,555), or £33,315 after the incentive; the iX2 eDrive20 Sport is priced identically, while the iX1 eDrive20 xLine starts at £35,315 after the grant. This undercuts the comparable petrol-powered X1 sDrive20i Sport, which starts at £37,540. The ECG requires brands to meet minimum criteria on emissions, range, sustainability and warranty support, including Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) verification.
