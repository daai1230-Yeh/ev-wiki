# BMW Just Hit 2 Million EVs Sold, And The iX3 Is Carrying The Team

**Author**: Nico DeMattia | **Date**: Aug 27, 2026, 1:53pm ET

---

*If you include plug-in hybrids, BMW is up to 3.5 million EVs sold.*

**Key points**

- BMW just hit an EV milestone: 2 million battery-electric cars sold.
- Counting PHEVs too, the number jumps to 3.5 million.
- BMW says one in two X3 orders in Europe is now electric.

When BMW launched the quirky i3 electric hatchback in 2013, EVs were far from mainstream — only a few cars like the Nissan Leaf, the Chevy Spark and the Tesla Model S were holding down the fort. Thirteen years later, BMW has sold its two-millionth EV (including electric Minis).

The two-millionth battery-electric vehicle to roll off BMW's assembly line was an i5 M60 xDrive, built at the Dingolfing, Germany plant and delivered to a customer in Spain. It's one of the brand's last-generation EVs; a new class of cars is already here to drive BMW's next phase of EV growth.

The first of BMW's Neue Klasse EVs, the iX3, is nearing 100,000 orders, according to a recent press release. In the second quarter of 2026, BMW's European EV sales increased 38% to 81,445 sales, mostly thanks to iX3 orders. In the first half of 2026, BEV sales accounted for 28% of BMW's European sales, and a third of those were iX3s. About half of all X3 orders were iX3s.

Including plug-in hybrids, BMW is up to 3.5 million electrified vehicles sold — meaning more than one in every four BMWs sold worldwide was electrified. While BMW's US EV sales have sagged this year, as they have for many other automakers, the segment has generally trended upward. BMW sold some 442,000 EVs globally last year, up slightly from the year prior.

New models could keep the momentum going. BMW has two more EVs ready to hit the market: the i3 and the iX5. Both offer sharp new styling, the brand's latest battery tech and high-tech cabins. The i3 in particular should be a major boost — it's a 3 Series, the brand's bread and butter, it's the best-looking BMW in ages, and it will go an estimated 440 miles on a charge.

Jochen Goller, BMW board member responsible for customer, brands and sales, said in a press release that the second Neue Klasse model, the BMW i3, is already seeing strong demand since orders opened ahead of its market launch.

BMW has come a long way since the original i3. At certain points in its EV journey it seemed unsure of its direction, especially as the i brand appeared dormant after the i3 and i8 stopped production. Now BMW's EVs are thriving.
