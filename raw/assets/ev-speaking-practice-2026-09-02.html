Sales Lead vs. Profit Squeeze

For most of early 2026, China's two biggest electric vehicle makers, BYD and Geely, were locked in an unusually even sales race, and analysts expected the rivalry to stay tight all year. Then the second quarter arrived, and the gap suddenly widened — not because Geely stumbled at home, but because BYD pulled further ahead overseas.

2026年上半年，中國兩大電動車龍頭比亞迪與吉利的銷售競爭幾乎不分軒輊，市場原本預期這場纏鬥會持續一整年。但進入第二季後，差距突然拉開——不是因為吉利在中國市場失利，而是因為比亞迪在海外市場進一步拉開領先。

In the first half of 2026, BYD sold about seven hundred and ninety thousand vehicles outside China, up sixty-eight percent from a year earlier and equal to roughly forty-four percent of its total sales. Geely's new-energy exports reached four hundred and seventy thousand units, an even steeper jump of one hundred and fifty-eight percent — yet still about three hundred and twenty thousand vehicles behind BYD's overseas total.

2026年上半年，比亞迪海外銷量約79萬輛，年增68%，佔總銷量約44%；吉利新能源車出口則達47萬輛，年增幅度更驚人，達158%——但出口絕對量仍比比亞迪少約32萬輛。

Overseas success, though, hasn't solved BYD's profitability problem. Net profit for the first half fell twenty point five percent year over year, even as combined revenue from its three premium brands — Denza, Fangchengbao, and Yangwang — jumped sixty-one percent. Longer inventory cycles from rapid overseas expansion, plus higher export and currency costs, have been quietly eating into the company's margins.

然而，海外的成功並未解決比亞迪的獲利問題。上半年淨利年減20.5%，儘管騰勢、方程豹及仰望三大高階品牌合計營收成長61%。海外快速擴張導致存貨周轉天數拉長，加上出口成本與匯率影響升高，正悄悄侵蝕公司的毛利。

The second quarter, however, looked like a genuine turning point. Boosted by high-margin overseas markets, BYD's quarterly net profit reached eight point two four billion yuan, up thirty percent year over year and enough to end four consecutive quarters of decline. Gross margin climbed back to eighteen point nine percent, its highest level in nearly a year.

不過，第二季看起來確實是一個真正的轉捩點。受惠於海外高毛利市場放量，比亞迪單季淨利達人民幣82.4億元，年增30%，終結連續四季的獲利衰退。毛利率也回升至18.9%，創近一年新高。

Geely, meanwhile, has taken a different path: improve the product mix first, then scale up abroad. Overall sales grew just one percent in the first half, but revenue rose fourteen point six seven percent, as average revenue per vehicle climbed from ninety-seven thousand to one hundred and twelve thousand yuan. Its premium Zeekr brand grew sales ninety-seven percent, with nearly forty percent of its models now priced above three hundred thousand yuan, and Geely has raised its full-year export target from six hundred and forty thousand to nine hundred and twenty thousand vehicles.

吉利則走了一條不同的路：先改善產品結構，再擴大海外規模。上半年整體銷量僅成長1%，但營收年增14.67%，平均單車收入由人民幣9.7萬元提高到11.2萬元。旗下高階品牌極氪銷量成長97%，其車款中近四成售價超過人民幣30萬元；吉利也將全年出口目標由64萬輛上修至92萬輛。

While Chinese automakers battle over hardware, scale, and price, two Japanese rivals are racing to catch up on software instead. On August thirty-first, Honda and Nissan announced their first major joint project since merger talks between the two companies collapsed in 2025: co-developing a next-generation in-vehicle operating system, along with the electronic control unit that will run it, with a target rollout in fiscal year twenty twenty-nine.

就在中國車廠在硬體、規模與價格上激烈廝殺之際，兩家日本車廠正轉而在軟體領域急起直追。8月31日，本田與日產宣布雙方自2025年合併談判破局以來，首個重大合作項目：共同開發次世代車載作業系統，以及運作該系統的電子控制單元（ECU），目標於2029財年導入。

Mitsubishi Motors, which is partly owned by Nissan, says it is considering joining the agreement. The shared goal is to catch up with Tesla and China's leading automakers on software-defined vehicles — cars that can gain new driver-assistance features or entertainment options through over-the-air updates long after they leave the factory floor.

部分股權由日產持有的三菱汽車表示，正考慮加入這項合作。雙方共同的目標，是在「軟體定義車輛」（software-defined vehicle）領域追上Tesla與中國主要車廠的腳步——這類車輛即使出廠後，仍可透過空中下載（OTA）更新獲得新的駕駛輔助功能或娛樂選項。

5. Useful Expressions3 min實用句型

"X pulled ahead of Y, mainly because ___."
用來說明「誰領先、原因是什麼」，適合分析銷售或競爭差距時使用。
"Even though X grew/rose, it still hasn't solved the problem of ___."
表達「即使成長了，問題仍未解決」的讓步語氣。
"Analysts expected ___, but instead ___."
描述「原本的預期」與「實際發生的結果」之間的落差。
"X has taken a different path: [do A] first, then [do B]."
描述一家公司或一個人採取的策略先後順序。
"The goal is to catch up with ___ on ___."
描述某一方希望在特定領域追上競爭對手。

6. Discussion5 min討論題

  1. BYD builds its own factories, ships, and dealerships overseas, while Geely relies more on local partners. Which expansion model do you think is more sustainable, and why?比亞迪在海外自建工廠、運輸船與銷售通路，吉利則較依賴當地合作夥伴。你認為哪種海外擴張模式比較永續？為什麼？
  2. BYD's sales are far ahead of Geely's, yet its profit fell while Geely's per-vehicle revenue rose faster. If you were an investor, which company would you rather back, and why?比亞迪的銷量遙遙領先吉利，但獲利卻下滑，而吉利的單車營收成長更快。如果你是投資人，你會比較想投資哪一家？為什麼？
  3. Geely raised its 2026 export target by roughly 44 percent. What risks come with such an aggressive jump in export volume?吉利將2026年出口目標上修了約44%。這麼激進的出口成長會帶來哪些風險？
  4. Do you think intense price competition among Chinese EV makers will eventually push smaller players out of the market? What might that consolidation look like?你認為中國電動車廠之間的激烈價格戰，最終會不會把較小的業者擠出市場？這樣的整併可能會是什麼樣貌？
  5. Honda and Nissan couldn't agree to merge in 2025, but they're now co-developing software. Is "cooperation without merging" a smart middle path, or does it just delay a harder decision?本田與日產在2025年未能達成合併，如今卻共同開發軟體。「合作但不合併」是聰明的折衷方案，還是只是延後了更困難的決定？
  6. Software-defined vehicles let automakers keep earning revenue after the sale, through OTA updates and subscriptions. How do you think this will change what it means to "own" a car?軟體定義車輛讓車廠能在售出後，透過OTA更新與訂閱持續獲利。你認為這會如何改變「擁有一台車」的意義？

7. Wrap-Up2 min總結

In sixty seconds, summarize today's story: BYD's overseas sales lead versus its profit struggles, and Geely's steadier, higher-margin growth. Then say which company you think has the stronger long-term strategy.

請用60秒總結今天的內容：比亞迪的海外銷售領先與其獲利困境，以及吉利較穩健、毛利較高的成長模式。接著說出你認為哪家公司的長期策略更強。

"In my view, ___ has the stronger long-term strategy, because ___, even though ___."
用來表達個人立場，並同時承認對立觀點的合理之處。
