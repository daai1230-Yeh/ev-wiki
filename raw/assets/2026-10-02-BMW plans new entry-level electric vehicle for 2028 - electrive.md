# BMW plans new entry-level electric vehicle for 2028

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-01

---

*Condensed summary of the article.*

- At its Capital Market Day 2026, BMW said it will unveil the all-electric iX4 (SUV coupé, likely built in Debrecen, Hungary) later this year.
- BMW plans to expand the high-volume entry segment with another Neue Klasse EV in 2028, focused on Europe; widely speculated to be the BMW i1 (internal code NB0). Body style unconfirmed.
- Neue Klasse tech (zonal E/E architecture) will roll out across all segments and drivetrains; BMW is pushing toward an "AI-Defined Vehicle".
- iX3: over 100,000 orders, 30,000+ delivered, order books full into Q2 2027, and margin parity with combustion models in key markets.
