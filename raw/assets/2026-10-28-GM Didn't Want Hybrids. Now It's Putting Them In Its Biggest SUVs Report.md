# GM Didn't Want Hybrids. Now It's Putting Them In Its Biggest SUVs: Report

**Author**: Tim Levin | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

General Motors plans to introduce hybrid versions of the Chevrolet Traverse, Buick Enclave and GMC Acadia, Reuters reports.

GM once envisioned leapfrogging hybrids straight to an all-electric future. Reuters reported on Tuesday, citing people familiar with the plans, that GM will build hybrid versions of the three-row SUVs, all built in Lansing, Michigan. GM itself announced it will build hybrids at the Lansing factory, adding that "details about nameplates and timing will be shared closer to launch."

GM ran hard at EVs over recent years, developing one of the country's broadest battery-powered lineups, but sells just one low-volume hybrid in the U.S., the Chevrolet Corvette E-Ray. It trails Ford, which sells trucks like the Maverick Hybrid and F-150 Hybrid in large numbers. Stellantis killed off its plug-in hybrids after fuel-economy regulations fell away, and has also lagged on regular hybrids as Honda, Hyundai and especially Toyota sell more of them.

In early 2024 GM first signaled a pivot, with CEO Mary Barra mentioning the return of plug-in hybrids on an earnings call; since then it has reaffirmed hybrid plans without naming vehicles. Hybrids stagnated around 2% of the U.S. market for years but are booming as EVs falter: in Q3 hybrids made up 16% of car sales versus roughly 6% for EVs.
