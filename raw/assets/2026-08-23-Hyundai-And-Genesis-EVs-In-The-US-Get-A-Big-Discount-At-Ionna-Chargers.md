# Hyundai And Genesis EVs In The U.S. Get A Big Discount At Ionna Chargers

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: Aug 21, 2026

---

*Thanks to two stackable discounts, owners can enjoy some of the cheapest EV fast chargers in the country.*

**Key points**

- Hyundai and Genesis EV owners in the U.S. can now charge at discounted rates at Ionna's Rechargeries.
- Two stackable discounts are available across Ionna's network.
- No special app or promo code is needed, but drivers must use their car brand's smartphone app or Plug & Charge.

Well-versed EV owners in the United States already know that Ionna's charging stations are among the best out there. Recently, JD Power put the up-and-coming network at the top of its list — no surprise, given how the stations are clean and the stalls always work.

Now, Hyundai and Genesis owners stateside get to enjoy Ionna's services at a discount. The two automakers have partnered with the charging operator to offer two stackable discounts on charging, available starting August 20.

There's a 10% discount that expires on September 30, and another 10% discount that will be available for the foreseeable future. The network currently charges a $0.39/kWh (plus tax) base rate for everyone. With the 20% discount, Genesis and Hyundai EV drivers can charge for $0.31/kWh.

The discounts are automatically applied to the charging session, but not if the on-site credit card readers are used. Instead, drivers need to initiate charging through their car manufacturer's smartphone app or by using Plug & Charge, if it has been set up before plugging in and the vehicle supports it.

According to Hyundai, one of Ionna's founding partners, five EVs are currently compatible with in-app charging and Plug & Charge: the 2022 and newer Ioniq 5, 2025 and newer Ioniq 5 N, 2026 and newer Ioniq 9, and 2025 and newer Kona Electric. Some 2022–2024 Ioniq 5 models only support in-app charging.

The Genesis GV60 and Genesis Electrified GV70 from the 2026 model year onward support both in-app charging and Plug & Charge, so owners can enjoy the Ionna discounts.

Interestingly, the Hyundai Ioniq 6, recently discontinued in the U.S., is not on the list of compatible models. Hyundai mentioned that in-app charging will be available on additional models later this year. Kia, also an Ionna partner, doesn't offer charging discounts for its EVs yet.

Earlier this year, BMW and Mini EV owners in the U.S. got a similar deal, with up to 20% off at Ionna Rechargeries through September 30.
