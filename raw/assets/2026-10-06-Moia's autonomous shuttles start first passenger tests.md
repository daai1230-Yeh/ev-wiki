# Moia's autonomous shuttles start first passenger tests

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-10-05

---

Beep, the US-based provider of autonomous shuttles, is launching the next test phase of the VW ID. Buzz fleet by Moia. In Orlando, passenger transport with selected test participants has begun.

Moia America, the US subsidiary of Volkswagen's mobility brand, is in an intensive testing phase as part of its collaboration with Beep. In the community of Lake Nona in Orlando (Florida), passenger trips using autonomous VW ID. Buzz AD vehicles have now begun.

Invited passengers can book the autonomous VW buses via the Beep app and use them on three fixed routes with designated pick-up and drop-off points. Interested parties can also join the Beep waitlist for future tests.

Paul DeLong, Co-President for MOIA America, called welcoming riders an important milestone as the company introduces its first public-facing autonomous mobility experience in the U.S.

This is the first time Moia vehicles are on the roads in traffic at normal speeds and with passengers. To ensure safety, the buses are accompanied by trained personnel who can actively intervene in the driving process. Passengers are asked to provide feedback.

The VW ID. Buzz AD fleet was provided by Moia America and equipped with autonomous driving technology from Mobileye for use with Beep. The goal is to supply Beep with 5,000 autonomous vehicles for its "Shared Autonomous Mobility Services" — not robotaxis for individual transport, but a cost-efficient, AI-powered solution for public passenger transport in the US.
