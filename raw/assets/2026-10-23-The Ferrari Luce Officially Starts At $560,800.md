# The Ferrari Luce Officially Starts At $560,800

**Author**: Nico DeMattia | **Date**: 2026-10-22

---

The first-ever electric Ferrari makes a Rolls-Royce Cullinan look like a bargain.

First reported by Car and Driver and confirmed to InsideEVs by Ferrari, the Luce starts at $560,800.

For that money you get four electric motors combining for 1,035 horsepower, powered by a 122 kWh battery pack that should give around 280 miles of range. That is little compared to most Teslas, Lucids, Rivians, BMWs and Hyundais, but few cars that beat it on range come near its power and performance.

The Lucid Air Sapphire is more powerful and faster for about half the price, but it isn't a Ferrari. The Luce is the most divisive-looking Ferrari ever, so it will be interesting to see how customers receive it. The author would wait for something like the Bentley Torcal, expected to be significantly less expensive.
