# electrive TODAY Digest — July 10, 2026

**Source**: electrive global daily newsletter

---

**VW reportedly plans plant closures from 2031**: Volkswagen's executive board is reportedly considering ending production at several German plants from 2031 onwards, including Zwickau, Emden, Hanover and Audi's Neckarsulm site.

**China targets 30% NEV fleet by 2030**: China has published its plan to limit the peak of CO₂ emissions. By 2030, vehicles with alternative powertrains could make up 30% of the nation's total vehicle fleet. The government also wants to expand charging and hydrogen infrastructure.

**Kia EV2 Long Range now eligible for UK Electric Car Grant**: Kia UK has confirmed that the EV2 Long Range is now eligible for the maximum Band 1 Electric Car Grant, reducing the vehicle's on-the-road price by £3,750.

**Fiat Topolino now available in US — but it's not road-legal**: Just days after opening UK order books for the Topolino, Fiat has made the diminutive electric quadricycle available in the USA, though it's yet to be made road-legal.

**Number of the day**: Daimler Truck sold 1,405 battery-electric trucks and buses in the second quarter of 2026, 21% more than in the same period last year.

**AESC starts production of 46120 cells for BMW iX5**: AESC has begun series production of its new 46120 cylindrical battery cells, which will power future BMW Group EVs. The BMW iX5 will be the first announced model to use the technology.

**Posco and Hyundai partner on novel electrical steel for electric motors**: The firms will develop high-silicon electrical steel — a precursor material for stator/rotor production — aiming to increase motor efficiency and advance the material technology to series application in EVs.

**Ford issues US recall of Mustang Mach-E electric crossovers**: The recall affects certain Mach-Es from the 2021, 2022, and 2023 model years due to a potential issue with the rear differential pinion shaft at risk of fracture.

**Ferrari Luce EV sells out China allocation at $586,600**: Ferrari's first electric model, the Luce, launched in China at 3.988 million yuan. The initial allocation of 88 vehicles reportedly sold out immediately.

**Allego switches operator backend to Ampeco**: Dutch charging provider Allego is switching to Ampeco for the backend of its charging network, to manage over 35,000 charging points across 16 European countries.

**Vattenfall transfers German B2B charging business to Mobility House**: Vattenfall is divesting its German fleet charging business and will gradually transfer it to The Mobility House Solutions, following a similar exit by TotalEnergies.

**FirstGroup brand to run zero-emission Sizewell C shuttle service**: Specialist Passenger Solutions will operate shuttle services for the Sizewell C construction site in Suffolk using a fleet of hydrogen and battery-electric buses.

**Delhi adds 300 electric buses to its public transport fleet**: Indian capital Delhi deployed 300 electric buses for public transport use and inaugurated four electric bus depots on the same day.

**Short circuit**: Volkswagen has replaced lawnmowers with 100 sheep at its solar park near Poznań, Poland, to help maintain the site while exploring how agriculture and solar power can share the same land.
