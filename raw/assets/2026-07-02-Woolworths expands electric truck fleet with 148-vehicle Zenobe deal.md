# Woolworths expands electric truck fleet with 148-vehicle Zenobe deal

**Author**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-02

---

*Note: the live electrive.com page could not be reached during this clip (Chrome connection was interrupted); this summary is reconstructed from search-indexed excerpts of the article and corroborating coverage (Zenobē, The Driven).*

Australian retailer Woolworths has announced a significant expansion of its electric fleet operations following a new deal with fleet electrification firm Zenobe, which will see 148 battery-electric trucks deployed in what's being described as the country's largest commercial electric truck rollout to date.

Under the agreement, Zenobe will supply and manage a fleet of Foton T5 electric trucks for Woolworths under its Electric Vehicle-as-a-Service model. This sees Zenobe provide an "end-to-end" EV leasing product that includes vehicles, charging infrastructure, maintenance and battery services, plus fleet support, all through a monthly payment.

The rollout will support Woolworths' last-mile grocery deliveries across New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland, South Australia and Western Australia. Deliveries are already underway, with the full fleet expected to enter service by the end of the year.

The agreement builds on a $6 million AUD investment by the Clean Energy Finance Corporation into Zenobe last year, which backed an earlier deployment of 60 electric trucks by the retailer.
