1. Warm-Up 暖身3 min

When people talk about electric vehicles, they almost always mean private cars. Do you think buses, trucks and ferries deserve more attention than they get?

大家談電動車時，講的幾乎都是私家車。你覺得公車、卡車和渡輪是不是應該得到更多關注？

Think about the buses in your city. If every one of them were replaced by an electric model tomorrow, what would you notice first as a passenger?

想想你所在城市的公車。如果明天全部換成電動車款，身為乘客的你，最先注意到的會是什麼？

Governments usually fund public transport with taxpayers' money. Is that a better use of public funds than giving subsidies to people who buy private electric cars?

政府通常用納稅人的錢補助大眾運輸。比起補貼買電動私家車的民眾，這樣運用公帑是不是更好？

2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文6 min

The most visible electric vehicles in the world may soon be the ones nobody owns. In late August, the Indian city of Chennai put 130 new electric buses into service, and on the other side of the world, Estonia awarded a contract for a five-megawatt charger that will keep an electric ferry moving between two islands.

世界上最顯眼的電動車，也許很快會是沒有人「擁有」的那些。八月下旬，印度清奈有 130 輛新電動公車投入營運；而在地球的另一端，愛沙尼亞則發包了一座 5 百萬瓦的充電站，用來讓一艘電動渡輪往返兩座島嶼之間。

Chennai's transport operator, the Metropolitan Transport Corporation, spent 2.345 billion rupees, or roughly 21 million euros, on this first batch. Eighty of the buses run on fifteen routes out of the Alandur depot, and the remaining fifty cover ten routes from the Central depot.

清奈的運輸業者「大都會運輸公司」為這第一批車投入 23.45 億盧比，約 2,100 萬歐元。其中 80 輛從 Alandur 車庫發車，行駛 15 條路線；其餘 50 輛則自中央車庫出發，服務 10 條路線。

This is only the opening move. The full order covers 750 low-floor electric buses, 500 of them air-conditioned and 250 without, at a total cost of 13.37 billion rupees, or about 120 million euros. Once delivered, they will be operated out of seven depots across the city.

而這只是第一步。整筆訂單共 750 輛低地板電動公車，其中 500 輛有空調、250 輛沒有，總金額 133.7 億盧比，約 1.2 億歐元。全數交車後，將由全市七座車庫調度營運。

The vehicle itself is unglamorous but well specified. Built by Switch Mobility, a subsidiary of Ashok Leyland, the twelve-metre EiV12 has a floor height of just 400 millimetres, seats 39 passengers and takes another 24 standing. A 235-kilowatt motor gives it a top speed of 75 kilometres per hour, while a 283-kilowatt-hour lithium-iron-phosphate pack delivers a range of up to 200 kilometres.

這款車本身不華麗，但規格扎實。由 Ashok Leyland 子公司 Switch Mobility 打造的 12 米 EiV12，地板高度僅 400 毫米，可坐 39 人、再站 24 人。235 千瓦的馬達使其極速達到每小時 75 公里，283 度的磷酸鋰鐵電池組則提供最高 200 公里的續航力。

Estonia's project is smaller in unit count but far more demanding technically. The Norwegian firm PSW Power and Automation will install two charging points on each side of the Virtsu to Kuivastu crossing, each capable of delivering up to five megawatts through a 1,500-volt architecture. That is roughly twenty times the power of a fast charger for a passenger car.

愛沙尼亞的案子車輛數少得多，技術難度卻高上許多。挪威公司 PSW Power and Automation 將在 Virtsu 至 Kuivastu 航線的兩岸各設兩個充電點，每個透過 1,500 伏特架構最高可輸出 5 百萬瓦——大約是小客車快充樁的二十倍功率。

The logic behind such enormous power is simple. A ferry sits in port for only a few minutes between crossings, so the battery has to take on a large amount of energy in a very short window. The new vessel, due at the end of 2028, will carry up to 380 passengers and 110 cars, and it has already come in at 50 million euros — about eight million more than originally budgeted.

這麼大功率背後的邏輯很單純：渡輪每趟之間只在港內停留幾分鐘，電池必須在極短的時間窗口內吃進大量電力。這艘預計 2028 年底完工的新船，可載 380 名乘客與 110 輛小客車，造價已達 5,000 萬歐元，比原先預算超出約 800 萬歐元。

Put side by side, the two projects tell the same story from different angles. Electrification is spreading fastest where vehicles run fixed routes, return to the same place every night, and are paid for by an operator rather than a household. Whether that quiet progress is a better investment than subsidising private cars is a question worth arguing about.

把兩個案子並排來看，其實是同一個故事的不同角度。電動化推進最快的地方，是那些路線固定、每晚回到同一個地點、而且由業者而非家庭買單的車輛。這種安靜的進展，是不是比補貼私家車更划算的投資，值得好好辯一辯。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型3 min

"This is only the first batch — another 620 units are still in the pipeline."

說明「這只是開始，後面還有」。把 620 units 換成你自己的數字即可。

"Put side by side, the two projects tell the same story from different angles."

比較兩件事時很好用的開場，比 "Compare A and B" 自然得多。

"The logic behind it is simple: the ferry only sits in port for a few minutes."

先說「道理很簡單」，再用冒號帶出理由，聽起來有條理又不囉唆。

"It came in at 50 million euros, about eight million more than originally budgeted."

"come in at" 用來講最終金額或數字落在哪裡，是很道地的商業說法。

"Electrification spreads fastest where vehicles run fixed routes and return to the same place every night."

用 "where + 條件" 來描述某件事在什麼情況下最容易發生，適合表達趨勢與觀察。

6. Discussion 討論題5 min

Chennai will run 750 electric buses out of seven depots. What is the hardest part of that plan — buying the buses, or building the charging infrastructure to keep them running?

清奈將由七座車庫調度 750 輛電動公車。這個計畫最難的部分是什麼——買車，還是建置讓它們持續運轉的充電設施？

The EiV12 has a range of only 200 kilometres. For a city bus, is that a serious limitation or is it more than enough? Explain your reasoning.

EiV12 續航僅 200 公里。對一輛市區公車而言，這是嚴重限制還是綽綽有餘？請說明你的理由。

Estonia's ferry is already eight million euros over budget and will not be ready until the end of 2028. When a green project overruns like this, should the government cancel it, or push through?

愛沙尼亞的渡輪已超出預算 800 萬歐元，且要到 2028 年底才完工。當綠色計畫像這樣超支時，政府應該喊停，還是硬著頭皮做完？

Some argue public money should go to buses and ferries, since one bus replaces dozens of cars. Others say subsidies for private EVs move the market faster. Which case do you find stronger, and why?

有人主張公帑應投入公車與渡輪，因為一輛公車能取代數十輛汽車；也有人認為補貼私人電動車能更快帶動市場。你覺得哪一方論點更有力？為什麼？

India chose lithium-iron-phosphate batteries for these buses rather than higher-energy chemistries. What trade-offs do you think went into that decision?

印度為這批公車選用磷酸鋰鐵電池，而非能量密度更高的化學配方。你認為這個決定背後有哪些取捨？

If your own city announced that all its buses would be electric within five years, what would you want the mayor to promise you first?

如果你居住的城市宣布五年內公車全面電動化，你最希望市長先向你承諾什麼？

7. Wrap-Up 總結2 min

In sixty seconds, summarise the two projects for your teacher: who is doing what, how much it costs, and what the two have in common.

用 60 秒向老師摘要這兩個案子：誰在做什麼、花了多少錢，以及兩者的共通點。

Then take a position with this frame: "If I had to choose where the next hundred million euros should go, I would put it into ______, because ______."

接著用這個句型表達立場：「如果我必須決定下一筆一億歐元該投向哪裡，我會投入 ______，因為 ______。」
