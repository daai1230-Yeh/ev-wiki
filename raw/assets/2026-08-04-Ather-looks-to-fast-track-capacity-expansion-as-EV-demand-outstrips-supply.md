# Ather looks to fast-track capacity expansion as EV demand outstrips supply

**Author**: ETAuto | **Date**: 2026-08-04

---

With adequate production capacity, Ather Energy CEO Tarun Mehta said the company could have sold an additional 13,000-15,000 units, as EV demand continues to outstrip supply.

Ather's underlying demand for electric two-wheelers is estimated at around 240,000–250,000 units per month, versus total industry e2W registrations of about 179,075 through June — a gap of over 60,000-70,000 vehicles versus underlying demand. Ather has already crossed 90 percent utilisation at its Hosur plant, with a new plant in Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra, under construction (initial capacity of 500,000 units annually, with a longer-term target of 1 million units annually). Paid pre-orders were up 158% year-on-year to 1.5 lakh units in Q1 FY27, alongside a 95% increase in customer enquiries.
