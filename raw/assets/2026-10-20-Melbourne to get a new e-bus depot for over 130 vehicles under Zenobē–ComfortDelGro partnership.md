# Melbourne to get a new e-bus depot for over 130 vehicles under Zenobē–ComfortDelGro partnership

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-19

---

Zenobē and ComfortDelGro Australia (CDC) announced on 13 October 2026 a partnership to electrify the Truganina bus depot in Melbourne's western suburbs, with charging infrastructure for more than 130 electric buses. It is part of CDC Victoria's delivery of the Victorian Government's Metropolitan Zero Emission Bus (ZEB) Franchises (three awarded in 2024) and will support the first electric bus fleet in Melbourne's western growth corridor.

The programme includes 65 dual-lead DC chargers, with provision for 20 more charging points, plus civil and electrical works. Zenobē handles power and charging modelling, grid connection design and charger installation, sized for current needs and future expansion. The investment value, charger power ratings and completion date have not been disclosed.

Elsewhere in Melbourne, Kinetic has expanded charging at its Sunshine West depot and fitted an overhead gantry charger at Preston. CDC Victoria began receiving 50 hybrid buses in 2021. In the UK, Zenobē has worked with ComfortDelGro subsidiary Metroline, delivering more than 450 charging points across five London depots, with more scheduled for 2027.

Services in Melbourne's west have been expanded by 470 weekly trips across routes 170, 180, 190 and 192, with higher frequencies and longer hours; electric buses will be introduced progressively, though vehicle numbers and timetable were not specified. Victoria aims to transition its public bus fleet to zero-emission by 2035.

CDC Victoria CEO Jeff Wilson called the depot a significant milestone for the rollout of cleaner, quieter journeys and welcomed Zenobē's global expertise. Zenobē ANZ Country Director Gareth Ridge said electrifying a depot at this scale is "an infrastructure and operational feat", designed around how CDC's fleet will operate today and grow over time.

**In brief**
- Capacity: more than 130 electric buses, with room to grow.
- Chargers: 65 dual-lead DC chargers plus provision for 20 more.
- Operator: CDC Victoria, a ComfortDelGro Australia subsidiary.
