# BMW opens battery assembly plant for the new iX5

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-11-16

---

BMW has officially opened its new battery assembly plant in Woodruff, South Carolina, and started series production. The factory assembles the high-voltage batteries for the new BMW iX5, marking an important milestone ahead of the electric SUV's production ramp-up.

BMW has been preparing its battery assembly plant in Woodruff since summer 2023, when the first test and pre-series batteries rolled off the production line. In July this year, the Munich-based carmaker also provided initial insights into battery production in Woodruff, giving a clearer picture of how the plant operates. Now, the facility has been officially inaugurated and series production of sixth-generation (Gen6) high-voltage batteries has begun. These batteries will be installed in the new BMW iX5 and future electric models at the neighbouring BMW plant in Spartanburg.

BMW unveiled the new BMW iX5 in June. For the first time, the X5 SUV is also available as a fully electric model. The iX5 features an advanced chassis and driver assistance systems designed to provide greater comfort and safety, as well as a high-voltage battery with a capacity of 141 kWh.

This is the battery now being assembled in Woodruff. The 46120 cylindrical cells are reportedly supplied by AESC. Following Debrecen in Hungary, Irlbach-Straßkirchen in Lower Bavaria and Shenyang in China, Woodruff is now the fourth location in the BMW Group's production network to manufacture Gen6 high-voltage batteries in series.

## Six electric models planned for Spartanburg

The batteries produced in Woodruff will not be used exclusively in the BMW iX5, as BMW Board Member for Production Raymond Wittmann explains: "By 2030, we will be assembling at least six fully electric models at Plant Spartanburg. The start of series production of Gen6 high-voltage batteries in Woodruff is therefore much more than the successful launch of a new site: it lays the foundation for the first-ever series production of fully electric BMW vehicles at Plant Spartanburg and on the American continent. This marks the beginning of the next chapter in the success story of the largest plant in our global production network."

For its in-house-developed Gen6 high-voltage battery, BMW has introduced advanced production processes designed to be intelligent and highly efficient. The company uses a cell-to-pack approach, integrating the cylindrical cells directly into the high-voltage battery without first assembling them into conventional battery modules. This eliminates a production step required in previous generations.

AI assistants and agents developed specifically for the plant analyse production data. Together with more than 300 employees and 250 robots, they are intended to help ensure defect-free battery production. Even before the plant was commissioned, digital twins of the manufacturing process and virtual reality applications helped with planning and staff training.

## Woodruff part of $1.7 billion investment

The project began in June 2023 with the groundbreaking ceremony for the Woodruff plant. Construction started in September 2023, equipment installation followed in autumn 2024, and pre-series test runs began in summer 2025. The investment in Woodruff forms part of a broader US strategy under which BMW has committed approximately $1.7 billion to electric mobility in the Spartanburg area, including partnerships with local battery cell suppliers such as AESC.
