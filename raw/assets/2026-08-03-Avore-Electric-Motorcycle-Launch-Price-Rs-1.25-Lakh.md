# Avore Electric Motorcycle Launch Price Rs 1.25 Lakh – 260 Km Range, 114 Kmph Top Speed

**Author**: Sagar Patel | **Date**: 2026-07-28

---

Avore Electric has entered India's electric motorcycle market with the launch of its first product, Avore EX. Developed by Ahmedabad-based Avore Electric from the house of Samarth E-Mobility, the new electric motorcycle is available in three variants — EX 2S, EX 2 and EX 1. Prices start at Rs 1,24,999 (ex-showroom).

Bookings for Avore EX are now open at Rs 799, while customer deliveries are scheduled to commence in around two months. The company says the motorcycle has been developed over three years, with much of its EV technology engineered in-house.

**Three variants, up to 260 km range**

Avore EX 2S sits at the top of the range and offers an IDC-certified range of 260 km. EX 2 is rated at 255 km, while the entry-level EX 1 offers 160 km on a single charge. Maximum motor output ranges from 7.5 kW to 10.5 kW, while all three versions are rated for 250 Nm of peak wheel torque. The range-topping EX 2S can achieve a claimed top speed of 114 km/h, whereas EX 2 and EX 1 top out at 100 km/h.

Power comes from a rare-earth-free Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor (PMSM). One key engineering highlight is Avore's Integrated Motor + Swingarm Powertrain, or Drive Link Arm, developed to improve weight distribution, structural rigidity and handling.

**20-80% charging in around 2 hours**

All three variants get a 1,500W onboard charger, allowing owners to plug the motorcycle directly into a standard 5A or 15A household socket without a separate charger installation. Avore claims a 20-80% charging time of around two hours, with a complete 0-100% charge taking approximately four hours. The battery pack has been homologated by NATRAX, the Battery Management System validated by ICAT, and the battery is covered by a 5-year/60,000 km warranty.

**Developed in India — 120+ IP applications filed**

Avore EX is based on the company's AVR electric motorcycle platform and uses its proprietary AVORE Source technology stack. The company claims to have developed nine critical vehicle systems in-house at its Ahmedabad R&D facility, including the battery pack, BMS, electric motor, motor controller, power control module, DC-DC converter, onboard charger, display and operating system. Avore says more than 120 intellectual property applications have been filed during development, and the motorcycle has undergone more than 1 lakh km of road testing and over 2,500 hours of dynamometer validation.
