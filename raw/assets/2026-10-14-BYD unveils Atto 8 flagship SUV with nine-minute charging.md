# BYD unveils Atto 8 flagship SUV with nine-minute charging

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

*Condensed summary of the electrive report, not verbatim.*

BYD showed the Atto 8 at the 2026 Paris Motor Show: a 5.04 x 2.00 x 1.76 m electric SUV seating up to seven, with a 115 kWh battery and over 600 km range. It is the first BYD in Europe with Flash Charging, based on the second-generation Blade Battery: 10-70% in five minutes and 10-97% in nine minutes under BYD's stated conditions; at -30 C charging takes only about three minutes longer. A compatible Flash Charger is required. BYD expects 3,000 in Europe by end of 2027 and plans 300 in Germany by end of 2026.

European launch is Q1 2027, replacing the BYD Tang as flagship. Pricing is unannounced; with the Tang now discounted from EUR 75,000 to EUR 47,990, the Atto 8 is unlikely to start below EUR 75,000.

## Versions
- **Design RWD:** 340 kW, 365 Nm, 0-100 km/h in 8.0 s, up to 653 km WLTP.
- **Excellence AWD:** 570 kW, 775 Nm, 0-100 km/h in 4.6 s, up to 602 km WLTP.

## Interior
Seven seats, roughly 2.4 m2 panoramic roof, leather/suede materials. 10.25-inch instrument cluster, 26-inch head-up display, 15.6-inch touchscreen with Google Maps, Assistant and Play Store. Heated and ventilated front and second-row seats with massage, three-zone climate control, a -6 to +50 C cooling/heating compartment, two 50 W wireless pads and four USB-C ports. Boot: 270 L behind the third row, up to 1,960 L folded, plus a 136 L frunk.

BYD EVP Stella Li: Flash Charging is "recharging as fast as refuelling, removing range anxiety."
