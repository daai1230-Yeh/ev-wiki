# South Africa: electric off-road safari vehicle revealed

**Author**: Stefan Koeller | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

Unlike many EVs used in tourism, the newly introduced Thula was not converted from an existing off-road vehicle but designed from the ground up as a purpose-built electric safari vehicle.

South African company Thula Solutions unveiled its first locally developed electric safari vehicle in Kruger National Park, built for quiet, emission-free wildlife observation. It runs a 65 kWh battery giving an estimated range of around 200 km — enough, the developers say, for roughly three days of typical safari operations before recharging, ideally at solar-powered lodges.

Most of the vehicle was manufactured in South Africa; only the electric motors and a small number of specialised components were imported. The chassis was developed with the Vehicle Dynamics Group at the University of Pretoria, while Cape Town-based Balancell supplied the battery technology.

Funding from the Industrial Development Corporation helped move the project past the prototype phase. The team now aims to produce around 50 vehicles per month at a dedicated production facility.
