# Busworld Europe 2027 takes shape: exhibitor list and floor plans already online

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-19

---

Busworld Europe returns to Brussels from 16 to 21 October 2027, with the exhibitor list and floor plans online one year ahead. The 2025 edition drew 45,427 visitors from 116 countries, 559 exhibitors and 238 buses and coaches.

Newcomers announced so far include battery maker CATL, bus manufacturer Asiastar and ITS provider Pysae. The show covers vehicles, propulsion, infrastructure, software and services for manufacturers, suppliers, operators and authorities. Visitor pre-registration is open ahead of full registration, and more on exhibitors, launches and technologies is expected in coming months.

**Congress**: the 2025 Busworld Europe Congress had 1,242 participants, including 22 European Commission representatives, across 39 sessions on zero-emission mobility, digitalisation, vehicle safety, socio-economic issues and long-distance coach operations. For 2027, Busworld ran a survey with Arthur D. Little and Ipsos among operators, industry representatives and policymakers to shape the agenda.
