# Xpeng G9L price revealed: luxury eSUV starts at €74,600

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

*Condensed summary of the electrive report, not verbatim.*

Orders are open for Xpeng's flagship G9L, launched in 64 countries and shown at the Paris Motor Show; German prices start at EUR 74,600 and European deliveries begin December 2026. The 5.12 m long extended G9 has an 800 V architecture, 540 kW peak DC charging (10-80% in under 12 minutes in optimal conditions; most European chargers such as the 400 kW Alpitronic HYC 400 cap lower), a 110 kWh (gross) NCM battery and a standard 22 kW AC charger.

## Variants (Ultra trim only at launch)
- RWD Long Range, 7 seats: 270 kW / 367 hp, up to 591 km WLTP, from EUR 74,600.
- AWD Performance, 6 seats: 430 kW / 585 hp, up to 553 km WLTP, from EUR 79,600.
- AWD Performance, 7 seats: 430 kW / 585 hp, up to 553 km WLTP, from EUR 85,600.

## Equipment
Nappa leather throughout; AI-controlled "Active Adaptive Seating" in the front (Xpeng claims a world first); second-row individual seats with heating, ventilation and ten-point massage; electrically folding third row. A 21.4-inch 3K retractable rear display, 23-speaker audio, a child-oriented zone, a 17.3-inch central screen with Google Maps, an 8.8-inch cluster and a head-up display.

## Driver assistance and production
Built on Xpeng's Turing AI chips; the VLA 2.0-based Next Generation Pilot is expected to enable Level 2++ driving via OTA from 2027, subject to regulation. European cars are built by Magna Steyr in Graz, likely via SKD assembly, alongside the G6, G9 and P7+. A pre-series G9L was to drive the 1,324 km from Graz to Paris as a demonstration.
