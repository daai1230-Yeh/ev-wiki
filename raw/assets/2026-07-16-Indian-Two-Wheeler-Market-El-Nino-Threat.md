# Indian two-wheeler market faces El Nino threat amid rural slowdown

**Author**: ETAuto Staff | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

---

As rural buyers adopt a wait-and-watch approach amid patchy rains, rising prices, and uncertain geopolitical conditions, India's two-wheeler market faces a growing El Niño threat. El Niño is expected to persist through the end of 2026, with India's southwest monsoon rainfall projected at 94% of the Long Period Average — classified as below normal.

Weaker rural incomes and discretionary spending could depress demand for entry-level two-wheelers and tractors, as rural consumers prioritize immediate working-capital needs over new vehicle purchases. Companies significantly dependent on rural sales — including Hero MotoCorp, Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor, and Eicher Motors — are seen as most exposed to the slowdown.
