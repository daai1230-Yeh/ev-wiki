# Europe registers 8,141 electric buses in H1 2026, growing +49%: Yutong first, Iveco takes second place with 1,156 units

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-08-31

---

Electric bus registrations in Europe reached 8,141 units in the first half of 2026, up 49% from 5,477 units in the same period of 2025.

The figures, released by DVV Media Group in its Q2 2026 European Bus Data report, cover buses above 8 tonnes GVW and exclude trolleybuses.

The 8,141 battery-electric registrations recorded in six months are equivalent to almost 69% of the 11,819 units registered during the whole of 2025 in the updated DVV dataset. The same source reports 7,843 electric buses in 2024.

## European electric bus registrations grow 49% in H1 2026

Registrations of buses with alternative drivetrains reached 11,315 units in the first six months of 2026, compared with 8,292 in H1 2025. Battery-electric buses accounted for 8,141 of the H1 2026 total.

Hybrid bus registrations increased from 1,348 to 1,449 units, while CNG buses rose from 1,195 to 1,328. Fuel-cell bus registrations increased from 272 units in H1 2025 to 397 in H1 2026.

Battery-electric buses therefore represented 71.9% of the four alternative-driveline categories.

The longer-term series published by DVV shows 4,152 electric bus registrations in 2022, 6,354 in 2023, 7,843 in 2024 and 11,819 in 2025.

The geographical perimeter comprises the EU27 plus the United Kingdom, Iceland, Norway and Switzerland, with the country and city-bus tables also including Serbia and Ukraine.

## Italy leads European electric bus registrations, followed by the UK and Poland

Italy was the largest national market for battery-electric buses in H1 2026, with 1,559 registrations, up from 369 units in H1 2025. The United Kingdom followed with 1,075 electric buses, down from 1,299, while Poland reached 1,060 units, compared with 148 one year earlier.

Norway registered 660 electric buses, followed by Germany with 553 and the Netherlands with 510. Portugal recorded 473 units, France 337, Sweden 326 and Spain 223.

Other markets included Belgium with 140 registrations, Austria with 128, Switzerland with 96, Finland with 88 and Lithuania with 69. Romania registered 343, while Denmark moved from 161 to 208. Greece recorded eight, compared with 100 a year earlier.

## Yutong leads manufacturers with 1,636 electric buses, Solaris exceeds 1,000 units

The manufacturer ranking places Yutong first with 1,636 electric buses registered in H1 2026. Iveco/Heuliez ranks second with a combined 1,156 units. Solaris follows with 1,022 units.

BYD registered 681 electric buses, followed by MAN with 584, Mercedes with 553 and Wrightbus with 542. Karsan reached 321 registrations, while VDL recorded 202, Alexander Dennis 157 and Volvo 153. The ranking continues with King Long at 146, Zhongtong at 131, Irizar at 109, Golden Dragon at 103 and CRRC at 100.

## Battery-electric buses reach 61.2% of the European city bus market

In H1 2026, 7,480 battery-electric city buses were registered out of a total city bus market of 12,215 vehicles. Electric buses therefore accounted for 61.2% of the city bus market, up from 57.9% in H1 2025 and 49.6% in H1 2024.

## Hydrogen bus registrations reach 397 units in H1 2026

Italy recorded the highest national volume with 178 hydrogen buses, followed by Poland with 119. Austria registered 34, France 27 and Germany 26.

Solaris accounted for 256 hydrogen buses. Neso Bus followed with 39 registrations, MCV with 36, Mercedes with 34, Wrightbus with 21 and Caetano with six.

## Hybrid and CNG buses in the first half of 2026

Hybrid bus registrations totalled 1,449 units. Germany was the largest market with 718 registrations, followed by Spain with 296 and France with 148.

CNG registrations reached 1,328 units. France registered 593 CNG buses, Italy 295 and Greece 189.

If battery-electric and fuel-cell registrations are combined, Yutong would remain first with 1,636 zero-emission buses, followed by Solaris with 1,278 units (1,022 BEV + 256 FCEV) and Iveco/Heuliez with 1,156.
