# Skywell pauses UK sales as importer ceases operations

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

Chinese EV manufacturer Skywell has suspended new vehicle sales in the UK after its importer, Innovation Automotive, ceased operations. The brand says it is seeking a new distribution partner, while arrangements for warranty, servicing and parts support are still being finalised.

Innovation Automotive ceased trading at the end of June, prompting the suspension. According to the former importer, all remaining vehicle stock was purchased and registered before the closure. Skywell entered the British market in 2024 and currently sells a single model, the BE11 electric SUV.

David Clark, General Manager at Innovation Automotive, attributed the decision to the investment required to remain competitive, telling The Sun: "The UK is one of the most competitive new car markets in Europe... it requires continuous, significant investment from the OEM or the appointed distributor to remain competitive." He stressed the closure was unrelated to the manufacturer: "The closure of Innovation Automotive is in no way a reflection upon Skywell, who are now seeking an alternative distributor for the UK market."

The former importer said it still holds a substantial inventory of spare parts and is working to transfer these to a new operator, with warranty administration also expected to transfer. Registration data cited by the importer shows 127 Skywell vehicles currently registered in the UK, including 64 registered in June during the wind-down; the brand was previously represented by 16 dealerships nationwide. Skywell's plans to expand its UK portfolio beyond the BE11 are now on hold pending a new importer.
