# Polestar 4 SUV variant announced, starting from €57,900

**Author**: Daniel Bönnighausen | **Date**: 2026-09-02

---

The Swedish-Chinese EV manufacturer has unveiled the Polestar 4 SUV. Unlike the Polestar 3, the new body variant of the 4 retains the 400-volt platform, like the coupe. In Germany, the SUV can now be ordered starting at €57,900, with first deliveries planned for the fourth quarter.

In February, Polestar announced plans to significantly expand its model range with four new models within three years. Alongside the now-launched Polestar 5, the manufacturer unveiled a new generation of the Polestar 2 for 2027 and the Polestar 7 for 2028. Another variant of the Polestar 4 was announced for the end of 2026 without further details at the time; this summer the existing model was updated and officially designated the Polestar 4 Coupé, and it's now clear the new variant is the Polestar 4 SUV.

The new model isn't a "raised" coupé — ground clearance remains identical at 166 mm. Differences begin behind the B-pillar: the roofline extends more linearly toward the rear, with a roof rail and a steeper, vertically positioned tailgate. The SUV also features a conventional rear window, whereas the Coupé relies exclusively on a camera and digital rear-view mirror.

**Dimensions and practicality.** The SUV is 4.84 m long, just over 2.14 m wide (including mirrors), with a wheelbase of almost three meters — identical to the Coupé. The only size difference is height: at 1.556 m, the SUV is 22 mm taller than the 1.534 m Coupé, giving rear passengers 25 mm of extra headroom. Boot space behind the rear seats is 530 liters (just 4 liters more than the Coupé), but loaded to the roof it offers 655 liters, and up to 1,665 liters with the 60:40 split rear seats folded (vs. 1,536 liters in the Coupé). Both variants include a 15-liter frunk. The SUV's roof rail supports up to 100 kg. Towing capacity is unchanged from the Coupé: 1,500 kg for Rear Motor, 2,000 kg for Dual Motor.

**Platform and performance.** Both body variants use the Geely Group's SEA platform with 400-volt architecture (the Polestar 3 uses the SPA2 platform with 800-volt architecture). Like the Coupé, the SUV comes in two drivetrain options: Rear Motor (200 kW, 343 Nm) or Dual Motor (400 kW combined, 686 Nm), with a 200 km/h top speed. Both use a 100 kWh gross battery. Despite the taller body and slight extra weight (35 kg Rear Motor, 20 kg Dual Motor), WLTP range is marginally better than the Coupé: 630 km for Rear Motor (vs. 620 km) and 600 km for Dual Motor (vs. 590 km). With the Performance Package, the SUV achieves 520 km vs. the Coupé's 500 km.

**Charging.** DC charging tops out at 200 kW, with a 10-80% charge taking about 30 minutes. AC charging is 11 kW standard (full charge in 11 hours), with a faster 22 kW charger available for an extra €800 or as part of the €5,900 Prime Package (which also includes Pilot Assist, a head-up display, an electric tailgate, and three-zone climate control).

**New features.** Vehicle-to-Load debuts across the model series, delivering up to 3.3 kW via an adapter (currently approved only for Germany). A new Camping Mode keeps climate control, entertainment, and ambient lighting running during extended stationary periods; both features will also come to the Polestar 4 Coupé via software update. Google Assistant is gradually being replaced by Google Gemini, though it's unclear if this applies to older model years too.

The SUV gets a new panoramic glass roof, larger than the Coupé's, extending to the tailgate as standard; an electrochromic version with adjustable transparency is optionally available exclusively for the SUV. Because it has a rear window, the SUV includes a conventional rear-view mirror, with the camera-based digital mirror available as an option.

**Pricing and availability.** In Germany, the Rear Motor starts at €57,900 — €2,000 cheaper than the currently-discounted Coupé's €61,900 list price. The Dual Motor costs at least €65,900, also €2,000 less than the equivalent discounted Coupé. Production is already underway in Busan, South Korea, having started in Q2. First deliveries are scheduled for Q4 2026, though the German configurator currently shows a delivery window of January–February 2027.
