# Dacia Hipster to launch in 2027 as an electric Kei-Car

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-28

---

According to a British media report (Autocar), Dacia will offer an electric microcar called Hipster in Europe from 2027. The L7e-class vehicle, shown as a concept around a year ago, is expected to be built on the same Chinese production line as the first-generation Spring.

Dacia unveiled the Hipster concept in October 2025 with no series plans. Autocar now reports production next year and a UK launch for under £10,000 (about €11,000), citing a source close to the project. It is expected to be built at the Shiyan plant by eGT New Energy Automotive (Renault, Nissan, Dongfeng JV) — while the recently unveiled second-generation Spring is to be produced in Slovenia instead.

The boxy car is a European take on Japan's kei cars, positioned below the Spring. L7e limits: unladen mass of 450 kg (excluding battery), 15 kW maximum power, 90 km/h top speed. As an L7e vehicle it would likely avoid the EU's special tariffs on Chinese-built M1-class EVs.

The concept measured 3.0 m long, 1.53 m high and 1.55 m wide, with four full-size seats and boot space of 70–500 litres. The concept's split tailgate is expected to be replaced by a side-hinged rear door on the production car.

Under ex-CEO Denis Le Vot the Hipster was meant to succeed the Spring, but Renault Group instead chose a Spring derivative based on the revived Renault Twingo. New Chief Growth Officer Fabrice Cambolive has reportedly revised the Hipster concept to cut costs further, seeking significant development support from China. The final version is expected to be unveiled next year. Dacia aims for a "no gimmicks" counterpoint to heavy, tech-laden EVs: the smartphone acts as key, navigation and infotainment display via a docking station, a Bluetooth speaker serves as the sound system, and side windows slide manually.
