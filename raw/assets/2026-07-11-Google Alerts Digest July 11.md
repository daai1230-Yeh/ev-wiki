# Google Alerts Digest — July 11, 2026

**Source**: Google Alerts daily summary emails (7:03am and 8:03am)

---

## 電動車 + 泰國 (EV + Thailand)

Thailand's Board of Investment (BOI) secretary-general and EV Board secretary Narit Therdsteerasukdi chaired a global business opportunity briefing at QSNCC in Bangkok on July 1, 2026, discussing Thailand's EV investment climate. Separately, Thai state oil company PTT is reportedly seeking to boost profitability by partnering with Foxconn on EV manufacturing. Thailand's BOI has also approved 9 investment projects worth $1.99 billion spanning AI data centers, aerospace, and wind power, with Thailand's EV supply chain investment surpassing $4.1 billion — seen as a reference point for Taiwanese component makers expanding into ASEAN.

## 電動巴士 (Electric Buses)

Taiwan's Foton Buscar (華德動能), an electric bus maker, has seen shipments rise sharply, from 113 units in 2024 to 205 in 2025, with revenue growth reported by market commentary. The company opened its Taichung Zhonggang plant to showcase electric bus battery-pack production lines and new flame-retardant coating technology. Separately, Taiwan-based Cartek (車王電) is developing a "smart electric bus," and NTU and Ohmio (喜門史塔雷克) piloted Taiwan's first driverless electric bus (EZ10), which can run up to 8 hours per charge at a top speed of 40 km/h during trials.

## 電動車 + 韓國 (EV + Korea)

Tesla's FSD v14 Lite has officially launched in South Korea, with HW3 owners beginning to receive the update. Separately, South Korean battery maker LG Energy Solution has activated a new cylindrical battery production line in Nanjing, China, to keep up with strong Tesla order demand.

## 電動車 + 印度 (EV + India)

Nissan unveiled the Tekton in India as a global debut, replacing the Kicks in Nissan's lineup as part of its "Re:Nissan" turnaround plan; the model will also be exported to Middle East and Africa markets, starting around ₹352,000 (~$4,230) with a top trim near ₹1.649 million. Commentary also noted that India's EV market prioritizes practicality — range, charging convenience, service network, and running costs — over brand/tech prestige, posing a challenge for models like the Model Y.

## 電動車 + 中國大陸 (EV + China)

Reports flagged Australian cybersecurity experts warning that Chinese-made vehicles could collect owner data, amid growing scrutiny of Chinese EV brands' data practices.

## 電動車 + 供應鏈 (EV + Supply Chain)

Commentary on TE Connectivity's role in the EV supply chain highlighted its core strength in high-reliability connectors and sensors, increasingly critical as EVs carry higher voltages and more complex electronics than combustion vehicles.

## 電動巴士 — NHTSA warning (from 8:03am alert)

The U.S. NHTSA has warned that autonomous vehicles must not interfere with emergency responders, stating that blocking ambulances is "not an edge case." Separately, Taiwan's Foton Buscar was reported to have spent roughly NT$30 million to strengthen safety systems and operations following a past electric bus fire incident — a move credited with helping it beat Chinese competitors for overseas orders.

## 特斯拉 (Tesla, from 8:03am alert)

Commentary noted Tesla is officially classified as a "consumer discretionary" stock in the S&P 500, but its share price moves more in line with AI-sector expectations (cloud capex, TSMC advanced-node exposure) than traditional auto peers. A daily EV industry roundup noted BYD signed an 11.275 GWh energy storage agreement with UAE's Masdar and hit a global milestone of 17 million new-energy vehicles produced; Tesla's third-generation Optimus robot was also mentioned. Separately, coverage of the New Jersey autonomous-vehicle sensor bill (also covered in InsideEVs, see separate clipping) noted the legislation could directly threaten Tesla's camera-only "pure vision" approach by requiring backup sensing modalities.
