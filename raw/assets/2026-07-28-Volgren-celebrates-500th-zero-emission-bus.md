# Volgren celebrates 500th zero-emission bus, alongside showcasing Marcopolo coach models, at Australasia Bus & Coach Expo 2026

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 21 July 2026

---

Marcopolo-owned Australian bus bodybuilder Volgren celebrated the delivery of its 500th electric bus in Australia while presenting its latest bus and coach portfolio at the Australasia Bus & Coach Expo 2026, held 15–16 July at the Sydney Showground. In recent years Volgren has expanded its zero-emission portfolio through electric bus projects with BYD, Volvo Buses and Scania, alongside a hydrogen bus developed with Wrightbus.

The company, part of Marcopolo since 2011, used the exhibition to present vehicles for urban, school, charter and coach operations, including the Paradiso G8 1300, the Audace 1050, and three battery-electric buses based on its Optimus Electric platform. The 500th electric bus was displayed with a dedicated commemorative livery.

Volgren's Optimus Electric body is designed to accommodate battery-electric, hybrid and hydrogen-powered chassis, features a lightweight aluminium body structure (approximately 73% recyclable), and is supplied with a 15-year structural warranty. The Paradiso G8 1300, described as the flagship of Marcopolo's global coach portfolio, is in the early stages of commercial expansion in Australia. The Audace 1050 was developed for school and charter operations.

Volgren has operated in Australia for nearly 50 years, currently running three manufacturing facilities, five service centres and around 550 employees. The company reports having delivered more than 10,500 buses and holding around 70% of the Australian urban bus body market.

Thiago Deiro, CEO of Volgren, said the expo "provided an important opportunity to demonstrate Volgren's strength as an Australian company committed to supporting local industry," adding that the 500th electric bus delivery "reflects the confidence operators place in our solutions and reinforces our commitment to helping drive the transformation of public transport."
