# MG IM6 first drive: 553 kW and air suspension for €62,990

**Author**: Kasimir Hupe | **Date**: 2026-10-01

---

*Condensed summary of the article.*

- The MG IM6 is a ~4.90 m electric SUV (SAIC) with 800 V architecture, rear-wheel steering, and 300 kW (RWD, €56,990) or 553 kW / 751 hp (AWD, €60,990) outputs; 100 kWh pack (96.5 kWh net, NMC), DC charging up to 396 kW (10–80% ≈ 17 min), AC 11 kW.
- WLTP range: 625 km (RWD), 545 km (AWD), 505 km with the optional €2,000 Drive Pack (adaptive air suspension, adaptive damping, 21-inch wheels). Drag coefficient 0.259 (IM5 saloon: 0.237).
- AWD: 0–100 km/h in 3.5 s, 802 Nm, top speed 239 km/h. Real-world consumption on the Jura test route was 23–24 kWh/100 km, implying roughly 400 km between charges.
- Versus the IM5 saloon: +208 L boot (665 L), more rear head/legroom, standard driver massage seat; air suspension only available on the SUV.
- Shared weaknesses: no one-pedal driving, weak regen in standard mode, single wireless charging pad, no head-up display, limited rear visibility.
- Rivals cited: Xpeng G6 AWD Performance (€51,600), Tesla Model Y Performance (€63,970), Audi Q6 Sportback e-tron quattro (€77,100).
- Verdict: a price-performance powerhouse and more practical than the IM5; efficiency is its weak point. MG IM5/IM6 on sale in Germany, France, Belgium, Netherlands since July 2026; SAIC plans Ferrol (Spain) production from 2028.
