1. Warm-Up 暖身 3 min

1.Have you seen many Chinese-brand electric cars on the road where you live? What do people think of them?

你住的地方常看到中國品牌的電動車嗎？大家對它們的看法如何？

2.When you shop for a car, what matters most to you: price, range, brand, or technology? Why?

買車時你最在意什麼：價格、續航、品牌還是科技？為什麼？

3.Should a country protect its own car industry, or let foreign competitors in? What are the trade-offs?

一個國家應該保護本國車業，還是開放外國對手進入？利弊各是什麼？

2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Europe has become the new battleground for Chinese and American carmakers. According to UBS, Chinese vehicles are being shipped to Europe at nearly twice the pace of local new-car registrations.

歐洲已成為中美車廠較量的新戰場。瑞銀指出，中國汽車輸往歐洲的速度，接近當地新車註冊量的兩倍。

UBS also warns that the real export numbers may be underestimated by a factor of two, because trucking capacity inside Europe is stretched and cars are stuck in logistics bottlenecks.

瑞銀也警告，由於歐洲境內卡車運能吃緊、物流出現瓶頸，中系車的實際出口量可能被低估了兩倍。

The European Union already imposes extra tariffs on Chinese battery-electric vehicles, and it is now studying new duties on plug-in hybrids. Even so, Chinese makers can still turn a profit, thanks to cheaper lithium batteries and lower vehicle costs.

歐盟已對中國純電動車加徵關稅，目前也正研擬對插電式混合動力車課徵新關稅。即便如此，憑藉較低的鋰電池與整車成本，中國車廠依然有利可圖。

Ford CEO Jim Farley says it is "too late" for Europe. Speaking at the Automotive News Congress in Detroit, he said Chinese EVs already make up about 12 percent of the European market, and as much as 25 percent in Mexico.

福特執行長法利表示，對歐洲來說「已經太遲了」。他在底特律的《Automotive News》大會上說，中國電動車已占歐洲市場約12%，在墨西哥更高達25%。

Farley urges the United States to take its time and be considerate. He also raises national security concerns, saying Chinese cars can "take videos of everything."

法利呼籲美國放慢腳步、審慎行事。他也提出國安疑慮，指中國車輛「可以把一切拍下來」。

Yet Ford is not avoiding China altogether. It is partnering with Geely to build vehicles in Spain, and it has worked with CATL on a battery project. Farley says Ford will partner where it lacks intellectual property, and compete everywhere else.

不過福特並未完全迴避中國。它與吉利合作在西班牙造車，也與寧德時代合作電池專案。法利說，福特會在自己缺乏智慧財產權的領域合作，其他領域則正面競爭。

Policy in Washington is unclear. A bill from Congress would ban any manufacturer with 15 percent or more Chinese ownership, but the administration has hinted it might allow Chinese EVs that are built in the U.S. Meanwhile, the EU's vote on Tesla's Full Self-Driving system has slipped from October to around the end of 2026.

華府的政策仍不明朗。國會的法案將禁止任何中國持股15%以上的車廠，但政府暗示可能允許在美國本土生產的中國電動車。同時，歐盟對特斯拉FSD的表決已從10月延到2026年底前後。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型 3 min

"X is shipping Y at nearly twice the pace of Z."
比較兩個速度或數量，表示接近兩倍。
Chinese makers are shipping cars to Europe at nearly twice the pace of local registrations.
"Even so, X can still ___, thanks to Y."
承認不利條件，但指出仍成立的原因。
Even so, Chinese makers can still turn a profit, thanks to cheaper batteries.
"It's too late for X to ___."
表達某事已經來不及。
Some people say it's too late for Europe to catch up.
"We should take our time and ___ before ___."
主張審慎、不要急著行動。
We should take our time and study the risks before opening the market.
"We'll partner where ___, and compete where ___."
說明合作與競爭並行的策略。
We'll partner where we lack technology, and compete where we are strong.

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

1.Farley says it is "too late" for Europe. Do you agree, or can European brands still fight back? How?

法利說歐洲「已經太遲」。你同意嗎？歐洲品牌還能怎麼反擊？

2.Argue for keeping Chinese EVs out of the U.S. for now. Then argue the opposite: that open competition helps drivers.

請先主張暫時不讓中國電動車進入美國，再主張相反立場：開放競爭對駕駛人有利。

3.Is the national security concern about connected cars realistic, or is it partly a way to protect local jobs?

聯網車的國安疑慮是否合理？還是部分只是為了保護本地工作機會？

4.Is it hypocritical for Ford to partner with Geely and CATL while asking for caution toward Chinese carmakers? Why or why not?

福特一邊與吉利、寧德時代合作，一邊呼籲對中國車廠謹慎，是否矛盾？為什麼？

5.Tariffs raise prices for consumers. Who benefits and who loses when a government taxes imported EVs?

關稅會推高消費者價格。政府對進口電動車課稅時，誰受益、誰受損？

6.If you could choose, would you buy a Chinese-brand EV? What would change your mind?

如果可以選擇，你會買中國品牌的電動車嗎？什麼會改變你的想法？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 2 min

60-second summary: In short — Europe has become the main battleground for Chinese and American carmakers. Chinese exports may be underestimated by as much as two times, and even with EU tariffs, cheaper batteries keep them profitable. Ford's CEO says it is too late for Europe, and he wants the U.S. to move slowly, yet Ford still partners with Geely and CATL. The big question is whether protection or competition serves drivers better.

60秒摘要：簡而言之，歐洲已成為中美車廠的主戰場。中國車的出口量可能被低估多達兩倍，即使有歐盟關稅，較便宜的電池仍讓他們有利可圖。福特執行長認為歐洲已太遲，並希望美國放慢腳步，但福特自己仍與吉利、寧德時代合作。核心問題是：保護與競爭，哪一個對駕駛人更有利？
