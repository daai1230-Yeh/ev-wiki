# Saint-Étienne orders 10 more Solaris trolleybuses, bringing fleet to 41 units

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-16

---

Solaris has won a contract to supply 10 articulated Trollino 18 trolleybuses to Saint-Étienne Métropole in France, with delivery in late 2027. The order, placed through the public transport purchasing body CATP (Centrale d'Achat de Transport Public), brings the Solaris trolleybus fleet there to 41 units: 23 twelve-metre and 18 articulated vehicles.

The cooperation dates to 2019, when Solaris entered the French trolleybus market with an order for 22 Trollino vehicles, delivered with Škoda Electric. 23 twelve-metre Trollinos have been delivered so far, and eight articulated Trollino 18 units followed in 2025. The city's renewal programme uses in-motion charging, letting trolleybuses run on onboard batteries away from overhead wires.

The new 18-metre Trollino 18 will have a 250 kW traction motor and onboard batteries for sections without overhead lines. Camera-based systems replace exterior mirrors, and the safety package includes driver fatigue and distraction monitoring, intelligent speed assistance, lane change assistance, and pedestrian and cyclist detection. The vehicles also get Solaris' eSConnect remote diagnostics system.
