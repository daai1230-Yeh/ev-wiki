# Nexeon secures £52.6m from UK National Wealth Fund

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

British battery materials developer Nexeon has secured backing from the UK's National Wealth Fund as part of a £100 million round to expand domestic operations and develop a pilot manufacturing facility. Other new investors include the Korea Development Bank and Honda Xcelerator Ventures.

The round includes £52.6 million from the National Wealth Fund. It will support R&D in the UK, expand Nexeon's advanced manufacturing technology unit and create skilled jobs. The Oxfordshire-based company develops silicon-based anode materials for lithium-ion batteries, which can increase energy density, improve charging speeds and potentially extend EV range.

Oliver Holbourn, CEO of the National Wealth Fund, said backing advanced battery technology is key to a globally competitive UK EV supply chain and will help unlock private capital, support high-value jobs and drive regional growth. Nexeon CEO Dr Scott Brown called it a strong endorsement of Nexeon's technology and role in enabling the next generation of lithium-ion batteries.

This follows Honda's May investment via Honda Xcelerator Ventures, which supports commercialisation of Nexeon's silicon anode technology and ramp-up of its first large-scale plant in Gunsan, South Korea. That plant is operational and produces silicon-carbon anode materials at industrial scale; in 2023 Panasonic agreed to use them in cells made at its Kansas, US plant. The new UK pilot facility is meant to underpin domestic production in addition to Gunsan.
