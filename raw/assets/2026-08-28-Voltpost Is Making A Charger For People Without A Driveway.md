# Voltpost Is Making A Charger For People Without A Driveway

**Author**: Rob Stumpf | **Date**: Aug 26, 2026, 3:59pm ET

---

*Voltpost's new chargers cut install costs by sticking to ceilings and walls—meaning apartment dwellers could be the real winners.*

**Key points**

- Voltpost is releasing two new EV chargers.
- Both products are meant to make retrofitting easy, which means apartment-dwellers may finally have a simple solution for EV charging.
- The company says its mounting methods cut install costs by as much as 70%.

There's an assumption baked into EV ownership advice: home charging. Most people assume anyone buying an EV is just charging in their driveway — after all, most of the convenience and savings in EV ownership comes from home charging.

But relying on public fast charging can often prove nearly as expensive as filling up at the pump, and you can't wake up with a fully juiced battery. That's where a new idea from Voltpost comes in.

Voltpost is best known for turning lampposts and telephone poles into EV chargers. Now it's tackling the driveway problem by introducing two new Level 2 chargers into its Voltpost Air retrofitting portfolio.

First is the Air Ceiling Mount, purpose-built for parking garages. As the name implies, it attaches to the ceiling of a garage with hanging cables to maximize the space available for parking. The company also introduced an Air Surface Mount that attaches directly to walls (and pedestals), aimed at bringing chargers to parking lots and curbside locations when a pole isn't available — the side of buildings, or a pedestal bolted into pavement.

Both setups use a retractable cable to keep the charging hardware tidy and off the ground. But the real win, according to Voltpost, is how easy the chargers are to retrofit around infrastructure already in place. No ripping up sidewalks or trenching — just mount on something already there, run some wires, and finish in hours rather than days. That approach cuts installation costs by as much as 70%, according to Voltpost.

Voltpost isn't solving apartment or destination charging everywhere overnight — no charging company is. Building and lot owners still need to actually install these chargers, and somebody has to foot the bill. But saving money by bolting chargers onto a building instead of trenching out a lot is a good place to start.
