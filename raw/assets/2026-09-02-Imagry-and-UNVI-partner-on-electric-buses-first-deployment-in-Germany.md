# Autonomous driving specialist Imagry and UNVI partner on electric buses, first deployment in Germany

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-08-08

---

Autonomous driving technology provider Imagry has partnered with Spanish bus manufacturer UNVI to integrate its AI-based, HD-mapless driving system into electric buses, with the first 10.3-metre vehicle set for deployment in Germany in Q3 2026.

The cooperation combines Imagry's AI-based autonomous driving technology with UNVI's M3-category electric buses. UBSI is involved as the vehicle integration, procurement, leasing and lifecycle support partner. Integration of the autonomous driving technology was completed in under three months, and the first vehicle is pre-certified for WVTA (GSR2).

Imagry provides AI-based autonomous driving technology covering perception, motion planning and vehicle control. The system operates without HD maps, enabling location-independent driverless operation. It's being integrated into UNVI's electric bus platform for applications including regular public transport in cities and towns, plus operations at airports, medical centres and university campuses. UNVI said the initial project involves a complete electric bus solution, but the cooperation could extend to integrating Imagry's technology into different UNVI body configurations.

UBSI supplied the vehicle and acts as the strategic partner for vehicle integration, procurement, leasing and lifecycle support, covering regulatory compliance, technical readiness, fleet deployment and operational support.

Carlos Rivo, Managing Director of UNVI: "UNVI has extensive experience in developing and manufacturing bus bodies across a wide range of vehicle platforms and operational applications. Our cooperation with Imagry shows how this flexible approach can also support innovative autonomous mobility projects."

Eran Ofir, CEO of Imagry: "We are excited to bring Imagry autonomous driving capabilities to additional markets via UNVI's electric bus models. Our flexible, cost effective, AI-based and field-proven technology, provides Public Transportation Operators and city planners with a quick and reliable way to meet their current and growing mobility challenges."

Ibrahem Badawi, CEO of UBSI: "Deploying autonomous public transportation is far more than supplying a vehicle, it demands seamless coordination across engineering, regulatory compliance, homologation, procurement, integration, and operational reliability."
