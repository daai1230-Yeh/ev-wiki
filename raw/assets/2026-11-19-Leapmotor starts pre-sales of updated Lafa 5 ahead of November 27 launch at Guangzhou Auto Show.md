# Leapmotor starts pre-sales of updated Lafa 5 ahead of November 27 launch at Guangzhou Auto Show

**Author:** Lei Kang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-19

- The updated Lafa 5 starts at 105,800 yuan ($15,760) in pre-sales, upgrading to an 800-volt high-voltage platform to offer a CLTC range of up to 640 km.

- The Lafa 5 delivered 9,486 units in October, up 37.96% month-on-month to a new monthly high for this year.

Leapmotor (HKEX: 9863) has kicked off pre-sales for the updated Lafa 5, reinforcing the market competitiveness of the compact electric hatchback through upgrades in electrical architecture, smart driving, and comfort features.

The Chinese EV maker announced on Thursday that pre-sales of the updated Lafa 5 have begun with a starting price of 105,800 yuan ($15,760), and show cars have now arrived at stores.

The current Lafa 5 was launched in China on November 27, 2025, and is currently available in 7 variants with guide prices ranging from 97,800 yuan to 129,800 yuan.

The updated Lafa 5 will officially go on sale at the 2026 Guangzhou Auto Show, which opens on November 27. Official launch prices are typically lower than pre-sales prices in China.

In terms of its core electric powertrain, the new model upgrades to a full-domain 800-volt high-voltage platform and supports 3C fast charging, taking just 16 minutes to charge from 30% to 80%.

The vehicle is upgraded with a 69.7-kWh battery pack, achieving a CLTC pure electric range of up to 640 km (398 miles).

Its 6-in-1 electric drive system delivers a maximum power of 185 kW (248 hp), enabling a 0-100 km/h sprint time of 6.06 seconds.

For chassis and safety, the updated Lafa 5 comes standard with frequency selective damping (FSD) shock absorbers, features a 50:50 front-to-rear axle load distribution jointly tuned by Chinese and European teams, and supports high-speed tire blowout stability control at up to 120 km/h.

On the intelligence front, the new model offers an advanced driver assistance solution powered by a LiDAR and a Qualcomm 8650 chip, integrating 28 perception hardware components and introducing an end-to-end large model.

Inside the cabin, the updated Lafa 5 adds a 50-inch augmented reality head-up display (AR-HUD), a 17.3-inch central control screen, dual front zero-gravity seats, and a 14-speaker sound system.

Measuring 4,430 mm in length, the Lafa 5 is a rear-wheel-drive compact battery electric hatchback whose main rivals include the Volkswagen ID.3, SAIC's MG4, the BYD Dolphin, and Nio Inc's (NYSE: NIO) Firefly.

In April this year, Leapmotor rolled out the sportier Lafa 5 Ultra, expanding the lineup's market coverage with an exclusive aerodynamic kit and stronger power performance.

The facelift comes as Lafa 5 sales rebound. The model delivered 9,486 units in October, up 37.96% month-on-month, marking its second consecutive monthly increase and hitting a new monthly high for this year, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.

From January to October this year, cumulative deliveries of the Lafa 5 reached 50,857 units. The model contributed 8.94% of Leapmotor's global deliveries in October, up from 6.51% in September.

Leapmotor delivered 106,127 vehicles globally in October, exceeding 100,000 units for the fourth consecutive month. In the first 10 months of the year, its cumulative deliveries reached 772,666 units, completing 77.27% of its full-year delivery target of 1,000,000 vehicles.
