# Chery iCar officially launches V25, expanding EREV lineup with up to 1,240 km combined range

**Author:** Lei Kang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-19

- The iCar V25 primarily targets young consumers, with an official guide price starting at 129,800 yuan ($19,340).

- It is iCar's second extended-range model and the third product in the brand's V series.

Chery's (HKEX: 9973) EV brand iCar officially launched its new boxy SUV, the V25, on Thursday, further expanding its extended-range electric vehicle (EREV) lineup at prices lower than pre-sales.

The V25 is available in 4 trims, with official guide prices ranging from 129,800 yuan ($19,340) to 159,800 yuan, while limited-time prices drop to between 119,800 yuan and 149,800 yuan.

Specifically, the limited-time starting prices for the RWD Pro and RWD Max are 119,800 yuan and 129,800 yuan, respectively, while the AWD Max+ and AWD Ultra are priced at 139,800 yuan and 149,800 yuan.

Pre-sales for the model began on October 19 with a price range of 139,800 yuan to 175,800 yuan, meaning the official launch guide prices are 10,000 yuan to 16,000 yuan lower than pre-sales prices.

The V25 is iCar's second EREV model following the flagship V27 launched in March this year, and the third product in the brand's V series, primarily targeting young consumers.

Continuing the family's signature boxy styling, the V25 measures 4,636 mm in length, 1,920 mm in width, and 1,855 mm in height, with a wheelbase of 2,820 mm.

The vehicle offers light off-road capability with a minimum ground clearance of 197 mm and a maximum wading depth of 600 mm, featuring a side-hinged tailgate and a 22.7-liter external storage box at the rear.

For the powertrain, the V25 is equipped with a 1.5T engine with a maximum power of 115 kW (154 hp) serving as a range extender, and is offered in 2 versions: single-motor RWD and dual-motor AWD.

The RWD version's motor delivers a maximum power of 185 kW, while the AWD version's dual-motor system produces a combined 335 kW. All variants come with a 33.67 kWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery pack, which charges from 30% to 80% in 15 minutes.

In terms of range, the RWD and AWD versions offer CLTC battery ranges of 210 km (130 miles) and 200 km, respectively, with CLTC combined ranges reaching 1,240 km and 1,210 km.

For driver assistance, the V25 is equipped with 27 sensors, including 1 LiDAR, and runs the Falcon 700+ system, supporting urban and highway Navigation on Autopilot (NOA).

The smart driving system is powered by Horizon Robotics' (HKEX: 9660) Journey 6P chip, delivering 560 TOPS of computing power.

Inside the cabin, it features a 15.4-inch central control screen powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8538 chip.

The launch of the V25 comes as iCar looks to revive its sales momentum. According to data compiled by CnEVPost, iCar sold 11,717 vehicles in October, down 5.45% year-on-year but up 7.91% month-on-month.

In the first 10 months of this year, iCar's cumulative sales totaled 87,264 vehicles, up 10.43% year-on-year.
