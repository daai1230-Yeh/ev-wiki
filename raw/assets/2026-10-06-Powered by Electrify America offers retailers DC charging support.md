# Powered by Electrify America offers retailers DC charging support

**Author**: guest author | **Date**: 2026-10-05

---

A turnkey charging package called Powered by Electrify America allows retailers in the United States to build their own branded charging networks backed by one of North America's largest DC fast charging networks.

The white-label offering is for US retail businesses wanting to operate their own charging stations. The package lets retailers design the charging experience and manage branding, pricing and loyalty programs, while Electrify America provides technical expertise and operational support. The Volkswagen subsidiary says the offer can help increase dwell time, drive incremental revenue, strengthen customer loyalty, and position retailers as destinations for EV drivers.

According to Electrify America, its "Powered by.." initiative already supports more than 100 stations and nearly 600 chargers across the network, including Wawa and Costco stores. These white-label stations become new charging destinations alongside Electrify America's coast-to-coast network of more than 5,600 chargers across 1,080 stations.

At the annual National Association of Convenience Stores (NACS) show in Las Vegas (6-9 October), Powered by Electrify America will be present to support and advise retailers wanting to add vehicle charging.
