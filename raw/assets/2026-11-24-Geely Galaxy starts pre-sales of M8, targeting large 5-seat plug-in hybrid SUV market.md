# Geely Galaxy starts pre-sales of M8, targeting large 5-seat plug-in hybrid SUV market

**Author:** Lei Kang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-24

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- The Geely Galaxy M8 starts at 199,800 yuan ($29,770) in pre-sales and shares the same dimensions as the 3-row, 6-seat M9.
- The plug-in hybrid SUV comes standard with a 53.42 kWh battery using CATL cells, delivering a CLTC battery range of up to 360 km.

Geely Auto's (HKEX: 0175) Geely Galaxy brand began pre-sales of its flagship 5-seat SUV, the Galaxy M8, on Tuesday, further expanding its large SUV lineup.

The plug-in hybrid SUV is offered in 3 variants, with pre-sales prices set at 199,800 yuan ($29,770), 209,800 yuan, and 235,800 yuan.

During the pre-sales period, customers can pay a 500 yuan deposit to deduct 2,000 yuan from the purchase price and receive a complimentary rear dual executive seat package valued at 12,800 yuan for a limited time.

The Galaxy M8 is expected to officially launch later this year, joining the Galaxy M series, which currently consists of the compact SUV M7 and the 6-seat large SUV M9.

The new model measures 5,205 mm in length, 1,999 mm in width, and 1,800 mm in height, with a wheelbase of 3,030 mm, matching the dimensions of the Galaxy M9 launched last year.

Unlike the 3-row, 6-seat M9, the Galaxy M8 adopts a 2-row, 5-seat layout, offering a standard trunk capacity of 924 liters that expands to 2,340 liters with the second-row seats folded down.

For power, the Galaxy M8 is equipped with the NordThor EM-P plug-in hybrid system consisting of a 1.5T engine and electric motors, available in 2WD and tri-motor AWD versions.

The tri-motor AWD variant delivers a combined maximum output of 640 kW (858 hp) and peak torque of 1,165 Nm, accelerating from 0 to 100 km/h in 4.5 seconds.

All variants come standard with a 53.42 kWh battery pack using cells from CATL (HKEX: 3750), providing a CLTC battery range of 360 km (224 miles) for the 2WD versions and 330 km for the AWD version.

With a full tank and full charge, the Galaxy M8 achieves a CLTC combined range of up to 1,560 km, with fuel consumption as low as 4.95 liters per 100 km when the battery is depleted.

The model supports peak 6C ultra-fast charging, capable of charging from 10% to 70% in as little as 8.4 minutes, according to official figures.

The Galaxy M8 comes standard with the Geely Afari Smart Driving (G-ASD) H5 driver-assistance system, featuring 27 sensors including a LiDAR, and supports urban navigation on autopilot and parking assistance.

Inside, it runs the Flyme Auto 2 smart cockpit system and features a physical Eva AI robot, a 9.1-liter heating and cooling box, and a 25-speaker audio system.

The pre-sales come as Geely Galaxy's sales remain strong. The brand sold a record 153,385 vehicles in October, up 20.32% year-on-year and 17.56% from September, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.

In the first 10 months of the year, Geely Galaxy sold 1,030,569 vehicles, up 2.80% year-on-year, accounting for about 40% of Geely Auto's total sales over the same period.
