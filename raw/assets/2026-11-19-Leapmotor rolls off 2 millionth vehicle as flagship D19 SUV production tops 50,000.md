# Leapmotor rolls off 2 millionth vehicle as flagship D19 SUV production tops 50,000

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-19

- As of the end of October, Leapmotor had delivered 772,666 vehicles this year, completing 77.27% of its annual target of 1 million units.

- The D19 is the first full-size flagship SUV in Leapmotor's D series, starting at 219,800 yuan ($32,740) in China.

Leapmotor's (HKEX: 9863) 2 millionth mass-produced vehicle has rolled off the assembly line, marking a new milestone in scaled production for the Stellantis-backed Chinese electric vehicle (EV) maker.

The company announced the milestone on Weibo on Thursday, while also revealing that the 50,000th mass-produced unit of its flagship D19 SUV has rolled off the line.

The production milestone aligns with the company's earlier expectations, as Leapmotor had previewed when releasing its October delivery data on November 1 that its 2 millionth vehicle would roll off the line in November.

The D19, the first full-size flagship SUV in Leapmotor's D series, was officially launched with deliveries beginning on April 16 this year, starting at 219,800 yuan ($32,740) in China.

Available in 2 powertrain options — battery electric vehicle (BEV) and extended-range electric vehicle (EREV) — the model is seen as a key product in the company's push upmarket.

According to data compiled by CnEVPost, the D19 saw 4,454 deliveries in October, up 47.58% from September, snapping 2 consecutive months of sequential declines.

The model reached a monthly delivery peak of 10,043 units in July, bringing its cumulative deliveries from its April launch through the end of October to 43,061 units.

In the first 10 months of the year, the company delivered 772,666 vehicles, up 65.88% year-on-year, bringing its historical cumulative deliveries to 1,968,769 units, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.

Leapmotor has set its full-year 2026 delivery target at 1 million vehicles, meaning it still needs to deliver 227,334 units in the final 2 months of the year, or an average of 113,667 units per month.

To further boost sales, Leapmotor earlier today began pre-sales for the updated Lafa 5 compact electric hatchback, starting at 105,800 yuan, with its official launch scheduled for November 27 at the Guangzhou Auto Show.
