# BASF supplies CATL: CAM shipments from Schwarzheide have begun

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 24.11.2026 - 13:30

---

BASF has begun deliveries to CATL. The supply contract includes the provision of high-performance cathode materials from BASF's plant in Schwarzheide (Germany). CATL also receives battery materials from BASF in China.

The deliveries stem from the global framework agreement signed in 2025. This saw BASF and CATL expand their collaboration – dating back to 2021 – on a global scale. For BASF, this marks a key step towards implementing its strategic focus on the battery materials sector.

Cathode active materials (CAM) are among the central components of lithium-ion batteries and significantly determine the performance and characteristics of the cells. The materials are specifically tailored to CATL’s technical specifications and provide the company with a critical precursor for cell manufacturing.

Sourcing cathode materials from German production shortens transport routes for CATL and strengthens regional supply security for vehicle manufacturers and cell plants in Europe. With the start of deliveries from Schwarzheide, the collaboration with CATL in Germany is shifting from product development and qualification to ongoing supply operations.

In Schwarzheide, BASF operates Germany’s only facility for producing such materials. In Changsha, China, BASF also supplies CATL from local production.

“We are proud to work with CATL and to have reached this important next milestone together,” said Dr. Daniel Schönfelder, Head of the Battery Materials business unit at BASF. “These first deliveries are the result of close collaboration between our teams across development, qualification, production and supply. This achievement reflects the complementary strengths of CATL and BASF and our shared commitment to execution, speed and agility. This provides a solid foundation for our future collaboration and reinforces our confidence in the European battery industry.”

Within the BASF Group, the battery materials business is one of the areas with which the company is addressing electromobility and the shift towards more sustainable technologies. To sharpen its focus on cathode materials, BASF is divesting its battery recycling business and selling its battery recycling division, including the relevant facilities in Schwarzheide and North America, to Cylib.
