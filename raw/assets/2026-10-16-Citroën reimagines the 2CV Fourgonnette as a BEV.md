# Citroën reimagines the 2CV Fourgonnette as a BEV

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-10-15

---

At the 2026 Paris Motor Show, Citroën is unveiling the 2CV Fourgonnette Concept, an electric reinterpretation of its iconic "boxy duck." The study builds on the equally new 2CV Concept, transferring its idea of a simple, lightweight, and relatively affordable electric car to a compact utility vehicle. However, there are no concrete plans for production as of yet.

The new concept vehicle adopts the characteristic silhouette of the historic 2CV Fourgonnette, known in Germany as the 'Kastenente'. Citroën positions the study as a battery-electric work vehicle with the simplest possible operation, high everyday practicality and deliberately minimal technology.

The manufacturer has not yet released technical data for the commercial vehicle study. Details such as battery size, range, charging power, payload or load volume are missing, as is a concrete timeline for a possible production version.
