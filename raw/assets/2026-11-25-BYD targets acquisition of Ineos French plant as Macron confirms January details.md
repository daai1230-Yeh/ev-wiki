# BYD targets acquisition of Ineos French plant as Macron confirms January details

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-25

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- BYD has entered exclusive negotiations with Ineos Automotive to acquire its vehicle manufacturing plant in Hambach, Moselle, France.
- French President Emmanuel Macron said details will be announced at the Choose France summit on January 18, 2027.

BYD has entered exclusive negotiations to acquire Ineos Automotive's vehicle manufacturing plant in Hambach, Moselle, France, a move that could accelerate its localized production footprint in Europe.

In a statement on Tuesday, BYD said the proposed transaction remains subject to consultation with the relevant works councils, adding that its aim is to build vehicles there tailored to European drivers.

French President Emmanuel Macron confirmed on the same day in a post on social media platform X that BYD is working with France on an industrial project in Moselle.

Macron said the development is a direct result of France's economic policy and European automotive initiatives aimed at accelerating automakers' local manufacturing in Europe, with more details to be announced at the Choose France investment summit on January 18, 2027.

Stella Li, Executive Vice President of BYD, said in the statement that the proposed investment in Hambach reflects the company's confidence in France and its ambition to keep growing in Europe, noting that BYD wants to offer European consumers vehicles built in France.

BYD said it is determined to keep the plant in production through the project, support local jobs, and create new opportunities for workers and suppliers in the region.

The Hambach plant currently employs nearly 900 workers, and if the acquisition is completed, it would become BYD's second car factory in Europe after its plant in Szeged, Hungary, according to Reuters.

From 1998 to 2024, the Hambach facility produced the Smart compact car developed by Mercedes-Benz and Swatch. Acquired by Ineos in 2020, the plant has been assembling the Grenadier off-roader and the Quartermaster pickup truck since 2022.

Ineos said that if a deal is reached, the 2 models will continue to be produced in Hambach in the medium term. However, the company has shortlisted US manufacturing sites and plans to begin production in the US in 2028, as the market currently accounts for about 70% of its global sales.

In mid-October, Li revealed at the 2026 Paris Motor Show that BYD's Hungary plant is scheduled to begin mass production in the fourth quarter, adding that the company plans to build a second and third factory in Europe, with France already on its shortlist.
