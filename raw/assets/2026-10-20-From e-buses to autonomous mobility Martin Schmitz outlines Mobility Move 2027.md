# From e-buses to autonomous mobility: Martin Schmitz outlines Mobility Move 2027

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-09

*(Sponsored content by Mobility Move; interview with Martin Schmitz, CTO of VDV)*

---

Mobility Move 2027 takes place in Berlin on 16–18 March. The 2026 edition drew more than 1,600 participants and 110 exhibitors. New for 2027: a start-up area, focuses on robotics, passenger information and bringing innovations into operation, and an expanded autonomous driving forum. Electric mobility remains a main pillar.

**Key points from the interview**
- **Growth**: by August, as many exhibitors had registered as by December last year, so a slight increase in exhibitors and visitors is expected. Mobility Move positions itself as a specialist conference and networking platform, not a conventional bus trade show; it began in 2008 as an electric bus conference.
- **Autonomous driving**: the 2026 forum drew great interest, so it will expand. Scaling beyond pilots will still take time; insights are expected from German projects KIRA+, MILA and Ahoi plus international ones. MILA (about 35 companies) develops an open, manufacturer-independent digital platform connecting autonomous vehicles with control centres, dispatching, booking systems and infrastructure data. VDV is also developing a standardised backend, and an AI forum addresses digital connectivity.
- **E-mobility**: many operators still need to invest in electrification; system integration and software offer efficiency potential.
- **Funding**: VDV advocates financial and regulatory support in Germany and the EU. Germany launched its fifth e-mobility funding call in 2026, and a programme exists for procuring individual autonomous vehicles, but significantly more funding is needed.
- **International reach**: exhibitors from around 20 countries, including Far East manufacturers, though the focus is Central Europe.
- **Atmosphere**: the organising core team has remained almost unchanged since 2008, and thematic grouping of exhibitors keeps the "family gathering" feel. Two evening events in the exhibition area support networking.
