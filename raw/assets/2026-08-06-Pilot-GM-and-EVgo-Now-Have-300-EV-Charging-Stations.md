# Pilot, GM, and EVgo Now Have 300 EV Charging Stations Nationwide

**Author**: Nico DeMattia | **Date**: Aug 4, 2026

---

After joining forces to build charging stations across America, Pilot, GM, and EVgo now have 300 total locations nationwide.

In 2022, Pilot Company, one of the larger travel-center companies in the U.S., partnered with EVgo and GM Energy to start building charging stations across the country. The goal was to create a network of charging stations that operated much like gas stations: open 24/7, overhead canopies to keep customers dry in bad weather, with somewhere to use the bathroom or grab a bite to eat. There are now 300 locations nationwide, the companies announced Tuesday, with an average rating of 9.41 out of 10 on PlugShare.

Many of the stations are as convenient as gas stations, with on-site restaurants and shops, bathrooms, and free WiFi. The charging stations themselves feature overhead canopies (rare among chargers) and windshield washing squeegees, and some locations have pull-through stations for vehicles towing trailers, so drivers don't have to unhitch to charge.

Pilot's 300 locations add up to 1,300 actual fast-charging stalls, meaning the companies are a little more than halfway to their goal of installing 2,000 chargers across 500 locations. It's part of a bigger trend: newer players from the worlds of gas stations and retail — chains like Wawa, Buc-ee's, Walmart, and Love's Travel Stops — are driving a lot of EV charging growth.
