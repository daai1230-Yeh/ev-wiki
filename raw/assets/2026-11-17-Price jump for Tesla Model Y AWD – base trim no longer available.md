# Price jump for Tesla Model Y AWD – base trim no longer available

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-11-16

---

After just over nine months, the entry-level version of the Tesla Model Y AWD has vanished from the configurator. It was the $41,990 option to get a base trim with dual-motor all-wheel drive. Now, the Model Y AWD starts at the Premium trim for $49,990. With rear-wheel drive, the entry-level model still starts at $39,990.

Without any official announcement, the Tesla Model Y AWD has just become more expensive. The price increase follows Tesla's removal of the entry-level Model Y All-Wheel Drive from its US online configurator, effectively ending the trim's run after just over nine months on the market.

Introduced in early February as part of a broader restructuring that saw Tesla drop the 'Standard' badge from its entry-level vehicles, the discontinued Model Y AWD was priced at $41,990 before destination fees – $2,000 more than the single-motor Rear-Wheel Drive model. The base AWD combined a dual-motor all-wheel-drive powertrain with the entry-level model's simplified equipment package. It accelerated from 0 to 60 mph in 4.6 seconds and offered an EPA-estimated range of 294 miles on standard 18-inch wheels.

## Tesla discontinues base Model Y with AWD

Inside and out, it shared its specifications with the base Rear-Wheel Drive trim, setting it apart from the higher-specification Premium models. It featured redesigned front and rear lights, passive shock absorbers and a lower maximum charging power of 225 kW. The cabin featured fabric and vegan-leather upholstery rather than the all-vegan-leather interior of the Premium lineup. The front seats were heated but lacked the ventilation function available in higher trims.

For audio and entertainment, the vehicle had a seven-speaker sound system instead of the 15-speaker setup with a subwoofer, and did without the eight-inch rear passenger touchscreen. Other cost-saving measures included a solid roof instead of the panoramic glass roof, standard cabin air filtration rather than a HEPA filter, a simpler suspension setup and manual steering-column adjustment instead of Tesla's usual power-adjustable system.

## Model Y AWD entry price jumps by 19 percent

With the removal of the $41,990 option, the US Model Y lineup now comprises five configurations. At the entry level, the Model Y Rear-Wheel Drive remains priced at $39,990 before destination fees, offering an EPA-estimated range of 348 miles and a 0-to-60-mph time of 6.8 seconds. Above it is the Premium trim, which starts at $45,990 for the single-motor Model Y Premium Rear-Wheel Drive (355 miles of range, 0 to 60 mph in 5.4 seconds). Buyers seeking dual-motor all-wheel drive must now opt for at least the Model Y Premium All-Wheel Drive, priced at $49,990 (327 miles of range, 0 to 60 mph in 4.3 seconds).

Above these are the Model Y Performance at $57,990 and the six-seat Model Y L Premium at $61,990. Compared with the discontinued base AWD version, the starting price of a configurable all-wheel-drive Model Y has increased by $8,000, or around 19 per cent.
