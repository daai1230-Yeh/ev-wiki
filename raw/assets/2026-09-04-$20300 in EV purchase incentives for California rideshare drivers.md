# $20,300 in EV purchase incentives for California rideshare drivers

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

California's Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) has approved a new incentive programme offering rideshare drivers up to $20,300 towards the purchase or lease of a new zero-emission vehicle. The Drivers Assistance Program targets low- and middle-income drivers working for companies like Uber, Lyft, and HopSkipDrive.

The CPUC approved the programme's initial implementation plan on 2 July as part of California's Clean Miles Standard, with applications opening in September for eligible drivers. The scheme, also known as Rideshare Incentives for Driving Electric (RIDE), targets high-volume rideshare drivers purchasing or leasing a battery-electric or hydrogen fuel-cell vehicle, aiming to cut greenhouse gas emissions from rideshare "Transportation Network Companies" (TNCs) by boosting the number of zero-emission vehicles on their platforms.

Eligible drivers can receive up to $20,300 for a new vehicle or up to $14,200 for a used one; the scheme is brand-agnostic. The programme also provides up to $1,170 per year toward charging costs, plus multilingual educational materials, one-on-one support, and online tools to compare vehicle costs and incentives. It's funded through a regulatory fee collected from TNCs and administered by the Center for Sustainable Energy.

The initiative is part of a wider California effort to maintain EV adoption after federal incentives were scrapped under the Trump administration in September 2025. The state separately runs the MyFirstEV programme, offering up to $3,500 for eligible first-time ZEV buyers and $1,750 for qualifying used vehicles.
