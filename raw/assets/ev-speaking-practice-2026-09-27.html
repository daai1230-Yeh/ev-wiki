2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Across Europe, Chinese automakers are expanding at a pace that's rattling Brussels — and it's happening in a way regulators didn't fully expect. When the European Union slapped anti-subsidy tariffs on Chinese-made battery electric vehicles back in 2024, Chinese manufacturers didn't retreat. They pivoted to hybrids instead, and hybrid sales have exploded ever since.

中國車廠正以驚人速度擴張歐洲版圖，讓布魯塞爾備感不安——而且事態發展並非監管機構原先預期的方向。2024年，歐盟對中國製純電動車加徵反補貼關稅，中國車廠並未因此退縮，反而轉攻油電混合車，混合動力車銷量自此一路飆升。

The numbers tell the story. Back in 2022, the EU imported just 659 Chinese-made hybrid vehicles. In the first seven months of this year alone, that figure jumped to 160,662. Plug-in hybrids followed the same curve, climbing from 56,706 units in 2022 to 217,764 in the January-to-July period this year. Hybrids now make up nearly 37 percent of Europe's entire car market — more than the roughly 21 percent held by fully electric vehicles.

數字說明了一切。2022年，歐盟僅進口659輛中國製混合動力車；光是今年前七個月，這個數字就暴增到160,662輛。插電式混合動力車也呈現相同走勢，從2022年的56,706輛，成長到今年1到7月的217,764輛。混合動力車目前已佔歐洲整體汽車市場近37%，超過純電動車約21%的市佔率。

Leading the charge are brands like BYD, Chery, and Leapmotor, all posting triple-digit sales growth. Geely — the group behind Volvo and Polestar — sold 205,000 vehicles in the EU over the first eight months of the year, making it the most popular Chinese brand outright. BYD's annual sales grew 163 percent to 177,000 units, and combined, Chinese brands have now outsold Tesla, which moved 142,000 vehicles over the same period.

領軍的是比亞迪、奇瑞、零跑等品牌，銷量都出現三位數的成長。吉利——旗下擁有富豪(Volvo)與極星(Polestar)——今年前八個月在歐盟賣出205,000輛，穩坐最受歡迎中國品牌的寶座。比亞迪年銷量成長163%，達到177,000輛；加總起來，中國品牌的銷量已經超過同期特斯拉的142,000輛。

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen says the EU's trade deficit with China — running at 1.18 billion euros a day — has reached what she calls an unsustainable tipping point. Brussels is now asking China to voluntarily cut its hybrid exports, with the threat of import quotas hanging over the negotiation. Germany's own auto industry association, long opposed to tariff walls, says it would now consider supporting tariffs on Chinese hybrids under the right conditions. Trade officials from both sides are set to meet on October 8th and 9th.

歐盟執委會主席馮德萊恩表示，歐盟對中國每天11.8億歐元的貿易逆差，已經到達她所謂「無法持續的轉捩點」。布魯塞爾現在要求中國「自願」減少混合動力車出口，並以配額限制作為談判籌碼。長期反對關稅壁壘的德國汽車工業協會，也表示在特定條件下會考慮支持對中國混合動力車課徵關稅。雙方貿易官員將於10月8日至9日會面。

Across the Atlantic, the response is even blunter. GM, Ford, Tesla, and other major automakers have jointly written to the Trump administration, urging it to block Chinese carmakers from selling, importing, or manufacturing vehicles in the US altogether. A bipartisan Senate bill — backed by lawmakers from Michigan and Ohio — would ban Chinese vehicles, software, and connected-car technology outright. One of its sponsors described subsidized Chinese EVs as an existential threat to the American auto industry, though President Trump himself has sounded more open to letting Chinese automakers build factories on US soil.

大西洋對岸的反應更加直接。通用、福特、特斯拉等主要車廠已聯名致信川普政府，要求全面阻止中國車廠在美國銷售、進口或製造車輛。一項由密西根州與俄亥俄州議員共同提出的兩黨法案，將全面禁止中國車輛、軟體與連網技術進入美國。其中一位提案者形容受補貼的中國電動車，是美國汽車業的「生存威脅」；不過川普總統本人的立場反而較為開放，甚至歡迎中國車廠在美國設廠。

While that political fight over passenger cars drags on, a very different kind of EV milestone quietly landed this week — in electric trucking. Tesla began its first customer deliveries of the Semi, the heavy-duty electric truck it first showed off as a concept back in 2017. Nearly a decade, and several delays, later, mass production only started this past April.

就在乘用車的政治角力持續延燒之際，一個截然不同的電動車里程碑，本週悄悄在電動卡車領域出現。特斯拉本週啟動了重型電動卡車Semi的首批客戶交付——這款車最早在2017年以概念車形式亮相，歷經近十年、多次延期後，才在今年4月正式進入量產。

The specs Tesla revealed beat its own original targets. The dedicated factory in Nevada can build 50,000 trucks a year — that's about 1,000 a week — with a fully loaded range of 500 miles, stretching to 600 miles when lightly loaded, and energy use around 1,700 watt-hours per mile, roughly 15 percent better than planned. Early customers include PepsiCo, DHL, and US Foods, while autonomous-trucking startup Einride has 500 units on order, and logistics firm IMC just placed an order for 50 to handle short-haul drayage at California ports.

特斯拉公布的規格數據超越了原先的目標。內華達州的專屬工廠年產能達5萬輛，等於每週生產約1,000輛；滿載續航里程為500英里，輕載時可達600英里，能耗約每英里1,700瓦時，較原訂目標省電約15%。早期客戶包括百事公司(PepsiCo)、DHL、US Foods，自駕卡車新創Einride已訂購500輛，物流商IMC也剛下單50輛，用於加州港口的短駁接駁運輸。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型 3 min

"X surged from A to B in just N months."
用來描述數據在短時間內暴增，強調變化的速度與幅度。
Hybrid imports surged from 659 to over 160,000 in just seven months.
"Some worry that ___, while others say ___."
用來同時呈現正反兩方意見，適合帶入政策辯論。
Some worry that tariffs invite retaliation, while others say they protect local jobs.
"Unless X happens, Y might follow."
條件句，用來表達某種後果的警告或威脅。
Unless China voluntarily cuts its exports, the EU might impose formal quotas.
"It's been almost a decade since ___, and only now has ___."
用來描述延宕已久、終於實現的事件，帶出時間跨度的張力。
It's been almost a decade since the Semi was first unveiled, and only now has mass production begun.
"While A is making headlines, B is quietly happening elsewhere."
用來連接兩則看似無關的新聞，做出對比或話題轉場。
While the tariff fight is making headlines, electric trucking is quietly hitting real milestones.

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

1.Do you think tariffs are an effective way to protect a domestic auto industry, or do they just delay an inevitable shift?

你認為關稅是保護本國汽車產業的有效手段，還是只是在拖延必然的產業轉變？

2.The EU is asking China to "voluntarily" limit hybrid exports — how realistic do you think a voluntary quota really is?

歐盟要求中國「自願」限制混合動力車出口——你覺得「自願配額」這種做法實際上有多少可行性？

3.American automakers and Chinese automakers are, in a strange way, on the same side here — U.S. brands want China locked out, and Chinese brands want the door open. What does that tell you about how "free market" competition really works?

美國車廠和中國車廠在這件事上其實立場很微妙——美國品牌希望把中國擋在門外，中國品牌則希望大門敞開。這讓你對「自由市場」競爭的實際運作方式有什麼看法？

4.One senator compared subsidized Chinese EVs to "automotive fentanyl." Do you think that kind of language helps or hurts a serious policy debate?

一位參議員把受補貼的中國電動車比喻成「汽車界的芬太尼」。你覺得這種措辭對嚴肅的政策辯論有幫助，還是反而有害？

5.Tesla took almost ten years to actually deliver the Semi. What does that gap between the 2017 concept and the 2026 delivery tell you about the difference between announcing new hardware and actually shipping it?

特斯拉花了將近十年才真正交付Semi。從2017年概念亮相到2026年正式交車，這中間的落差，讓你對「發表新硬體」和「真正量產交付」之間的差異有什麼想法？

6.If you ran a logistics company, what would you weigh more heavily when choosing an electric truck — loaded range, charging infrastructure, or the sticker price? Why?

如果你經營一家物流公司，選擇電動卡車時，你會更看重滿載續航里程、充電基礎設施，還是車輛價格？為什麼？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 2 min

60-second summary: In short — EU tariffs on Chinese EVs backfired into a hybrid import boom, pushing Brussels and Washington toward tougher trade measures, while Chinese brands like Geely and BYD now outsell Tesla in Europe. Meanwhile, in a very different corner of the EV world, Tesla finally started delivering its long-delayed Semi truck, hitting real production numbers nearly a decade after it was first announced.

60秒摘要：簡而言之，歐盟對中國電動車課徵的關稅，意外催生了混合動力車進口熱潮，促使布魯塞爾與華府採取更強硬的貿易措施；同時，吉利、比亞迪等中國品牌在歐洲的銷量已經超越特斯拉。另一方面，在電動車世界截然不同的角落，特斯拉終於開始交付延宕多年的Semi電動卡車，在概念亮相近十年後，交出了實際的量產數字。
