# BP divests fuel stations and charging network in Austria

**Author**: Stefan Koeller | **Date**: 21.07.2026

---

BP is selling its Austrian network of gas stations and charging stations to the Swiss energy company Volenergy. The deal is expected to be completed by the end of the year.

BP has announced the sale of 250 fuel stations, its electric vehicle charging infrastructure, and the associated fleet business in Austria to Volenergy. The company has not disclosed the number of charging stations included in the deal.

The transaction is expected to complete by the end of 2026, pending regulatory approvals. BP had already announced its intention to divest its fuel station and charging network in Austria last year, with an initial target completion date of the end of 2025.

This move follows BP's recent divestment of its fuel and charging business in the Netherlands, where the energy company Catom acquired the relevant business units.
