# BYD sells more than 250,000 BEVs in a single month for first time

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-02

---

BYD recorded a fourth consecutive month of growth in August, marking a return to steady growth for the Chinese manufacturer after a period of declining sales figures. Strong exports continue to offset weakening demand in its home market.

In August, BYD sold exactly 440,293 new energy vehicles (NEVs) — comprising battery-electric vehicles (BEVs) and plug-in hybrids — a 17.8% increase compared to the same month last year. Breaking sales down into domestic and export figures reveals two parallel trends: the Chinese market remains weak, with BYD's domestic sales declining to 250,827 units (-14.3% year-on-year), while exports have surged 134% year-on-year to 189,466 units.

Compared to July, both domestic and export sales saw slight increases of 5.1% and 5.0% respectively. CN EV Post interprets these figures as evidence that BYD's recovery from a weak start to the year is continuing.

By vehicle type: BYD sold 433,384 passenger cars in August (+16.7% year-on-year, +5.4% vs. July), while commercial vehicle sales rose to 6,909 units (+225% year-on-year, -15.1% vs. July).

By degree of electrification: sales of battery-electric passenger cars grew disproportionately, reaching a new record of 256,230 units (+28.4% year-on-year, +9.9% vs. July) — the first time BYD's BEV sales have exceeded the quarter-million mark in a single month. Plug-in hybrid sales remained largely stagnant at 177,154 units (+3.1% year-on-year, +0.5% vs. July), and have even declined significantly compared to August 2024.

CN EV Post notes that battery-electric passenger cars accounted for 59.1% of BYD's NEV sales in the passenger car segment in August, up from 53.7% a year earlier, indicating BYD's sales mix is shifting further toward all-electric models. Foreign markets remain the most important growth driver: the 189,466 units exported in August represent 43% of BYD's total sales, the same proportion as in July. BYD's monthly exports first exceeded 100,000 units in November 2025, and have grown consistently since, aside from seasonal fluctuations.

The cumulative annual balance also reflects this catch-up trend, though the previous year's figures haven't yet been fully matched. In the first eight months of 2026, BYD sold 2.67 million NEVs worldwide, a 6.8% decline compared to the same period last year — though the deficit has narrowed significantly compared to the first half of the year (-15.7%).
