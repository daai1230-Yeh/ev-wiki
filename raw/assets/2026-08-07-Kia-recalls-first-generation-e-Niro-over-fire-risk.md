# Kia recalls first-generation e-Niro over fire risk

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

Kia has launched a global recall for the first-generation e-Niro, addressing insufficient battery monitoring that could increase fire risk. Only first-generation units are affected.

The recall covers vehicles built between 19 May 2020 and 18 March 2022. Globally, 14,117 vehicles require inspection, including 11,331 in Europe and 1,791 in Germany.

The issue lies in inadequate monitoring logic in the battery management system (BMS): under certain operating conditions the high-voltage battery can lose control, potentially overheating cells and increasing fire risk while driving, parked, or charging.

At authorised workshops, technicians will update the BMS software (about 30 minutes) and inspect the high-voltage battery's condition. If damage is found, the entire battery pack will be replaced free of charge — a repair that can take up to six hours. Kia is running the recall under internal campaign code "261261".

Affected vehicles are all first-generation Kia e-Niro, built late 2018 through March 2022. The second-generation model (marketed as Niro EV) uses different battery components and is unaffected. Kia discontinued Niro EV sales in Germany in November 2024 after the EV3 launch; internationally only remaining Niro EV stock is being sold, with the Niro now offered exclusively as a hybrid.

Kia's parent Hyundai has previously issued similar recalls for the Kona Electric and Ioniq Electric over comparable BMS issues. Affected owners will be contacted directly to schedule a workshop appointment. Kia states no property damage or personal injury linked to this defect has been reported to date.
