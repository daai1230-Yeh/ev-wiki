# Leapmotor surpasses 1,000 retail locations across Europe

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-09-23

---

Chinese EV brand Leapmotor, with strong support from the Stellantis Group, has surpassed the milestone of 1,000 dealership locations in Europe. This achievement was reached just two years after Leapmotor's launch in Europe.

Leapmotor has announced that it now operates over 1,020 retail locations across Europe. These span 36 countries, from Iceland in the west to Bulgaria in the east, as well as the island of Malta. Most recently, Norway and several Balkan nations have been added, with further European markets to follow.

The rapid expansion since the European launch two years ago with the C10 and T03 models has been driven primarily by close collaboration with Stellantis, which acquired a 20 per cent stake in Leapmotor in 2023. The partnership had a dual purpose: to strengthen Stellantis' presence in China (though little has been reported on this front so far) and to introduce Leapmotor's electric vehicles to Europe. As part of this effort, the joint venture Leapmotor International was established to oversee European operations.

From the beginning, a key element of the strategy was to open Leapmotor showrooms at "selected dealerships of Stellantis brands across Europe". In other words, Leapmotor vehicles would be sold alongside models from brands like Fiat, Opel, and Peugeot. This explains how Leapmotor managed to exceed 1,000 locations in Europe in just two years.

A large number of sales points does not automatically translate to strong sales. However, Leapmotor seems to have resonated with European buyers: the company reports that last month, 27 per cent of its vehicles were delivered to Europe. With Leapmotor's total sales in August reaching 103,129 units, deliveries to Europe exceeded 27,800 vehicles.

Leapmotor is also steadily expanding its European lineup: following the T03 microcar and the C10 SUV (BEV and range extender), the B10 compact SUV (BEV and range extender) and the B05 electric fastback sedan are now on Europe's roads. The B03X electric compact car is already available for order. Additionally, the B03 – another electric compact car, known as the A05 in China – is ready to launch.
