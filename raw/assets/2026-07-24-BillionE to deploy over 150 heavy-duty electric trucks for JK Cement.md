# India: BillionE to deploy over 150 heavy-duty electric trucks for JK Cement

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Indian e-mobility-as-a-service (eMaaS) platform BillionE has secured a contract to deploy over 150 heavy-duty electric trucks for JK Cement, having already commissioned 20 units as a pilot project. BillionE flagged off the first batch from JK Cement's plant in Muddapur, Karnataka, helping deliver cement to stockyards across Karnataka, Maharashtra and Goa — one of the largest electric truck deployments for cement logistics in the region. The initial fleet uses Montra Electric's Rhino 5538 55-tonne tractor-trailer trucks, with BillionE partnering with ChargeZone for fast-charging support across the three states.
