# Amara Raja powers up ₹9,500 crore battery manufacturing push with first lithium-ion cell facility

**Source**: ETAuto Daily Morning Newsletter (Fri, Jul 17, 2026) | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies has commissioned its Customer Qualification Plant (CQP) at the company's Giga Corridor in Telangana, inaugurated by Telangana Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy. The company has invested approximately ₹500 crore in the facility as part of its cumulative Phase 1 investment of more than ₹1,500 crore across its ₹9,500 crore Giga Corridor programme.

The CQP has been commissioned with an initial capacity of 60 MWh, employing a workforce of over 100 people, and is designed to manufacture cylindrical and prismatic lithium-ion cells across multiple chemistries. The company's first 2 GWh commercial cell manufacturing facility, Giga 1, is scheduled to commence production next year (targeted for 2027).

By introducing this intermediate manufacturing step, Amara Raja aims to accelerate the industrialisation of new cell technologies, reduce execution risk as it scales manufacturing, and provide customers with cells produced on lines that closely mirror future commercial production. Cells from the facility are expected to be supplied to customers for validation beginning in August 2026.
