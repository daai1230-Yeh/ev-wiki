# Delhi EV Policy 2.0: Govt doubles down on BEVs, hybrids left out

**Author**: Insights Editor | **Date**: 2026-07-04

---

The Delhi government officially notified its landmark Delhi Electric Vehicle (EV) Policy 2026, introducing aggressive regulatory mandates to eliminate vehicular pollution in the national capital. The policy is operational from July 1, 2026, until March 31, 2030, and focuses exclusively on promoting pure battery electric vehicles (BEVs), completely excluding strong hybrids from tax reliefs or cash incentives.

Key incentives: electric two-wheeler buyers get direct subsidies of ₹30,000 in year one, scaling down to ₹20,000 in year two and ₹10,000 in year three. Electric auto-rickshaws are eligible for ₹50,000 (Year 1), ₹40,000 (Year 2), and ₹30,000 (Year 3). All eligible pure EVs get a 100% lifetime waiver on road tax and registration fees, capped for passenger cars at vehicles costing up to ₹30 lakh ex-showroom.

Delhi Transport Department data shows commercial goods carriers drive 33% of vehicular pollution, while two- and three-wheelers collectively generate 46%. Two-wheelers make up nearly two-thirds of the capital's vehicle population but represent less than 8% of current electric registrations.

Key timelines: from January 1, 2027, only pure electric three-wheelers and light goods carriers get fresh registrations. From April 1, 2028, new petrol/CNG scooters and motorcycles will be discontinued. Schools must convert at least 10% of transport fleets to EVs within two years, 20% within three, and 30% by March 31, 2030. A paperless subsidy portal (evsubsidy.delhi.gov.in) was launched for direct benefit transfers within 60 days of purchase.

Challenges include pushback from manufacturers (e.g., Maruti Suzuki called the hybrid exclusion "surprising") who argue hybrids are a necessary transitional technology, financial stress risk for lower-income auto-rickshaw and delivery operators, and grid capacity concerns as fast chargers scale up. The policy restricts subsidized EV buyers from selling or re-registering vehicles outside Delhi for three years.
