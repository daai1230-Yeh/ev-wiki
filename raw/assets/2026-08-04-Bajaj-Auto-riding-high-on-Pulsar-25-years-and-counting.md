# Bajaj Auto riding high on Pulsar, 25 years and counting

**Author**: ETAuto | **Date**: 2026-08-04

---

As the iconic Pulsar motorcycle brand marks 25 years, Bajaj Auto has launched a global "My Pulsar Story" campaign inviting Pulsar riders from around the world to share their journeys.

The campaign runs from 21 July to 4 September 2026, with riders posting stories on Instagram — photos, reels, videos, artwork, music, performances or AI-generated content — using the hashtag #MyPulsarStory and tagging @mypulsarofficial. It highlights how the brand has connected riders across continents, from Mumbai to Mexico City, Bogotá to Bengaluru, and Rio de Janeiro to Buenos Aires. More than 20 million Pulsar motorcycles have been sold worldwide since the model's 2001 launch. Weekly winners receive prizes, and one winner per participating country will receive a new Pulsar NS400Z.
