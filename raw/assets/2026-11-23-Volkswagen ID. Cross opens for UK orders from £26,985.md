# Volkswagen ID. Cross opens for UK orders from £26,985

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-11-20

---

Volkswagen has opened UK order books for its new all-electric ID. Cross, with prices starting at £26,985. However, entry-level versions will not be available to order until January 2027, meaning customers currently have a choice of two models with the larger battery.

Order books are now open for the range-topping versions of the ID. Cross Life and the ID. Cross Style. Both feature a 52 kWh battery and 155 kW electric motor and come with a starting price tag of £30,260 and £33,675 respectively. However, the cheaper 37 kWh versions with a 99 kW motor won't be available to buy until January 2027.

According to VW UK, these start at £26,985 for the Life and £30,400 for the Style. VW has confirmed that the 52 kWh models are eligible for the government's £1,500 Electric Car Grant (ECG), add that qualification is still pending for 37 kWh versions.

Specs-wise, the larger-battery models promise a combined WLTP range of up to 264 miles, while the 37 kWh versions have a stated range of 194 miles. They can be charged with up to 105 and 88 kW of electricity respectively. All models share the same dimensions, coming in at 4.15 metres long with a 475-litre boot. The compact eSUV also supports vehicle-to-load (V2L) functionality and can tow trailers weighing up to 1,200 kg in the case of the 52 kWh battery model.

First unveiled in July, the ID. Cross is based on Volkswagen Group's MEB+ platform, which it shares with the ID. Polo, Cupra Raval and Skoda Epiq. The four models form the group's new Electric Urban Car Family, with more than 80 per cent of their components and technologies shared across the range. The ID. Cross takes the SUV role in this line-up, sitting alongside the ID. Polo and targeting customers looking for a more practical, elevated alternative to a conventional small hatchback.

As we reported in September, series production of the ID. Cross has already started at VW's plant in Navarra, Spain, where the Skoda Epiq is also built. The model is part of a wider expansion of the group's manufacturing operations in Spain, with the ID. Polo and Cupra Raval being produced at the Martorell plant.
