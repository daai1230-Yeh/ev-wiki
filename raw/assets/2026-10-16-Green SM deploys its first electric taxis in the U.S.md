# Green SM deploys its first electric taxis in the U.S.

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-10-15

---

A little over a month after entering Europe, Vietnamese electric ride-hailing service provider Green SM has expanded into North America, beginning with the US. This is the VinFast affiliate's ninth market globally.

Green SM deployed its first electric taxis in the U.S. in Los Angeles, California, on 13 October. Customers can book rides through its mobile app available on the App Store and Google Play or traditionally by calling its hotline or hailing a taxi on the street.

As a launch promotion, Green SM is giving customers a 50 per cent discount, capped at 12 dollars per ride, on the first five trips. It is also offering a complimentary Green Pass membership that gives customers ten vouchers, each providing a 30 per cent discount capped at ten dollars per ride.

While Green SM operates both electric scooters and cars in Asian markets, it only uses cars in the U.S. Its Los Angeles fleet consists of only VinFast's VF 8, a mid-size SUV and the most affordable model from the brand in the US, priced from $39,900.

VinFast offers the VF 8 in the U.S. in two dual-motor AWD variants, one packing 260 kW and 500 Nm of torque and one with 300 kW and 620 Nm of torque. Both feature an 87.7 kWh NMC battery pack.
