# Rivian boosts Q2 revenue and reduces losses

**Author**: Stefan Koeller | **Date**: 2026-08-03

---

US electric vehicle manufacturer Rivian increased its revenue by 27 per cent in the second quarter, while reducing its net loss by around 25 per cent compared to the same period last year.

Rivian Automotive generated revenue of $1.658 billion in Q2 2026, up 27 per cent year on year. Revenue from the automotive business increased by 23 per cent to $1.143 billion, while the Software & Services segment grew by 37 per cent to $515 million. Of that total, $308 million (60 per cent) came from the joint venture with Volkswagen to develop software and a zonal E/E architecture. Volkswagen is now Rivian's largest shareholder, with a 15.9 per cent stake.

The net loss attributable to common shareholders for April–June amounted to $837 million, $278 million lower than the $1.115 billion loss recorded in the same quarter of the previous year.

Rivian produced 12,613 battery-electric vehicles at its plant in Normal, Illinois, in Q2 and delivered 12,194 units. Following the mid-June launch of deliveries for its flagship model, the R2, Rivian raised its full-year 2026 delivery forecast from 62,000–67,000 to 65,000–70,000 vehicles.

Rivian also announced that Amazon is now operating more than 40,000 of its electric delivery vans across thousands of North American cities.
