# New Jersey awards nearly $15 million for electric schoolbuses

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-11-18

---

The New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) has awarded nearly $15 million in grants to school districts and transport providers to purchase 44 battery-electric school buses and install 40 charging stations.

The funding comes from the third round of the state's Electric School Bus Grant Program. The programme has now supported the purchase of 145 electric school buses and 129 charging stations across New Jersey.

In October 2024, New Jersey announced its first $15 million allocation for 48 buses. A second round in January 2026 awarded over $18 million for 53 buses and 41 chargers.

The initiative was authorised in 2022 with up to $45 million over three years, funded through the NJ Board of Public Utilities' Clean Energy Fund.

Grants are available to school districts that own their buses and to private contractors, covering vehicles and charging infrastructure. At least half of selected applicants must operate in communities designated as overburdened by pollution. Recipients receive technical assistance via the New Jersey Fleet Advisor tool and a personalised Fleet Electrification Roadmap with vehicle recommendations, cost projections and on-site infrastructure assessments.

"The use of electric school buses greatly reduces the exposure of those most vulnerable to the harmful pollutants in diesel emissions – our children," said DEP Commissioner Ed Potosnak.
