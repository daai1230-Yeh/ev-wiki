# Renault and EDF unveil bidirectional charging offering

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-10-12

---

The Renault Group and Électricité de France (EDF) will launch a bidirectional charging offering in France next summer, comprising an electric vehicle, a wallbox, and intelligent charging management. Owners of Renault, Alpine, and Dacia EVs will be able to feed power from the battery back into the grid.

Renault and EDF, with EDF subsidiaries IZI by EDF and Dreev, announced a collaboration on intelligent charging (V2G, V2H, V2X). In the initial phase a package for private customers is planned for next summer, sold through Renault Group’s dealer network, including home charging station installation (by IZI and EDF) and an intelligent charge-management service.

The agreement covers three areas for single-family homes: distribution and installation of charging devices; services to simplify and optimise charging; and services that let stored vehicle energy power household applications or return to the grid.

Jérôme Faton (Renault Group) says the partnership responds to French consumers’ concerns about purchasing power and the energy transition by helping manage charging costs and unlock value from energy. Christelle Vives (EDF Business Services) notes Dreev’s smart-charging solutions are already deployed to more than 100,000 customers across France and Europe.
