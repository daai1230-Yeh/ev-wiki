# Olectra reaches 4,000 electric buses deployed in India as 297 units join HRTC fleet

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-08-31

---

Olectra Greentech says it has reached 4,000 electric buses deployed on Indian roads, with its 4,000th vehicle handed over as part of a 297-unit deployment for Himachal Road Transport Corporation.

The milestone was announced by the Hyderabad-based manufacturer on 30 July 2026 during a flag-off ceremony for the 297 Olectra electric buses entering the HRTC fleet in Himachal Pradesh.

According to Olectra Greentech, it is the first company in India to reach 4,000 electric buses deployed in the country. The manufacturer states that its vehicles are currently operating across multiple Indian states and cities.

India registered 2,944 electric buses between January and June 2026. According to data published by the Vahan Dashboard, Switch Mobility, JBM and PMI Electro Mobility Solutions together represented 70% of total registrations. Compared to the same period of 2025, the Indian e-bus market grew +40%.

Olectra has registered 280 units in the first half of this year, with its market share dramatically decreasing to 9.5% vs the 22.3% achieved last year.

## Olectra reaches 4,000 electric buses deployed in India

The 4,000th electric bus was symbolically handed over to Himachal Pradesh Chief Minister Sukhvinder Singh Sukhu and Deputy Chief Minister Mukesh Agnihotri during the ceremony.

The event also marked the induction of 297 Olectra electric buses into the HRTC fleet and was attended by representatives of the Government of Himachal Pradesh, HRTC and Olectra Greentech.

Olectra states that its 4,000 electric buses have collectively travelled more than 730 million kilometres — approximately 182,500 kilometres per vehicle across the deployed fleet. The company reports more than 650,000 tonnes of avoided CO2 emissions.

## 297 Olectra electric buses enter HRTC fleet in Himachal Pradesh

The latest deployment adds 297 vehicles to the HRTC fleet. The company did not provide technical specifications for the buses in the announcement, including vehicle length, battery capacity, charging system or claimed operating range.

Olectra Greentech cites its existing order book and contracts with state transport undertakings among its current electric bus activities in India. The company has not disclosed the breakdown of the 4,000 deployed vehicles by model, operator or individual state.

Mahesh Babu, Managing Director of Olectra Greentech Ltd., said: "Becoming the first company in India to deploy 4,000 electric buses on Indian roads is not just an Olectra milestone, it is a defining milestone for India's electric mobility industry."
