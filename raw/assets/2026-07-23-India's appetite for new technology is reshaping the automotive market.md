# India's appetite for new technology is reshaping the automotive market, says Harman India MD

**Date**: 22.07.2026

---

Krishna Kumar G, Managing Director of Harman India, says India's aspirational buyers are accelerating the adoption of connected, AI-driven and software-defined vehicle technologies, reshaping the country's automotive market.

According to Kumar, people's aspirations are higher and affordability has improved, so consumers are willing to spend more on cars with better features. Automakers are adding technology once reserved for luxury models to vehicles at much lower price points, as buyers increasingly expect the same digital experience inside their cars that they enjoy on smartphones and other connected devices.

Kumar said the automotive industry is entering a defining phase in which intelligence, software and seamless connectivity will shape every aspect of the driving experience. He expects the connectivity segment to emerge as a major growth driver as connected vehicles become mainstream, predicting that "there will not be many cars which will not have any connection at all. All cars will be connected. Some kind of connection."

Harman International has said it aims to triple its revenue from Indian operations over the next three years, with the company already maintaining its largest employee base in India.
