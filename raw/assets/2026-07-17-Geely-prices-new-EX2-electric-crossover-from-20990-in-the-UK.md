# Geely prices new EX2 electric crossover from £20,990 in the UK

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

Geely Auto UK has announced pricing and specifications for the new EX2. The compact electric crossover starts at £20,990 on the road, offers up to 214 miles (344 km) of WLTP range and comes with rear-wheel drive across the range.

The EX2 is offered in three trim levels — Pro, Max and Ultra — and is built on Geely's Global Intelligent New Energy Architecture (GEA) with rear-wheel drive throughout. The entry EX2 Pro combines a 35 kWh battery with a 60 kW motor for up to 155 miles (249 km) WLTP. The Max and Ultra use a larger 47 kWh battery and an 85 kW / 150 Nm motor, raising WLTP range to up to 214 miles (344 km).

All variants support DC fast charging up to 80 kW and a 6.6 kW onboard charger. Standard equipment on the Pro includes a 14.6-inch central touchscreen, an 8.8-inch digital cluster, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, electrically adjustable front seats, keyless entry/start, LED daytime running lights and vehicle-to-load (V2L). The Max (from £23,490) adds the larger battery and more powerful motor. The range-topping Ultra (from £25,490) adds two-tone paint, ambient lighting, a 360-degree camera, wireless charging, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, a powered tailgate and a six-speaker audio system. A white interior is a £200 option. Six exterior colours are offered: Moon White, Comet Grey, Star Silver, Nebula Beige, Aurora Green and Jupiter Pink.

Geely launched the EX2 as the Xingyuan in China in October 2024; per CAAM data it was China's best-selling new energy vehicle last year, with 465,775 units delivered. International expansion began in November 2025, launching in Brazil as the EX2. General Manager of Geely Auto UK Michael Yang said the EX2 "represents an important step in our mission to make intelligent electric mobility accessible to more customers across the UK," combining a practical platform, advanced safety technologies, connectivity and attractive pricing.
