# Lucid cuts cash burn and secures additional $1.75 billion

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-11-16

---

Lucid reduced its cash burn by 30 per cent in the third quarter of 2026 compared with the previous quarter. At the same time, Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, the Public Investment Fund (PIF), is increasing its financing for the electric carmaker by a further $1.75 billion.

Lucid reported revenue of $350 million for the third quarter of 2026, up four per cent compared with the same period last year. At the same time, its free cash flow consumption fell by 30 per cent compared with the second quarter, to $1.04 billion.

The company sees this as the first progress resulting from its restructuring programme, launched in the summer. Following the second quarter, Lucid announced plans to better align production capacity with demand, reduce inventory and cut operating costs. According to the company, it has already achieved savings of $800 million this year. Overall, it aims to improve cash flow by $1.4 billion over the full year.

Lucid has been restructuring its business for several months. In October, the manufacturer introduced Lucid Recharged, a certified pre-owned vehicle programme in Europe, to reach additional customer groups. The company also announced plans to save around $1.4 billion in 2026 and postpone the launch of its midsize models until the second half of 2027.

Lucid is also gaining additional financial flexibility through an expansion of its financing from Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF). The existing credit facility is being increased by $1.75 billion.

## Lucid secures additional $1.75 billion from PIF

At the end of the third quarter, Lucid had total liquidity of around $2 billion. Including the additional PIF financing, this figure rises to $3.75 billion on a pro forma basis.

According to the company, these funds are sufficient to finance ongoing operations and key investments through the fourth quarter of 2027. This should enable Lucid to bridge the gap until production of its new midsize model range is scheduled to begin.

Development of the new midsize models remains on schedule, according to Lucid. Testing, validation and final prototype work continued in the third quarter, while pilot production has now entered its final phase. Series production is still scheduled to begin in the second half of 2027. Lucid plans to announce specific dates for the unveiling and production launch in the first quarter of 2027.

The manufacturer also reports progress at its second plant in Saudi Arabia. According to Lucid, the AMP-2 production facility is in the final stages of industrialisation and commissioning. Its official opening is scheduled for early 2027.

In parallel, Lucid is advancing its autonomous driving activities. In its partnership with Uber and Nuro, all planned project milestones had been achieved by the end of the third quarter. More than 100 vehicles were already operating as part of the programme by August.

Lucid has also announced a partnership with European mobility platform Bolt. The initial goal is to deploy at least 25,000 vehicles. Lucid identifies these projects, alongside its cash flow improvement programme, the expansion of manufacturing in Saudi Arabia and the new midsize model range, as its four main strategic priorities.
