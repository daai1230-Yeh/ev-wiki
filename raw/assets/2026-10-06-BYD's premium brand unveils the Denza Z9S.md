# BYD's premium brand unveils the Denza Z9S

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-05

---

The premium brand Denza from the Chinese BYD Group has unveiled the Denza Z9S, a fully electric mid-size to full-size sedan that starts at around 34,000 euros in China. Depending on the variant, the range is said to reach up to 1,100 km according to the Chinese test cycle.

In Europe, Denza marked its debut in spring with the Denza Z9GT, an all-electric shooting brake that starts at €144,500 in Germany. The new Z9S is not a successor to the Z9GT but will stand alongside it as an independent electric sedan.

The Z9S is a more compact alternative, measuring 5.09 m in length, 1.98 m in width and 1.49 m in height, with a 3.025 m wheelbase. It is available with single-motor (rear-wheel drive, 370 kW) and triple-motor (all-wheel drive, up to 890 kW combined) drivetrains.

Equipped with BYD's second-generation Blade Battery (102.3 kWh), the Z9S achieves ranges of 780 km CLTC (632 km WLTP), 920 km CLTC (745 km WLTP), and 1,100 km CLTC (891 km WLTP). The high-performance version accelerates from 0 to 100 km/h in 2.68 seconds.

All variants feature BYD's Flash-Charging technology, charging from 10% to 70% in 5 minutes and 10% to 97% in 9 minutes. Denza says the vehicle can also charge from 20% to 97% in 12 minutes at temperatures as low as -30°C.

On board is the "God's Eye" driver assistance system (version 5.0), supporting urban and motorway navigation as well as safety features such as automatic emergency braking and emergency steering assist.

The interior features a wrap-around design with a 17.3-inch freestanding central display, a 13.2-inch digital instrument cluster, and a 50-inch head-up display. Additional features include Nappa leather seats, a suede headliner, and metallic accents. Rear passengers get a 7-inch display in the centre armrest.

Pricing: base model 255,800 yuan (~€34,000), flagship fast-charging variant 275,800 yuan (~€36,700), E3 performance variant 325,800 yuan (~€43,400). This positions the Z9S as a direct competitor to the Tesla Model 3, which in China starts between 235,500 and 339,500 yuan. Chinese industry media say the Z9S offers significantly more value for money.
