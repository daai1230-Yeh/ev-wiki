# Mars and Rewe deploy 47 battery-electric trucks on pilot corridor

**Author**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-07

---

*Note: the live electrive.com page could not be reached during this clip (Chrome connection was interrupted); this summary is reconstructed from search-indexed excerpts of the article.*

Food giant Mars and grocery retailer Rewe have announced their first continuous test corridor where goods are transported electrically, closely collaborating to establish a cross-border, end-to-end route for zero-emission transport of food products using a total of 47 battery-electric trucks.

The end-to-end corridor spans the Netherlands and Germany. Mars products are transported by battery-electric truck from chocolate factories in Veghel (Netherlands) and Viersen (North Rhine-Westphalia), as well as from a pet food factory in Verden (Lower Saxony), to Mars's logistics centre in Minden. From there, goods continue to Rewe's central warehouse in Oranienburg (Brandenburg), and subsequently via electric trucks to over 300 REWE stores and a REWE delivery service warehouse.

Mars currently operates 23 battery-electric trucks on its portion of the route, while Rewe deploys 24, totalling the 47 vehicles. Since Mars and Rewe began using electric trucks on their respective sub-routes, the partners report logging over 2.4 million electric kilometres, saving around 750,000 litres of diesel, and avoiding nearly 2,600 tonnes of CO₂e (well-to-wheel). The newly launched end-to-end corridor, spanning from production all the way to the point of sale, is set to take their electrification efforts to the next level.
