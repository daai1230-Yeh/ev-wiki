# Pilot expands EV charging network to more than 300 locations

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 2026-07-18

---

Pilot Travel Centers added 50 new DC fast-charging sites in the first half of 2026, expanding its coast-to-coast EV charging network to more than 300 locations. According to the company, the network now provides EV charging at more than one-third of its locations.

The expansion spans 25 states and forms part of a wider programme of investment by the Tennessee-based company. Following the latest additions, the network exceeds 300 charging locations nationwide. Data from the DCFC Tracker indicates that the network currently comprises 309 charging stations with 1,323 individual charging stalls.

Pilot has developed the charging network in partnership with EVgo, which operates more than 1,200 charging stations and over 5,200 charging ports across the United States.

Pilot highlighted that its charging offer is designed to integrate EV charging with the services traditionally available at roadside travel centres. "Pilot's charging experience is differentiated by offering the same convenience, access and reliability that non-EV drivers have come to expect," the company stated. "Pilot's coast-to-coast EV network offers a comprehensive, exceptional experience with fast chargers and 24/7 monitoring, and many locations offer drivers canopies to escape the weather and their favourite Pilot eats offerings."

The charging expansion coincides with broader investments in Pilot's travel centre network. During 2026, the company opened new sites in Chicopee, Massachusetts, and Ponce de Leon, Florida, while completing modernisation projects at 18 existing locations across 13 US states and Canadian provinces.

"Pilot is focused on investing in areas that matter most to create spaces our guests want to return to and are worth staying for our team members," Sean Marrero, President of Retail at Pilot, said. "Whether you're stopping for a charge, a full tank, or a fresh meal this summer, we are making the most of every mile for every traveller on the open road."
