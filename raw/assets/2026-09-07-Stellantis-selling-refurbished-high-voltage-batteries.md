# Stellantis selling refurbished high-voltage batteries

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 04.09.2026

---

Stellantis' circular-economy unit SUSTAINera is extending its refurbished-parts lineup — previously focused on combustion vehicles — to EVs and hybrids, now offering refurbished high-voltage batteries, e-compressors, and electrified dual-clutch transmissions for most Stellantis BEV/PHEV models. Parts are refurbished to OEM spec with the same performance and warranty as original MOPAR parts, and Stellantis claims up to 80% raw-material savings and 40% lower CO2 versus new parts; SUSTAINera has also previously repurposed EV batteries for stationary storage (e.g., Rome Airport).

Stellantis will showcase the program at Automechanika Frankfurt (Sept 8–12) as a live workshop demo for dealers, workshops, and aftermarket professionals.
