# Opel Agila: sister model to Citroën's electric 'Duck' looks likely

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

Stellantis is planning two new entry-level electric models from 2028: the revival of Citroën's 'Duck' (2CV) and the Fiat Koala. According to Italian magazine Quattroruote, Opel will follow with a small electric city car on the same platform a few months later, reportedly called the Opel Agila. Neither Opel nor Stellantis has confirmed this.

The Stellantis E-Cars family announced in May begins with an electric reinterpretation of the 2CV (a concept was shown at the Paris Motor Show); Fiat is developing the Koala. Both are due in 2028.

## Return of a familiar model name

The Agila ran from 2000 to 2014 as a compact high-roof city car; the first generation was based on the Suzuki Wagon R+, the second on the Suzuki Splash. Quattroruote considers a tall, practical body style plausible for the new car, with short overhangs and a spacious interior.

Like its siblings, the car could measure around four metres, meeting the criteria for the planned M1E class of small electric cars under 4.20 m in the EU Auto Package, which would count with a factor of 1.3 as a "Super Credit" in manufacturers' CO₂ fleet calculations.

No reliable technical details exist yet. The target price for the E-Cars family is around €15,000, though Stellantis has not confirmed it as a binding list price.

## Stellantis focuses on affordable electric cars from Italy

Stellantis officially announced E-Cars in May 2026 to address the shrinking selection of affordable city cars in Europe; the 'E' stands for European, emotional, electric and eco-friendly. The cars will be developed and produced in Europe, with selected partners helping to cut cost and speed up launch. Production is planned at Pomigliano d'Arco near Naples (currently home of the Fiat Panda), with the first cars in 2028. In September the group reaffirmed plans for three small electric cars from the plant, but has not confirmed that one will be an Opel Agila.
