# India's e2W market penetration races towards 10% mark; TVS, Bajaj, Ather lead June sales

**Author**: Mayank Gupta | **Date**: 2026-07-04

---

June 2026 was a decent month for India's electric two-wheeler industry, with overall sales surpassing 1.93 lakh units — a YoY growth of 75 percent, the second-highest monthly sales after the record set in March 2026. MoM sales were up 12 percent. In H1 CY2026, total electric two-wheeler sales stood at 9,70,611 units, up 53 percent YoY.

Top 10 electric two-wheeler brands, June 2026 sales:

TVS Motor Company: 46,999 units (24% share, +76% YoY) — remained the segment leader.
Bajaj Auto: 43,234 units (22% share, +80% YoY).
Ather Energy: 31,188 units (16% share, +95% YoY).
Hero MotoCorp Vida: 21,792 units (11% share, +175% YoY) — first time market share reached double digits.
Ola Electric: 16,144 units (8% share).
Greaves Electric Mobility (Ampere): 10,928 units (6% share, +152% YoY).
River Mobility: 4,391 units (2% share, +214% YoY).
BGauss Auto: 3,916 units.
E-Sprinto: 1,878 units.
Simple Energy: 1,365 units.

With rising petrol prices and controversy around E20 fuel, electric two-wheeler sales are expected to keep climbing in coming months.
