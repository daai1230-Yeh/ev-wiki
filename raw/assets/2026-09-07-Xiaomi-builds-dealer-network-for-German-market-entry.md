# Xiaomi builds dealer network for German market entry

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 04.09.2026

---

At IFA Berlin, Xiaomi signed non-binding letters of intent with eight German auto-retail groups (including Emil Frey Germany, Ernst Dello Group, and Hahn Automobile) to build distribution and service coverage ahead of its planned 2027 German market entry — relying at least partly on existing dealer networks rather than starting from scratch. Exact store counts, models, pricing, and launch timing within 2027 remain undisclosed.

Xiaomi confirmed international EV sales plans back in March 2025 and opened a Munich R&D/homologation center last September to prepare for Europe. It has delivered over 700,000 EVs domestically since the SU7 launched in 2024, later adding the YU7 SUV, but its "Smart EV" division still posted a ¥2.6 billion (~€315M) operating loss in Q2 2026, even as the loss narrowed quarter-on-quarter.
