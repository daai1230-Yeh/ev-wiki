# This Chinese Automaker Just Beat BYD With An EV Battery That Recharges In 8 Minutes

**Author**: Andrei Nedelea | **Date**: Jul 29, 2026

---

Hongqi says its experimental battery can add 60% charge in under four minutes with a peak charging rate of 12C.

It was only last year that five-minute EV charging debuted in China; now Chinese automakers and battery manufacturers already treat it as a benchmark to beat. CarNewsChina reports that FAW Group's Hongqi brand has developed a battery that charges from 10% to 70% in just 3 minutes 41 seconds, and from 10% to 97% in 8 minutes 3 seconds, tested at 77°F (25°C).

The battery, developed with specialist Lishen Battery, can reach a peak charging rate of 12C — meaning it can charge at 12 times the pack's capacity, so an 80-kWh battery would peak at roughly 960 kW. FAW says a new anode, electrolyte, and carbon coating process reduced internal resistance, while liquid cooling kept the temperature difference across the pack within 5.4°F during charging.

FAW previously announced it was developing a 1,000-volt EV architecture capable of charging to 80% in five minutes, and the two projects could be connected. However, the automaker hasn't revealed the battery's capacity, peak charging power, or chemistry, nor which vehicle would receive it or when. This is purely a one-off battery test under controlled conditions.

This signals Hongqi joining a rapidly moving charging race among Chinese automakers. BYD demonstrated a one-megawatt charging system last year, adding more than 50% into a production Han L sedan in under five minutes; its Super e-Platform supports up to 1,000 kW and can theoretically add nearly 250 miles of range in five minutes. CATL's third-generation Shenxing LFP battery can reportedly go from 10% to 80% in 3 minutes 44 seconds, reaching 98% in 6 minutes 27 seconds (on paper so far), while claiming over 90% capacity retention after 1,000 ultra-fast charging cycles.

Geely's Lynk & Co 10 has demonstrated a 10%-to-70% charge in 4 minutes 22 seconds, reaching 97% in 8 minutes 42 seconds while briefly peaking around 1,100 kW. Geely's Zeekr brand has gone further on peak power, with an updated 001 charging from roughly 4% to 80% in under seven minutes while exceeding 1.3 megawatts peak. CATL has also announced a commercial-vehicle battery claiming a 20%-to-80% charge in under seven minutes.

Hongqi may not have won the charging race outright, or demonstrated its claims in a production vehicle like BYD and Zeekr have, but it shows how quickly EV charging technology is advancing in China, where automakers have a real drive to outdo one another.
