2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Europe's electric car market is booming, and cheaper models are a big reason why. According to a new report from the advocacy group Transport & Environment, electric car sales in the European Union reached 1.64 million units between January and August, up 45 percent from a year earlier.

歐洲電動車市場正在暴衝，而平價車款是一大原因。倡議組織運輸與環境（T&E）的新報告指出，歐盟1月到8月的電動車銷量達164萬輛，比去年同期成長45%。

Electric cars now make up roughly 22 percent of new car sales, and the number of models on offer has grown by 50 percent. Five of the top twenty best-selling electric models start below 30,000 euros, based on German prices before incentives. The group expects sales of cars priced under 25,000 euros to be seven times higher than in 2024.

電動車目前約占新車銷量的22%，可選車款數量增加了50%。銷量前二十名的電動車中，有五款以德國未計補助的價格計算，起價低於30,000歐元。該組織預期，售價低於25,000歐元的車款今年銷量將是2024年的七倍。

Renault is a good example. After the success of the 5 E-Tech, it launched the even cheaper Twingo, and its electric sales rose 63.2 percent in the first half of 2026. In August alone, the Kia EV2 recorded 4,429 registrations, the Cupra Raval 3,608, and the new Volkswagen ID. Polo 1,876 in its first month.

雷諾就是好例子。5 E-Tech成功之後，雷諾又推出更便宜的Twingo，2026年上半年電動車銷量成長63.2%。光是8月，Kia EV2就登記了4,429輛，Cupra Raval為3,608輛，全新的Volkswagen ID. Polo在上市首月也有1,876輛。

But price is not the whole story. Higher fuel prices are giving drivers an economic reason to switch, and expensive models still sell well. The Tesla Model Y, Skoda Elroq, BMW iX1, BMW iX3 and Volkswagen ID.4 each passed 6,000 registrations in August. European brands supply nearly 60 percent of the electric models on sale, while Chinese automakers hold 21 percent.

但價格並不是全部。油價上漲讓駕駛人有了轉換的經濟理由，而高價車款依然賣得很好。8月，Tesla Model Y、Skoda Elroq、BMW iX1、BMW iX3和Volkswagen ID.4的登記量都超過6,000輛。歐洲品牌提供了近60%的在售電動車款，中國車廠則占21%。

Across the Channel, the UK just had its strongest month ever for battery-electric cars. In September, registrations rose 36.3 percent to 99,199, and the market share reached 28.3 percent. Over the first nine months, registrations hit 454,945, with a year-to-date share of 26.2 percent, compared with 20.2 percent a year ago.

在英吉利海峽另一邊，英國剛創下純電車史上最強的單月成績。9月登記量成長36.3%、達99,199輛，市占率達28.3%。前九個月累計登記454,945輛，年度累計市占率為26.2%，去年同期為20.2%。

Yet the industry says it is still not enough. The UK's zero-emission vehicle mandate requires a 33 percent electric share of new car sales in 2026. With the market expected to reach 2.183 million units, that would need another 265,000 electric registrations in the final quarter alone. The industry body SMMT warns that targets continue to outpace demand.

然而業界認為仍然不夠。英國的零排放車輛法規要求2026年純電車占新車銷量33%。以預期2,183,000輛的市場規模計算，光是第四季就還需要再增加265,000輛純電車登記。業界組織SMMT警告，目標持續跑在需求前面。

Supporters of the mandate say it pushes carmakers to launch affordable models, which is exactly what is happening in Europe. Critics say the UK has the toughest targets and the highest energy costs in the world, and that forcing the pace risks heavy discounting. Both sides agree on one thing: more choice at lower prices is bringing buyers in.

支持法規的人認為，它促使車廠推出平價車款，這正是歐洲正在發生的事。批評者則說，英國擁有全球最嚴格的目標與最高的能源成本，強推速度恐導致大幅折價促銷。雙方都同意一件事：更多選擇與更低價格，正在把買家吸引進來。

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

1.Five of Europe's top twenty electric cars start below 30,000 euros. Is affordability the main reason EV sales are growing, or are fuel prices just as important?

歐洲前二十名電動車中，有五款起價低於30,000歐元。平價是電動車銷量成長的主因，還是油價同樣重要？

2.The UK mandate requires a 33 percent electric share, but the market is at about 26 percent. Should the government keep the target, relax it, or add more subsidies? Defend one option.

英國法規要求33%的純電占比，市場卻只有約26%。政府應該維持目標、放寬，還是增加補助？請為其中一個選項辯護。

3.Argue that strict targets force carmakers to build cheaper EVs. Then argue that they simply lead to heavy discounting and hurt profits.

請先主張嚴格目標迫使車廠生產更便宜的電動車，再主張它們只會導致大幅折扣、傷害獲利。

4.The report comes from an advocacy group, while the UK warning comes from the industry body. How should we read statistics from groups that have their own interests?

這份報告來自倡議團體，而英國的警告來自業界組織。面對各有立場的團體所發布的數據，我們該如何解讀？

5.European brands supply nearly 60 percent of the electric models on sale, and Chinese brands 21 percent. What does that tell us about competition in the affordable segment?

歐洲品牌提供近60%的在售電動車款，中國品牌占21%。這對平價車市場的競爭說明了什麼？

6.Would a cheap, small electric car be enough for you, or would you still want a bigger, more expensive one? Explain your reasoning.

一輛便宜的小型電動車對你來說夠用嗎，還是你仍想要更大、更貴的車？請說明理由。
