# America Now Has More Than 80,000 Public Fast Charging Ports

**Author**: Suvrat Kothari | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

*Condensed summary of the InsideEVs report, not verbatim.*

According to charging data platform Paren, the US added 4,106 public fast-charging ports across 664 stations in Q3 2026, down 12% in ports and 20% in stations year over year, as EV sales cooled after the rollback of federal EV-friendly policies. The network nonetheless passed 80,000 fast-charging ports for the first time, with a stronger Q4 expected. July set a record of 17.6 million public fast-charging sessions.

- **Operators:** Tesla added 1,287 ports (533 fewer than a year earlier) plus 124 via Superchargers sold to third parties, still the top contributor. Ionna (targeting 30,000 fast chargers in North America by 2030) was second with 378 ports, nearly triple last year's pace; Walmart third with 344.
- **Geography:** California led with 87 new stations, then Texas (66), North Carolina and Georgia (26 each). 42% of new openings were in CA, TX, FL, IL and NY. Dallas-Fort Worth and Houston grew most; rural areas got only 10% of new ports.
- **Power:** 73% of new ports deliver at least 250 kW (60% a year ago); 400 kW stations are the most popular new install. Tesla has 42 locations and 487 ports with V4 500 kW cabinets. Peak rates: Lucid Gravity, Porsche Cayenne Electric and BMW iX3 around 400 kW; Mercedes GLC and Volvo EX60 about 370 kW; Cybertruck reported 500 kW.

Newer sites are often at gas stations and travel centres, and cheaper models such as the Rivian R2, Hyundai Ioniq 5 and Kia EV6 can also use higher-powered chargers.
