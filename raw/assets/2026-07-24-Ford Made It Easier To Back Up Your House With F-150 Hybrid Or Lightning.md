# Ford Just Made It Easier To Back Up Your House With An F-150 Hybrid Or Lightning

**Source**: InsideEVs | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Ford has partnered with Global Power Products to make the GenerLink transfer switch compatible with the F-150 Lightning and F-150 Hybrid, offering a more permanent home-backup solution than running extension cords. Both trucks include Pro Power Onboard, which delivers power through a 240-volt bed outlet; owners can plug in appliances directly or run cords into the house, but Ford's new setup centers on a 12-inch cord called the Ford Vehicle Connector. The hardware costs $1,100, with installation at the meter running $250–$450, or $1,000–$1,500 for a next-to-meter install.
