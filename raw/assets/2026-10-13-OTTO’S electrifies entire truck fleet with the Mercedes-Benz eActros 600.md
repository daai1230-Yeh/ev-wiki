# OTTO’S electrifies entire truck fleet with the Mercedes-Benz eActros 600

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-12

---

Swiss retailer OTTO’S is converting its entire truck fleet to electric drive. 22 all-electric Mercedes-Benz eActros 600 trucks will supply its 140+ Swiss branches, charged with solar energy from the company’s own rooftops.

Swiss manufacturer Lanz + Marti built the bodywork for the complete series of 22 vehicles, plus two-axle trailers with plywood box bodies so the trucks can run as road trains.

With the eActros 600’s range of up to 500 km, OTTO’S says most routes need no intermediate charging; CEO Mark Ineichen says around 80% of daily routes fall within this distance. The trucks complete two to four tours daily and return to headquarters in Sursee (canton Lucerne) for charging.

Most electricity will come from the PV system at headquarters; the company also plans to feed solar power from branches into its in-house grid to run charging infrastructure. The announcement gave no details on charging capacity, investment costs, or full-commissioning timeline.

The eActros 600 (in series production since November 2024) has three LFP battery packs totalling 621 kWh, about 500 km range, and CCS charging at up to 400 kW.
