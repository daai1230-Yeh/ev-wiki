# Zeekr sets November 27 launch for 2027 7X as it refreshes core SUV lineup

**Author:** Lei Kang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-19

**KEY TAKEAWAYS**
- The 2027 Zeekr 7X will officially launch on the first day of the Guangzhou Auto Show, featuring a 900-volt architecture across the lineup and a new 85-kWh battery option.
- The 7X is a core sales pillar for Zeekr, contributing about 30% of the brand's deliveries in the first 10 months of this year.

Geely Auto's (HKEX: 0175) premium EV brand Zeekr will officially launch the 2027 Zeekr 7X all-electric SUV at the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27, bolstering the market competitiveness of its mainstay model through another major update.

The upgraded model will officially hit the market on the first day of this year's Guangzhou Auto Show, which is also its media day, according to a statement released by Zeekr on Thursday.

The 2026 Guangzhou International Automobile Exhibition (Auto Guangzhou) will run from November 27 to December 6 at the Canton Fair Complex, with a total of 115 new models set to debut during the show, according to the event's organizers.

Zeekr began pre-sales of the 2027 7X in China on November 5 with a starting price of 229,800 yuan ($34,230), unchanged from its predecessor.

Customers who place orders before the official launch are eligible for a limited-time offer where a 2,000 yuan deposit counts as 7,000 yuan toward the purchase price, and display vehicles have already arrived at stores nationwide.

Key changes in the revamp include upgrading the entire lineup to a 900-volt high-voltage architecture and adding a variant equipped with an 85-kWh Shendun Golden Battery, filling the capacity gap between the previous 75-kWh and 103-kWh batteries.

The updated model is offered in 3 variants — RWD Max, RWD Max+, and AWD Ultra — equipped as standard with 75-kWh, 85-kWh, and 103-kWh batteries, respectively.

The RWD Max+ has a standard CLTC range of 680 kilometers (423 miles), and opting for CATL's (HKEX: 3750) 103-kWh Qilin Battery brings its CLTC range up to 802 kilometers.

The 75-kWh and 85-kWh variants take 10.5 minutes to charge from 10% to 80%, while the 103-kWh version needs just 10 minutes, according to data released by Zeekr.

The new model will also debut the Geely Afari Smart Driving (G-ASD) 4.1 assisted driving system, and comes standard across the lineup with tire blowout stability control, active noise cancellation, and electromagnetic damping systems.

The Zeekr 7X initially launched in September 2024 and received an update in October 2025, meaning Zeekr is refreshing the model's lineup again after an interval of about 1 year.

The SUV has entered more than 50 countries and regions worldwide, with cumulative global orders surpassing 200,000 units as of late August this year.

As a sales mainstay for Zeekr, the 7X delivered 97,165 units from January to October this year, up 62.17% year-on-year and accounting for 30.28% of the brand's 320,887 total deliveries over the same period, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.

In October alone, the model delivered 10,359 units, up 35.25% year-on-year while down 13.97% month-on-month.
