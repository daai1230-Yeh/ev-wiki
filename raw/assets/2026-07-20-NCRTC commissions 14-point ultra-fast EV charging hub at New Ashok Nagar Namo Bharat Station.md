# NCRTC commissions 14-point ultra-fast EV charging hub at New Ashok Nagar Namo Bharat Station

**Author**: Rail Analysis India | **Date**: 2026-07-17

---

The National Capital Region Transport Corporation (NCRTC) has commissioned a high-capacity electric vehicle (EV) charging station at New Ashok Nagar Namo Bharat Station, marking another significant step towards promoting sustainable urban mobility in Delhi-NCR. The newly operational facility is among the largest public ultra-fast EV charging stations in Delhi and has been developed to support the growing demand for electric vehicle infrastructure while encouraging the transition to cleaner modes of transportation.

The charging station has been designed to cater to a wide range of electric vehicles, including private cars, commercial fleets, and electric buses. With advanced fast-charging capabilities, the facility is expected to reduce charging times and provide greater convenience for EV users travelling through the Namo Bharat corridor.

The newly commissioned station features 14 DC charging points, enabling multiple electric vehicles to charge simultaneously. The charging infrastructure includes 7 DC charging points of 240 kW for regular fast charging, 4 DC ultra-fast charging points of 360 kW for quicker turnaround of vehicles, and 3 DC ultra-fast charging points of 480 kW specially designed for heavy-duty electric vehicles, including electric buses and commercial fleets.

The charging facility has been equipped with advanced safety and monitoring systems to ensure secure and reliable operations. Users can conveniently access the station through a dedicated mobile application that helps them locate the charging facility and use its services seamlessly.

With the commissioning of the New Ashok Nagar facility, EV charging stations are now operational at nine Namo Bharat stations: Sahibabad, Guldhar, Duhai, Muradnagar, Modinagar South, Modinagar North, Meerut South, Anand Vihar and New Ashok Nagar.

The installation of EV charging stations forms an integral part of NCRTC's broader vision of creating an environmentally responsible public transport ecosystem. By integrating charging infrastructure with the Namo Bharat network, the organisation aims to provide seamless multimodal connectivity while supporting India's transition towards low-emission mobility.
