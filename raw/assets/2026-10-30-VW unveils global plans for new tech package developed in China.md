# VW unveils global plans for new tech package developed in China

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-10-29

---

Volkswagen Group China has presented a new technology package and plans for marketing it outside China. In future, the company aims to use its local developments for global markets and expand its China location into a central innovation and export hub.

Volkswagen Group China unveiled its new technology package – comprising software, electronics and a platform – under the name VEGA at its Technology Day in Hefei. VW is currently the only non-Chinese automaker with a locally developed technology foundation in the Chinese market.

## CMP and CSP as the foundation for future VW models in China

The China Main Platform (CMP) is designed for battery-electric and plug-in hybrid models in the A-segment. It is expected to achieve a CLTC range of over 600 km, with energy consumption of around 11 kWh per 100 km. Charging from 30 to 80 per cent takes 25 minutes. For the premium segment, the China Scalable Platform (CSP) features an 850-volt architecture, enabling charging from 10 to 80 per cent in 13 minutes. Fully electric CSP models are set to achieve ranges exceeding 800 km, with consumption below 13 kWh per 100 km.

## ADAS essential in China

Software subsidiary Carizon has developed its first in-house L2++ solution, HyperSense 8 (HS 8), which will enter series production in 2026 across seven new electrified models from FAW-Volkswagen, SAIC Volkswagen and VW Anhui. HS 8 supports navigation-assisted driving (NOA) on highways and in urban traffic, plus automated parking.

VW employs a virtual test ground based on its AI world model GAIA 2.0, which completes around five million simulation kilometres daily based on 30 million real-world scenario segments, and is set to scale to ten million per day.

## Level 4 autonomy planned from 2029

Level 3 systems are scheduled to reach series production maturity by 2027; VW already holds an official licence for real-world road testing in Hefei. The Group aims for Level 4 by 2029, based on the third generation of the CEA electronic architecture (CEA 3.0) and a proprietary control chip called C7H.

## China-developed technologies to be used globally

Four years ago the Group launched its strategic transformation under the motto "In China, for China". Now it is evolving into "In China, Beyond China", turning the Chinese location into a research hub and export node. Models from the Volkswagen and Jetta brands produced in China are already exported to the Middle East, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan and parts of Sub-Saharan Africa. By the end of 2026 exports are set to expand to Mexico, Egypt and Vietnam, followed by ASEAN countries and South America. South America is expected to become the first international region to integrate these results into its local portfolio starting in 2030, followed by South Africa.
