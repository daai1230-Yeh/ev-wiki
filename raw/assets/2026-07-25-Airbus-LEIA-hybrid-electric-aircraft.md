# Airbus tests hybrid-electric energy architecture for commercial aircraft

**Date**: 25 July 2026

---

Airbus, with partners across the EU, UK and US, is pursuing a hybrid-electric non-propulsive energy architecture for next-generation commercial aircraft through LEIA (Large Scale Integration Demonstrator of Hybrid Electrical Architecture), a project supported by the EU Clean Aviation Joint Undertaking. LEIA will advance high-voltage generation and distribution for electrical aircraft systems on short-to-medium range aircraft, bridging component-level innovation and aircraft-level maturity to prepare technologies for future integration and certification.

Airbus is leading integration of advanced electrical components — scalable motor/generators, next-generation electronic controllers, and power distribution equipment, among other aircraft systems — focused not on electric main propulsion but on a hybrid-electric energy architecture for non-propulsive systems. The systems will be validated through a comprehensive testing programme culminating in an aircraft-level ground demonstration in 2027, with integration tests (including battery integration and energy management) taking place at Airbus's STEP laboratory in Ottobrunn, Bavaria.
