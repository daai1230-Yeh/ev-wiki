# INIT enters autonomous fleet management market

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

*Condensed summary of the electrive report, not verbatim.*

Karlsruhe-based public-transport IT provider INIT acquired 67.74% of Norwegian software company Applied Autonomy (Kongsberg, founded 2017) on 1 October 2026; it joins INIT's Integrated Systems unit. Its xFlow platform does not develop self-driving tech (unlike Mobileye or Wayve) but handles the operational layer: integrating autonomous vehicles from different makers, real-time monitoring, remote support, and data for other systems; later it will work with INIT's MOBILE-ITCS to manage mixed autonomous and conventional fleets.

**Examples:** In Stavanger, Norway, Karsan's battery-electric autonomous e-ATAK bus (trials since 2022, public service without safety driver since May 2026) uses Adastec's flowride.ai for driving and xFlow for control-centre monitoring and remote support. xFlow is also used in an autonomy project at Rotterdam Airport.

**INIT's wider role:** it leads BRAVE10k, aiming to prepare the integration of over 10,000 autonomous vehicles into German public transport by 2030 (standard interfaces, procurement, approval, scaling), and in September 2026 joined MILA.mobility (about 30 partners, led by Leipziger Verkehrsbetriebe) on an open, manufacturer-neutral platform linking autonomous vehicles to control, dispatch and booking systems.
