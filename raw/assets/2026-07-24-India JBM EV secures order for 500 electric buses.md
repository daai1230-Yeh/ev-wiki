# India: JBM EV secures order for 500 electric buses

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

JBM Electric Vehicles, a subsidiary of India's JBM Auto, has secured an order for 500 electric buses from Indian electric commercial vehicle leasing platform Drivn, formalised via a memorandum of understanding. JBM EV will supply the buses to Drivn in phases over the next twelve months. The specific models have not been disclosed, but the initial batches are reported to be luxury intercity coaches.
