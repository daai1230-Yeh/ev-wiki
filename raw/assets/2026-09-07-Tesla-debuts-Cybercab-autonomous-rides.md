# Tesla debuts Cybercab autonomous rides

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 04.09.2026 (updated 07.09.2026)

---

Tesla quietly launched its Cybercab robotaxi to the public in Austin, Texas — no livestream, no press, Musk reportedly absent — opening bookings via its Robotaxi app for the two-seat, steering-wheel-free vehicle that began series production in April at Giga Texas. Tesla already ran robotaxi rides in several US cities using converted Model Ys, but this is the first public use of the purpose-built Cybercab itself. Neither service is fully autonomous in practice: Tesla operates a remote command center (as Waymo and Zoox do) to intervene when needed, and Model Y rides initially used in-car safety drivers before shifting oversight to chase vehicles. Tesla currently has approval for only 45 Cybercab units in Texas.

**Update, Sept 7:** NHTSA said Tesla self-certified the Cybercab as meeting Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards, and the agency has opened an audit into the process and technical data behind that certification — including how Tesla determined certain FMVSS requirements don't apply to the vehicle.
