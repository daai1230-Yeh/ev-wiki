# 'Completely Insane': China Has 650 New Car Models So Far This Year

**Author**: Rob Stumpf | **Date**: 2026-07-17

---

Chinese automakers have released around 650 new models since January. Of those, about 30 models per month are completely brand new cars. Faster development cycles and consumer needs are driving the innovation.

Chinese EVs have already made a splash in Europe, and some Chinese firms are gearing up to enter Canada too. But one thing that may go unnoticed is just how many new cars China is really pumping out — not just in quantity of units produced and exported, but in the sheer number of new models and refreshes hitting the market.

Around 650 models have launched, according to Bloomberg — roughly four cars either released or refreshed every single day between January 1 and June 30, 2026.

BYD's Executive Vice President, He Zhiqi, called that number "completely insane" on social media, adding that the domestic market in China is "not just fierce, but brutal."

That 650 figure includes facelifts, refreshes, and all-new models. Data from the China Automotive Technology and Research Center shows about 30 genuinely brand-new vehicles — with no previous entry in China's national vehicle database — have been released each month since January.

For comparison, only 29 new or refreshed vehicles were launched for the U.S. market in 2024. Over the next four years, a study by Bank of America Securities predicts 159 new models will launch in the U.S. — a stark contrast to the 650 cars launched in China over just six months.

Part of the reason for the uptick is a significant decrease in development cycles, as automakers compete with one another for market share amid slipping sales, ever-increasing competition, and an ongoing price war — pushing companies to one-up each other with updated vehicles to draw attention. AI has also played a role, from vehicle design to in-car tech, along with advances in driver assistance systems, denser batteries, and charging tech.

BYD's Zhiqi welcomed the competition in the spirit of fueling innovation, noting the company intends to become the world's largest automaker within five years, having once again taken the EV sales crown from Tesla. "Competition also breeds prosperity," he said.
