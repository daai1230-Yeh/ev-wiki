# First of 112 Solaris Trollino IMC trolleybuses enters service with AMT Genoa

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 21 July 2026

---

Municipal carrier AMT has introduced the first Solaris Trollino trolleybuses into passenger service in Genoa, marking the start of deployment of a 112-vehicle fleet. The vehicles entered service on 20 July, operating on AMT's route 20 between Via Rimassa and Piazza Vittorio Veneto.

The procurement process dates back to 2024, when AMT Genoa selected Solaris and Hess in a tender for a fleet of trolleybuses and flash-charged e-buses, initially covering 112 articulated trolleys. Škoda Electric supplies the traction equipment, with vehicle assembly carried out by Solaris. The first Trollino 18 was unveiled in Genoa in April 2025 ahead of deliveries.

The new vehicles are 18-metre Solaris Trollino 18 articulated trolleybuses with a capacity of 133 passengers, equipped with In Motion Charging (IMC) technology allowing operation both under overhead catenary and in battery-electric mode. Four rooftop and rear-compartment battery packs enable operation on sections without overhead wires. The passenger area includes 36 seats, two wheelchair spaces with an access ramp, 40 USB charging ports, LED interior lighting and air conditioning. The fully enclosed driver's cab includes a six-camera video surveillance system, four cameras for automatic passenger counting, and seven service cameras monitoring trolley poles and doors.

The introduction comes while AMT undergoes financial stabilisation: its 2024 financial statements showed a net loss of €55.9 million and total debt of approximately €280 million, including more than €73 million owed to banks and over €85 million to suppliers. In July 2026 the Liguria Region approved a €40 million support package, with the Municipality of Genoa and regional authority confirming additional measures to strengthen the company's financial position.
