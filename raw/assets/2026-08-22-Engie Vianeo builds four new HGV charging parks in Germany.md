# Engie Vianeo builds four new HGV charging parks in Germany

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-08-22

---

Engie Vianeo plans to build four new truck charging parks at motorway service areas in Germany. Two of these are planned on the A7 near Homberg (Efze) and at Gramschatzer Wald, with the others located on the A2 near Lippetal/Hamm and on the A9 near Pfaffenhofen/Schweitenkirchen. MCS charging will also be available.

Engie Vianeo has announced the installation of two HGV charging sites at 24-Autohöfe locations and two additional sites under the Euro-Rastpark brand. As a subsidiary of the French Engie Group, the company oversees the entire operating model, from investment and construction to ongoing operation.

Two locations have now been confirmed: at the 24-Autohof Homberg (Efze), a bottleneck on the A7 near Kassel, Engie Vianeo plans to commission three 400-kilowatt charging points and one MCS charger (Megawatt Charging System) with 1,000 kW in spring 2027. "With the central transhipment warehouse of the European logistics network ELVIS, an Amazon distribution centre, and a Palletways logistics hub, the site is characterised by high logistics expertise," the stakeholders explained.

Four 400 kW charging points and one 1,000 kW MCS charger are also scheduled for installation at the 24-Shell Autohof Gramschatzer Wald near Würzburg in the first half of 2027.

Roxanne Ruscheinsky, Member of the Management Board at 24-Autohöfe, commented: "Service areas are among the most efficient locations for developing infrastructure for alternative fuels... We are therefore delighted to expand our offering with ENGIE Vianeo to include high-performance and reliable charging infrastructure for electric HGVs."

Jan-Niklas Ellerich, Managing Director of ENGIE Vianeo in Germany, added that the company's goal is to establish 25,000 charging points across Europe by 2030, including 1,000 for heavy electric commercial vehicles.

ENGIE Vianeo has been active in the German market since July 2025, with reference projects including the Port of Kiel and mandates from Autobahn GmbH for the federally funded HGV fast-charging network. In total, around 50 sites in Germany are currently being developed by ENGIE Vianeo with charging infrastructure specifically for commercial vehicles.

As the second partner for the charging sites, Engie Vianeo has introduced service area developer and operator Euro-Rastpark, which will build HGV charging parks at Lippetal/Hamm (on the A2) and Pfaffenhofen/Schweitenkirchen (on the A9). At Lippetal/Hamm, six 400-kW charging points and two 1,000-kW MCS chargers are planned by July 2027; at Pfaffenhofen/Schweitenkirchen, six 400-kW charging points are set to go live as early as January 2027.
