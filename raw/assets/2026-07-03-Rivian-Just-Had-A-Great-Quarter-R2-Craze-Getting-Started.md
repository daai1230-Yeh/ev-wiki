# Rivian Just Had A Great Quarter. And The R2 Craze Is Just Getting Started

**Author**: Mack Hogan | **Date**: 2026-07-02

---

Rivian delivered 12,194 vehicles in Q2 2026, a 14.4 per cent year-over-year gain despite the loss of the federal EV tax credit, beating its own guidance of 9,000–11,000 deliveries. The jump came right after the launch of the R2, which the company says drove quarter-over-quarter growth alongside continued gains for the R1 and EDV. For context, the numbers remain small next to the industry's giants — Tesla delivered about 480,126 vehicles worldwide last quarter alone, and Toyota delivered 673,971 in the U.S. alone.

The strong quarter led Rivian to raise its full-year delivery forecast from 62,000–67,000 to 65,000–70,000, after delivering 42,247 EVs in 2025. The company says it has capacity to build up to 155,000 R2s per year at its Normal, Illinois plant, not counting the additional capacity coming online at its planned Georgia facility, where it also intends to build the R3. The R2 will also be Rivian's first model sold in Europe, with a smaller form factor better suited to export markets than the R1 line.

The article notes the R2 faces a tougher market than the one the Model Y entered in 2021 — the EV market is now oversupplied, the federal tax credit is gone, and new competitors are arriving — but frames Rivian's beaten guidance and raised outlook as a promising start to its "make-or-break" product offensive.
