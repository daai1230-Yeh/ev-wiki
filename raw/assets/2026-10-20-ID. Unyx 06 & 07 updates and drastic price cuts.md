# ID. Unyx 06 & 07: updates and drastic price cuts

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-10-19

---

Volkswagen Anhui (a joint venture between VW and the JAC Group) has revised its ID. Unyx 06 and ID. Unyx 07 models and is now offering them at a lower price on the Chinese market. This makes the ID. Unyx 07 the first BEV model from VW in China to cost less than 100,000 Yuan (~€13,300).

Just a few months after launch, VW Anhui has updated both China-specific models. The update introduces a LiDAR-based driver assistance system for urban driving, parking and manoeuvring. Both vehicles also get a new 77.5-kWh battery pack, increasing maximum CLTC ranges to up to 630 km for the ID. Unyx 06 and 670 km for the ID. Unyx 07.

**Competitive pressure in China**

Current prices are 20,000 Yuan lower than the list prices at market launch in May 2026. The electric sedan ID. Unyx 07 now starts at 109,900 Yuan (~€14,630) and spans four variants up to 149,900 Yuan (~€19,955). The SUV coupé ID. Unyx 06 comes in three variants, from 129,900 Yuan (~€17,290) to 169,900 Yuan (~€22,620).

As part of China's 'Double 11' shopping festival, the entry-level ID. Unyx 07 Lite is available for 99,900 Yuan (~€13,300), the first time a Volkswagen electric car has been available in China for under 100,000 Yuan. The ID. Unyx 06 Lite is priced at 119,900 Yuan (~€15,690).

These early updates and price adjustments highlight the pressure facing Western automakers in China, where domestic competition is fierce. As early as September, the joint venture, in which Volkswagen has held a 75 per cent majority since the end of 2020, cut the promotional price of the larger ID. Unyx 08 by 40,000 Yuan to 189,900 Yuan (~€25,280).
