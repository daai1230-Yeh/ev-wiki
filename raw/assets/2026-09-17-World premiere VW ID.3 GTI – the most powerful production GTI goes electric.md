# World premiere: VW ID.3 GTI – the most powerful production GTI goes electric

**Author**: Daniel Bönnighausen | **Date**: 2026-09-16 13:30

---

Volkswagen is expanding its electric GTI family. Following the ID. Polo GTI, the ID.3 GTI is now the second model to carry the iconic badge. Technically, the compact sports car is closely related to the ID.3 GTX Performance but features its own GTI tuning. Market launch is planned for the first quarter of 2027.

So far, Volkswagen has grouped its sportiest electric models under the GTX badge. With the exception of the ID.3 GTX, these feature an electric all-wheel-drive system. Visually, they already draw on familiar GTI design language – such as its red trim elements. However, the electric sports models had not yet borne the GTI badge itself.

Three years ago, with the ID. GTI Concept, Volkswagen had already signalled its intention to carry the GTI label into the electric age. Here, the "I" stands for "Intelligence" – whereas in the classic GTI, it refers to "Injection", a nod to the fuel injection technology of the internal combustion engine.

Fifty years after the debut of the first GTI, the Wolfsburg-based manufacturer unveiled its first fully electric model bearing the legendary badge in May: the ID. Polo GTI. Now, the ID.3 GTI follows as its second. At a media workshop in Hamburg, we were able to preview the compact sports car—though we have not yet driven it.

## New platform, familiar technology

Volkswagen describes the ID.3 GTI as "developed based on the ID.3 Neo". This holds true for the body and platform: like the ID.3 Neo, the GTI is built on the advanced MEB+ architecture. However, for the powertrain, Volkswagen relies on familiar technology.

The APP550 electric motor, already known from the ID.3 GTX Performance, delivers 240 kW and 545 Nm. Its power is sent exclusively to the rear axle – making the ID.3 GTI the first GTI to feature rear-wheel drive. With launch control activated, the ID.3 GTI accelerates from 0 to 100 km/h in 5.6 seconds, just 0.1 seconds faster than the GTX Performance. The top speed is electronically limited to 200 km/h. Thus, the GTI is more of a new shell with a familiar powertrain than a completely new development.

While Volkswagen confidently announces the ID.3 GTI as the "most powerful production GTI of all time", a closer look at this claim is warranted. The previous record holder was the Golf GTI Edition 50, with an output of 239 kW, which has only been delivered since the beginning of this year. The electric GTI thus surpasses it by just one kilowatt.

## Long range, moderate charging speed

The ID.3 GTI also draws its energy from a battery with a net capacity of 79 kWh. Volkswagen estimates a WLTP range of up to 601 km. At a fast charger, the battery is expected to charge at a maximum of 183 kW; 2 kW less than the previous GTX.

The charging process from 10 to 80 per cent is projected to take around 29 minutes instead of 27 minutes. However, these are still forecasted values. In the past, VW has often allowed higher charging power for its ID. models than officially stated. How this plays out in practice with the electric GTI remains to be seen.

## GTI tuning

While the previous GTX was technically closely aligned with the other ID.3 variants, Volkswagen aims to position the GTI more strongly through its own suspension tuning. The basic layout consists of a MacPherson front axle and a five-link rear axle. As with all GTI models, the so-called progressive steering is also standard. In addition, the ID.3 GTI is equipped as standard with the adaptive chassis control system, DCC.

For the GTI tuning, engineers have adjusted the springs, dampers and stabilisers, among other components. The elastokinematics of the front axle have also been modified to achieve more agile steering behaviour. Special tyres have been developed as well. How significantly the GTI differs in driving dynamics from the technically closely related GTX Performance will only become clear after a test drive.

## A button for GTI mode

Another new feature is the 'GTI' driving profile. It is activated via an illuminated button at the six o'clock position on the steering wheel and complements the familiar modes: 'Eco', 'Comfort', 'Sport' and 'Individual'. This adjusts the powertrain, progressive steering and DCC sport suspension accordingly. At the same time, the new launch control can now be activated.

As soon as the steering wheel button with the GTI emblem is pressed, a red signal from the 'ID. Light' indicates that the systems are switching. Simultaneously, the ambient lighting changes to red. In the 'Digital Cockpit', the 'GTI' view is activated in parallel. The experience is accompanied by an artificially generated combustion engine sound. Additionally, lap times and a retro display in the style of a late Golf I can be called up.

## New front, classic insignia

As part of the ID.3 Neo, Volkswagen has completely redesigned the front end, following the 'Pure Positive' design language of chief designer Andreas Mindt. The upper third is dominated by slim LED headlights, which in the GTI are standard as the latest version of the 'IQ.Light – LED Matrix Headlights'. Between them stretches a glass-covered light strip with an illuminated VW logo.

A red stripe above the light strip references a classic GTI feature, evoking the red-framed grille of the first Golf GTI from 1976. This is complemented by the typical honeycomb grille, vertical LED elements and two red-painted elements in the bumper. The exterior colour 'Tornado Red' also makes a return.

In the side view, the standard 20-inch alloy wheels immediately catch the eye. Named "Wörthersee", they pay homage to the legendary GTI gathering in Austria. The wheel with its eight twin spokes was already seen in a similar form on the ID. Polo GTI concept model.

Unlike the previous ID.3 GTX, the roof, rear spoiler and tailgate are painted in the body colour, which makes the roofline appear longer. A total of five paint finishes are available: 'Tornado Red Uni', 'Moonstone Grey Uni', 'Scale Silver Metallic', 'Grenadilla Black Metallic' and 'Glacier White Metallic'.

## Red on black in the interior

Volkswagen remains true to the GTI line in the interior as well. The ID.3 GTI adopts its cockpit layout from the ID.3 Neo. On a single line of sight are the 10.25-inch digital driver display and the 12.9-inch touchscreen of the new infotainment generation, 'Innovision', which replaces the previous 'Discover' system.

The GTI signature is defined by the colour scheme: red on black dominates. A fine red stripe runs across the entire width of the dashboard, and a red 12 o'clock marker adorns the new multifunction sports steering wheel. The red is also reflected in the top sports seats, whose 'Superclark' pattern reinterprets the tartan check of the first Golf GTI seats from 1976. Optionally, the entire seat system will be available in the microfibre 'ArtVelours Eco Soul'. The seats can optionally be ordered with full electric adjustment, including memory and massage functions.

## Assistance and extras

In terms of assistance systems, Volkswagen equips the ID.3 GTI comprehensively as standard and offers the next generation of Travel Assist as an option. Since the system now uses online data, it is called 'Connected Travel Assist' and, according to Volkswagen, is the first VW model in which the software responds to traffic lights.

Another new feature is one-pedal driving. For everyday use, the ID.3 GTI supports the Vehicle-to-Load function via an adapter, allowing it to power external devices – or, in combination with the optional bike rack mount (75 kg load capacity), recharge e-bikes between trips. Optional extras also include a panoramic roof, the 360-degree camera 'Area View', a head-up display with augmented reality and a Harman Kardon sound system.

## Early 2027 market launch

Technically, the new ID.3 GTI is very similar to the ID.3 GTX Performance. The differences are primarily in the GTI-specific suspension and steering tuning, the dedicated driving profile and the design and interior orientation. The rear-wheel drive, in particular, marks a break with GTI tradition: for the first time, power in a GTI is delivered exclusively to the rear axle.

Whether this tuning will also set the GTI apart dynamically from the GTX Performance cannot yet be assessed after the static presentation. Volkswagen has also not yet announced a price. The ID.3 GTI is scheduled to launch in the first quarter of 2027.
