# US procurement alliance orders 2,500 Tesla Semi trucks

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-23

---

A new shipping alliance called ZET SCALE has placed an order for 2,500 Tesla Semi trucks in the USA. The alliance is a purchasing coalition that aims to acquire electric trucks at lower prices through bulk purchasing power.

ZET SCALE stands for 'Zero-Emission Truck Shipper-Carrier Alliance Leading Electrification' and was launched by Catalyst Mobility (the non-profit freight organisation formerly known as CALSTART) and the Smart Freight Centre. The alliance's aim is to pool demand from major freight companies, enabling hauliers to purchase battery-electric trucks in bulk at lower prices.

A first major tender has now led to a contract award: Tesla secured the position as the primary supplier in the initial allocation, while Kenworth, Volvo, and RIDE were named as secondary suppliers. The tender covers 2,500 battery-electric Class 8 trucks – "a single deal that nearly doubles the US fleet of electric Class 8 fleet", the partners state. "After evaluating price, range, charging capability, and production capacity, the Alliance selected Tesla as its primary OEM for the initial 2,500-trucks." The secondary suppliers have agreed to honour the prices offered in the tender. However, it remains unclear whether utilising these alternative models will reduce Tesla's share of the order or if they are in addition to Tesla's portion.

All truck manufacturers offering Class 8 electric trucks in North America were invited to participate. The 2,500 trucks will be distributed across ten freight hubs in the coming years – Los Angeles, Stockton, Bakersfield, Seattle/Tacoma, Houston, Dallas, San Antonio, Chicago, Atlanta, and the Newark/New York area. ZET SCALE does not provide a more detailed delivery timeline.

Founding members of ZET SCALE include Microsoft and PepsiCo, among others, and additional members are welcome. Pooling demand leads to lower prices through large-volume orders, while manufacturers improve cost efficiency through higher production volumes. In the long term, the alliance aims to bring more than 10,000 electric vehicles onto US roads.

The alliance is supported by a financing company called ZET Financial, which places the orders and operates a 'ZET Lease' programme designed to remove residual value risk from fleet balance sheets. According to Michael Berube, President and CEO of Catalyst Mobility: "You can have good technology and still not have a market. What is different about ZET SCALE is that the demand was organized so manufacturers could price at scale. That is how costs move, and that is how clean trucks leave the pilot phase."

Christoph Wolff, CEO of the Smart Freight Centre, added: "Every part of the freight industry wants cleaner trucks, but until now each company faced that transition — and its risks — alone. ZET SCALE aligns shippers, carriers, manufacturers and financiers around a single, real demand signal."

Dan Priestley, Head of the Tesla Semi Programme, commented: "ZET SCALE's goals are exciting because they take the operational cost advantage and predictability that electricity provides and amplifies it at scale. We are proud to have been the primary selection in this RFP."

The new order eclipses Tesla's recently announced order from Einride, which ordered 500 Semi trucks in August. In parallel, Tesla has also secured an order for 50 electric trucks from IMC Logistics, including Standard Range Semis for port transport and Long Range Semis for routes between Southern California and inland warehouses. Meanwhile, Tesla plans to expand the Semi into Europe starting in 2027.
