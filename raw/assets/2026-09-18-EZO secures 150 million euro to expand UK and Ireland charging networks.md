# EZO secures €150 million to expand UK and Ireland charging networks

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-09-17 16:15

---

Irish charging provider EZO has secured €150 million in senior debt financing to expand its EV charging network across the UK and Ireland. This will fund the design, installation, operation and maintenance of more than 3,000 charge points — with a combined capacity of over 100 MW.

According to the company, the infrastructure will primarily be deployed through long-term concessions with public-sector partners. EZO estimates that the new charging network could generate more than €1 billion in electricity sales over the next 20 years, with part of the revenue shared with the public sector.

The financing is being provided by a consortium that includes Aberdeen Investments and Standard Life. EZO describes the structure as unusual for the charging sector, as revenues generated by the financed infrastructure will be ringfenced to repay the debt over a seven-year period. The company sees this model as a way to accelerate the deployment of charging infrastructure that is already backed by long-term public sector agreement.

Calling the agreement a "significant vote of confidence in EZO", Conor Kelly, CEO of EZO majority stakeholder Rubicon Capital Management said: "We understand this to be the first example of the application of this framework of financing in the EV charging sector and it is expected to become the preferred method of financing this collateral going forward."

CEO Ollie Chatten added: "Our focus is on working with strong public and private sector partners to build charging networks at scale across Ireland and the UK. The backing of our investment partners gives us the financial strength to deliver on those commitments".

Based in Ireland, EZO's expansion into the UK has gathered pace in recent months. In June, the charging provider secured a £176m contract to deploy and operate EV charging infrastructure across four Midlands councils. This 15-year project is expected to add 250 rapid and ultra-rapid chargers and support more than two million residents. And, earlier this year, EZO began the rollout of its EV charging network in North Scotland.
