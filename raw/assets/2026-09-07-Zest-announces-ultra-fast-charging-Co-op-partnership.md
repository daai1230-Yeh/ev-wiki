# Zest announces ultra-fast charging Co-op partnership

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 04.09.2026

---

UK charge-point operator Zest signed a multi-year deal with regional grocer Heart of England Co-op to fund and operate high-power EV chargers at nine supermarkets across the Midlands (including Coventry, Nuneaton, Wellesbourne, and Southam), though exact charger specs weren't confirmed (Zest's "ultra-rapid" tier has previously meant 150–300 kW). Both companies frame it as supporting EV adoption and sustainability commitments. Zest already works extensively with UK co-operative grocers, notably a larger, separate agreement with Central Co-op covering sites across the Midlands, Lancashire, and East Anglia — Central Co-op and Heart of England Co-op are distinct, unaffiliated businesses despite the shared "Co-op" branding.
