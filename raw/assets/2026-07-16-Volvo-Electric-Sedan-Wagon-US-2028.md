# Volvo Could Bring An Electric Sedan And Wagon To U.S. By 2028: Report

**Author**: Nico DeMattia | **Date**: Jul 15, 2026

---

Volvo is reportedly considering bringing an electric sedan and wagon back to the U.S. by 2028, according to a source cited by Automotive News. Volvo discontinued the S60 sedan in 2024 and killed the S90 sedan and V60 wagon last year, exiting the North American sedan market entirely to focus on crossovers and SUVs.

The reversal may be tied to Polestar's exit from the U.S. market after the Department of Commerce barred cars using Chinese software over national-security concerns; Volvo, though owned by Geely, has so far been spared that restriction.

If they arrive, the sedan and wagon won't be new exclusive models — both are already in development for Europe on Volvo's SPA3 platform, the same architecture underpinning the EX60, with an 800-volt system and expected range around 400 miles. They're likely to be badged S60 and V70 in Europe. Volvo is reportedly eyeing a starting price around $50,000 and anticipates only about 10,000 combined annual U.S. sales between the two models. Volvo declined to comment on future products.
