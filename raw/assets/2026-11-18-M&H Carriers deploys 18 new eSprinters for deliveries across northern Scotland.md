# M&H Carriers deploys 18 new eSprinters for deliveries across northern Scotland

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 2026-11-17

---

The Scottish haulier and distributor M&H Carriers has expanded its fleet by 18 new Mercedes-Benz eSprinters. They will be operating across the Highlands and north-east Scotland. With increased range, the new electric vans will be able to manage long routes to remote communities even during cold weather periods.

With the new addition, the company is bringing its total electric fleet to just under 30 vehicles. The company bought the vans in partnership with Mercedes-Benz Vans UK and dealer Western Commercial. The first five new vehicles went on the road in July, and the remaining 13 are now starting work at depots in Inverness, Elgin, Aberdeen, and Perth.

M&H Carriers introduced its first electric vans in 2021. Since then, the company has learned what is required to make electric deliveries work in northern Scotland, considering its long routes, remote communities, and temperature drops in winter.

Fraser MacLean, Managing Director at M&H Carriers, noted: “We’ve been operating electric vans for five years, but until now, we’ve struggled to find vehicles where the range is capable of more than just local deliveries close to our depots. The Mercedes-Benz eSprinter has given us the confidence to take that next step.”

“The range and reliability were the big factors for us, but ultimately, the vehicles have to make commercial sense too,” MacLean added. “We’re operating in some of the coldest and most challenging parts of the UK, so if we can make electric vans work here, it shows what’s possible for other delivery businesses.”

The company’s progress has also been recognised by Sam Clarke, Company Director at Road to Zero Ltd, who has extensive experience operating electric commercial vehicle fleets: “As someone who proudly owned the UK’s largest full electric van fleet in the past, operating in dense city centres and having worked with Fraser and M&H Carriers over many years, to see their zero-emission progress in some of the most geographically challenging landscapes in the UK is seriously impressive.”

The current Mercedes-Benz eSprinter is available with three usable battery sizes, 56 kWh, 81 kWh, and 113 kWh, offering a WLTP range of up to 440 kilometres on the largest pack. All versions are rear-wheel drive and can be ordered with either a 100 kW or 150 kW electric motor, delivering up to 400 Nm of torque. The model is offered as a panel van in two lengths or as a chassis cab, with permissible gross vehicle weights of 3.5 or 4.25 tonnes.
