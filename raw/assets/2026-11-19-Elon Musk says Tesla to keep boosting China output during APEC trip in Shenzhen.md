# Elon Musk says Tesla to keep boosting China output during APEC trip in Shenzhen

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-19

- Musk made his first public visit to Shenzhen, praising China's architecture, natural landscapes, and railway system.

- Tesla's Shanghai factory delivered 810,889 vehicles in the first 10 months of this year, up 21.42% year-on-year.

Tesla (NASDAQ: TSLA) CEO Elon Musk said during his first public visit to Shenzhen that the US EV maker will continue to increase production in China.

Musk made the remarks today during a meeting in Shenzhen with Ren Hongbin, chairman of the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade and chair of the APEC CEO Summit 2026, according to a Thursday report by Yuyuan Tantian, a media brand affiliated with China Central Television (CCTV).

During the meeting, Musk described himself as a super fan of China, praising the country's architecture, natural landscapes, and railway system.

Musk arrived in Shenzhen on November 18 with a US delegation to attend events related to the 2026 APEC Economic Leaders' Week held in the city.

Participating remotely in a US program interview, he called Shenzhen China's largest center for advanced technology and said that maintaining good trade and relations between China and the US is to some extent good for the world.

The Tesla CEO then flew from Shenzhen to Shanghai to meet with the Giga Shanghai team, according to a Weibo post published Thursday by the company's vice president, Grace Tao.

"Musk's brief 24-hour visit to China included a special flight from Shenzhen to Shanghai," Tao wrote. She said he spoke highly of the Shanghai Gigafactory's manufacturing capabilities, as well as the execution and manufacturing standards demonstrated by the Chinese team.

Giga Shanghai is Tesla's largest production base and export hub globally. It began operations at the end of 2019 and currently has an annual capacity of about 1 million vehicles, mainly producing the Model 3 and Model Y.

In January-October this year, the Shanghai plant delivered 810,889 vehicles, up 21.42% year-on-year, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.

As local sales in China currently face pressure, strong export performance from the Shanghai factory has supported its overall output growth.

In the first 10 months of this year, Tesla's retail sales in China totaled 389,905 vehicles, down 15.00% year-on-year. Over the same period, cumulative exports from the Shanghai factory reached 420,984 vehicles, up 101.28% year-on-year.

In October, Tesla's domestic retail sales in China fell 66.10% year-on-year to 8,817 vehicles, while exports from the Shanghai plant reached 59,012 vehicles, accounting for a record 87% of its 67,829 wholesale sales that month.

Tesla disclosed earlier this month during the ninth China International Import Expo (CIIE) that the parts localization rate at Giga Shanghai has further risen to 98% from its previous statement of over 95%.

Currently, Tesla has signed more than 400 local tier-1 suppliers in China, over 60 of which have entered its global supply chain system.
