# BEV registrations rose sharply across Europe in September

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-22

---

According to ACEA, 290,117 new battery-electric passenger cars were registered in the EU in September, up 72.9 per cent year-on-year. All powertrains combined: 971,048 cars (+9.3%). BEV share reached 29.9 per cent in September and 22.7 per cent year to date.

January–September: 1,931,465 new EU electric cars (+48.5%); BEV share up from 16.1 to 22.7 per cent, second to non-plug-in hybrids (36.3%).

## Germany and France drive growth

- Germany: 88,599 BEVs in September (+94.7%), 34.5% share; 604,144 YTD (+58.1%)
- France: 65,243 (+107.5%); 387,342 YTD (+79.1%)
- Denmark: 17,784 (+36.9%); 124,049 YTD (+40.3%)
- Belgium: 125,220 YTD (+15.7%)

These four markets accounted for about 64 per cent of all EU BEV registrations.

## Hybrids remain the dominant powertrain

Full/mild hybrids: 3,090,530 YTD (36.3%). Plug-in hybrids: 860,257 (10.1%, +18.6%), with Italy at 121,647 (+72.4%), Spain +34.7%, Germany 247,489 (+13.7%). Petrol fell 19.1% to 1,802,078 (share 27.7→21.2%); diesel fell 19.5% to 602,206 (share 9.3→7.1%).

## Growth in non-EU states

EFTA: 27,115 BEVs in September (+36.3%); UK: 99,201 (+36.3%); YTD EFTA 163,398 (+10.9%), UK 454,947 (+30.2%). EU+EFTA+UK: 416,433 in September (+59.9%); 2.55 million YTD (+41.9%).

## BYD, Chery and Leapmotor see strong growth

EU September: BYD 28,309 (+111.4%; 206,002 YTD, +154.4%); Chery group 20,707 (+223.8%; 137,003 YTD); SAIC 22,978 (+24.8%; 186,650 YTD); Leapmotor 10,229 (+255.7%; 72,760 YTD, +393.6%). Across EU+EFTA+UK, September: BYD 49,953, Chery 52,801, SAIC 41,987, Leapmotor 14,594.
