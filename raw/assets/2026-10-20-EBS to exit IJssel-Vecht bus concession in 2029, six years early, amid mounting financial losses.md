# EBS to exit IJssel-Vecht bus concession in 2029, six years early, amid mounting financial losses

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

Public transport operator EBS will end its IJssel-Vecht bus concession in the Netherlands in July 2029, six years ahead of the original 2035 expiry, after an agreement with the provinces of Gelderland, Flevoland and Overijssel over financial losses on the contract, which started in 2022. The provinces will amend the concession and launch a new tender, while EBS keeps operating services until July 2029.

According to local trade media PV Personenvervoer, the provinces will provide additional financial contributions to maintain service continuity, but the largest share of the losses stays with EBS. The financial terms are not final and no amounts have been disclosed; the provinces say the contributions fit within existing public transport budgets.

The decision follows talks after provincial representatives and EBS employees were told in early July about substantial losses. EBS faces operational deficits with no prospect of restored profitability. The concession was awarded after a competitive tender in 2022, when EBS was selected from three bidders. The provinces cite operational setbacks, higher costs and external factors including electricity grid congestion.

**New tender**: the three provinces will prepare a new procurement for bus services from 2029, with the geographical and contractual structure to be defined in the coming months. Gelderland intends to set up a separate concession for its part of the network, though the provinces plan to keep cooperating under the RRReis brand. There is no immediate change of operator.
