# Zijin unveils 350 tonne battery-electric dump truck

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 20.08.2026 - 13:30

---

Zijin Longking is expanding its range of electric mining vehicles with the ZL230E. The new battery-electric dump truck can transport up to 230 tonnes of material and is said to charge its battery to 90 per cent in 25 minutes.

Chinese mining equipment manufacturer Zijin Longking unveiled the ZL230E in early August. The vehicle was developed in-house and is designed for intensive, continuous operation in open-pit mining. With a tare weight of 120 tonnes and a maximum payload of 230 tonnes, it achieves a permissible gross weight of up to 350 tonnes, while its dump body can hold up to 125 cubic metres when heaped.

For propulsion, Zijin Longking uses a multi-axle system with independently driven axles. Two electric motors, each delivering 800 kW, provide a combined output of 1.6 MW. Power is distributed to individual axles depending on driving situation, ensuring high sustained performance during loaded uphill drives and long haul routes. Battery capacity is around 1,400 kWh, with a charging time of 25 minutes to reach 90 per cent state of charge. The ZL230E is also equipped with an exceptionally rigid frame, redundant braking and steering systems, dual chassis control, and an intelligent energy management system.

The ZL230E is not an isolated project. At the end of 2025, Zijin Longking introduced the LK220E, a self-developed battery-electric dump truck. The ZL230E, previously developed under the designation LK350E, expands the portfolio into a significantly higher payload class.

The company is gaining practical experience at sites including the Julong Copper Mine in Tibet, where several Zijin Longking battery-electric dump trucks with 140-tonne payloads are already in operation. Each vehicle moves more than 2,000 tonnes of material per day, achieves availability of over 90 per cent, and consumes more than 17 per cent less energy than comparable diesel vehicles.

Zijin is pursuing various energy supply concepts: the ZL230E relies on rapid recharging, while swappable batteries are used at other sites — at the Xinjiang Zijin Zinc Mine, battery swapping is carried out automatically. In future, the company plans to combine electric trucks with wind and solar energy, stationary storage, and charging and battery-swapping infrastructure.

Zijin aims to replace conventional mining trucks with electric models within three years. The company estimates the cost per tonne-kilometre of its electric trucks in Chinese mines is already around 29 per cent lower than internal combustion vehicles. Zijin has not specified when the ZL230E will enter mass production.
