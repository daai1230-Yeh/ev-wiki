# VinFast unveils new VF 5 and VF 6 with refreshed design, improved range

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-11-18

---

Vietnamese automaker VinFast has unveiled facelifted versions of the VF 5 and VF 6 small electric SUVs, with revised styling, longer ranges and new features. Both launched first in Vietnam, with deliveries expected after Lunar New Year (6 February 2027).

## The new VF 5

The front gets a straighter, higher bonnet edge, a subtler chrome strip replacing the deep V-shaped trim, and lower projector LED headlights. New 17-inch two-tone aero wheels feature on the sides; at the rear, slim LED units sit at either end of a full-width red strip.

Inside: a flatter, slimmer dashboard, two-spoke steering wheel, pull-up door releases, an electronic parking brake and a column-mounted gear lever, freeing space for a wireless charger. A 10.1-inch touchscreen replaces the 8-inch unit, but there is no instrument cluster. Rear air vents and a 360-degree camera are added.

The VF 5 measures 3,987 × 1,772 × 1,595 mm (20 mm longer, 49 mm wider, 16 mm taller). Premium trim costs 540 million dong (~€19,000); Green trim for commercial customers 460 million dong (~€16,200).

A new 72 kW / 180 Nm motor powers the Premium (28 kW less power, 45 Nm more torque). A 43 kWh battery delivers 370 km NEDC (+44 km), charging 10–70% in under 28 minutes. The Green uses the same motor with the old 37.23 kWh LFP pack, now charging 10–70% in under 25 minutes (from 33), for 320 km NEDC.

## The updated VF 6

Subtle exterior changes: smaller bumper recesses, slimmer arrowhead headlamp housings, semi-recessed door handles, removed grey cladding and new 19-inch aero wheels. Inside, a minimalist design similar to the VF 5, a floating touchscreen and a repositioned wireless charging pad.

In Vietnam, Comfort and Premium trims cost 646 million dong (~€22,700) and 715 million dong (~€25,100), with 10-inch and 12.9-inch screens respectively; a digital instrument cluster is now included.

The VF 6 is 4,246 × 1,827 × 1,616 mm with 190 mm ground clearance (+20 mm). A single 130 kW / 250 Nm motor is used. A 60 kWh battery replaces the 59.6 kWh LFP pack, delivering 510 km NEDC (Comfort) and 480 km (Premium).

VinFast does not sell the VF 5 in the EU, but could start exporting the new VF 6 in Q2 2027.
