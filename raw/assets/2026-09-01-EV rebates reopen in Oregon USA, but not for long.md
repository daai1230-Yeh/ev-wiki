# EV rebates reopen in Oregon USA, but not for long

**Author**: electrive guest author | **Date**: 2026-08-31

---

In the US state of Oregon, the Clean Vehicle Rebate Program has reopened for eligible vehicles bought or leased through to 4 November 2026. According to the Oregon Department of Environmental Quality, this round of funding is expected to run out by then and will therefore suspend the program again by 5 November.

Electric vehicles have proven popular in Oregon, and the state's rebate programs tend to run out quickly. In previous years, the Oregon Department of Environmental Quality had to halt its rebate program due to sheer demand. According to local media, last year's rebate lasted three and a half months before it was suspended again.

Those who would like to own or lease an electric vehicle in the state will now have little more than two months to make use of EV rebates, as the state has limited funds for the program — currently about $8.5 million. If the last three years is anything to go by, those wanting to make use of the program should get in quickly.

For those who applied before 4 November but missed out because funds were depleted, the DEQ will have a waiting list who may get paid out once the agency has more funds, expected in spring 2027.

Rebates are available at dealerships but can also be applied for afterwards. According to local media, there are different rebates available:

The standard rebate is available regardless of income to residents, businesses, non-profits and government agencies. Rebates come at $2,000 for a new all-electric vehicle, $1,500 for a new plug-in hybrid and $375 for a new electric motorcycle.

The "charge-ahead" rebate is for low-income households, who can receive $7,500 for a new electric car or $5,000 for a new plug-in hybrid. Used electric cars can come with a rebate of $4,000, depending on price, or up to $2,500 for a plug-in hybrid.
