# Rivian boosts revenue thanks to R2 and VW partnership

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-30

---

Rivian's Q3 2026 revenue grew 38% year-on-year to $2.155 billion. Deliveries and the software and services business both grew strongly; 16% of revenue came from the Volkswagen partnership. Rivian raised its annual forecast.

- Production: 19,751 vehicles (+84%); deliveries: 19,248 (+46%), both records. The new R2 was already the best-selling model in Q3, with a second shift now running at the Normal, Illinois plant.
- Automotive revenue was $1.55 billion (+36%), with a gross loss of $80 million (vs. $130 million a year earlier). R2 ramp-up added about $120 million in extra manufacturing costs.
- Software and Services revenue was $605 million (+45%), gross profit $256 million (42% margin), more than the group's total gross profit of $176 million. $354 million (59%) of that segment came from the VW joint venture (up from $308 million in Q2). Rivian now also licenses components of its AI voice assistant to the JV; revenue from the project is expected until around mid-2028.
- VW support: a $1 billion ten-year credit facility was fully drawn on 7 October; a further $460 million equity investment is planned by January 2028 at the latest, or when the first VW vehicle with Rivian hardware and software (the planned €20,000 ID. EVERY1) reaches the road, subject to conditions.
- Net loss attributable to shareholders narrowed to $913 million (from $1.173 billion); adjusted EBITDA improved from -$602 million to -$435 million.
- Outlook: 2026 deliveries now expected at 67,000–70,000 (previously 65,000–70,000); adjusted EBITDA guidance improved by $100 million to -$1.85 to -$1.75 billion; capex lowered by $300 million to $1.4–1.5 billion.
