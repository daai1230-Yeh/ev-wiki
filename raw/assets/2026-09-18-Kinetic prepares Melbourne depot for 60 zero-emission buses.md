# Kinetic prepares Melbourne depot for 60 'zero-emission' buses

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-09-17 16:00

---

Kinetic, Australasia's largest bus operator, has completed electrification upgrades at its Sunshine West depot in Melbourne. This will enable 66 buses to be charged at the site — up from 24 previously.

Where its own fleet is concerned: the public transport operator has referred broadly to supporting the deployment of 'zero-emission buses', which includes 20 hybrid buses currently in service on routes within Melbourne's bus networks. However, it seems fairly safe to conclude that Kinetic seeks to expand its fleet of electric buses. It states that its new upgrades "lift the number of zero-emission buses the site can charge from 24 to 66" with infrastructure capable of supporting up to 82 "as more buses arrive". The depot itself was one of the first in Victoria to charge electric buses.

Works included the installation of three overhead gantries featuring 38 charge points, with capacity for up to 54 later down the line; two fast chargers for 'high-capacity opportunity charging' plus a second electrical kiosk to increase power capacity on site; plus the introduction of automated charging technology and load management software to distribute overnight charging on-site. Kinetic states this is designed to "[keep] demand within the depot's power limits while ensuring every bus is ready for the morning". These changes were delivered in partnership with the Victorian government.

Matthew Campbell, Kinetic's managing director for Australia, said: "By the end of this year, Sunshine West will operate 60 zero-emission buses. For a depot of this size, serving a part of Melbourne that relies heavily on its bus network, that is a significant shift. We have built in headroom deliberately. The infrastructure here can support more zero-emission buses than we will have at the depot this year, so we are ready as more arrive."
