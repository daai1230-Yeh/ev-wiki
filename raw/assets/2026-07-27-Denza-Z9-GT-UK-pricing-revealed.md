# Denza Z9 GT UK pricing revealed

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 27.07.2026

---

BYD sub-brand Denza has confirmed UK pricing for its flagship Z9 GT. The plug-in hybrid Super DM version comes with a starting price tag of £95,000, while the fully-electric version of the premium EV starts at an eyewatering £105,000.

The announcement follows Denza's European debut in April, when BYD officially introduced its premium brand to the continent at an event in Paris, initially focusing on the Z9 GT before expanding with additional models such as the D9 plug-in hybrid MPV. Sales began through newly opened Denza stores in France, Germany, and the UK, with the all-electric version priced from €115,000 and €101,000 for the plug-in hybrid in Germany.

Now, order books are open in the UK, with first customer deliveries set to begin in Q4. The all-electric Z9 GT features a tri-motor all-wheel-drive powertrain producing 1210Nm of torque — enabling 0-62mph in 2.7 seconds and a top speed of 167mph, with a 122kWh battery promising 373 miles of WLTP range. The Super DM plug-in hybrid combines a 2-litre turbocharged petrol engine with three electric motors, quoting an electric-only WLTP range of up to 126 miles and a total driving range of up to 500 miles, accelerating 0-62mph in 3.6 seconds.

Denza has highlighted novel chassis technologies, including dual-chamber air suspension integrated with a Vehicle Motion Control system, and a rear axle steering system allowing manoeuvres such as crab walking up to 8.5 degrees, a 360° compass turn, and a turning radius of just 5.35m. The introduction of the Z9 GT also brings BYD's 'Flash Charging' system to the UK for the first time, with speeds up to 1500kW enabling recharge from 10 to 70 per cent in five minutes, or 10 to 97 per cent in nine minutes — adding up to 323 miles of WLTP range.
