# SEG Automotive joins Easelink's Matrix Charging initiative

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Easelink and SEG Automotive are moving the automated charging technology Matrix Charging towards series production, forming a partnership to drive its industrialisation and market introduction. Beyond initial OEM projects, SEG Automotive plans to integrate the technology into its own product portfolio under a technology licence, offering OEMs tailored solutions. SEG Automotive will also join the Matrix Charging Interest Group (MCIG).
