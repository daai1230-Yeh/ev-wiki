# Colombia plans 1,200km electric truck charging corridor

**Author**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-06-22

---

*Note: the live electrive.com page could not be reached during this clip (Chrome connection was interrupted); this summary is reconstructed from search-indexed excerpts of the article.*

Colombia plans to establish its first zero-emission freight transport corridor, called "Ruta-E," with charging infrastructure along the roughly 1,195–1,200km route connecting Bogotá, Barranquilla, and Cartagena. Charging stations for electric trucks will be installed along the route at intervals of no more than 100km.

The "Ruta-E: Clean Energy in Motion" project will be jointly implemented by Colombia's Ministry of Transport, the organisation CALSTART, and several companies from the logistics, energy, and automotive sectors, including DHL, BYD, Auteco Blue, TCC, and Voltrelli.

The project aims to accelerate the electrification of freight transport, stimulate investment in charging infrastructure, and increase the availability of electric trucks in Colombia, with more than 1,000 electric trucks expected to operate along the corridor by 2032. It is part of Colombia's broader strategy to decarbonise the transport sector and is intended to serve as a model for additional zero-emission freight corridors in the country.
