# EVgo to deploy Tesla Superchargers across the US

**Author**: Carla Westerheide | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

EVgo will install hundreds of Tesla V4 Superchargers at its own charging locations in the US through Tesla's Supercharger for Business programme. Construction starts this autumn, with first sites entering service in H2 2026. Individual sites will feature up to 20 stalls, mostly at retail and restaurant locations, branded "EVgo Superchargers" and folded into EVgo's existing network. EVgo will own the stations and set pricing, while the V4 hardware integrates into Tesla's ecosystem — sites will appear in Tesla navigation, letting Trip Planner auto-route drivers there.

This follows Tesla's move last year to open Supercharger technology to third-party operators who can purchase, brand and operate the hardware under their own networks.

The V4 Superchargers support up to 500 kW / 1,000 volts, compatible with both 400V and newer 800V EVs. They include Tesla's Magic Dock system for NACS and CCS vehicles to charge without an adapter, plus the longer charging cables from Tesla's latest hardware to accommodate varied port locations.

EVgo says network demand has grown more than 700% over the past three years. The company expects 35+ NACS-equipped vehicle models available in the US by end of 2026, alongside the 3+ million Tesla vehicles already sold there.

Badar Khan, EVgo CEO: "EVgo is making it easier for EV drivers to find convenient fast charging, and we're bringing more infrastructure options to communities across America to serve the growing demand for public charging. With this deployment, we look forward to welcoming more Tesla and NACS drivers to the EVgo network."
