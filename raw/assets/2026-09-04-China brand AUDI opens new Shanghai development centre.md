# China brand AUDI opens new Shanghai development centre

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

Audi and its Chinese partner SAIC have opened an innovation and technology center in Shanghai for their joint AUDI brand (uppercase, without rings). Initially, four new vehicle models are to be developed there, focused on driving dynamics and handling, AI-powered smart cockpits, and driver assistance systems.

So far there are only two AUDI-brand models: the battery-electric estate AUDI E5 Sportback, introduced in November 2024, and the battery-electric SUV AUDI E7X, presented in December 2025. In April this year, Audi and SAIC agreed to expand their cooperation with a new development centre and four new models on a new advanced platform.

The new AUDI Innovation & Technology Center (AITC) in Shanghai will employ up to around 300 people working on the next-generation Advanced Digitised Platform (ADP), which will underpin the four new models. "This is a historic milestone for the AUDI brand," said Fred Schulze, CEO of the Audi-SAIC cooperation and Chairman of the AITC Board. "The new AITC will become the innovation driver for our next generation of products."

SAIC Motor Vice President and Chief Engineer Zu Sijie said the cooperation aims to set new benchmarks for intelligent premium battery-electric vehicles. Audi China CEO Johannes Roscheck said the centre underscores a long-term commitment to China, "bringing together Audi's German engineering precision with local market innovation."

Before the four ADP 2.0-based models launch from 2028, a third model based on the first version of the ADP is set to debut in 2027. The ADP is considered an evolution of the electric platform used by SAIC's premium brand IM Motors.
