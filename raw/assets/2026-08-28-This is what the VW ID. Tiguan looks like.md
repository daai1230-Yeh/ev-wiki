# This is what the VW ID. Tiguan looks like

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 27.08.2026

---

The VW ID.4 is expected to be renamed the ID. Tiguan after its facelift. The new name is considered confirmed. And the exterior design has also been leaked ahead of the world premiere – even in moving images: a well-known auto YouTuber has sneaked a peek of VW's hope-bearer in a short video, partially wrapped but with clearly visible features.

The successor to the ID.4 will celebrate its world premiere in October, but interest in the revamped electric SUV is already immense. Spy shots have been circulating for months, and at the beginning of the year, IG Metall Emden confirmed the name change to ID. Tiguan — a long-anticipated move, widely expected following the introduction of the ID. Polo and the analogous switch from ID.3 to ID. Neo. Earlier this year, journalists — including the editorial team from electrive — were given a preview of the production model during Volkswagen's week-long press event in Hamburg, but an embargo prevented publication at the time.

## What does the VW ID. Tiguan look like in detail?

There has been much speculation that Volkswagen might align the ID.4's distinctive electric look more closely with the Tiguan's exterior, even though the ID. Tiguan is intended to remain recognisable as a standalone model. This now appears to be the case: YouTuber Thomas Majchrzak published a short video on his channel "Autogefühl" briefly showcasing the partially camouflaged successor from the front and rear. Visible through the foil, the front end becomes more angular and features a continuous light signature — both hallmarks of Volkswagen's current design language, as seen in recently launched vehicles for the Chinese market. The rear view also signals a departure from the existing ID.4 design, appearing less fragmented and featuring angular lights.

Volkswagen will fully unveil the ID.4's successor on 9 October, along with complete technical specifications. The electric SUV will switch to the updated MEB+ platform, receive a new entry-level motor, and the base variant's batteries are expected to adopt the more cost-effective LFP cell chemistry. The recessed door handles will also be replaced with traditional pull handles.

## Where will the VW ID. Tiguan be produced?

The revamped model will be built in Emden. IG Metall stated earlier this year that production of the ID. Tiguan in Emden is expected to continue until the end of 2031. The ID.4 was originally produced exclusively at VW's Zwickau plant in Saxony; later, an additional production line was established in Emden, initially running alongside the combustion models Passat and Arteon.

The Arteon did not receive a successor, and the Passat relocated to Bratislava with its generational change. That gap was filled by the electric ID.7, including its estate variant. There are rumours about this MEB model being renamed ID. Passat, but this has not been confirmed by VW or IG Metall.

The ID. Tiguan will be produced exclusively in Emden. ID.4 production in Zwickau is being phased out, while the Saxon plant will retain the Audi Q4 e-tron and likely also the Cupra Born and partially the VW ID.3. The ID.5 SUV coupé, built in Zwickau, will be discontinued and will not relocate.

According to Volkswagen, the shift to familiar model names with the ID. prefix is intended to add emotional appeal to the electric models and create a closer connection to the established model ranges — something the ID.3 and ID.4 never fully achieved. The first model of this new era was confirmed around IAA Mobility 2025, when the name ID. Polo was announced for the near-production study of the small electric car, originally planned as the ID.2. The accompanying SUV model was renamed from ID.2 X to ID. Cross, similarly referencing the combustion T-Cross.
