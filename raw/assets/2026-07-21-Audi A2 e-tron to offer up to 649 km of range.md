# Audi A2 e-tron to offer up to 649 km of range

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 21.07.2026

---

Audi is preparing the A2 e-tron as its new entry-level electric model for the compact segment. All four powertrain variants will be available to order immediately following the world premiere this autumn. Prices will start at €38,200. New spy shots now show the EV almost completely undisguised and reveal further details of its design.

Audi has announced the starting price of the upcoming A2: the entry-level version will cost €38,200. New spy shots also provide a clearer look at the final design.

Among the most striking details are the very small winglet-style door handles on both the front and rear doors. The elongated roofline and the rear window, split by a spoiler, also recall the original Audi A2. The new model also appears to do without a rear window wiper. Footage published on YouTube shows slim daytime running lights at the front, while the main headlights are positioned below them.

Audi first confirmed the A2 e-tron as its new entry-level electric model in March and unveiled a camouflaged prototype in May. Since then, the vehicle has been undergoing a range of validation tests. In Lapland, Audi evaluated the battery, thermal management and driving dynamics in low temperatures. Wind tunnel testing focused on aerodynamics, while further testing on public roads is intended to validate the chassis and driver assistance systems under everyday conditions.

Audi has now also released the first technical specifications. The A2 e-tron will be available with four power outputs of 125, 140, 170 and 240 kW. The lowest stated WLTP energy consumption is 13.0 kWh/100 kilometres, while the maximum WLTP range is 649 kilometres. That would make the A2 e-tron one of the more efficient electric cars in its segment. Audi has not yet officially confirmed battery capacities or DC charging performance. However, one statement on the company's German website is noteworthy: "All powertrain variants available from the world premiere."

Technically, the A2 e-tron is expected to be closely related to the new Volkswagen ID.3 Neo and Cupra Born. Much points to the model being based on the Volkswagen Group's updated MEB architecture. Audi has not yet specified the exact platform or the resulting technical details. Reports suggesting battery capacities ranging from 50 to 79 kWh and DC charging capacities of up to 183 kW therefore remain unconfirmed.

The entry-level version is expected to start at €38,200. According to the information released so far, the 125 kW model will come equipped with 18-inch alloy wheels, LED headlights, adaptive cruise control and a reversing camera. Heated seats and a heated steering wheel are set to become standard from the 140 kW version onwards.

The A2 e-tron is scheduled to make its world premiere this autumn, when the model will also become available for configuration. Production will take place at Audi's main plant in Ingolstadt. For Audi, the compact EV is strategically important because it expands the brand's electric portfolio well below the Q4 e-tron. With a starting price of €38,200 and a range of up to 649 kilometres, Audi is positioning the model not as a low-cost small car but as an electric entry point into the compact segment.
