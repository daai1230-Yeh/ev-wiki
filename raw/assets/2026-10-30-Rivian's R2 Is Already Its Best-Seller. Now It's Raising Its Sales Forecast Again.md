# Rivian's R2 Is Already Its Best-Seller. Now It's Raising Its Sales Forecast Again

**Author**: Suvrat Kothari | **Date**: 2026-10-29

---

The R2 is already Rivian's best-selling model just months after deliveries started, the company said in its third quarter earnings report Thursday. The midsize electric SUV already outsold the R1T, R1S, and its electric delivery van between July and September.

The company is now revising the baseline for its annual delivery target from 65,000-70,000 units to 67,000-70,000 units. After a record 19,751 deliveries in Q3, it had previously revised the baseline from 62,000 to 65,000 units.

"We had great results with R2 becoming our best selling vehicle by volume in the third quarter," CEO RJ Scaringe said. The new guidance indicates Rivian is gearing up for its best-ever quarter of deliveries: to hit the low end of its target, it would need to deliver at least 25,193 vehicles. Interim CFO Derek Mulvey said the majority of those deliveries will be R2s.

Q3 revenue grew 38% to $2.1 billion, thanks to increased deliveries, regulatory credits, and growth in software and services. Thanks to its partnership with the Volkswagen Group, software and services revenue grew 45% to $605 million. Rivian is still not profitable, reporting a net loss of $913 million for the quarter, an improvement from a $1.17 billion loss a year earlier.

Rivian started a second shift of R2 production and expects its run rate to increase over the coming quarters, helping it end the year with a positive automotive gross margin.

Rivian launched the R2 in June with a Performance Launch Edition trim costing a little under $60,000. A Premium model at $53,990 is supposed to arrive this year, with a $44,990 Standard version joining in 2027. The SUV will help fund future models like the R3 and R4, and the company's autonomy ambitions.

Rivian is working on a more advanced driver assistance system under its Autonomy+ offering to rival Tesla's FSD (Supervised), including point-to-point driving on city streets, with a limited rollout expected by the end of this year. Rivian also plans to deliver up to 50,000 robotaxis to Uber starting in 2028, and recently acquired UK-based synthetic data startup Third Dimension.
