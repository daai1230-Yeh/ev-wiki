# Euro Bus Expo 2026 overview: BYD unveils electric coach, Custom Denning enters UK market, Higer launches diesel city bus (and more)

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-11-09

---

Electric coaches moving closer to commercial deployment, new manufacturers targeting the British market and established players updating their vehicle portfolios: these were the main developments at Euro Bus Expo 2026, held at the NEC Birmingham from 3 to 5 November. The exhibition also marked the return of some major manufacturers that had been absent from the 2024 edition, including Volvo Buses, Wrightbus and Daimler Buses.

The exhibition brought together more than 80 buses, coaches and minibuses. Among the main announcements was the world premiere of the BYD C12 DUO battery-electric coach, alongside the UK presentation of the next-generation Scania Touring, including a battery-electric configuration, and the Scania Irizar i6S Efficient plug-in hybrid coach, winner of the Sustainable Bus Award 2027. Australian manufacturer Custom Denning presented its Element 2.2 battery-electric city bus, while Higer introduced the right-hand-drive Azure 9 diesel midibus. Zhongtong and CBC also brought new vehicles, including CBC's China-built Voyage E14 electric coach.

## The state of the market in the UK

According to SMMT, UK bus and coach registrations fell by 32.1% in the first nine months of 2026, to 5,162 units (7,605 in the same period of 2025). Zero-emission registrations declined by 16.3% to 1,606 units, although their market share increased from 25.2% to 31.1%, reflecting a sharper contraction in diesel and other fuels (-37.5%).

Wrightbus led with 922 registrations (+32.1%, 17.9% share), followed by Alexander Dennis (707, +0.4%), Mercedes-Benz (688, -35.7%), Ford (627, -64%) and Peugeot (543, -30.3%). Yutong registered 359 (-48.8%), Volvo 339 (-24%) and BYD 267 (+1.1%).

## The vehicles on display, manufacturer by manufacturer

**Alexander Dennis** – Next-generation Enviro200EV single-decker (9.9–11.7 m, 400 kWh battery, up to 43 seats / 77 total capacity), with an energy consumption figure of 0.594 kWh/km and a first order from a TfL operator. New AD24 fixed-fee parts, service and maintenance contracts (5, 7, 10 or 15 years) replace the pence-per-mile model.

**BYD** – World premiere of the C12 DUO battery-electric coach for the UK: two-axle, approx. 12 m, up to 57 seats, LFP Blade batteries of 429 or 495 kWh, range up to 340 miles (547 km), approx. 4 m³ luggage capacity. Also on display: B11 single-decker and BD11 double-decker.

**CBC** – New Voyage coach range: HD12 diesel (12.36 m, 51 passengers, Cummins X11 294 kW) and E14 battery-electric grand tourer (13.9 m, 59 seats, 714.7 kWh CATL LFP, 320 kW motor, CCS2 up to 300 kW). Manufactured with FTBCI in Xiamen, China; CBC handles UK specification, homologation and aftersales.

**Custom Denning** – First UK appearance with the Element 2.2 battery-electric city bus (approx. 10.9 and 12.1 m, stainless-steel monocoque, 411.51 kWh CATL LFP, Driventic drive 320 kW continuous / 390 kW peak, 300 kW CCS2, 571 km SORT 3 range, 0.72 kWh/km). Partnership with TSUK for distribution; demonstrators early 2027, deliveries from March–April 2027; new European HQ and service centre in Ilkeston, Derbyshire.

**Daimler Buses** – Return with the Mercedes-Benz Tourismo M/3 diesel coach; fourth-generation NMC4 batteries for the eCitaro (pack capacity 98→111 kWh, almost 13% higher energy density; up to 666 kWh solo / 777 kWh eCitaro G; up to 600 km range).

**Irizar** – 18.7 m articulated ie tram battery-electric bus for airport operations, plus two i6S Efficient coaches.

**Higer** – Via Harris Bus & Coach, RHD Azure 9 diesel midibus (8.99 m, Cummins B4.5, Voith); also V10 midicoach and V12 coach. Harris expanding towards 50 dealer locations in UK and Ireland.

**Neoplan** – Tourliner P10 diesel coach (approx. 13.1 m, 12.4-litre MAN diesel 353 kW, 53 seats), PSVAR-compliant.

**Scania** – Battery-electric Touring HD (approx. 14.85 m, three axles, 59 seats, 534 kWh, 330 kW motor, CCS2 up to 325 kW) and the Scania Irizar i6S Efficient plug-in hybrid (13-litre diesel 338 kW plus 230/290 kW e-machine, 89 kWh battery, up to 70 km electric range, DC charging up to 130 kW).

**Temsa** – HD coach (12.3 / 13.1 m, DAF MX-11) and MD9 midicoach (up to 39 seats). More than 600 Temsa vehicles operating in the UK; coach-segment share above 10%.

**VDL Bus Group** – RHD VDL Futura 3 (12.9 m, 57 seats, camera mirrors, ADAS; >20% fuel efficiency improvement) and Van Hool Astron (13.4 m, DAF MX-13).

**Volvo Buses** – First cooperation with Spanish bodybuilder UNVI: Volvo B13R – UNVI XL (12.8 m, D13K 500 hp diesel, HVO/B100-compatible, 53 seats).

**Wrightbus** – StreetDeck Electroliner 2.0 / Gen2 (442 kWh LFP, 250 kW, up to 84 passengers, TfL BVS 2.6) and a diesel double-decker converted to battery-electric by its NewPower division. Over 4,000 zero-emission buses delivered across the UK and Europe; considering additional UK factories and international expansion. First Bus ordered 43 more StreetDeck repowers – the first to use CATL batteries. The hydrogen fuel-cell coach project has slipped to a possible 2027 or 2028 introduction (target range up to 1,000 km).

**Yutong** – Via Pelican Bus & Coach: TC9 midicoach, GT12 and GT14 coaches, E12 single-decker and latest U11DD battery-electric double-decker.

**Zhongtong** – RHD H12 Plus diesel coach (approx. 12.25 m, DAF MX-11, 53 seats) and N11D City Bus double-decker.
