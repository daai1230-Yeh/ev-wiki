# BYD Fang Cheng Bao launches its largest SUV Tai 9 with foldable steering wheel on top trim

**Author:** Phate Zhang　｜　**Date:** 2026-11-19

- The Tai 9 debuts BYD's DMS hybrid platform and features a foldable, retractable steering wheel on its top trim.

- The Tai 9 starts at 279,900 yuan ($41,700), lower than the brand's hardcore off-road flagship Bao 8.

BYD's (HKEX: 1211) personalized sub-brand Fang Cheng Bao officially launched its flagship Tai 9 SUV on Thursday, entering China's premium large family SUV market with competitive pricing and new technologies.

The Tai 9 is available in 3 variants with an official starting price of 279,900 yuan ($41,700), while higher trims are priced at 299,900 yuan and 329,900 yuan. With trade-in subsidies, the starting price drops to 273,900 yuan.

This pricing positions the Tai 9 between the urban family-focused Tai 7 and the hardcore off-road flagship Bao 8, which currently start at 189,800 yuan and 419,800 yuan, respectively.

As Fang Cheng Bao's largest SUV, the 6-seat plug-in hybrid features a 2+2+2 seating layout and measures 5,270 mm in length with a 3,130 mm wheelbase, exceeding the 5,195 mm length of the Bao 8.

The Tai 9 is the first model built on BYD's new DMS all-around super hybrid platform, which integrates the fuel efficiency of DM-i, the performance of DM-p, and the off-road capability of DMO, along with rear-wheel drive vectoring control technology.

The platform gives the 5.3-meter-class full-size SUV a turning radius of only 3.5 meters, delivering compact-car agility in a flagship-sized body, Fang Cheng Bao said.

Combining energy efficiency, performance, and off-road capability, it creates a hybrid chassis solution suited for urban commuting, long-distance road trips, and outdoor off-roading, the brand said.

The foldable steering wheel on the top trim is a major highlight. Powered by a steer-by-wire digital chassis, it makes a global debut with a 180-degree foldable, retractable steering wheel that stows away within 10 seconds to free up resting space for the driver.

In driving mode, it retains a conventional D-shaped rim and physical buttons, balancing traditional driving feel with next-generation cabin design for advanced smart driving.

The powertrain pairs a 1.5T engine with dual front and rear electric motors delivering a combined 400 kW (536 hp) for all-wheel drive, enabling 0 to 100 km/h acceleration in 4.8 seconds and a WLTC pure electric range of 310 kilometers (193 miles).

Equipped with a 66.48 kWh second-generation Blade Battery supporting BYD's flash charging technology, the Tai 9 offers a CLTC pure electric range of up to 400 kilometers, a combined range of 1,630 kilometers on a full tank and full charge, and a depleted-battery fuel consumption of 6.4 liters per 100 kilometers.

All variants come standard with LiDAR and the God's Eye B 5.0 smart driving assistance system, alongside the DiSus-A intelligent body control system, rear-wheel steering, and leopard-turn functionality to balance urban commuting and light off-roading.

Pre-orders for the Tai 9 opened on November 9. Fang Cheng Bao previously said it would build up inventory in advance to shorten delivery wait times, while also planning to launch the Shark plug-in hybrid pickup in China between late November and early December.

Fang Cheng Bao sold 43,872 vehicles in October, up 41.29% year-on-year, bringing its cumulative sales for the first 10 months of the year to 329,308 units, up 124.99% year-on-year, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.
