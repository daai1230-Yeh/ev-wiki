# The Volvo EX60's Excellent Range Somehow Got Even Better

**Source**: InsideEVs | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Volvo had estimated the 2027 EX60 P10's range at 322 miles, but the official EPA rating came in even higher: the EX60 P10 AWD's window sticker will show 330 miles of range on a full charge — a 2.4% increase over the initial estimate. A higher-range EX60 P12 AWD variant will join the lineup later, with a whopping estimated 400 miles of range.
