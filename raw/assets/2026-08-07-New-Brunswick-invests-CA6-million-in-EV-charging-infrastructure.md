# New Brunswick invests CA$6 million in EV charging infrastructure

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

The Canadian province of New Brunswick will invest over CA$6 million across three projects to expand its public charging network and electrify the government's vehicle fleet, covering 16 new fast chargers including in provincial parks.

The bulk of funding, CA$5 million, goes to 16 new fast chargers within the province's eCharge network; the Energy Department will work with NB Power to install them in areas "underserved" by the private sector.

Energy Minister René Legacy: "Growing New Brunswick's network of electric vehicle chargers – especially fast-charging ports – is key to reducing range anxiety for electric vehicle drivers. NB Power does a great job of managing the network of public chargers, which is essential in cutting down reliance on fossil fuels, and reassuring for those who are considering making the switch to electric."

An additional CA$100,000 funds new charging stations at provincial parks République, Mactaquac and Herring Cove, aimed at improving visitor experience and supporting regenerative tourism.

CA$955,000 is allocated to replace ageing government fleet vehicles with battery-electric models and install supporting charging infrastructure, including three new regional charging hubs in Moncton, Saint John and Miramichi, intended to cut fleet emissions and long-term operating costs.

Transportation and Infrastructure Minister Chuck Chiasson: "New Brunswick remains committed to ensuring the vehicles in its government fleet are both modern and environmentally sustainable. This additional funding will help us continue this trend, by expanding upon our existing inventory of electric vehicles and increasing the fleet's charging infrastructure across the province."
