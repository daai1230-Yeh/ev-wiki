# Hyundai Creta Electric Prices Now Start At Rs 10.99 Lakh With BaaS

**Author**: Saptarshi Mondal | **Date**: 2026-07-02

---

Hyundai has introduced a Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) option for the Creta Electric to lower its upfront cost. Under the plan, prices start at Rs 10.99 lakh, with buyers charged Rs 3.90 per kilometre driven for the battery — effectively an EMI on the battery rather than a full purchase. The regular Creta Electric range is priced from Rs 18.03 lakh to Rs 24.70 lakh (ex-showroom), so BaaS cuts about Rs 7 lakh off the entry price. Hyundai hasn't yet given a variant-wise BaaS price breakup.

Hyundai is now the seventh carmaker to offer BaaS in India, following JSW MG Motor (the first, with the Windsor EV in 2024), Citroen, Kia, Maruti and Toyota; Mahindra and Vinfast remain the only major EV players without a BaaS option. Alongside the new pricing plan, Hyundai has added an integrated side step to the Creta Electric for easier ingress/egress and now offers a 7.4kW AC wallbox charger as standard on HC variants (previously 11kW only).

The Creta Electric comes with two battery options — 42kWh (420km claimed range) and 51.4kWh (510km) — powered by a single front-axle motor producing 135hp or 171hp depending on variant. It competes with the recently launched Tata Sierra EV, the Maruti e Vitara, the Mahindra BE 6, and the upcoming Toyota Urban Cruiser EV.
