# Vietnam: Hai Phong acquires 99 VinFast VF 8s for government fleet

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 21.07.2026

---

Hai Phong, a port city located about 120 kilometres southeast of Hanoi in Vietnam, has acquired 99 VinFast VF 8 electric SUVs for government use. The city recently delivered the entire fleet to local authorities for deployment.

According to the Hai Phong Department of Finance, the city procured the 99 VF 8s from the Au Lac–Thang Long Joint Venture through a public tender, for nearly 84 billion dong — around 5 billion dong, or 5.5 per cent, below the tender package's maximum approved value.

Hai Phong has allocated the VF 8 fleet to 98 of its 114 communes, wards and special zones. The initiative supports the city's goal of converting all buses and public vehicles (excluding specialised vehicles) to electric or other green-energy models by 2030.

The VF 8 is a mid-size SUV that caters mainly to overseas markets and is not among VinFast's best-selling models in Vietnam. Its sales volume is so low that the company typically does not disclose model-specific sales figures. Last year, of the 175,099 EVs VinFast delivered on the domestic market, the VF 8, VF 9 and Lac Hong models together accounted for just 13,962 units.

VinFast offers the VF 8 in Vietnam in FWD and AWD variants. The FWD variant uses a single motor producing 150 kW and 310 Nm of torque and accelerates from 0 to 100 kph in 11.8 seconds, with an 87.7 kWh battery pack delivering a range of 562 kilometres (NEDC). The AWD variant adds a rear motor, increasing output to 300 kW and torque to 620 Nm, reducing the 0-100 kph sprint to 5.58 seconds, with a WLTP range of 457 kilometres.

The VF 8 normally costs 898 million dong (approx. €29,800) in FWD guise and 1,079 million dong (approx. €35,900) in AWD. VinFast is currently selling it at discounted prices as it prepares to phase out the current generation, having launched the second-generation VF 8 in May, priced from 999 million dong (approx. €33,200), with deliveries beginning this month.

Sources: haiphong.gov.vn, vinfastauto.us
