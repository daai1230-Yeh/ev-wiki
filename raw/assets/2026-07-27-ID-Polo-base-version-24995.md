# ID. Polo base version now available for €24,995

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 27.07.2026

---

Initially, when order books for the ID. Polo opened in April following its world premiere, the most anticipated version of VW's new electric compact was missing. Instead of being available from €25,000 as promised, the price list started at €33,795 for the mid-range 'Life' trim.

This has now changed: Volkswagen has opened the configurator for a base version called 'Trend'. For the first time, this makes the electric Polo available for just €24,995. In addition to Germany, the new base version can also be ordered in other European countries, such as France and the Netherlands.

According to the manufacturer, the 'Trend' trim includes a range of driver assistance systems as standard, such as Side Assist and Lane Keep Assist (including Emergency Assist). Also included are features like LED headlights with high-beam assist, a digital cockpit with a 10-inch display, a 13-inch infotainment system, a multifunction leatherette steering wheel, and automatic climate control.

The ID. Polo Trend comes with a relatively small 37kWh LFP battery, which, combined with an 85kW electric motor, offers a WLTP range of 323km. While this may not be ideal for long distances, it is more than sufficient for numerous urban trips on a single charge. If a longer journey is required, the vehicle supports DC fast charging at up to 88kW, allowing the battery to charge from 10 to 80 per cent in just 23 minutes.

Volkswagen has recently begun offering this smaller battery in the two higher trim levels, 'Life' and 'Style', which can alternatively be equipped with a larger 52kWh NMC battery. However, the larger battery is not yet available for the more affordable 'Trend' variant.
