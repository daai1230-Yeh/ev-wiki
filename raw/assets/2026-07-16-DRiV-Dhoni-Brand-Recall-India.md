# DRiV banks on Dhoni to drive brand recall as it targets $500 mn India business by 2030

**Author**: Murali Gopalan | **Date**: Jul 16, 2026

---

DRiV, the dedicated aftermarket business group of Tenneco, has appointed former India cricket captain Mahendra Singh Dhoni as its national brand ambassador — the company's first-ever celebrity endorsement — as it targets a $500 million India business by 2030.

Despite strong recall for its legacy sub-brands, DRiV is turning to Dhoni to build awareness of its umbrella brand across more than 30 automotive aftermarket product lines spanning ride performance, braking, filtration, and engine components. The company frames the partnership as reflecting shared values of leadership, consistency, humility, and performing when it matters — traits DRiV says define both Dhoni and its brand portfolio.
