# Transport for Wales deploys 14 Wrightbus double-deck buses and orders six battery-electric vehicles

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 23 July 2026

---

Wrightbus has placed 14 new double-deck diesel buses into passenger service on the TrawsCymru network, while Transport for Wales (TfW) has confirmed a further order for six single-deck battery-electric buses for delivery in 2027. The announcement came as Wrightbus marked its 80th anniversary and the same week it launched business operations in Australia.

TrawsCymru is the Welsh Government-funded network of longer-distance bus services, connecting towns and rural communities across Wales — particularly corridors with limited or indirect rail links — and designed to integrate with the national rail network. Representatives from TfW recently visited Wrightbus headquarters in Ballymena, Northern Ireland, to begin configuration work on the forthcoming electric buses and tour the production facilities.

Wrightbus employs more than 2,300 people across Northern Ireland, Germany and Malaysia, with a global supply chain supporting a further 7,500 jobs. Founded in 1946, the company highlights its development of aluminium bus construction, low-floor bus technology, the New Routemaster for London, and the first hydrogen double-deck bus.

The new vehicles form part of TfW's fleet modernisation programme for TrawsCymru, intended to expand the network and support bus-rail integration within the wider T Network. TfW is also working with local authorities across Mid Wales in preparation for bus franchising.

Christian Reynolds, Managing Director of Wrightbus, said: "It's fantastic to see our buses carrying passengers across Wales, helping to improve connectivity for communities. We're equally delighted to be building on that success with a further order of six electric buses."

Lee Robinson, Executive Director for Regional Transport and Integration at TfW, said the new vehicles are "part of our investment to modernise the fleet across the network, helping to improve the customer experience and strengthen the T Network."
