# 201 electric buses in Northern Italy: AIR Pullman and STAV consortium opens €113.8 million tender

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-07

---

Consorzio Autoservizi Lombardi, the consortium of Italian bus operators AIR Pullman and STAV, has launched a €113.84 million tender for 201 battery-electric buses, split into three lots covering standard-length urban buses, Class II low-entry vehicles and articulated buses. According to Autobusweb, each lot is a 48-month framework agreement including full-service maintenance. Bids are due 5 November 2026, with contracts awarded on the most economically advantageous tender criterion.

## Lots

- **Lot 1:** 150 Class I urban low-floor buses, 11.31-12.4 m, three doors, €79,908,450.
- **Lot 2:** 21 Class II low-entry buses, 11.31-12.4 m, two doors, €11,258,583.
- **Lot 3:** 30 articulated low-floor buses up to 18.75 m, three doors, €22,673,490.

Combined value: €113,840,523 for 201 vehicles.
