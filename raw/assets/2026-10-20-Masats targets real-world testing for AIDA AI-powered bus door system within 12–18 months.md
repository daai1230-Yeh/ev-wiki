# Masats targets real-world testing for AIDA AI-powered bus door system within 12–18 months

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-19

---

Door manufacturer Masats is developing AIDA (Artificial Intelligence Driven Access), an AI-based bus door system that automates door opening through passenger recognition, and is targeting testing on real vehicles within the next 12–18 months. At FIAA 2026 in Madrid, Marc Ball-llosera Massa, Business Development and Product Strategy Manager – Bus & Coach, outlined the latest work: faster response times, fewer false detections, more compact processing hardware and better recognition of different access scenarios, including wheelchair ramps.

A main technical challenge is training the algorithms to tell relevant boarding situations from movements that should not trigger the doors, plus the processing power needed in a compact onboard setup. The next stage moves the technology from exhibition demonstrations to testing on actual vehicles under real operating conditions.
