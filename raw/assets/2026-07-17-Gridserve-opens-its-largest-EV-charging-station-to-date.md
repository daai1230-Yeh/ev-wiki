# Gridserve opens its largest EV charging station to date

**Author**: Ciaran Daly | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

Gridserve has opened its fifth "Electric Forecourt" in the UK, and what it calls its flagship EV charging destination. The site combines charging bays with a retail building, co-working, a solar array and more.

Resembling a miniature motorway service station, the new Markham Vale Electric Forecourt sits at Junction 29A of the M1, which Gridserve says is "at the heart of the East Midlands strategic road network." The facility features 39 rapid charging bays — 36 for passenger EVs and three for larger vehicles like electric HGVs, coaches, vans or tow vehicles — plus three accessible bays at the main entrance for drivers with disabilities.

Each bay features a 400 kW charging tower from ABB, hardware Gridserve says makes "Markham Vale one of the most powerful public charging locations in Europe," supported by a new grid connection. Each charger is said to be powered by "100 per cent net zero" energy, supplemented by 138 rooftop 455 W solar panels, with rainwater harvesting to reduce mains water use.

Beyond charging, the site incorporates a retail building with a Costa Coffee, a convenience store, lounge facilities, free Wi-Fi, and co-working and meeting spaces. The building is open 6am–9pm daily, while the charging bays operate 24 hours a day year-round. General Manager Ross Watson said the site "brings a world class facility and an entirely new scale of charging provision to the region." The facility brings Gridserve's UK network to more than 200 locations; the company says Zap Map has recognised it as the UK's "most used" public EV charging network.
