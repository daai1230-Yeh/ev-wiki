# BMW sees BEV deliveries rise in Q3 2026

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-10-13

---

*Condensed summary of the electrive report, not verbatim.*

BMW Group delivered 110,257 all-electric cars in Q3 2026, up 7.2% year on year, reversing H1's 7.4% decline. Year to date (Jan-Sept) BEV deliveries are 314,552 (-2.7%), plus 131,650 plug-in hybrids. BEV share was 20% in Q3 and 18.4% year to date.

- **Europe:** 81,941 BEVs in Q3 (+37.5%), 34.7% BEV share; Germany +38.5% (BMW, Mini and Rolls-Royce).
- **Mini:** 220,247 deliveries in nine months (+6.8%), 37.2% fully electric (roughly 82,000 BEVs).
- **Group total:** 551,072 vehicles in Q3 (-6.3%); 1,707,799 year to date (-4.9%). China is the drag: -24.8% in Q3, -21.8% in nine months; Europe and the US grew.
- **Neue Klasse:** the iX3 has over 100,000 European orders in a year; the iX4 was just unveiled, the electric i3 sedan has launched, and the Chinese iX3 arrives in November. Sales board member Jochen Goller cited strong order intake for electric models.

Comparison: Volkswagen Group BEV share in Q3 was about one in eight (Europe +19.6%, worldwide -4.7%); Mercedes-Benz 16.8% (68,400 BEVs, +61%).
