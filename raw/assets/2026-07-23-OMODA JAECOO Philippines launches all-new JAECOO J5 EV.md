# JAECOO J5 EV brings back-to-back sales victory to the Philippines on July 22

**Date**: 21.07.2026 | **Source**: BMPlus (BusinessMirror)

---

OMODA & JAECOO Philippines officially launched the all-new JAECOO J5 EV on July 22, 2026, at the Glorietta Palm Drive Activity Center in Makati City, bringing to the Philippine market an electric SUV that has already proven itself in the region.

The JAECOO J5 EV closed the first half of 2026 as the No. 1 bestselling pure EV in both Thailand and Indonesia — back-to-back market leadership in two of Southeast Asia's most competitive automotive markets. It now arrives in the Philippines not as a contender but as a proven performer, delivering 461 km of NEDC range.

Ahead of its official Philippine launch, the JAECOO J5 EV demonstrated its real-world performance by travelling 539.2 kilometres on a single charge — equivalent to 117% of its declared 461-kilometre NEDC driving range — during an endurance challenge observed by the Automobile Association Philippines (AAP).

Designed for modern lifestyles, the JAECOO J5 EV blends premium styling, intelligent technology, advanced safety systems and all-electric performance. The retail price is around P1,399,000, inclusive of a 7-year/200,000-kilometre bumper-to-bumper warranty and an 8-year/160,000-kilometre battery and electric motor warranty.

The July 22 launch also showcased OMODA & JAECOO's vision for intelligent mobility, reinforcing the brand's continued investment in the Philippine market through its growing dealership network, customer support ecosystem and expanding portfolio of new energy vehicles. The brand is also advancing innovation beyond mobility through initiatives such as AiMOGA, its intelligent robotics ecosystem, while strengthening its global presence through partnerships including the FIFA World Cup 2026.
