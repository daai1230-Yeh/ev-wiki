# One Of Our Favorite Hyundai EVs Is Suddenly Cheaper Than Ever

**Source**: InsideEVs Daily Newsletter (July 16, 2026) | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

Hyundai's grin-inducing electric crossover — the 641-horsepower Ioniq 5 N — is now a whopping $6,300 cheaper than before, and it also has a native NACS port. For the 2026 model year, Hyundai slashed the starting price of its go-fast EV by $6,300, bringing the final figure to $61,500, making one of the best performance EVs on the market more attainable than ever.

Beyond the price cut, the 2026 Ioniq 5 N gains a native Tesla-style NACS charge port. The updated N Drift Optimizer mode now offers ten stages to choose from (previously only one was available), and the car now includes a dual-amp Level 1/Level 2 combination charging cable, plus CCS-to-NACS adapters for both Level 2 (AC) and DC fast charging.
