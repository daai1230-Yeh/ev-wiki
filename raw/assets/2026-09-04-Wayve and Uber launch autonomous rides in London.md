# Wayve and Uber launch autonomous rides in London

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

Wayve and Uber have launched their autonomous ride service in London as planned, though a safety driver remains onboard for the time being.

Londoners can now book autonomous vehicles from Wayve via the Uber app for UberX, Uber Electric, and Uber Comfort at no additional cost, across the entire London urban area excluding airports. The vehicles are battery-electric Ford Mustang Mach-E models equipped with the Wayve AI Driver plus cameras and environmental sensors. Passengers can accept an offered Wayve ride in advance or switch to a conventional Uber.

Only a small number of vehicles are on the road at launch, with fleet size undisclosed. The service is not yet fully driverless — a trained, Transport for London-licensed driver remains on board to monitor and intervene if necessary, so the partners call these "supervised autonomous rides."

The launch follows Private Hire Vehicle licences issued in August for several Wayve vehicles, satisfying part of the regulatory requirements, with further approvals for fully driverless operation still pending. Interest has grown quickly: more than 100,000 Londoners had registered for autonomous rides via Uber by August, and over 140,000 users have now activated the option in the app.

London is the first market in a broader Wayve-Uber collaboration that aims to deploy Wayve's technology in twelve markets. A Tokyo service using the Nissan Leaf and Wayve's AI driving system based on Nvidia Drive Hyperion is set to launch later this year. The companies have not disclosed when London might transition to rides without a safety driver.
