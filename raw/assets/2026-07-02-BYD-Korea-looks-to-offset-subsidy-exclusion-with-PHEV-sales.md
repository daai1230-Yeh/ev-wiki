# BYD Korea looks to offset subsidy exclusion with PHEV sales

**Author**: Lee Min-hyung | **Published**: July 2, 2026 4:27 PM KST

---

Chinese EV maker BYD Korea is redirecting its business strategy toward plug-in hybrid electric vehicles after losing government subsidy eligibility for its all-electric models. The company, which recently became South Korea's fourth-largest imported automaker, faced subsidy exclusion starting Wednesday due to "weak profile in supply chain contribution" under revised evaluation criteria.

The government's new EV subsidy assessment covers five categories: technological capability, supply chain contribution, environmental policy response, after-sales sustainability, and safety management. BYD Korea was the only brand to lose existing subsidy eligibility in this evaluation round, having only entered Korea's passenger vehicle market in January 2025.

To counteract this disadvantage, BYD Korea is aggressively promoting the Sealion 6 PHEV, priced at an unusually competitive 37.5 million won for a plug-in hybrid. This pricing disrupts Korea's PHEV market, where competitors like Toyota's RAV4 PHEV exceed 60 million won, positioning BYD's offering similarly to standard hybrid SUVs from Kia and Hyundai.

Industry analysts suggest that if the Sealion 6 gains positive consumer reception for its electric range and fuel efficiency, BYD could achieve substantial sales growth comparable to its previous EV success. The company projects PHEV sales will outpace its electric vehicle lineup by approximately three-to-one, capitalizing on growing domestic demand for hybrid alternatives amid global EV market slowdown.
