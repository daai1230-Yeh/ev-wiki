# Hyundai Motor India integrates access to 30,000 charging points through app

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Hyundai Motor India has integrated access to over 30,000 public EV charging points into its myHyundai app, creating one of India's largest integrated charging networks on a single platform, open to EV owners of all brands. The app covers charging facilities within an average 25km radius across India, including major highways such as Delhi-Gurgaon-Jaipur, Mumbai-Pune-Bengaluru and Chennai-Vellore-Bengaluru, and lets users navigate to stations, book slots and pay digitally. Hyundai itself operates 183 DC fast-charging stations across 105 cities, with plans to expand to 600 by 2030. Since January 2023, more than 300,000 EV users have used Hyundai's charging infrastructure via the app, delivering over 5 million kWh of electricity.
