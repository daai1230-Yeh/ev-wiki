# Illinois opens new EV rebate round from 1 August

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-22

---

Illinois will reopen its EV rebate programme on 1 August, running through 31 December 2026, with $14 million in funding offering up to $4,000 toward eligible new and used battery-electric cars, prioritising lower-income households. The prior application cycle (28 October 2025–31 May 2026) drew 3,499 applications, of which the Illinois EPA approved 3,001 rebates, including 1,658 awarded to low-income applicants.
