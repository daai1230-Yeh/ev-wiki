# GZM hands over 73 electric buses and 45 charging stations

**Date**: 25 July 2026

---

Poland's Upper Silesian-Zagłębie Metropolis (GZM) has completed delivery of 73 battery-electric buses and 45 chargers, supplied by Solaris and Irizar, entering service with four public transport operators as part of a PLN 305 million (~€70.5 million) fleet renewal programme, largely financed through Poland's National Recovery and Resilience Plan (KPO).

The fleet comprises 42 electric buses from Solaris (ordered last October) and 31 Irizar ie buses — 16 articulated 18-metre vehicles and 15 standard 12-metre buses. The vehicles will enter service in Sosnowiec, Świerklaniec, Katowice and Gliwice. GZM has also expanded charging infrastructure with 45 chargers, most installed at operators' depots, with one charging point deployed at the Kościuszki Street bus terminus in Będzin.
