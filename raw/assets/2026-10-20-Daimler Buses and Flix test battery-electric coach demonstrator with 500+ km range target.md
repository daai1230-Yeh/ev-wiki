# Daimler Buses and Flix test battery-electric coach demonstrator with 500+ km range target

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-15

---

Daimler Buses and its research partners carried out a joint test drive of the ELCH (Electrified Coach) battery-electric coach technology demonstrator in Neu-Ulm, Germany, as part of a publicly funded project targeting a range of over 500 km. The drive took place in early October during the final in-person consortium meeting, according to a Daimler Truck press release. The project, funded by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy, runs until the end of 2026.

ELCH was launched in 2022 to develop a modular electric drivetrain and two demonstration vehicles for real-world testing. It supports Daimler Buses' plan to offer battery-electric coaches under the Mercedes-Benz and Setra brands by 2030. In September 2025, Setra also began testing its H2 Coach demonstrator, a 13.9 m S 517 HD with a cellcentric fuel cell system of up to 300 kW and a stated range of at least 800 km.

Daimler Truck has not disclosed the demonstrator's battery capacity, charging power, consumption or measured range, nor a production timetable or launch date for a derived coach. The project covers drivetrain, aerodynamics, weight, operating strategies and economics.

**Consortium**: coordinated by Daimler Buses, with KIT (ITIV: vehicle data analysis and operating strategy), RPTU (iMAD and SAM: lightweight construction and aerodynamics), the University of Mannheim (MISES: economic viability under different operating profiles) and Flix SE (operational data from long-distance services and application scenarios).

**In brief**
- ELCH is a publicly funded research project coordinated by Daimler Buses to develop and validate a battery-electric coach concept.
- The demonstrator targets more than 500 km range.
- It ends at the end of 2026, while Daimler Buses aims to offer battery-electric coaches by the end of the decade.
