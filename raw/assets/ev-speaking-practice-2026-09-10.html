2. Read Aloud 6 min

China's car market is telling two very different stories at once. In August, retail sales of passenger vehicles inside China fell 23.6 percent year-on-year to just 1.541 million units — yet in the same month, passenger vehicle exports surged 77.5 percent to reach 894,000 units, a new record pace. A country whose home market is shrinking is simultaneously breaking its own export records.
中國車市同時上演兩個截然不同的故事。8月，中國境內乘用車零售銷量同比下降23.6%，僅154.1萬輛；然而同一個月，乘用車出口卻激增77.5%，達到89.4萬輛，創下新的出口速度紀錄。一個內需正在萎縮的國家，同時也在刷新自己的出口紀錄。
The domestic slowdown is not a one-month blip. Cumulative retail sales for the first eight months of 2026 are down 20.8 percent year-on-year, at 11.716 million units. The pain is concentrated in traditional gasoline cars: retail sales of conventional fuel vehicles plunged 40 percent year-on-year in August, to roughly 540,000 units, while wholesale shipments of those cars fell 29 percent, to 840,000 units.
這波內需放緩並非單月的異常波動。2026年前8個月累計零售銷量同比下降20.8%，僅1,171.6萬輛。壓力集中在傳統燃油車上：8月常規燃油車零售銷量同比暴跌40%，約54萬輛；批發出貨量則下降29%，為84萬輛。
Even electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles, usually the bright spot, felt the chill: new-energy vehicle retail sales dropped 10.1 percent year-on-year in August, to 1.005 million units, though that was up 5.7 percent from July. Still, because gasoline cars fell even faster, new-energy vehicles' share of domestic retail hit a record high of 65.2 percent — nearly two out of every three cars sold in China last month were electrified.
就連通常表現亮眼的電動車與插電式混合動力車也感受到寒意：8月新能源乘用車零售銷量同比下降10.1%，為100.5萬輛，不過較7月成長了5.7%。儘管如此，由於燃油車跌幅更大，新能源車在境內零售中的佔比反而創下65.2%的歷史新高——換句話說，上個月中國賣出的車子，將近三分之二都是電動化車款。
Dealers are absorbing much of the pressure. China's dealer inventory warning index — where anything above 50 percent signals an unhealthy buildup of unsold stock — climbed to 62.3 percent in August, up 5.3 percentage points year-on-year. That's the third straight month of increases: 57.2 percent in June, 61.1 percent in July, and now 62.3 percent in August.
經銷商正承受大部分的壓力。中國汽車經銷商庫存預警指數——超過50%即代表庫存壓力偏高、不健康——8月攀升至62.3%，同比上升5.3個百分點。這已是連續第三個月上升：6月為57.2%，7月為61.1%，8月來到62.3%。
Exports have become the release valve. BYD and Geely both set new monthly export records in August, and the secretary-general of China's passenger car association now expects full-year exports to reach 12 million vehicles in 2026, climbing further to somewhere between 18 and 20 million vehicles a year by 2030.
出口正成為紓解壓力的出口閥。比亞迪與吉利雙雙在8月創下單月出口新紀錄，中國乘用車市場信息聯席分會秘書長預估，2026全年出口量將達到1,200萬輛，到2030年更將進一步攀升至每年1,800萬至2,000萬輛之間。
Not every brand is riding the wave equally. Xiaomi, the newest entrant to China's EV race, has already signed a distribution deal with German dealers ahead of its planned 2027 European launch. But Seres, which builds the Huawei-backed AITO brand, was a late mover overseas — and its total sales plunged 44 percent last month, a reminder that timing matters in global expansion.
不是每個品牌都同步搭上這波浪潮。中國電動車市場的最新進入者小米，已在計畫2027年進軍歐洲前，與德國經銷商簽署配銷協議。但由華為協力打造AITO品牌的賽力斯，在海外布局進場較晚——上個月總銷量暴跌44%，提醒著全球擴張中時機的重要性。
The export boom has also caught regulators' attention. Last week, Chinese authorities issued new guidance for automakers' overseas operations, warning against frequent or steep price cuts and other practices that could damage consumers or brand reputation abroad. BYD, Chery, and Geely have all pledged to comply — though the guidance does not yet spell out penalties for violations.
這波出口熱潮也引起監管機關的關注。上週，中國監管機構針對車廠的海外業務發布新指引，警告避免頻繁或大幅降價等可能損害消費者權益或品牌聲譽的行為。比亞迪、奇瑞與吉利均已承諾遵守，儘管該指引目前尚未明訂違規處罰方式。

5. Useful Expressions 3 min

"X fell / plunged / dropped by ___ percent year-on-year."
用來描述數據「同比下降」，plunged 語氣比 fell 更強烈，適合形容急遽下滑。
"X surged / soared to ___, a new record."
用來描述數據「激增至」某個新高點，surge/soar 都帶有「快速上升」的語感。
"While X is happening, Y is happening at the same time." (contrast)
對比句型，用來凸顯兩個同時發生但方向相反的趨勢，例如「內需萎縮」對比「出口暴增」。
"Analysts / officials expect X to reach ___ by ___."
用於引述預測或展望，例如「官員預期到2030年出口量將達到1,800萬至2,000萬輛」。
"There are growing concerns that ___."
表達「日益增長的憂慮」，適合用來討論政策風險或潛在後果，例如貿易反彈。

6. Discussion 5 min

  1. Why might a country's auto exports surge even as its domestic sales collapse? Do you think this pattern is sustainable in the long run?
    為什麼一個國家的汽車出口會在內需崩跌的同時激增？你認為這種模式長期能持續嗎？
  2. Do you think Chinese regulators can realistically prevent domestic price wars from spilling over into overseas markets?
    你認為中國監管機關真的能有效阻止國內的價格戰蔓延到海外市場嗎？
  3. What risks do trading partners face when a country floods global markets with competitively priced, fast-growing EV exports?
    當一個國家以極具競爭力的價格大量外銷快速成長的電動車，貿易夥伴國會面臨哪些風險？
  4. Seres' overseas sales dropped 44 percent partly because it entered international markets late. How important is timing when a company expands globally?
    賽力斯海外銷量暴跌44%，部分原因是進軍國際市場的時機較晚。你認為企業全球擴張時，時機有多重要？
  5. Is a 65 percent EV penetration rate, like China's domestic market now has, realistic for other countries to reach? What would need to change?
    像中國內需市場目前65%的電動車滲透率，你認為其他國家有可能達到嗎？需要哪些條件改變？
  6. If you were a policymaker in Europe or North America, how would you respond to a 77.5 percent surge in Chinese auto exports?
    如果你是歐洲或北美的政策制定者，面對中國汽車出口激增77.5%，你會如何回應？

7. Wrap-Up 2 min

In 60 seconds, summarize today's story in your own words: what happened to China's domestic car sales in August, what happened to its exports, and why regulators are now paying closer attention.
請用60秒，用自己的話總結今天的內容：8月中國境內車市發生了什麼事、出口發生了什麼事，以及監管機關為何開始更加關注。
"In my opinion, ___ because ___."
用這個句型表達你自己的立場，例如對「出口能否真正解決產能過剩問題」發表個人看法。
