# INIT acquires 67.74% of Norwegian Applied Autonomy and its xFlow platform for autonomous bus fleet management and remote supervision

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-14

---

INIT has acquired a 67.74% stake in Norwegian company Applied Autonomy, a specialist in software for the management and remote supervision of autonomous vehicles in public transport, airports and logistics. The deal closed on 1 October 2026 and was announced on 8 October. It brings Applied Autonomy's xFlow platform, already deployed in autonomous bus operations in Norway and the Netherlands, into INIT's portfolio.

Founded in 2017 and based in Kongsberg, Applied Autonomy develops manufacturer-independent software to integrate, monitor and manage autonomous vehicles. xFlow offers real-time monitoring, remote operator functions and technical supervision of driverless vehicles, and supplies operational data to other transport management systems. Combined with INIT's MOBILE-ITCS control system, it can manage mixed fleets of conventional and driverless vehicles: xFlow handles technical supervision while MOBILE-ITCS handles fleet management and control-centre operations.

**Deployments**: in April 2026 the Norwegian Public Roads Administration authorised autonomous operation without an onboard safety driver in Stavanger, using a Karsan Autonomous e-ATAK with ADASTEC driving technology and xFlow for fleet management and remote supervision (operators Vy and Kolumbus). In Rotterdam, two xFlow-equipped autonomous buses have run since 2025, including the world's first autonomous airport bus; staff supervise and intervene remotely through xFlow.

**BRAVE10k**: launched in July 2026 with about 20 partners, this three-year programme, funded by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE) and supervised by TÜV Rheinland, addresses standardised procurement, approval and operation of autonomous public transport vehicles, aiming at more than 10,000 such vehicles in Germany by 2030.

Applied Autonomy CEO Olav Madland said that partnering with INIT will let the company scale quickly and significantly accelerate its growth.
