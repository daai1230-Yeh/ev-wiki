# Polestar ends production of the Polestar 2 in China

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 2026-10-28

---

Production of the Polestar 2 in China is coming to an end after more than six years, where over 200,000 units of the electric fastback were built in Luqiao. A completely new successor is set to launch in early 2027. For Polestar, the model changeover coincides with an already challenging transitional phase.

The last units of the Polestar 2 have reportedly already rolled off the production line in Luqiao, China. While the company has yet to officially announce the end of production, it is already directing customers in several European markets towards available stock vehicles rather than accepting new configurable orders.

The Polestar 2 has been manufactured in Luqiao since March 2020. According to media reports, just over 200,000 vehicles were produced there. Polestar last reported more than 190,000 units sold. The model was particularly important for Norway, where more than 16,000 Polestar 2 cars were sold. The roots of the Polestar 2 trace back to the Volvo era: in 2016, Volvo unveiled the 40.1 and 40.2 concept studies. While the 40.1 later evolved into the XC40, the 40.2 eventually became the Polestar 2. The model received its most significant technical update in 2024, when Polestar switched key variants from front- to rear-wheel drive and comprehensively revised the powertrain.

A completely new successor is expected to arrive in early 2027. Polestar has announced that the next generation will not be a mere facelift but a fundamentally new vehicle. Norwegian broadcaster TV 2 Broom reports it will be a new crossover, but Polestar has not yet officially confirmed the body style, platform, or production location. In markets such as Norway and Sweden, new configurations are no longer being accepted.

In the first half of 2026, global sales rose only slightly by 0.4 per cent to 30,423 vehicles, while revenue fell by around four per cent to 1.36 billion US dollars. At the same time, Polestar significantly reduced its operating loss. For the full year, the company lowered its growth forecast from a low double-digit to a low to mid-single-digit percentage range, citing the phasing out of the Polestar 2's lifecycle and changes in its US business.

From model year 2027, Polestar will no longer be allowed to sell new vehicles in the US due to the Connected Vehicle Rule. Around 80 per cent of sales now come from Europe. Polestar started 2026 strongly with 13,126 vehicles sold in the first quarter, its best Q1 to date, but the Polestar 2 model change and US restructuring slowed growth.

The Polestar 4 SUV launched in September 2026 is already being produced in Busan, South Korea. First deliveries are scheduled to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026, while the configurator indicates a delivery window of January to February 2027 for the German market.

The Polestar 2 was the foundation of the brand and, for years, its most important volume model. The successor must now not only impress technically but also fill a gap while Polestar expands its portfolio and shifts its focus more strongly towards Europe.
