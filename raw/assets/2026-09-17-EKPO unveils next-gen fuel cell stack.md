# EKPO unveils next-gen fuel cell stack

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-09-16 16:00

---

EKPO Fuel Cell Technologies will showcase the next stage of development of its NM20 fuel cell stack at IAA Transportation 2026. The joint venture between ElringKlinger and OPmobility aims to advance the industrialisation of hydrogen-based powertrains for heavy-duty applications.

Two years after the premiere of the NM20 fuel cell stack at IAA Transportation 2024, EKPO Fuel Cell Technologies is back at the trade fair in Hanover once more with a showcase of the next stage of its high-performance fuel cell technology. The focus of this development is on the scalability of the platform, optimised manufacturing and system architectures, as well as simplified integration into various heavy-duty applications.

The new NM20 generation builds on insights from ongoing technology projects and a closer integration of development and production. EKPO has further developed the stack, module, interfaces and manufacturing processes as a fully integrated system. From the bipolar plate to the housing, the architecture has been revised, standardised and aligned with industrial production processes. This is intended to improve automatability, quality assurance and traceability.

## EKPO reduces dimensions and weight

The new generation stands out primarily due to its more compact dimensions and reduced weight: the module volume has been reduced by around 20 per cent, while the weight of the fully assembled module has decreased by 39 kilograms to 178 kilograms. Additionally, the weight of the Cell Row Assembly (CRA) – the active block made of stacked individual cells – has been reduced by around 25 per cent.

To simplify integration into vehicle and system architectures, functions such as media distribution, water separation, media monitoring, sensor technology, Cell Voltage Monitoring (CVM), high-voltage connection and interfaces for additional system components have been directly incorporated into the stack architecture. Additionally, the company relies on a modular kit principle with standardised, reusable components for different power levels.

The NM20 family covers a broad power spectrum and includes a single-stack module with 323 cells and over 200 kW, a max variant with over 300 kW, and a twin-stack module with 646 cells and over 400 kW. The twin-stack module achieves a maximum continuous current of 1,000 amperes and operating pressures of up to 3.0 bara, with a designed product lifespan of 25,000 operating hours and efficiency levels between 56 and 69 per cent.

## €177 million funding from IPCEI programme

In addition to its use in heavy-duty transport on road, rail and water, the platform qualifies for stationary energy supply and backup power systems due to its operating temperatures of up to 95°C. The development and commercialisation of the NM20 stack are supported within the framework of the European IPCEI (Important Project of Common European Interest) funding programme with up to €177 million, as reported. Recently, EKPO found a partner in the Swiss company Akkodis, a specialist in digital engineering, for a demonstration truck featuring the technology, which is now being developed.
