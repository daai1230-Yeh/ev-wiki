# More Indian EVs head to distant shores as Maruti, Tata, M&M drive 14-fold export surge

**Source**: ETAuto | **Date**: 27 July 2026

---

Electric car exports from India reached 15,641 units in the April–June 2026 quarter, up from 1,122 units a year earlier — a 14-fold surge, according to industry data. Domestic players Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra and Tata are capitalising on the trend, expanding capacity and strengthening their product portfolios.

Maruti Suzuki plans annual production of around 70,000 electric vehicles, the bulk earmarked for export, with ambitions to become India's top EV producer this year and to export to Japan and Europe; the company shipped 2,900 units of the Suzuki eVitara from Pipavav Port in Gujarat to 12 European countries, including the UK, Germany, France, Norway, Denmark, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Sweden, Hungary, Iceland, Austria and Belgium. The company is also planning to make India a manufacturing hub capable of exporting EVs to 100 countries.

Tata Motors, which holds more than 80% of India's electric car market, competes domestically with China's MG Motor and homegrown Mahindra & Mahindra, and is testing export markets for its EVs.
