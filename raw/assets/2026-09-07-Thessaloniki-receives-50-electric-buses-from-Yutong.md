# Thessaloniki receives 50 electric buses from Yutong

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 05.09.2026

---

Thessaloniki's transit authority OASTH has put 50 new 18-meter Yutong battery-electric articulated buses (likely the Yutong U18, 528–704 kWh depending on battery option, ~300 km range, up to 130 passengers) into service on routes X1, 12, 27, and 32, bringing OASTH's electric bus fleet to around 160. OASTH hired 170 additional drivers alongside the winter-timetable rollout and plans new routes and higher frequency. This follows a 2024 joint Yutong procurement by Thessaloniki and Athens.
