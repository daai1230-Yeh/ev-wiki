# Can India democratise ADAS? Tata Motors & Mobileye on future of automotive safety

**Date**: 22.07.2026

---

During the 7th ETAuto Tech Summit, Sven Puska, CTO of Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles, and Elie Luskin, VP India & China at Mobileye, discussed whether advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) can be democratised for the Indian market, and what it will take to make hands-free driving broadly accessible.

The panel highlighted cost optimisation as the key to democratising ADAS in India — panelists noted that effective ADAS can be delivered with a single camera and a cost-effective chip, lowering the barrier for adoption in a price-sensitive market. The conversation also emphasised the importance of human behaviour and driver acceptance in making ADAS technology genuinely effective on Indian roads, where traffic conditions differ significantly from markets where these systems were originally developed.

Panelists framed regulation as a turning point for the industry, suggesting that 2026 is largely about execution while 2027 is expected to bring wider deployment as rules evolve. Mobileye's ADAS solutions have already been adopted by manufacturers including Mahindra & Mahindra and Tata Motors, and the company has separately forecast a 28 per cent increase in hands-free driving adoption in India's passenger vehicle market by 2027.
