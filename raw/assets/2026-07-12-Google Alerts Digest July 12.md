# Google Alerts Digest — July 12, 2026

**Source**: Google Alerts daily summary emails (7:22am and 9:05am)

## 電動巴士 (Electric Buses)

Taoyuan Airport's T3 automated shuttle bus contract drew scrutiny after reports it was subcontracted to a Korean manufacturer; the airport operator says the contractor holds Ministry of Transportation-certified electric bus demonstration qualifications and the arrangement complies with tender rules. Separately, Taiwan's Foton Buscar (華德動能) is targeting a 32% market share in Japan's electric bus market, leaning on a deepening partnership with Sumitomo Corporation. Commentary also covered how smart/autonomous features are becoming central to electric bus competitiveness for 2026, as commercial e-buses — with fixed routes and controlled environments — become an early battleground for higher-level autonomous driving, driving demand for lidar and high-performance computing chips.

## 電動車 + 韓國 (EV + Korea)

## 電動車 + 印度 (EV + India)

India's GST cut on lithium batteries (28% → 18%) plus reduced import tariffs is aimed at accelerating EV adoption by lowering component costs. Separately, Delhi is drafting an SOP for recycling EV and other e-waste batteries as India's electronic waste volumes grow alongside EV adoption.

## 電動車 + 中國大陸 (EV + China)

China's power load hit a record high (1.518 billion kW on July 10), driven partly by data-center and EV charging/swapping demand. Separately, "cockpit-style" three-wheeled/enclosed mopeds are selling well in the US but banned in China; and Zotye Auto (眾泰汽車), a legacy Chinese automaker, was put under investigation for disclosure violations after seven straight years of losses and a debt ratio near 99%.

## 電動車 + 供應鏈 (EV + Supply Chain)

Commentary linked domestic Taiwan EV supply-chain maturity to Yulon's valuation. Separately, Benchmark Mineral Intelligence data showed global EV registrations up 7% YoY in June (fourth straight month of growth), with Europe up 31%; Taiwan LED makers (Lite-On/Everlight-related suppliers) reported strong Q2 revenue on rising China EV customer demand and supply-chain "de-China" effects.

## 電動車 + 美國 (EV + US)

A Deloitte survey found nearly 70% of Americans still prefer gasoline vehicles despite the EV trend. Separately, Stellantis's Topolino brand launched a two-seat EV in the US starting at $13,995 (~NT$450,000) via select dealers; and Subaru's next-generation Solterra EV (338 hp AWD version) is generating buzz ahead of its expected autumn US launch.

## 電動車 + 墨西哥 (EV + Mexico)

No EV-specific coverage; alert results were dominated by unrelated geopolitical commentary (Iran/US/Mexico).

## 電動車 + 歐洲 (EV + Europe)

Global EV registrations rose 7% YoY in June, with Europe up 31% (Benchmark Mineral Intelligence). Commentary questioned whether Tesla's reliance on a single core model (Model Y) is sustainable for its European market share as competitors close the completeness/update-speed gap. Separately, Europe's auto industry faces structural pressure from EV-transition missteps, Chinese competition, and Trump-era tariffs, with commentary warning VW and other automakers may need to close production lines, hitting Germany and Italy hardest.

## 特斯拉 (Tesla)

From the 7:22am alert: Tesla reportedly dismantled its Model S/X production line at Fremont in 46 days to fully retool for Optimus humanoid robot production. From the 9:05am alert (Tesla-focused): Tesla (TSLA) shares rose to $413.13 (intraday +~5.00%), with markets focused on Robotaxi service expansion to Miami, Florida, following Austin, Texas. Cadillac issued a full US recall of the Vistiq over third-row powered seats that could trap occupants. Separately, commentary discussed Musk/xAI's Grok being integrated with Tesla FSD for voice control, and speculation on what a Q4 earnings beat would signal about Tesla's cost control, product mix, and energy business versus a full revenue recovery.
