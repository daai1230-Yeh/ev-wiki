# From vehicle procurement to system architecture: IMC Trolleybus Conference heads to Lyon on 11–13 November

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-10-16

---

The 9th International IMC Trolleybus & eBRT2030 Conference takes place in Lyon, France, on 11–13 November at the Novotel Lyon Bron Eurexpo, organised by trolley:motion and UITP. It focuses on integrated electric bus systems, infrastructure planning, energy management and the economic assessment of electrification strategies, in particular how to define system boundaries when comparing depot charging, opportunity charging and In-Motion Charging (IMC). Case studies cover Prague, Pescara, Verona, Nancy, St. Gallen, Arnhem, Gdynia, Shanghai, Winterthur and Medellín. Technical sessions run 12–13 November, ending 13:30 on the 13th, followed by a 14:00–16:00 visit to Lyon's IMC trolleybus system with Keolis.

**Day 1 (12 Nov)**: conditions for implementing IMC trolleybuses and other electric bus configurations. Speakers include Kevin Plimbley (TransLink, Vancouver trolleybus transformation), Magalie Campmas (SYTRAL Mobilités, Lyon's e-bus corridors), Arnd Stephan (TU Dresden), Kay Axhausen (ETH Zürich), and Alex Naef (Carrosserie Hess) with Patrik Hatzig (Métropole Grand Nancy) on IMC corridor design.

**Corridor projects**
- Prague (Jan Barchanek, DPP): a high-demand airport corridor with bi-articulated trolleybuses, about 55–60% of the route electrified overhead; eBRT2030 examines charging power, peak loads, energy use, battery life and CAPEX/OPEX.
- Pescara (13 Nov, Maxmilian Di Pasquale, TUA Abruzzo): the La Verde corridor between Pescara and Montesilvano with IMC.
- Verona: planning of a new e-bus corridor, illustrating differing useful lives of vehicles, batteries, power electronics and infrastructure.

**eBRT2030**: also covers Athens (hybrid charging combining trolleybus overhead infrastructure, depot charging and opportunity charging; digital twins) and Rimini (MetroMare corridor: predictive maintenance, battery life, TCO, active safety, passenger counting).

**Energy and technology**: Fawzi Fattel (Kummler+Matter, St. Gallen), Jan Röhl (Kruch Railway Innovations, Gdynia simulation), Ibrahim Diab (Innoveho, smart grid in Arnhem), Dirk Uwe Sauer (RWTH Aachen, batteries), Thoralf Knote and Hermann von Kleist (Fraunhofer IVI, infrastructure costs and smart charging), Philipp Sinhuber (ebusplan, planning mistakes).

**Day 2 (13 Nov)**: implementation, governance and financing, with Shanghai (Edward Chen, Sunwin), Winterthur (Bruno Schwager) and Medellín (Andres Emiro Diez Restrepo, UPB); financing and procurement from Tali Trigg (EBRD), Christoph Schaafkamp (KCW), Gabriele Grea (Redmint), Peter Brandl (Bracon) and Maria Natália Paulino Araújo Alcântara (Steer); closing with a white paper session led by Richard Kayser (VCDB).
