# EU: 270,000 electric cars registered in June

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Electric car registrations increased significantly across the EU in June: 61% more EVs hit the road compared to the same month last year. According to ACEA, 270,557 new battery-electric passenger cars were registered in the EU in June, up 60.7% from June 2025. That follows 203,417 new electric cars registered in May, showing the pace of registrations accelerating. The EU's overall passenger car market grew 13.6% year-on-year in June, meaning EV growth significantly outpaced the broader automotive market.
