# electrive COMMERCIAL Digest — July 9, 2026

**Source**: electrive COMMERCIAL newsletter (heavy electric commercial vehicles)

---

**Editorial note**: The Netherlands has introduced a new distance-based road toll for trucks over 3.5 tonnes. Electric trucks are also subject to the toll, though at a significantly reduced rate versus older diesel vehicles. As of 2025, the Netherlands was the only European country to match Switzerland in share of new electric truck registrations — almost one in five new trucks registered there was electric.

**Mars and Rewe deploy 47 battery-electric trucks on pilot corridor**: Food giant Mars and grocery retailer Rewe have announced their first continuous test corridor where goods are transported electrically, covering routes from Mars' food factories through storage to Rewe's consumer markets.

**FirstGroup brand to operate ZEV shuttle service at Sizewell C**: Specialist Passenger Solutions (SPS) has landed a contract to operate shuttle services at the Sizewell C nuclear plant construction site in Suffolk, with a fleet including both hydrogen and electric buses.

**Iveco delivers 47 electric midi-buses to Sardinia**: CTM Cagliari has taken delivery of 47 electric Iveco E-Way midi-buses, funded through Italy's National Recovery and Resilience Plan.

**BasiGo expands Rwandan e-bus fleet to 52 vehicles**: BasiGo has added 18 electric intercity buses in Rwanda, increasing its deployed fleet to 52 vehicles.

**BRAVE10k targets 10,000 autonomous transit vehicles**: A German research consortium aims to prepare for the integration of more than 10,000 autonomous public transport vehicles by 2030.

**Wren Kitchens begins electrification of heavy goods fleet**: UK retailer Wren Kitchens has started electrifying its heavy goods fleet with 10 Mercedes-Benz eActros 600 electric trucks, aiming for an all-electric HGV fleet.

**Netherlands introduces lorry toll — with battery-electric trucks liable**: The Netherlands has introduced a distance-based toll for trucks weighing 3.5 tonnes or more; battery-electric trucks also pay, at a reduced rate.

**WattEV opens seventh electric truck charging depot**: Fleet electrification firm WattEV opened its seventh heavy-duty truck charging depot, in Fresno, California — the first of four planned sites in Northern California.

**Woolworths orders 148 electric trucks**: Australian retailer Woolworths is expanding its electric fleet via a new agreement with Zenobe, covering 148 battery-electric trucks in what's described as Australia's largest commercial e-truck rollout to date.

**Colombia plans electric truck charging corridor**: Colombia's "Ruta-E" plan aims to create the country's first zero-emission freight corridor, building charging infrastructure along the 1,195km route between Bogotá and Cartagena.
