# Chinese electric SC01 sportscar to be built in Italy from May 2027

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-11-21

---

The company FIPA plans to establish a new production site for EVs in the Marken region of eastern Italy. From the end of May 2027, a Chinese electric sports car - the SC01 - will be assembled there. By 2030, the company aims to employ 285 people and later expand production to include light electric vehicles.

FIPA (Fabbrica Italiana Produzione Autoveicoli) will assemble electric vehicles for the European market in Italy starting in 2027. To this end, the company has signed a contract for an existing industrial complex in the Marche region, located between the Apennines and the Adriatic Sea. The first model to be produced at the new site will be the SC01 electric sports car, developed by Chinese startup Tianjin Gongjiangpai Auto Technology. The Chinese version of the model features a 320-kW all-wheel-drive system and 560 Nm of torque. Weighing just under 1.4 tonnes, it is said to accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in 2.9 seconds. The performance specifications for the European version are not yet known. The first SC01 assembled in Italy is scheduled to be handed over at the end of May 2027 during the official opening of the plant.

The exact location of the plant is to be announced in the coming weeks. In the first expansion phase, 2,000 square metres of production space will be dedicated to the assembly of the SC01. This will be supplemented by a 2,750-square-metre warehouse and a 20,000-square-metre logistics area. Within twelve months of the start of production, an additional 11,000 square metres of production space will be added – with FIPA aiming to then expand the production area to a total of 24,000 square metres within two years.

## SC01 as the first model

The SC01 is an electric sports car jointly developed in China and Italy. According to the company, the technical development originates from China, while the European version is being further developed and adapted for the European market in Italy. The prototypes are currently undergoing testing on Italian roads. FIPA describes itself as the sole authorised importer and manufacturer of the SC01 for the European Union and is also responsible for European homologation.

Production is scheduled to begin in spring 2027. From January to March, the existing buildings will first be renovated. Between April and May, the production lines will be installed, and European homologation will be completed. By the end of May, production is expected to start, and the first SC01 assembled in Italy will be handed over. FIPA will announce specific delivery dates for customers and the vehicle allocation process at a later date.

## 285 jobs planned by 2030

In the first production phase for the SC01, around 50 employees will be hired. However, FIPA has longer-term plans for a significantly larger site: by 31 December 2030, a total of 285 jobs are to be created in production and indirect company areas. The company intends to recruit and train workers primarily from the Marche region and neighbouring areas.

Behind FIPA is entrepreneur Wenyu Sun, who also founded the Italian company ITB AUTO. Over the past three years, ITB AUTO has established a sales and service network with more than 40 dealers for Desner-branded light electric vehicles, which are adapted Chinese models, such as those from Wuling. This is a similar model to Ari Motors in Germany.

With the new plant, FIPA now aims to take further steps towards its own vehicle development and production. In addition to assembling the SC01, the company also plans to develop and homologate its own vehicle models. Following the launch with the sports car, production lines for light EVs are also to be established at the plant.
