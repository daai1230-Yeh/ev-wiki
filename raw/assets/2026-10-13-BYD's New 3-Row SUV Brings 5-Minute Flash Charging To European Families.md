# BYD's New 3-Row SUV Brings 5-Minute Flash Charging To European Families

**Author**: Nico DeMattia | **Date**: 2026-10-12

---

The BYD Atto 8 can recharge from 10-70% in 5 minutes, and hit 97% in under 10.

The BYD Atto 8 SUV debuted at the 2026 Paris Motor Show on Monday. It packs the 115 kWh second-generation Blade battery with around 405 miles of WLTP range for a seven-seat SUV. In reasonable ambient temperatures it can charge from 10-70% in five minutes; four more minutes reaches 97%. In extreme cold (as low as -22°F) it takes 12 minutes. This beats any non-Chinese EV on the European market.

It is the first BYD-branded EV in Europe with Flash charging tech (the Denza Z9GT got the tech earlier). BYD wants to install 3,000 of its T-shaped Flash chargers across Europe by the end of 2027.

The base rear-wheel-drive version has the 405 miles of range; the dual-motor Excellence AWD has around 374 miles, 764 horsepower, and reaches 62 mph in 4.9 seconds. The interior is nicely done if a bit generic, with ample space and luxury for families.
