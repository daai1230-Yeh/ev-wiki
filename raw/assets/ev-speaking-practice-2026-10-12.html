1. Warm-Up 暖身3 min

Do you think electric cars, or hybrids, make more sense for most families right now? Why?

你覺得現在對大多數家庭來說，電動車還是油電混合車比較合理？為什麼？

When a government puts a tax on imported cars, who really pays for it in the end?

當政府對進口車課稅時，最後真正買單的是誰？

Have you ever seen a Chinese car brand on the road, in Taiwan or abroad? What was your impression?

你在台灣或國外的路上看過中國品牌的汽車嗎？你的印象如何？

2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文6 min

Chinese carmakers have found a new way into Europe, and it is not fully electric. It is hybrids.

中國車廠找到了打進歐洲的新路，而且不是純電動車，而是混合動力車。

In October 2024, the European Union added duties of 7.8 to 35.3 percent on Chinese-made electric cars, on top of a 10 percent base tariff. Plug-in hybrids were not part of that investigation, so they only faced the regular 10 percent.

2024 年 10 月，歐盟對中國製純電動車加徵 7.8% 至 35.3% 的反補貼稅，另加 10% 基礎關稅。插電式混合車不在調查範圍內，因此只適用一般的 10% 關稅。

Carmakers followed the gap. Hybrid imports from China rose from about 3,800 vehicles in October 2024 to roughly 50,400 in July 2026.

車廠順著這個縫隙調整策略。中國混動車進口量從 2024 年 10 月約 3,800 輛，增加到 2026 年 7 月約 5 萬 400 輛。

In the first four months of this year, the value of hybrid imports reached 3.18 billion euros. That is the first time it has beaten pure electric cars, at 2.81 billion euros.

今年前四個月，混動車進口金額達 31.8 億歐元，首度超過純電車的 28.1 億歐元。

Now Brussels may respond. According to Bloomberg, Europe's trade chief has told officials that Chinese hybrid exports could fall by millions of vehicles over four years, about half of what was expected. A safeguard with a tariff quota could start as early as December.

現在布魯塞爾可能出手。根據彭博報導，歐盟貿易執委已告知官員，未來四年中國混動車出口可能減少數百萬輛，約為原先預期的一半。附有關稅配額的保障措施最快 12 月實施。

Chinese plug-in hybrids hold about 29 percent of the Western European plug-in market today. If the EU pushes that down to around 15 percent, the volume is almost cut in half.

目前中國插混車在西歐插混市場約占 29%。若歐盟將其壓低到 15% 左右，銷量幾乎會減半。

Another debate is about Beijing's own policy. China gives exporters a 13 percent VAT rebate, worth about 16.8 billion yuan to BYD in the first half of 2026. That is more than BYD's net profit of 12.3 billion yuan. Some analysts say that ending the rebate would calm the trade tension and also cool the price war at home.

另一個爭論是北京自身的政策。中國給予出口商 13% 的增值稅退稅，2026 上半年對比亞迪價值約 168 億人民幣，超過其 123 億人民幣的淨利。部分分析師認為，取消退稅既能緩和貿易緊張，也能為國內價格戰降溫。

6. Discussion 討論題5 min

1. Hybrids slipped through a gap in the EU's tariffs. Is it fair for Europe to close that gap now, or should the rules have been clearer from the start?

1. 混動車鑽過了歐盟關稅的縫隙。現在歐洲把縫隙補上公平嗎？還是規則一開始就該更清楚？

2. Supporters of the quota say it protects European jobs. Critics say it makes cars more expensive for consumers. Which argument is stronger to you?

2. 支持配額的人說這能保護歐洲就業；批評者說這會讓消費者買車更貴。你覺得哪個論點比較有力？

3. Chinese brands went from almost unknown to outselling Japanese and Korean brands in Europe in five years. What do you think explains that speed?

3. 中國品牌五年內從幾乎無名，到在歐洲銷量超越日韓品牌。你覺得這速度怎麼來的？

4. Would ending China's export rebate be a fair compromise, or would it just hurt Chinese workers and carmakers? Argue one side, then the other.

4. 取消中國出口退稅是公平的妥協，還是只會傷害中國的工人與車廠？先替一方辯護，再換另一方。

5. If you were a Chinese carmaker, would you build factories in Europe or look for other markets, such as Southeast Asia? What are the trade-offs?

5. 如果你是中國車廠，你會在歐洲設廠，還是轉向東南亞等其他市場？取捨是什麼？

6. Does trade protection like this slow down the global shift to electric vehicles, or does it help local industries catch up? Explain your view.

6. 這類貿易保護會拖慢全球電動化，還是幫助本地產業追上？說明你的看法。

7. Wrap-Up 總結2 min

Give a 60-second summary: what Europe is considering, why Chinese hybrids grew so fast, and what the two sides are arguing about.

請用 60 秒摘要：歐洲正在考慮什麼、中國混動車為何成長這麼快，以及雙方在爭論什麼。

Finish with one sentence of your own: "Personally, I think… because…, although I understand why others see it differently."
