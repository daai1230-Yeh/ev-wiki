# 'The electric truck is no longer a technology problem' – Professor Markus Lienkamp on charging electric trucks

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

In an interview with electrive, TUM Professor Markus Lienkamp argues the electric truck itself is no longer the biggest hurdle to heavy-duty electrification — the real challenge lies in grid connections, depot charging and intelligent energy management. He says the technology risk is largely resolved, with manufacturers now building battery-electric heavy-duty trucks as second- and third-generation series-production vehicles with proven drivetrains and batteries. One of the costliest mistakes, he notes, is sizing charging infrastructure purely on peak-load calculations: simulations show uncontrolled fast charging at a depot can create peak loads of several megawatts, whereas intelligent charging management with lower charging power can achieve the same fleet operation with only a fraction of the required grid connection capacity. He also discusses where megawatt charging fits in and why battery swapping is unlikely to prevail in Europe.
