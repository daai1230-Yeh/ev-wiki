# APM Terminals orders 96 electric yard trucks for New Jersey port

**Date**: 26.08.2026

---

APM Terminals is electrifying more of the equipment at its container terminal in Elizabeth, New Jersey, and has ordered 96 electric yard trucks from Orange EV as well as 34 electric gantry cranes from Konecranes.

APM Terminals Elizabeth has ordered 96 Orange EV HUSK-e XP electric terminal trucks. Orange EV builds its Class 8 electric terminal tractors in Kansas City and supplies the vehicles together with charging and servicing solutions.

Elizabeth is one of the largest container terminals in the Port of New York and New Jersey and handles around 28 per cent of the port's container throughput, with more than 600 vessels calling at the terminal each year and around 800,000 truck visits annually.

APM Terminals is procuring the electric equipment as part of a wider upgrade of the site, which also includes infrastructure and operational changes, as well as training for employees who will operate and maintain the new equipment.
