# Archer and BETA plan 250 charging sites for electric aircraft

**Author**: Jasmin Apitzsch-Krause | **Date**: 21.07.2026

---

Archer Aviation, BETA Technologies, and Macquarie Capital plan to establish a US-wide charging network for electric aircraft. By 2030, the new ACES consortium aims to equip more than 250 locations with interoperable CCS charging infrastructure.

The planned network will cover airports and vertiports in major US metropolitan areas, including sites in California, Texas, Florida, and New York. The initial focus is on regions where Archer and BETA intend to operate battery-electric aircraft. BETA Technologies will supply the charging hardware, which is based on the Combined Charging Standard (CCS), supported by the General Aviation Manufacturers Association (GAMA).

The concept envisages using the same charging infrastructure for diverse applications. Archer is planning passenger flights with its electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft, while BETA and its customers could use the charging points for cargo and medical transport. At larger airports, CCS-compatible chargers may also power electric ground vehicles.

Macquarie Capital will provide financial and strategic advisory services within the consortium and support activities such as securing capital for site acquisition, development, and construction. Additional infrastructure operators, aircraft manufacturers, and investors will have the opportunity to join the network.

The announcement comes as the US seeks to accelerate the practical deployment of electric aircraft. In March, the FAA selected eight projects as part of its eVTOL Integration Pilot Program (eIPP), spanning 26 states and including urban air taxis, regional passenger services, cargo, emergency, and offshore flights. Archer and BETA are among the participating manufacturers.

Whether the announced 250+ sites will be realised by 2030 depends not only on the development and financing of the infrastructure but also on how quickly the certification and commercial deployment of eVTOLs and other electric aircraft progress.
