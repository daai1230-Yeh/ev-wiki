# USA: Hyundai cuts Ioniq 5 N price for 2026 model year

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-22

---

Hyundai has cut the US starting price of the 2026 Ioniq 5 N by $6,300, to $59,900 before a $1,600 destination charge. The updated model swaps its CCS charging inlet for a North American Charging Standard (NACS) port, giving direct access to Tesla's Supercharger network, and includes CCS-to-NACS adapters for AC and DC charging plus a dual-voltage Level 1/Level 2 portable charging cable as standard. Hyundai says the price cut is part of a broader strategy to make its N performance brand more accessible; the update also brings a revised drift mode.
