1. Warm-Up 暖身 3 min

Would you buy a very small, cheap electric car for city driving? Why or why not?

你會買一輛很小、很便宜的電動車來在城市裡開嗎？為什麼？

In your country, do people prefer local car brands, or are they open to foreign ones?

在你的國家，大家偏好本土品牌，還是願意接受外國品牌？

What matters most to you when you choose a car: price, range, brand, or reliability?

挑選汽車時，你最在意什麼：價格、續航力、品牌，還是可靠度？

2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Japan has long been a graveyard for foreign carmakers. General Motors, Ford and Hyundai all gave up on the market after failing to win over loyal local buyers.

日本長期以來是外國車廠的墳場。通用、福特和現代汽車都因為無法打動忠誠的本地消費者而退出市場。

Now BYD wants to break that curse. This summer it unveiled the Racco, a small electric kei car designed for Japan's narrow streets, and it collected more than a thousand orders within weeks.

現在比亞迪想打破這個魔咒。今年夏天它發表了專為日本窄路設計的小型電動輕型車 Racco，短短幾週就收到超過一千張訂單。

The target is smart. In 2025, Japan sold more than 1.6 million kei cars and trucks, over a third of all new vehicles, and Honda, Suzuki and Daihatsu together hold about 80 percent of that segment.

這個目標很聰明。2025 年日本賣出超過 160 萬輛輕型轎車與卡車，占新車總銷量三分之一以上，而本田、鈴木和大發合計掌握該級距約八成市占。

But the road is rough. After a ¥150,000 subsidy, the Racco costs about two million yen, or roughly 12,600 dollars, and Tokyo has reportedly raised subsidies for Japanese, European and American models while cutting them for BYD.

但前路崎嶇。扣除 15 萬日圓補助後，Racco 約 200 萬日圓，也就是大約 12,600 美元；據報日本政府提高了日、歐、美系車款的補助，卻砍了比亞迪的補助。

Then Suzuki struck back. Its e SKY goes on sale on November 16 at 2.12 million yen, even cheaper than the Racco, with a range of 310 kilometers, and it uses an LFP battery supplied by BYD itself.

接著鈴木反擊。e SKY 將於 11 月 16 日開賣，定價 212 萬日圓，比 Racco 還便宜，續航 310 公里，而且使用的是比亞迪自己供應的磷酸鐵鋰電池。

The stakes are high because only 3.9 percent of new cars in Japan were electric in August, compared with 29 percent in Europe and 43 percent in China. If a car can finally match the price of a gasoline model, that could change the market.

賭注很大，因為 8 月日本新車只有 3.9% 是純電車，歐洲是 29%，中國則高達 43%。如果有一款車終於能和燃油車價格相當，市場可能就此改變。

It all comes down to whether ______.

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

Why do you think foreign carmakers have struggled in Japan for so long? Is it culture, taste, or policy?

你覺得外國車廠為什麼長期在日本吃力？是文化、品味，還是政策？

Some say Japan's subsidy changes protect local industry; others say they are unfair trade barriers. Which side do you find more convincing?

有人說日本調整補助是在保護本土產業；也有人說這是不公平的貿易障礙。你覺得哪一方更有說服力？

Suzuki beat BYD on price using BYD's own LFP battery. What does that tell us about the global EV supply chain?

鈴木用比亞迪自己的 LFP 電池，在價格上擊敗了比亞迪。這告訴我們全球電動車供應鏈什麼事？

Is 1.6 million yen, roughly ten thousand dollars, a realistic psychological price barrier? What would make you switch to an EV?

大約一萬美元的價格，真的是消費者心理門檻嗎？什麼條件會讓你改買電動車？

Japan's EV share is only 3.9 percent. Would hybrids be a better path for Japan than going fully electric?

日本純電車占比只有 3.9%。對日本來說，走油電混合會不會比全面電動化更好？

Do you think a small kei-style EV could work in your own country's cities? What would need to change?

你覺得輕型電動小車在你自己國家的城市行得通嗎？需要改變什麼？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 2 min

Give a sixty-second summary: What is BYD trying to do in Japan, what stands in its way, and what did Suzuki's e SKY change?

用 60 秒做摘要：比亞迪在日本想做什麼？有哪些阻礙？鈴木 e SKY 又改變了什麼？

Finish with one sentence of your own opinion: "Personally, I think ______, because ______."

最後用一句話說出你的立場：「就我個人而言，我認為＿＿＿，因為＿＿＿。」
