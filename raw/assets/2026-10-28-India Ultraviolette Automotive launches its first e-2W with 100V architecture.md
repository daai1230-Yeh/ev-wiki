# India: Ultraviolette Automotive launches its first e-2W with 100V architecture

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-10-27

---

Indian electric two-wheeler maker Ultraviolette Automotive has introduced a 100-volt electrical architecture called 'VX-100' and launched the X-47 Venom crossover motorcycle as its first application.

VX-100 comprises an in-house 'SRB-11' battery pack, a 'Light Fury' motor and a new motor controller. The SRB-11 (11 kWh) is the largest pack in an Indian two-wheeler; versus the SRB10 it has 25% more energy density, cools twice as fast, is 15% lighter and charges twice as fast, supporting DC charging up to 12 kW. The aviation-derived Light Fury motor is 25% lighter and twice as power-dense, using soft magnetic composites and a trapezoidal coil topology, and spins up to 9,000 rpm. The silicon-carbide controller weighs 2.5 kg – 40% lighter, with three times the power density.

The X-47 Venom comes in standard, plus, Recon and Recon+ variants. Standard and plus use a new 7.5 kWh pack (227 km IDC range); Recon and Recon+ use the 11 kWh pack (343 km IDC). Plus variants include a 1.6 kW on-board charger. A 30 kW motor gives 0–100 km/h in 5.9 s – the quickest Indian-made motorcycle – and 155 km/h top speed. Features include cruise control, an IP67 7-inch touchscreen with navigation, a 35 W USB-C port, rear-camera parking assist, front and rear dashcams, and a 77 GHz rear radar providing blind spot monitoring, lane change assist, overtake alert and rear collision warning.

Base price is 300,000 rupees (~€2,800); the first 5,000 customers get a 35,000-rupee discount (265,000, ~€2,500). Ultraviolette has 55 showrooms in India, growing to 100 by Q1 2027.

In Europe, where it is present in 20 countries, the X-47 Venom launches at EICMA on 3 November, with deliveries in early 2027, said co-founder and CTO Niraj Rajmohan. So far only the F77 is sold there, with about 500–700 delivered since July–August 2025.

The Bengaluru factory (30,000 units, expandable to 50,000–60,000) builds the F77 and X-47. A new 250,000-unit 'BIGGA' factory in Hosur, Tamil Nadu (expandable to 500,000) will build the Tesseract premium scooter and Shockwave enduro motorcycle, both with VX-100; Europe is planned for these too. The F99 – a fully faired 400 V bike with 90 kW, 200 Nm, 180 kg, 0–100 km/h in 3 s and ~265 km/h – will be announced after the Tesseract launch in Q1 2027; production specs may vary.
