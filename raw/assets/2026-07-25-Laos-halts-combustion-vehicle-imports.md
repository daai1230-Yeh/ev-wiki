# Laos halts combustion vehicle imports until end of 2026

**Date**: 25 July 2026

---

Laos has largely suspended imports of gasoline and diesel vehicles until the end of 2026, with the import of new petrol and diesel vehicles prohibited since 1 June 2026. The government hopes the time-limited suspension will accelerate the ramp-up of electromobility, cut fossil fuel reliance, and expand charging infrastructure.

Exemptions remain for vehicles used in passenger transport, machinery, trucks for specific production and development projects, and special utility and emergency vehicles. Laos is combining tax advantages for battery-electric vehicles with the temporary import restriction, pricing guidelines, and planned charging infrastructure expansion. Authorities are also exploring support measures to mitigate financial losses for affected vehicle importers during the transition.
