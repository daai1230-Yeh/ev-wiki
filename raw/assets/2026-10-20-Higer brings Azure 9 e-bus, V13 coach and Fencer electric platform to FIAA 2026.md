# Higer brings Azure 9 e-bus, V13 coach and Fencer electric platform to FIAA 2026

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-09-23

*(Sponsored content by Higer)*

---

Higer Bus is showing four products at FIAA 2026 (IFEMA Madrid, opened 22 September): the V13 coach, the 9-metre Azure 9 battery-electric city bus, the Fencer f6 Integral EV bus and the Fencer f1 Integral EV chassis. At the opening press conference, V Series Chief Engineer Jeff Lee and FENCER Chief Engineer Wave Xiao presented the ranges, and REMO, Higer's electric mobility partner in Spain, outlined cooperation on operational analysis, custom vehicle development, charging infrastructure and after-sales. More than 100 European media and industry customers attended. Higer says its buses are sold in more than 30 European countries and regions, with new-energy models above 80% of European sales, and that the Azure 9 already runs in Italy, Luxembourg, Belgium and Iceland.

**V13 coach**: 13.15 m long, 3.85 m high, more than 10 m³ of luggage space; 10.25-inch digital cluster and touchscreen with voice control; full-LED lighting; laterally movable aisle-side seats; headrests, footrests and entertainment screens on every seat; optional wheelchair lift at the front wheel area; operating noise 2 dB below the EU limit; lightweight design to cut fuel use; multiple powertrain options.

**Azure 9**: 9 m fully flat low-floor battery-electric city bus for feeder, urban and shuttle routes, with a 268.7 kWh battery (supplier unnamed), independent front suspension, ECAS air suspension with lifting and kneeling, and a full-vehicle CAN bus and T-BOX for cloud monitoring.

**FENCER** (Higer's high-end zero-emission sub-brand, built on a self-developed integrated chassis platform): the f6 is an intercity bus with battery pre-heating and pre-cooling during charging, an integrated high-voltage system to lower maintenance costs, and ADAS. The f1 chassis integrates chassis, powertrain and ADAS, supports fast charging up to 360 kW and uses Higer's 5/6-in-1 high-voltage components; it is designed for different urban bus applications and is displayed separately to show its architecture.
