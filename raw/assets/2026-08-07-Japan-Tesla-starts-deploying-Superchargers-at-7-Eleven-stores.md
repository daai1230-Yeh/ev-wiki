# Japan: Tesla starts deploying Superchargers at 7-Eleven stores

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 06.08.2026

---

Tesla has started deploying Superchargers at Japan's 7-Eleven convenience stores, beginning with an outlet in the Shibokuchi area of Kawasaki City (Kanagawa Prefecture) — its 150th Supercharger location in the country.

The first 7-Eleven site has four Superchargers at 250 kW peak output, accessible 24/7. Tesla charges 37 yen (€0.20)/min up to 60 kW, 78 yen (€0.43) at 60–100 kW, 122 yen (€0.67) at 100–180 kW, and 200 yen (€1.10) at 180–250 kW. A five-minute grace period after charging ends is followed by an idle fee of up to 100 yen (€0.55)/minute if half or more of the station is occupied. No Destination Chargers were installed at this location.

Days after opening the Supercharger site, Tesla began offering Model Y test drives at 7-Eleven stores. By end of March 2027, Tesla plans to expand 7-Eleven Superchargers to about ten outlets in Japan.

Two more Japanese Supercharger sites have opened since — at MALera Gifu mall (Motosu City, Gifu Prefecture) and Aeon Mall Kashiihama (Fukuoka) — each with four 250 kW stalls, bringing the national network to 152 locations.

Tesla operates 40 stores in Japan (including two pop-ups) and will open its 41st in Osaka City on August 8, with a Reuters report indicating plans to grow to at least 60 outlets. The company aims to become Japan's leading imported car brand as early as 2027; the Model Y was the top-selling foreign car brand model in Japan in May.
