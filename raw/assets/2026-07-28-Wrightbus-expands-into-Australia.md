# Wrightbus expands into Australia, showcasing zero emission bus portfolio at Australasia Bus & Coach Expo

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 16 July 2026

---

Wrightbus has officially launched operations in Australia, introducing its battery-electric and hydrogen bus range as part of its international expansion strategy. The official unveiling took place at the Australasia Bus & Coach Expo in Sydney on 15 July, where the company showcased three vehicles: the Kite battery-electric single-deck bus, the Kite hydrogen fuel cell single-deck bus and the StreetDeck 6×2 battery-electric double-decker.

Wrightbus's Australian business will be led by Daniel Castro as Regional Director Asia Pacific and Julian Gurney as Head of Sales for Australia and New Zealand. The launch coincides with Wrightbus's 80th anniversary; more than 3,000 zero-emission buses are currently operating across the UK and Europe, covering over 200 million emission-free kilometres. The company says its Australian business will focus on long-term collaboration with operators, public authorities and industry partners while establishing local capabilities and regional partnerships.

Both Kite models have already received approval under the Transport for New South Wales (TfNSW) Procurement Panel, allowing supply to eligible operators. The StreetDeck 6×2 double-decker follows an order for 20 units placed by City Bus in Hong Kong. Wrightbus operates manufacturing facilities in the UK and Malaysia, supported by more than 2,300 employees, and notes its first exports to Canberra took place around 30 years ago. Invest Northern Ireland is supporting the expansion through business development and international market support.

Jo Bamford, Executive Chairman of Wrightbus, said: "Australia represents a hugely important market for Wrightbus, and we are delighted to be back with a renewed focus and a long-term commitment to supporting the country's transition to zero-emission public transport."

Wrightbus Managing Director Christian Reynolds added that the company's vehicles are "already proving themselves every day in cities around the world" and framed the expansion as building partnerships and ecosystems rather than simply supplying buses.
