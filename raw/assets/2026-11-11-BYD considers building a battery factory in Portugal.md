# BYD considers building a battery factory in Portugal

**Author**: Florian Treiss | **Date**: 2026-11-10

---

BYD is reportedly considering building a battery factory in Portugal - potentially at the former Galp refinery in Matosinhos near Porto. It remains unclear whether battery cells or complete battery systems would be manufactured there. Such a battery factory would align well with a potential BYD car factory in Spain.

Chinese electric vehicle and battery manufacturer BYD is reportedly considering building a battery factory in Portugal. According to the Portuguese business newspaper Expresso, the company is already in discussions with the Portuguese government and the national trade and investment agency AICEP regarding a potential investment. The former oil refinery site of Portuguese energy company Galp in Matosinhos, near Porto, is being considered as a possible location. A source familiar with the matter states that the project is already relatively advanced.

However, there has been no official confirmation from BYD to date. Portugal's Minister of Economy, Manuel Castro Almeida, merely confirmed a "fruitful dialogue" with a Chinese automaker regarding the establishment of a plant in Portugal, without providing further details. The city administration of Matosinhos also states that it has not yet been formally informed about the project. Nevertheless, Mayor Luísa Salgueiro described such an investment as a "very welcome project".

## Former refinery site as potential location

The former Galp refinery in Matosinhos was closed in 2021. The approximately 240-hectare site near the Atlantic is currently being dismantled. Of this, 100 hectares have already been transferred to the city administration. A 40-hectare site is planned for the University of Porto, while another 20 hectares are set to become a park. The remaining areas do not yet have a designated use. According to Galp, the ongoing demolition work is expected to be completed by the end of 2026, followed by further soil remediation measures.

The location's advantages include its proximity to the Port of Leixões and excellent transport links. Additionally, the planned lithium extraction at the Barroso mine in northern Portugal is set to begin production in 2028. A battery factory in Matosinhos could therefore benefit from shorter transport routes for some of the required raw materials in the future.

## No details yet on the type of battery production

It remains unclear exactly what BYD intends to produce in Portugal. Whether the focus will be on the production of battery cells, the manufacturing of modules, and/or the assembly of complete battery packs is not yet known. BYD has the expertise for all these production steps: the company develops and manufactures its own lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cells as well as the associated battery systems.

A key in-house development is the so-called Blade Battery. Introduced in 2020 and now in its second generation, this battery technology is based on particularly long, flat LFP cells. BYD uses a cell-to-pack concept, where the cells are integrated directly into the battery pack without traditional intermediate modules. This saves space and increases pack efficiency.

## Potential battery hub for the Iberian Peninsula

According to Expresso, the potential factory could serve as a production hub for the entire Iberian Peninsula, including a potential future car factory for BYD in Spain. Besides Spain, France is also being considered as a potential location for further investments. In Hungary, BYD is already constructing its first European passenger car plant in Szeged.

Meanwhile, Portugal is actively seeking investments in the battery industry. Chinese cell manufacturer CALB is already planning a battery factory in Sines, an investment of around two billion euros with a planned production capacity of 15 GWh per year.

However, it remains uncertain whether BYD will indeed invest in Matosinhos. No details have yet been provided regarding the potential investment amount, production capacity, or number of jobs.
