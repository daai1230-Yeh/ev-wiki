# Switzerland: VMCV orders up to 47 Yutong electric buses

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 24.11.2026 - 16:30

---

Swiss public transport operator VMCV has signed a framework agreement with Yutong for the delivery of up to 47 electric buses. The Chinese brand won the tender against three competitors. The contract is worth around 30 million francs, equivalent to just under €33 million euros.

The Montreux-based ‘Transports publics Vevey–Montreux–Chillon–Villeneuve’ (VMCV) operates a public transport network along the shores and surrounding areas of Lake Geneva. It aims to transition its bus fleet to battery-electric vehicles. The corresponding tender for a five-year framework contract – covering up to 47 electric buses and charging infrastructure – has now been awarded to Yutong, as reported by the portal Magyarbusz. For the Chinese bus manufacturer, this marks the second success in the Swiss market this year, following a tender won in Zurich over the summer.

VMCV’s network serves municipalities including Vevey, Montreux, La Tour-de-Peilz, Clarens and Villeneuve. In 2025, the company reported carrying over 11.17 million passengers and covering a total of approx. 3.22 million kilometres with its buses. In addition to urban and interurban bus routes, VMCV also operates a trolleybus network, where it has already been using battery-electric trolleybuses from Van Hool since 2020.

Now, the operator also plans to increasingly electrify its conventional bus routes. VMCV specifically tendered for battery-electric midi, solo and articulated buses for urban and suburban services. Out of four bidders, Yutong emerged as the winner. The 47 electric vehicles within five years represent the contractually agreed upper limit. A first fixed order under the framework agreement has not yet been placed. magyarbusz.info
