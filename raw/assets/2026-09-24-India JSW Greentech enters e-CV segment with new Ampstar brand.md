# India: JSW Greentech enters e-CV segment with new Ampstar brand

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-09-23

---

JSW Greentech, a subsidiary of Indian conglomerate JSW Group, has announced its entry into the electric commercial vehicle segment with a new brand called Ampstar. It plans to launch a wide range of locally developed and manufactured buses and trucks under the new brand.

The company has set up a greenfield EV manufacturing facility spanning 90 acres in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra. It has installed an annual production capacity of 15,000 EVs, including 10,000 buses and 5,000 trucks, although it can flexibly allocate the volume between buses and trucks. The company will begin expanding once it has utilised 50 to 70 per cent of the capacity, and it could bring annual capacity to 25,000 vehicles within the existing plant.

JSW Greentech has invested 25 billion rupees (~€229 million) in its electric commercial vehicle business and allocated a significant share to in-house development. It says it has developed the vehicle platform; the vehicle control unit (VCU); a four-in-one unit integrating the power distribution unit, DC-DC converter, steering pump inverter drive and air compressor inverter drive; and the base logic, software and algorithms for the entire electric drivetrain.

CEO Sumit Mittal said at the brand launch on 22 September that JSW Greentech is targeting a localisation of 50 per cent for the electric trucks within six months and around 75 per cent within nine to twelve months, similar to that of electric buses. Parth Jindal, Managing Director of JSW Cement and JSW Paints, said the subsidiary will participate in government tenders for buses and initially focus on B2B contracts for trucks. Customers will get multiple choices, including outright purchase, leasing, Battery as a Service (BaaS), and usage-based models, charging per-tonne for trucks and per-kilometre for buses.

JSW Greentech plans to offer electric bus models from seven to 18 metres for staff, intercity, school, and urban transportation. Initially, it has introduced a nine-metre city bus, a twelve-metre staff bus, and a twelve-metre city bus.

The twelve-metre staff bus features a 302 kWh LFP battery pack that supports dual-gun CCS2 charging and delivers a range of 200 km. It can climb grades of up to 18 per cent and reach 80 km/h. It comes in two versions, with 45 pushback seats or 52 high headrest seats, with air suspension at both ends, a 360° camera system, USB charging points, and ADAS.

In the truck category, JSW Greentech has introduced a 55-tonne model in 4×2 tractor-trailer, 6×4 tractor-trailer, 6×4 tipper, and 8×4 tipper versions. It uses a 282 kWh LFP battery pack with dual-gun CCS2 charging as standard, in fixed or swappable configurations. The swappable pack can be replaced in seven minutes under ideal conditions.

Speaking to electrive, Mittal revealed that the company is working on a 400 kWh fixed battery pack expected in three to four years. The 282 kWh unit delivers a range of 140 to 150 km, while the 400 kWh unit should allow around 200 km.

The 55-tonne truck has a 420 kW motor and a six- or four-speed automated manual transmission, and can climb grades of up to 27 per cent. It has a sleeper cabin for long-haul operations and an air-suspended driver's seat.

Mittal said JSW Greentech is awaiting final approvals and expects to start both bus and truck production within a month. The company estimates it will deliver 500 to 2,000 EVs between October 2026 and March 2027. Jindal indicated the new brand will have a healthy order bank from the beginning, with plans to sign deals for about 3,000 trucks on launch day.
