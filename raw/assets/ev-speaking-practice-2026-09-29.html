1. Warm-Up 暖身 3 min

1.If you could choose, would you rather own a car with a battery from a famous, proven supplier, or one made by the carmaker itself? Why?

如果可以選，你會想要用知名、久經考驗的電池供應商，還是車廠自家做的電池？為什麼？

2.Think about a product you use every day. What would happen to you if the one company that makes its key part suddenly raised prices?

想想你每天使用的某樣產品。如果唯一生產關鍵零件的公司突然漲價，你會受到什麼影響？

3.When a big factory project gets delayed by two years, is that usually bad news, or can it be a smart decision? Why?

當大型工廠計畫延後兩年，通常是壞消息，還是有可能是聰明的決定？為什麼？

2. Read Aloud 朗讀短文 6 min

Something unusual is happening in the world's biggest battery market. Over the past four months, shares of CATL, China's battery giant, have fallen about 38 percent, wiping more than 1.4 trillion off its market value, according to a Hong Kong report. The trigger was a wave of carmakers changing their battery suppliers, which the media quickly labeled the "de-CATL" storm.

全球最大的電池市場出現不尋常的變化。根據一則香港媒體報導，過去四個月中國電池龍頭寧德時代的股價下跌約38%，市值蒸發超過1.4萬億。導火線是多家車廠密集調整電池供應商，被媒體冠上「去寧德化」風暴的稱號。

Li Auto is a good example. In 2023 it added second suppliers, but only for its cheaper models. This September, it announced that its whole lineup will use in-house batteries, including 5C fast-charging cells. It also invested 2.65 billion yuan in Sunwoda, one of its new suppliers, while Xiaomi brought in two more suppliers for its own battery design.

理想汽車就是個好例子。2023年它只在較便宜的車款引進第二供應商；今年9月則宣布全系列改用自研電池，包括5C超充電池。它還斥資26.5億元入股新供應商欣旺達動力，而小米也為自己定義的電池設計引進另外兩家供應商。

Analysts point to three reasons. First, cost: the battery makes up 30 to 40 percent of an electric car's price. In the first half of this year, CATL earned a net profit of 43.3 billion yuan, more than the combined profit of over a dozen major listed carmakers. Second, control over how the car is designed. Third, supply security, so no single supplier can lock a carmaker into one technology.

分析師指出三個原因。第一是成本：電池佔電動車成本的30%到40%。今年上半年，寧德時代淨利達433億元，比十多家主流上市車廠的獲利總和還高。第二是對整車設計的自主權。第三是供應鏈安全，避免單一供應商把車廠綁死在同一種技術路線上。

Beijing did not like the story. A media outlet run by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology published a commentary arguing that the "de-CATL" label was a media invention. Adding suppliers, it said, is normal business, and turning it into a fight between carmakers and battery makers helps no one. One professor added that rivals have simply narrowed the technology gap.

北京並不喜歡這種說法。工信部旗下媒體刊出評論，認為「去寧德化」是輿論炒作出來的標籤；增加供應商是正常的商業行為，把它變成車廠與電池廠的對立對誰都沒有好處。一位教授則補充，其他供應商只是縮小了與寧德時代的技術差距。

The same pressure is visible in the West. Volkswagen's battery unit, PowerCo, has pushed back its plant in St. Thomas, Ontario, from 2027 to 2029. The factory was planned to produce up to 90 gigawatt-hours of cells a year, and construction began in 2025. The company blames shifting demand, new technology, and long-term strategy.

西方也感受到同樣的壓力。福斯旗下的電池公司PowerCo，把安大略省聖托馬斯工廠的投產時間從2027年延到2029年。這座工廠原規劃年產最高90吉瓦時電芯，2025年已動工。公司將原因歸咎於需求變化、新技術與長期策略。

PowerCo insists it is not stepping back. Its procurement chief says the project is about getting the pacing right, and a new general contractor will grow the site from about 60 workers to roughly 1,300 at peak. Still, Volkswagen is reportedly looking for an investor, and there are rumors that the Chinese cell maker Gotion could join its factory in Spain.

PowerCo堅稱並未退縮。採購長表示，重點是把步調拿捏好；新的總承包商將讓工地人數從約60人增加到高峰期約1,300人。不過據報導福斯正在尋找投資人，也有傳聞中國電池廠國軒高科可能加入其西班牙工廠。

So who really controls the battery? For carmakers, it is a balancing act between cost, control, and risk. For suppliers, it is a reminder that even a market leader can be replaced. And for governments, it raises a bigger question: whether a secure supply chain means building at home or simply buying from more places.

那麼，到底是誰掌握了電池？對車廠而言，這是成本、掌控權與風險之間的平衡；對供應商而言，這提醒了即使是市場龍頭也可能被取代；對政府而言，則帶出更大的問題：供應鏈安全究竟是要在本地生產，還是向更多地方採購。

5. Useful Expressions 實用句型 3 min

"X has lost N percent of its value in just N months."
描述股價或數據在短時間內大幅下跌。
CATL has lost about 38 percent of its value in just four months.
"The main reasons are, first ___, second ___, and third ___."
條理分明地列出多個原因，適合說明成因。
The main reasons are, first, cost, second, control, and third, supply security.
"It's less about ___ and more about ___."
強調事情的本質，糾正常見的說法。
It's less about abandoning CATL and more about not depending on one supplier.
"On the one hand ___, but on the other hand ___."
呈現兩面看法，適合討論有爭議的議題。
On the one hand, in-house batteries give control, but on the other hand, they are expensive to develop.
"That depends on whether ___."
表達有條件的看法，讓回答更有彈性。
Whether it's a smart delay depends on whether demand really is shifting.

6. Discussion 討論題 5 min

1.Do you think carmakers are right to reduce their dependence on one battery supplier, or is it better to build a deep partnership with one? Choose a side and defend it.

你認為車廠減少對單一電池供應商的依賴是正確的，還是與單一供應商建立深度合作比較好？請選邊並說明理由。

2.The Chinese ministry-backed media said the "de-CATL" story was exaggerated. How much do you trust official comments like that? Why?

官方媒體說「去寧德化」被誇大了。你對這類官方評論有多信任？為什麼？

3.Should a company that earns far more profit than its customers be worried, or is that a sign of a healthy business?

如果一家公司的獲利遠高於它的客戶，這值得擔心，還是代表它經營得很健康？

4.PowerCo delayed its Canadian plant by two years. Is this a sensible response to shifting demand, or a sign that the EV transition is slowing? Give both views.

PowerCo把加拿大工廠延後兩年。這是對需求變化的合理因應，還是電動車轉型放緩的訊號？請說明兩種觀點。

5.If your country wanted a secure battery supply chain, would you build factories at home or buy from many different countries? What are the risks of each?

如果你的國家想建立安全的電池供應鏈，你會在國內設廠，還是向許多不同國家採購？各有什麼風險？

6.Would the brand of the battery influence your decision when buying an EV? Which would matter more to you: the battery brand or the price of the car?

電池的品牌會影響你購買電動車的決定嗎？電池品牌與車價，哪一個對你更重要？

7. Wrap-Up 總結 2 min

60-second summary: In short — CATL's share price has dropped about 38 percent in four months as carmakers like Li Auto and Xiaomi diversify suppliers and build in-house batteries, driven by cost, control, and supply security. Beijing calls the "de-CATL" idea an exaggeration. Meanwhile, Volkswagen's PowerCo pushed its Canadian gigafactory from 2027 to 2029, showing that even the battery build-out is being paced more carefully.

60秒摘要：簡而言之，隨著理想、小米等車廠分散供應商並自研電池（出於成本、自主權與供應鏈安全考量），寧德時代股價四個月下跌約38%；北京則認為「去寧德化」是誇大之詞。同時，福斯旗下PowerCo把加拿大超級工廠從2027年延到2029年，顯示電池產能的擴張步調也變得更為謹慎。
