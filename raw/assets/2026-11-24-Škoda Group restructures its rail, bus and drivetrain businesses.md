# Škoda Group restructures its rail, bus and drivetrain businesses

**Author**: Victoria Zippmann | **Date**: 24.11.2026 - 12:45

---

Škoda Group is reorganising its business to improve efficiency and place greater emphasis on its products and services. As part of the changes, Škoda Vagonka will be integrated into existing subsidiary Škoda Transportation, while Škoda Electric will be split into two joint-stock companies. Further changes are planned for 2027.

The first step in the restructuring of Škoda Group involves its subsidiary, Škoda Vagonka a.s., which is based in the Ostrava region (Czech Republic) and manufactures rail vehicles. On 1 November 2026, the company was fully integrated into Škoda Transportation a.s., headquartered in Plzeň. Škoda Transportation also manufactures rail vehicles. Together with Škoda Pars k.s. and Škoda Ekova a.s., Škoda Transportation a.s. now forms the new Rolling Stock Division. Škoda Pars k.s. specialises in modernising, maintaining, and repairing trains and other rail vehicles, while Škoda Ekova a.s. provides the same services for tram vehicles.

Tomáš Ignačák, Deputy CEO of Škoda Transportation a.s., commented: “Many people may continue to say they work at Vagonka, as the name has become familiar in the region. However, Škoda Group is evolving into a leading global player across the public transport sector, and our customers throughout Europe, Asia and the Americas look for the Škoda heritage, which for them represents a guarantee of quality, reliability and innovation.”

The second restructuring will take place on 1 January 2027 at Škoda Electric a.s. in Plzeň. The company will be split into two independent joint-stock companies, each continuing the existing business areas as separate entities. The former trolleybus and battery-electric bus division will operate as Škoda Group Bus a.s. and serve as the operational hub of the new Bus Division.

Karel Majer, CEO of Škoda Electric a.s. for the Bus Division, explained: “The creation of a standalone Bus Division is a logical step in the further development of our zero-emission road transport activities. Our ambition is to build on our leading position in the trolleybus segment, a field in which we have been active for 90 years, while significantly strengthening our presence in the battery-electric bus market, which offers substantial growth potential in the years ahead. The new organizational structure will enable us to devote maximum attention to this business area and further enhance our competitiveness both domestically and internationally”

The division for electrical components, such as traction motors and battery systems for rail and road vehicles, will be absorbed into the future Škoda Group Components and Systems a.s. and assume operational leadership of a new Components and Systems Division.

Jaromír Šilhánek, Board Member of Škoda Electric a.s. for the Components and Systems Division, stated: “We develop a wide range of innovative traction and battery systems with high added value for different types of rail and road vehicles, supplying not only companies within the Škoda Group but also many highly respected customers worldwide. This change will allow us to focus even more effectively on product development, manufacturing, sales and service.”
