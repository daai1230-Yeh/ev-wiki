# Rivian Is Selling More Vans Than R1S And R1T EVs Combined

**Author**: Iulian Dnistran | **Date**: 2026-10-28

---

The lovable electric van was the fourth-best-selling EV in America in the third quarter, beating all of its competitors hands down.

Rivian's long-term success is deeply linked to the R2, but until the new mid-size SUV becomes widely available, an unsung hero is carrying the startup forward. In Q3, the Rivian Commercial Van (RCV) was the company's best-selling model, beating all of its competitors combined, as well as the R1S and R1T combined. According to Cox Automotive data, it sold 8,469 units from July through September, up 220.9% year over year. That places it fourth among best-selling EVs in America, after the Tesla Model Y, Tesla Model 3, and Hyundai Ioniq 5.

Year to date through September 30, the van sold 15,685 units, up 130.4% from 6,806. The second-best-selling electric van in Q3 was the discontinued Chevrolet BrightDrop with 1,344 sales, mostly spurred by aggressive GM discounts, followed by the Mercedes-Benz eSprinter (396), Ford E-Transit (184) and Ram ProMaster EV (157).

With the R2 now on sale, interest in the more expensive R1T and R1S has waned by roughly 30%. The R1S sold 5,582 units in Q3 2026 (-31.8%) and the R1T 1,732 (-27.2%); combined 7,314, which is 870 fewer than the van. Rivian sold 3,456 R2s.

A big part of the RCV's success is the Amazon deal for 100,000 electric delivery vehicles by 2030 — Rivian has delivered roughly 50,000 so far. After Amazon exclusivity ended in 2023, the van became available to any U.S. company, with prices starting at $79,900 for the short-wheelbase Delivery 500 (161 miles of range, 2,663-pound payload). It is currently front-wheel drive only; a dual-motor AWD version and a larger, longer-range battery are in the works.

| Model | Q3 2026 | YTD 2026 |
|---|---|---|

| Chevrolet BrightDrop | 1,344 | 2,300 |
| Mercedes-Benz eSprinter | 396 | 913 |
| Ford E-Transit | 184 | 677 |

*Data from Cox Automotive*
