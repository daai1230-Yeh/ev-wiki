# Geely E5 review – a successful electric debut at a competitive price

**Source**: electrive | **Date**: 2026-07-23

---

Geely has launched in Europe with its own brand presence, modern design and competent technology. The electric SUV E5 enters the popular but competitive compact segment.

The Geely E5 delivers a convincing debut across Spain, Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Hungary, Italy and the UK. German prices for the Pro version, featuring a 60.2 kWh battery (58.41 kWh net), start at a competitive €37,990. The mid-range Pro+ trim, with the larger 68.4 kWh battery (66.33 kWh net), is priced at €39,990. The top-tier Max+ trim includes extras such as a panoramic roof and premium audio, bringing the price to €42,990.

Measuring 4.61 metres in length, the model targets the high-volume C-segment, aiming to appeal to a broad range of buyers with solid range, modern technology, and an attractive entry-level price. Geely combines generous space, practical LFP batteries, and modern infotainment into a compelling package. A standout feature is the eight-year or 200,000-kilometre warranty, which the Chinese manufacturer hopes will convince even the most sceptical buyers.
