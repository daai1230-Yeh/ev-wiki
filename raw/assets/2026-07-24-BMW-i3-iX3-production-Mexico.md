# Production of BMW i3 and iX3 in Mexico remains on track for 2027

**Date**: 24 July 2026

---

BMW will start building the Neue Klasse i3 and iX3 at its San Luis Potosí plant in Mexico starting in 2027, investing €800 million in the plant including a new assembly facility for sixth-generation high-voltage batteries. The iX3 is expected to begin Mexican production in August 2027, roughly two years after it first launched at Debrecen, while the i3 Sedan will be assembled at San Luis Potosí from 2028.

San Luis Potosí becomes one of only four BMW production sites worldwide selected to assemble Neue Klasse vehicles, alongside Debrecen (Hungary), Munich (Germany), and Shenyang (China).
