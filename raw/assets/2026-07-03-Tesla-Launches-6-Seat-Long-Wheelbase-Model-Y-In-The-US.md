# Tesla Launches 6-Seat Long-Wheelbase Model Y In The U.S. For $61,990

**Author**: Tim Levin | **Date**: 2026-07-02

---

Tesla launched the Model Y L in the U.S., a version of the Model Y that debuted in China last year featuring a slightly stretched wheelbase, more interior space and six seats. Sales kick off with an all-wheel-drive Launch edition priced at $61,990 — $16,000 more than the regular Model Y Premium AWD. EPA range is rated at 325 miles, with 0-60 mph in 4.4 seconds. The car looks nearly identical to the standard Model Y apart from a longer wheelbase and a slightly raised roofline over the third row.

Inside, the Model Y L adds captain's chairs for the second row and two more seats behind it; the first two rows get heated and ventilated seats, and the third row gets seat heaters as well. It's Tesla's latest move to boost sales without a brand-new model, following last year's cheaper, more basic Model Y and Model 3 trims that helped offset the loss of the EV tax credit. Tesla already offers a $2,500 seven-seat option on the standard Model Y, but the Y L's third row is billed as more usable — positioned to help recapture sales lost from the discontinued three-row Model X. The Launch Series includes 12 months of free Full Self-Driving (Supervised) and 12 months of free Supercharging.

The launch comes as Tesla has struggled to grow U.S. sales without a new mainstream model, with the Cybertruck falling well short of Musk's stated 250,000-unit annual sales target.
