# European MAN partners invest in service and charging network

**Author**: Cora Werwitzke | **Date**: 2026-09-03

---

MAN Truck & Bus looks set to invest significantly more in the development of its service network by 2030 than previously planned. Thanks to dealers and partners, a total of around €700m will now flow into the further expansion and development of the European service and sales network by the end of 2030.

In spring, MAN announced plans to invest €300 million in its own network. Now, MAN's dealers, service partners and importers are also planning to invest around €400m in their MAN service locations across Europe. Around one-third of the planned investments will specifically flow into electromobility and digitalisation — from high-voltage training for service staff to battery repair centres and publicly accessible charging points at MAN branches.

"The scale of these investments is unprecedented in MAN's history," said Friedrich Baumann, Board Member for Sales and Customer Solutions at MAN.

The charging network expansion is being carried out in cooperation with energy company E.On, first announced in 2024. The partners aim for up to 400 charging points at up to 170 locations across Europe, including up to 125 sites in Germany alone. Operational locations already named include Berlin-Wildau, Fürstenwalde and Karlsfeld in Germany, Eugendorf in Austria, and Čestlice in the Czech Republic.

MAN already has a dense network: around 1,200 MAN-owned and partner businesses employing approximately 7,000 people across Europe, plus more than 2,000 service points and cooperation partners through its MAN Mobile24 breakdown and mobility service. In Germany alone, the network comprises over 340 locations.

Baumann said the investment reflects rising competition, including from Chinese truck brands set to appear at IAA Transportation and bus brands like Yutong and BYD that are already competitive: "The commercial vehicle business is significantly more complex and service-intensive than the passenger car business... we need to be very close to our customers and offer top-notch service."

MAN's goal is for almost 80 percent of customers to have a travel time under 30 minutes to the nearest MAN service location, prioritised in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, the UK, Italy, Spain, Poland and Turkey, averaging seven new branches per year in those markets. New locations are already underway near Lisbon, Portugal, and in Kaunas, Lithuania, with long-standing partner Adampolis Group.
