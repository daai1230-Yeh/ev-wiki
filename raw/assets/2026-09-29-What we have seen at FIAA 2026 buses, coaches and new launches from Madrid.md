# What we have seen at FIAA 2026: buses, coaches and new launches from Madrid

**Author**: Editorial Staff | **Date**: 2026-09-28

---

The 2026 edition of FIAA in Madrid brought new bus and coach products, including the 15-metre Irizar i3 electric, the European-homologated Marcopolo–Volvo Paradiso G8, the Iveco Bus eFlexy and a new 13-metre BYD–Castrosua Low Entry; Farizon and Zeroid made their first appearances.

## Sustainable Bus Awards 2027 (Madrid, 23 September)

- Urban: Solaris Urbino 18 Hydrogen New Generation (fuel cell electric)
- Intercity: BYD B12.b HF (battery-electric)
- Coach: Irizar i6S Efficient Scania PHEV (plug-in hybrid)

FIAA also hosted a stop of the Sustainable Bus Tour 2026, with McKinsey (Dr. Lena Bell, Dr. Tobias Schneiderbauer) on the competitive landscape and autonomous driving, operators (Moventis, ANAV, Arriva Spain) on funding and operators' growing role in batteries, charging and energy management, and manufacturers/suppliers (IVECO BUS, BYD Europe, Otokar Europe, ZF) on partnerships, local content and EU regulation.

## Manufacturer by manufacturer (summary)

- **Anadolu Isuzu**: Kendo hybrid, Grand Toro diesel coach (Cummins), NovoCiti Volt battery-electric.
- **BYD**: 13 m BYD–Castrosua Low Entry (Class I/II, up to 55 passengers, 560 kWh battery, CCS2/pantograph/"double-gun" charging up to 384 kW); BYD B12.b HF high-floor Class II with Blade batteries integrated in the chassis (launched end 2025).
- **Daimler Buses**: six buses incl. eCitaro and eIntouro; 900 units registered in Spain in 2026 (>20% market share).
- **Farizon (Geely)**: C12E 12 m low-floor e-bus, ~90 passengers, CATL LFP 422 kWh (466/512 options), 215 kW nominal / 350 kW peak; 13 m Low Entry version due early 2027.
- **Ferqui / Zeroid**: eSenda 7.49 m midibus, 19 passengers, 120–140 kWh battery, up to 325 km SORT range.
- **Higer**: F6 Integral EV (Fencer, now fully Higer-developed), Azure 9 urban, V13 coach.
- **Indcar**: aims to be Europe's largest minibus maker; now also bodying Mercedes Sprinter after acquiring CarBusNet.
- **Irizar**: 15 m three-axle i3 electric, 380 kW nominal / 400 kW peak, 528 kWh battery, up to 65 seated + 40 standing.
- **Iveco Bus**: eFlexy DRT electric van (Ducato base, Olmedo conversion), 205 kW motor, 110 kWh, >300 km range, deliveries from Q1 2027.
- **Karsan**: e-Jest, e-Atak, e-Ata Low Entry; will offer diesel buses in Europe from this autumn; >2,500 e-buses worldwide; 6.8% European share in H1 2026.
- **Marcopolo–Volvo**: European-homologated Paradiso G8 coach (13 m, 12.40 m version); first markets Italy and France.
- **Otokar**: Autonomous eCentro (deployed in an Austrian Postbus pilot), eKent, e-Territo, Navigo, Ulyso.
- **Pagliani–Zonson**: Zonson City Joy 12 battery-electric low-floor bus.
- **Solaris**: Urbino 15 LE on new modular e-bus platform; new intercity ZE model slipped to H1 2027; new assembly facility in Środa Wielkopolska (+~500 buses/yr, capacity toward 2,000/yr); new 47-ha plant planned for 2029; North American Urbino to be shown at APTA EXPO Chicago.
- **Temsa**: new-generation Prestij and battery-electric Avenue Neo 9e.
- **Unvi**: new Unvi XL coach body on Mercedes OC 500 RF chassis.
- **Yutong**: IC12E battery-electric Class II, T-series electric coach, U-range electric city bus.
- **Zeroid**: world premiere of Force Central Low Entry platform (135/170 kWh, up to 352 km SORT), eCitybus+ for GLOBAL (Gran Canaria).
