# Moto expands UK fast-charging at Exeter Hub

**Author**: Carrie Hampel | **Date**: 21.07.2026

---

In the United Kingdom, Moto Hospitality has opened its new "ultra-rapid" charging hub with charging bays with capacities of up to 400kW at its Exeter charging site. This launch significantly expands the existing electric car and truck charging infrastructure at the service station, which is now reported to be the largest electric vehicle charging hub in the country.

Capacity for electric vehicle charging has been expanded at the Exeter Mega Hub with the rollout of 24 Moto Charge ultra-rapid EV charging bays. These add to the site's existing infrastructure of 32 Tesla and 44 Gridserve high-power charging bays. The charging hub caters to both private motorists and commercial fleets with what now numbers 100 ultra-rapid charging bays for electric cars, vans or small trucks, and four dedicated charging bays for electric heavy goods vehicles (eHGVs).

The company considers the expansion of capacity at Exeter to be a key milestone in its plan to invest more than £500 million across its nationwide estate. Moto is the UK's largest motorway service station operator, with over 60 sites across the national highway network.

Daniel Kunkel, CEO of Gridserve, noted, "EV charging hubs like Exeter show what's possible when long-term infrastructure planning, grid investment and cutting-edge technology come together to support the shift to electric transport for everyone. We're proud of our partnership with Moto, both here at Exeter and beyond."

The expansion is part of Moto's wider ambition to quadruple the existing 1,000 charging bays to more than 4,000 electric vehicle charging bays across its network.
