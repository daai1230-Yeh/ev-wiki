# Volt opens Singapore's largest public e-bus charging hub

**Author**: Sagar Parikh | **Date**: 2026-07-16

---

Volt, the EV charging business of Singapore-based Keppel's Infrastructure Division, has opened the country's largest public electric bus charging hub, developed in collaboration with Setsco, a homegrown testing, inspection and certification company.

The hub, at 2E Jalan Papan in Jurong, has an installed charging capacity of 2.76 MW and allows up to 46 EVs to charge simultaneously, offering a mix of 120 kW fast chargers and 360 kW ultra-fast chargers (Volt has not disclosed the split). It will serve as a key fast-charging base for ComfortDelGro Bus (CDGB)'s electric buses in western Singapore, and is also open to electric cars and light- and heavy-duty electric goods vehicles. Cars able to use the ultra-fast chargers can charge in as little as ten minutes — benefiting models such as Lotus' Emeya and Eletre, Porsche's Taycan and Cayenne Electric, Xpeng's G6 and X9, and Zeekr's 7X.

Volt is developing the hub in phases; once fully operational it will support up to 70 EVs simultaneously (no timeline given). At the July 2024 announcement, the company said it planned to support up to 80 electric buses at once, with five 360 kW ultra-fast chargers in the first phase. Executive Director Lim Yong Wei called it "a significant milestone in Volt's efforts to scale up EV infrastructure." CDGB, Singapore's largest private electric bus fleet operator, has appointed Volt as its charging partner. Volt currently operates nearly 500 chargers across more than 150 sites in Singapore, over 50 per cent of them DC fast chargers.
